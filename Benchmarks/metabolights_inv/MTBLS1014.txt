Study Identifier	MTBLS1014
Study Title	1H HR-MAS NMR based metabolomics of cancer cells responding to two different doses of the diruthenium trithiolato complex [(p-MeC6H4iPr)2Ru2(SC6H4-p-But)3]+ (DiRu-1)
Study Description	The trithiolato bridged diruthenium complex DiRu-1 [(p-MeC6H4iPr)2Ru2(SC6H4-p-But)3]+ is highly cytotoxic against various cancer cell lines, but its exact mode of action remained unknown. The present 1H HR-MAS NMR based metabolomic study performed on the ovarian cancer cell lines A2780, its cisPt resistant variant A2780cisR and on the healthy cell line model HEK-293 treated with two doses of DiRu-1 was designed to reveal its mode of action. The resulting changes in the metabolic profile of the cell lines were studied using HR-MAS NMR of cell lysates and subsequent statistical analysis. We show that DiRu-1 in a 0.03 µM dose has significant impact on the levels of a number of metabolites, such as glutamine, glutamate, glutathione, cysteine, lipid, creatine, lactate, and acetate, especially pronounced in the A2780cisR cell line. Overall, the metabolic changes observed suggest that redox homeostasis, the Warburg effect and the lipid metabolism are affected by DiRu-1.

Study Protocol Description	In each group 10 independently prepared samples have been measured, leading to a total number of 90 measurements (10 x 3 x 3; 3 cell lines, 3 groups per cell line: control, low, high dose). The cells were firstly seeded in 25 cm^2 flasks with a density of 8 x 10^4 cells/cm^2 and left to grow for 24 h at 37 °C in a humidified incubator in the presence of 5% CO2. Afterwards, the medium in which the cells grew up was disposed. Subsequently, the cells were washed with 5 mL PBS. </br></br> For drug treatment, 5 ml of fresh DMEM or RPMI-1640 medium respectively, were added to the flasks containing 3 µl aqueous solutions of DiRu-1 to reach a final concentration of 0.03 µM or 0.015 µM, respectively or solely H2O (controls). After 24 h incubation, the medium has been disposed and cells were washed with 5 ml PBS. Next, trypsin/EDTA was added, and after cca. 5 min, when the cells dettached, 1 ml of DMEM was added and cells were transferred into new Falcon tubes. Cell count was determined using 10 µl 0.4% Trypan Blue Stain for 10 µl of cell suspension. An automatic cell counter was used. The cells were further centrifuged and after disposal of the supernatant resuspended in freezing medium (10 ml FBS, 9 ml DMEM, 1 ml DMSO). The cell suspension was transferred into cryovials and kept frozen at -80 °C.	No extractions were carried out in this study.	The cells were thawed at 37 °C, transferred into 1 ml preheated medium, and washed three times with PBS. The pellet was resuspended in 20 µl PBS-D2O, sonicated for 30 s and finally frozen in liquid N2 and stored at -80 °C. Each sample was thawed at 37 °C directly before measurement. The cell lysate was pipetted into a 4 mm MAS rotor and the volume was regulated with a 12 µl insert.	The rotor has been inserted into a dual inverse 1H-13C HR-MAS probe-head of a 500 MHz spectrometer (Avance II, Bruker BioSpin) and left to equilibrate with the temperature set to 272 K while being spun at 5 kHz at the magic angle. The measurements followed the equilibration.	2 types of one dimensional 1H NMR spectra both with presaturation of the water resonance have been recorded for each sample, a 1D NOE spectrum, using the pulseprogram noesypr1d, taken from the Bruker pulse sequence library and a 1D PROJECT spectrum applying a T2-filter of 102.4 ms for suppression of broad components. The spectra were recorded with a spectral width of 6002 Hz (12 ppm), 32768 data points, 4 s relaxation delay, 512 scans for NOESY and 1024 scans for PROJECT, respectively.	All spectra have been processed using an exponential multiplication with a line broadening of 1 Hz and calibrated with respect to the phosphocholine resonance at 3.23 ppm. The baseline correction has been performed independently for 2 regions: the upfield (0.350-4.793 ppm) part and the downfield (5.575-9.001 ppm) part for avoiding the residual water resonance. 3 poor quality samples have been excluded from the study.	2D 1H-1H TOCSY (dipsi2phpr from the Bruker pulse sequence library) and 2D 1H J-resolved experiments (jresgpprqf from the Bruker pulse sequence library) were recorded for 2 samples of each group to help with the assignment of individual metabolites.

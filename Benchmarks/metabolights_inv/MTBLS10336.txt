Study Protocol Description	<p>An HFpEF mouse model was established using a high-fat diet and Nω-Nitro-L-arginine methyl ester hydrochloride (L-NAME). Feces of HFpEF and control mice were collected aseptically, placed into sterile frozen tubes, snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored in a -80 °C refrigerator.</p>	<p>Stool samples (50 mg), with 200 μL H2O and 800 μL acetonitrile:methanol (1:1, containing isotope internal standards) were transferred into new tubes and homogenized at 40 Hz for 4 min, and sonicated in an ice-water bath for 5 min. This homogenization-sonication cycle was repeated 3 times, and samples were incubated at -40 °C for 2 h. Then, the samples were centrifuged at 12,000 rpm and 4 °C for 15 min, and 800 μL of the supernatant from each sample was transferred to a new tube and dried with a centrifugal concentrator. Subsequently, 160 μL of 60% acetonitrile was added to reconstitute the dried samples, and tubes were vortexed until the powder was dissolved, followed by centrifugation at 12,000 rpm and 4 °C for 15 min. Finally, 100 μL of the supernatant from each sample was transferred into a glass vial for LC-MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p>The LC separation was carried out using an ACQUITY UPLC System (H-Class, Waters), equipped with a Waters Atlantis Premier BEH Z-HILIC Column (1.7 µm, 2.1 mm x 150 mm). The mobile phase A was mixed with H2O and acetonitrile (8:2), containing 10 mmol/L ammonium acetate, and the mobile phase B was mixed with H2O and acetonitrile (1:9) containing 10 mmol/L ammonium acetate. The mobile phase A and B were adjusted to pH 9.0 with aqueous ammonia. The auto-sampler temperature was set at 8 °C and the injection volume was 1 μL.</p>	<p>A SCIEX 6500 QTRAP+ mass spectrometer was used for assay development, with typical ion source parameters: Curtain Gas = 35 psi, IonSpray Voltage = +5000 V / -4500 V, Temperature = 400 °C, Ion Source Gas 1 = 50 psi, Ion Source Gas 2 = 50 psi.</p>	<p>Raw data files generated by LC-MS/MS were processed with SCIEX Analyst Work Station Software (1.6.3), and metabolites quantification was analyzed with BIOTREE Bio Bud (2.1.2).</p>	<p>The metabolites were identified with standard.</p>

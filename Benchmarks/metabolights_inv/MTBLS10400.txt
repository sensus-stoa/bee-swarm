Study Title	Illuminating the dark space of neutral glycosphingolipidome by selective enrichment and profiling at multi-structural levels
Study Description	<p>Glycosphingolipids (GSLs) are essential components of cell membranes, particularly enriched in the nervous system. Altered molecular distributions of GSLs are increasingly associated with human diseases, emphasizing the significance of lipidomic profiling. Traditional GSL analysis methods are hampered by matrix effect from phospholipids and the difficulty in distinguishing structural isomers. Herein, we introduce a highly sensitive workflow that harnesses magnetic TiO2 nanoparticle-based selective enrichment, charge-tagging Paternò–Büchi reaction, and liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry. This approach enables mapping over 300 distinct GSLs in brain tissues by defining sugar types, long chain bases, N-acyl chains, and the locations of desaturation and hydroxylation. Relative quantitation of GSLs across multiple structural levels provides evidence of dysregulated gene and protein expressions of FA2H and CerS2 in human glioma tissue. Based on the structural features of GSLs, our method accurately differentiates human glioma with/without isocitrate dehydrogenase genetic mutation, and normal brain tissue.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Lipid standards and polar lipid extract from porcine brain were purchased from Avanti polar lipids, Inc (Alabaster, AL, USA) and Cayman Chemical (Ann Arbor, Michigan, USA). Organic solvents, salts, PB reagents and antibodies were obtained commercially. The nomenclature of glycosphingolipids (GSLs), details for the synthesis of TiO2 MNPs, protocols for lipid extraction, total fatty acid analysis, western blotting and qRT-PCR are provided in the Supplementary information of the paper associated with the study. The human brain tissue samples were provided by Huashan Hospital, Fudan University, China. Informed consent was obtained from all participants. Procedures related to these samples were compliant with ethical regulations set by the Ethical Review Board of Tsinghua University (IRB No. 20180030).</p>	<p><strong>Extraction and derivatization of brain tissue</strong></p><p>Lipid extraction was performed using a modified Folch method. Briefly, a 25 mg sample of brain tissue was placed in a 10 mL centrifuge tube. 1 mL of deionized water was added, and the tissue was homogenized using a handheld homogenizer (Jingxin Technology) at 40,000 Hz for 5 min. Subsequently, 1 mL of methanol (MeOH) and 2 mL of chloroform were added, and the tube was agitated for 10 min. The mixture was then centrifuged at 12,960 x g for 10 min, and the bottom layer was extracted and transferred to another tube. This extraction process was repeated once. The bottom layers from both extractions were combined and dried under a flow of nitrogen. Then, 1 mL of MeOH was added to resuspend the extracts, which were stored at -20 °C for further analysis.</p><p><br></p><p>The Paternò-Büchi derivatization was performed using a homemade flow microreactor. GSLs enriched from 100 μg of porcine brain lipids or lipid extracts from 400 μg of human brain tissue were mixed with 10 mM <strong>2-acetylpyridine</strong> in a 100 μL buffer (acetonitrile/H2O, 5:1 v/v). The solution was deoxygenated with nitrogen for 10 min and then injected into the flow microreactor for 15 s of UV irradiation (~254 nm).</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Selective enrichment of GSLs</strong></p><p>The lipids and IS (HexCer 18:1;O2/15:0) were mixed with TiO2 MNPs in loading buffer (ACN/aqueous NH3, 94/6, v/v, 400 μL) before subjection to 1 h agitation. TiO2 MNPs were separated by magnet and washed by washing buffer (MeOH/aqueous NH3, 96/4, v/v, 20 mM NH4HCO3) 2 times (2 × 400 μL). GSLs were released by 2 h agitation in eluting buffer (MeOH/FA, 95/5, v/v). We mixed a total of 40 μg of 3 phospholipid standards representative of PC, PE, and SM and 5 GSL standards (0.008 – 2 μg each), including HexCer 18:1;O2/15:0, HexCer 18:1;O2/18:1, HexCer 18:1;O2/24:1, HexCer 18:1;O2/18:0;2OH, and Hex2Cer 18:1;O2/17:0 with 5 mg of TiO2 MNPs and 400 μL of loading buffer.</p><p><br></p><p>For the enrichment of GSLs from porcine brain lipid extracts, 100 μg of porcine brain lipids were mixed with 5 mg of TiO2 MNPs, 100 pmol of IS (HexCer 18:1;O2/15:0), and 400 μL of loading buffer. For the enrichment of GSLs from human brain lipid extracts, lipids from 400 μg of human brain tissue was mixed with 5 mg of TiO2 MNPs, 200 pmol of IS (HexCer 18:1;O2/15:0), and 400 μL of loading buffer. The enrichment process followed the same as described above. Further details are provided in the Supplementary information.</p>	<p>All LC separations were performed on a Shimadzu Prominence LC-20AD system. Intact GSLs and derivatized GSLs were analyzed using an XBridge BEH C18 (2.5 µm, 2.1 mm x 100 mm; Waters). Mobile phase A consisted of ultrapure water with 10 mM of ammonium formate and 0.1% formic acid as additives. Mobile phase B comprised ACN and IPA in a 9:1 (v:v) ratio. Initially, mobile phase B was set at 90% and maintained for 1 min. Then, it was increased to 93% at 10 min, held at 93% for 2 min and subsequently elevated to 99% at 32 min. It remained at 99% for 3.5 min and was then reduced to 90% at 36 min. The flow rate was set to 0.5 mL/min, and the column temperature was maintained at 40 °C.&nbsp;The injection volume is set as 5 µL.</p>	<p>MS analysis was performed using a X500R QTOF mass spectrometer (SCIEX, Toronto, Canada) employing data collection modes of targeted MS/MS and Sequential Window Acquisition of All Theoretical Mass Spectra (SWATH). The MS parameters were optimized as follows: ESI voltage at 5000 V, curtain gas at 30 psi, interface heater temperature at 400 °C, nebulizing gas 1 and gas 2 at 30 psi, declustering potential at 80 V. ESI positive calibration solution X500 (SCIEX, Toronto, CA) was used to perform calibration for m/z.&nbsp;</p>	<p>SCIEX OS (3.0.03339) was used for data acquisition and processing. Explore was utilized for manual data evaluation, while Analytics was employed for peak integration.&nbsp;</p>	<p>GSL identification was carried out manually based on RPLC retention time, accurate m/z and fragmentation rules.&nbsp;</p>

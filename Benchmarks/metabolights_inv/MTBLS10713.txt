Study Title	Systemic Inflammation Induces Cholestasis via Upregulating a Short Variant of FTO to Remove the m6A Methylation in the 3’-UTR of Cyp7b1 mRNAs in liver
Study Description	The cholestasis frequently occurs in patients with inflammatory processes. However, the mechanisms of inflammation in inducing cholestasis are still unclear. Here, by using lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced cholestasis mouse models, we found that most genes encoding enzymes responsible for bile acid biosynthesis were downregulated in the hepatic tissues. Notably, among these genes, only Cyp7b1 was directly reduced by LPS in hepatocytes. The content of UDCA was decreased in the liver of inflammation-model mice which might be caused by the downregulation of Cyp7b1, and UDCA supplementation could alleviate the cholestasis and liver injury caused by LPS. Mechanistically, we observed that the level of m6A methylation in the 3’-UTR of Cyp7b1 mRNA was decreased, and inhibition of m6A methylation could reduce the expression of Cyp7b1 in Hep1-6 cells. Finally, a novel short variant FTO was identified to be upregulated in the liver of mice with LPS, and knockdown of the short variant FTO could rescue the expression of Cyp7b1 by improving the m6A modification in its mRNA, and then mitigate the cholestasis and liver injury in mice with LPS treatment. Collectively, our study uncovers that LPS-induced inflammation results in cholestasis by upregulating the expression of a short variant FTO, which subsequently leads to the reduction of Cyp7b1 mRNA by removing its m6A modification at the 3’-UTR region.

Study Protocol Description	<p>&nbsp;All the animal experiments were approved by the Experimental Animal Ethics Committee of Guangdong Pharmaceutical University. The male C57BL/6 mice were purchased from Hunan Lex Jingda Laboratory Animal Co., Ltd. (Changsha, Hunan Province, China). Mice were housed in the SPF mouse facility, at 25 degree, 12hr light-dark cycle, 60-65% humidity, with free access to water and food. After at least one week of acclimatization, the 9-week-old mice were used for experiments.</p>	<p>Targeted bile acid metabolomics</p><p>The targeted bile acids metabolomics were performed by Gene Denovo Biotechnology Company (Guangzhou, China) using the LC-MS/MS technique. To prepare the samples, the hepatic tissues from each mice were homogenized in ice-cold methanol (1mg/6μL), completely shaken for 1min, stood for 5min, and centrifuged at 12,000rpm for 10min. Then, the extraction was dried and dissolved in 200 μL methanol containing 50 ng/mL internal standards, then centrifuged at 12,000rpm for 15min.&nbsp;</p><p><br></p><p>Targeted metabolomics for 24, 25and 27-hydroxycholesterol</p><p>(1) Weigh an appropriate amount of sample (details provided in the results table) and place it into a 10 mL centrifuge tube. Add 850 μL of 0.5 M potassium hydroxide ethanol solution, grind at 60 Hz for 5 min, and ultrasonicate at 4 °C for 30 min.</p><p>(2) Place the tube in a 37 °C water bath for saponification over 4 h.</p><p>(3) After the reaction, add 1 mL of water, vortex for 30 s, and centrifuge for 15 min. Collect all the supernatant for SPE extraction.</p><p>SPE Extraction:</p><p>Conditioning 1:&nbsp;1 mL of n-hexane : isopropanol (1:1, v/v)</p><p>Conditioning 2:&nbsp;1 mL of pure water</p><p>Conditioning 3:&nbsp;2 mL of methanol</p><p>Sample Loading:&nbsp;Entire supernatant</p><p>Washing:&nbsp;4 mL of methanol : water (75:25, v/v)</p><p>Elution:&nbsp;2 mL of n-hexane : isopropanol (1:1, v/v)</p><p>The eluate is dried under a nitrogen evaporator or rotary concentrator. The residue is reconstituted in 200 μL of isopropanol : methanol (1:1, v/v), centrifuged, and the supernatant is collected. It is then diluted 10-fold with isopropanol : methanol (1:1, v/v) solution for instrumental analysis.</p>	<p>Targeted bile acid metabolomics</p><p>The internal standards used were glycochenodeoxycholic acid-d4 and lithocholic acid-d4.

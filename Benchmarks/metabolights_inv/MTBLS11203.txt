Study Identifier	MTBLS11203
Study Title	Neuropeptide SP protects against colitis and linked anxiety-like behavior via gut microbiota and metabolite inositol: A multi-omics analysis of the gut-brain axis
Study Description	<p>The gut-brain axis facilitates two-way communication between gut inflammation and psychiatric comorbidities associated with inflammatory bowel disease (IBD). In this study, we examined the neuroprotective effects of the neuropeptide substance P (SP) on colitis. Taking into account the interactions between the intestinal microbiota and host metabolism, along with the influence of microbiota-derived metabolites on gut-brain signaling, we conducted an untargeted metabolomic analysis.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p> All mice were euthanized under anesthesia with 10% chloral hydrate, and the colonic contents were collected for further analysis.</p>	<p>The LC/MS system for metabolomics analysis is composed of Waters Acquity I-Class </p><p>PLUS ultra-high performance liquid tandem Waters Xevo G2-XS QTof high resolution mass </p><p>spectrometer. The column used is purchased from Waters Acquity UPLC HSS T3 column </p><p>(1.8um 2.1*100mm) </p><p>Positive ion mode: mobile phase A: 0.1% formic acid aqueous solution; mobile phaseB: 0.1% formic acid acetonitrile </p><p>Negative ion mode: mobile phase A: 0.1% formic acid aqueous solution; mobile phase </p><p>B: 0.1% formic acid acetonitrile </p><p>Injection volume 1μL&nbsp;</p>	<p>The LMS system for metabolomics analysis was performed by Waters AcquityI-ClassPLUS ultra high performance liquid chromatography in tandem with Waters</p><p>The XevoG2-XSQTOF high resolution mass spectrometer was constructed using the AcquityUPLCHSST3 column purchased from Waters</p><p>(1.8um2.1*100mm) [3].</p><p>Positive ion mode (POS) : mobile phase A: 0.1% formic acid solution; Mobile phase B: 0.1% acetonitrile formate</p><p>Negative ion mode (NEG) : mobile phase A: 0.1% formic acid aqueous solution; Mobile phase B: 0.1% acetonitrile formate</p><p>Sample volume 1ul</p>	<p>Waters Xevo G2-XS QTOF high resolution mass spectrometer can collect primary and</p><p>secondary mass spectrometry data in MSe mode under the control of the acquisition</p><p>software (MassLynx V4.2, Waters). In each data acquisition cycle, dual-channel data</p><p>acquisition can be performed on both low collision energy and high collision energy at</p><p>the same time. The low collision energy is 2V, the high collision energy range is</p><p>10~40V, and the scanning frequency is 0.2 seconds for a mass spectrum. The parameters</p><p>of the ESI ion source are as follows: Capillary voltage: 2000V (positive ion mode) or -</p><p>1500V (negative ion mode); cone voltage: 30V; ion source temperature: 150°C; desolvent</p><p>gas temperature 500°C; backflush gas flow rate: 50L/ h; Desolventizing gas flow rate:</p><p>800L/h.</p>	<p>The raw data collected using MassLynx V4.2 is processed by Progenesis QI software</p><p>for peak extraction, peak alignment and other data processing operations, based on the</p><p>Progenesis QI software online METLIN database and Biomark’s self-built library for</p><p>identification, and at the same time, theoretical fragment identification and mass</p><p>deviation All are within 100ppm.</p>	<p>After normalizing the original peak area information with the total peak area, the</p><p>follow-up analysis was performed. Principal component analysis and Spearman correlation</p><p>analysis were used to judge the repeatability of the samples within group and the</p><p>quanlity control samples. The identified compounds are searched for classification and</p><p>pathway information in KEGG, HMDB and lipidmaps databases.According to the grouping</p><p>information, calculate and compare the difference multiples, T test was used to</p><p>calculate the difference significance pvalue of each compound. The R language package</p><p>ropls was used to perform OPLS-DA modeling, and 200 times permutation tests was</p><p>performed to verify the reliability of the model. The VIP value of the model was</p><p>calculated using multiple cross-validation. The method of combining the difference</p><p>multiple, the P value and the VIP value of the OPLS-DA model was adopted to screen the</p><p>differential metabolites. The screening criteria are FC&gt; P value1. The difference</p><p>metabolites of KEGG pathway enrichment significance were calculated using</p><p>hypergeometric distribution test.</p>

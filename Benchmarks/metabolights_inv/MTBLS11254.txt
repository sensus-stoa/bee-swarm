Study Identifier	MTBLS11254
Study Title	The novel protein circPETH-147aa regulates metabolic reprogramming in hepatocellular carcinoma cells to remodel immunosuppressive microenvironment
Study Description	Metabolic reprogramming fuels cancer cell metastasis and remodels the immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment (TME). We report here that a circRNA, circPETH, packaged by extracellular vesicles (EVs) from tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) to hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) cells, facilitates glycolysis and metastasis of recipient HCC cells. Mechanistically, circPETH-147aa, encoded by circPETH in a m6A-driven manner, promotes PKM2-catalyzed ALDOA-S36 phosphorylation via MEG pocket. Furthermore, circPETH-147aa impairs anti-HCC immunity by elevating HuR-dependent SLC43A2 mRNA stability and driving methionine and leucine deficiency in cytotoxic CD8+ T cells. Importantly, by virtual and experimental screening, we found that the novel small-molecule Norathyriol was an effective inhibitor that targets the MEG pocket on circPETH-147aa surface. Norathyriol reverses circPETH-147aa-facilitated acquisition of metabolic and metastatic phenotypes by HCC cells, increases anti-PD1 efficacy and boosts cytotoxic CD8+ T-cell function. Our findings determine Norathyriol as a promising anti-HCC agent that contributes to the attenuation of advanced HCC resistance to immune checkpoint blocker (ICB) therapies.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Culture medium of Hep3B (Liver cancer cell line) cells with circPETH knockdown or not were collected.</p>	<p>Samples were removed from the -80 °C refrigerator and thawed on ice. After the samples were thawed, 500 µL of 80% methanol/water (pre-cooled at -20 °C) was added and swirled for 2 min at 2500 rpm. Samples were put in liquid nitrogen for quick freezing for 5 min, taken out the ice to thaw for 5 min, swirled for 2 min and mixed well. The previous step was repeated 3 times. Samples were centrifuged at 4 °C at 12000 rpm for 10 min and then 300 μL of supernatant were transferred to a new centrifuge tube. Refrigerated at -20 °C for 30 min, centrifuge at 4 °C and 12000 rpm for 10 min. Supernatant 200 μL was taken from the protein precipitation plate for analysis.</p>	<p>The sample extracts were analyzed using a SCIEX ExionLC AD UPLC system and a Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 (1.8 µm, 2.1 mm x 100 mm; Waters) column. Mobile phase A was ultra-pure water (containing 0.05% formic acid) and B phase, acetonitrile (containing 0.05% formic acid). Flow rate was 0.35 mL/min. Column temperature was 40 °C, sample size 2 µL and mobile phase gradient: 0 min A/B is 95:5 (v/v), 8 min A/B is 5:95 (v/v), 9.5 min A/B is 5:95 (v/v), 9.6 min A/B is 95:5 (v/v), 12 min A/B is 95:5 (v/v).</p>	<p>An AB SCIEX QTRAP 6500+ System, equipped with an ESI Turbo Ion-Spray interface, operating in positive ion mode and controlled by Analyst 1.6 software (AB Sciex) was used. The ESI source operation parameters were as follows: ion source, turbo spray; source temperature 550 °C; ion spray voltage (IS) 5500 V; curtain gas (CUR) were set at 35 psi; DP and CE for individual MRM transitions was done with further DP and CE optimization. A specific set of MRM transitions were monitored for each period according to the plant hormones eluted within this period.</p>	<p>MultiQuant 3.0.3 software was used to process the mass spectrum data, referring to the retention time and peak type information of the standard products, and integral correction was performed for the mass spectrum peaks detected in different samples to ensure the qualitative and quantitative accuracy. Preparation: 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000, 2000, 5000, 10000 and 20000 ng/mL standard solution of different concentrations, to obtain the mass spectrum peak intensity data of the corresponding quantitative signal of each concentration standard; The Concentration Ratioor Concentration ratio of external standard to internal standard is the horizontal coordinate, and the peak Area ratio or Area Ratioor Area is the vertical coordinate, and the standard curves of different substances are drawn. The ratio of the integrated peak area of all samples was calculated by substituting the linear equation of the standard curve and further substituting the calculation formula to obtain the content data of the substance in the actual sample.</p>	<p>MWDB (Metware Database) is constructed based on standard products, and qualitative analysis of mass spectrometry data is carried out.</p><p>Quantification was performed using Multiple Reaction Monitoring (MRM) analysis using triple quadrupole mass spectrometry. In the MRM model, the four-pole first screens the precursor ions (parent ions) of the target substance, and excludes the ions corresponding to other molecular weight substances to preliminarily eliminate the interference. After ionization induced by the collision chamber, the precursor ions break to form a number of fragment ions, and then the fragment ions are filtered through the triple four-pole to select the required characteristic fragment ions, and eliminate the interference of non-target ions, so that the quantification is more accurate and the repeatability is better. After the mass spectrometry data of different samples were obtained, the chromatographic peaks of all target objects were integrated and quantitative analysis was performed by standard curves.</p>

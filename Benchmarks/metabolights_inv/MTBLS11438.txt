Study Protocol Description	<p>Lung tissues were collected after 14 days lactobacillus johnsonii treatment in acute lung injury mice </p>	<p>200 mg of lung samples were mixed with 400 μL of extraction solution for metabolite extraction. The sample solution was ground in a frozen tissue mill for 6 min and then extracted by low temperature ultrasonication for 30 min, and the sample was centrifuged at 4°C for 5 min at 13,000 g, then supernatant was taken for analysis, QC was used as the quality control sample</p>	<p>The 2 μL sample was separated on a HSS T3 column and then entered into the mass spectrometry detection. Mobile phase A was 95% water + 5% acetonitrile and mobile phase B was 47.5&amp; acetonitrile + 47.5% isopropanol + 5% water, both containing 0.1% formic acid. The flow rate was 0.40 mL/min, the column temperature was 40 ℃, and the separation gradient gradient was set as follows: 0-1 min, 0-5% B mobile phase; 1-2 min, 5-25% B mobile phase; 2-9 min, 25-100% B mobile phase; 9-13 min, 100% B mobile phase; 13-13.1 min, 100-0% B mobile phase; 13.1-16 min, 0% B mobile phase.</p>	<p>The mass spectra were performed in positive and negative ion scanning modes with a mass scan range of m/z: 70-1050, positive ion voltage 3500 V, negative ion 2800 V, sheath gas 40 psi, auxiliary heated gas 100 psi, ion source heating temperature 400 °C, cyclic collision energies of 20-40-60 V. The resolution of the MS1 was 70,000, and the resolution of the MS2 was 17,500. resolution 17500.</p>	<p>Subsequently, the LC-MS raw data were imported into the metabolomics processing software Prognesisi QI (Waters Corporation, Milford, USA) for baseline filtering, peak identification, integration, retention time correction, and peak alignment, and finally obtained a data matrix containing retention time, mass-to-charge ratio, and peak intensity data.</p>	<p>The matrix was then matched with the public databases HMDM (www.hmdb.ca/) and Metlin (http://www.metlin.scripps.edu/), as well as majorbio library, to obtain the metabolite information. After searching the library, the matrix data were uploaded to the cloud platform (http://www.cloud.majorbio.com) for data analysis, and the final analytical data matrix was obtained by 80% removal of missing values, sum normalisation, and Log10 logarithmic processing.</p>

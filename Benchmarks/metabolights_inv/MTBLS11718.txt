Study Protocol Description	<p>Fresh fecal samples were collected from each participant. The specimens were divided into 3 x 100 mg portions and promptly snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen. The procedure of transporting and handling the samples on ice was completed within 20 min. Post snap-freezing, the samples were preserved at -80 °C pending extraction for analysis.</p>	<p>For metabolomic analysis, 100 μL of each sample was placed in an EP tube and combined with 400 μL of methanol that included isotopically-labeled internal standards. After vortexing for 30 s, the samples underwent sonication in an ice-water bath for 10 min, followed by incubation at -40 °C for 1 h to induce protein precipitation. Following centrifugation at 12,000 rpm (13,800 x g) for 15 min at 4 °C, the supernatant was moved to a new vial. A pooled quality control sample was created by combining equal volumes of each supernatant.</p>	<p>Metabolomic analyses were conducted utilizing a UHPLC system (Vanquish, Thermo Fisher Scientific) with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 (2.1 mm × 100 mm, 1.8 μm) column connected to an Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer. The mobile phase comprised water containing 5 mmol/L ammonium acetate and 5 mmol/L acetic acid (component A) along with acetonitrile (component B) The auto-sampler was maintained at 4 °C, with a 2 μL injection volume.</p>	<p>The Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer, operating in information-dependent acquisition (IDA) mode and managed by Xcalibur software, evaluated the full scan MS spectrum. The ESI source settings were: sheath gas flow rate of 50 Arb, auxiliary gas flow rate of 15 Arb, capillary temperature at 320 °C, full MS resolution at 60,000, MS/MS resolution at 15,000, collision energy in NCE mode at 10/30/60, and spray voltage at 3.8 kV (positive mode) or -3.4 kV (negative mode).</p>	<p>Data processing began with raw data conversion to mzXML format utilizing ProteoWizard, followed by peak detection, extraction, alignment and integration through a custom R program based on XCMS.</p>	<p>Metabolite annotation was conducetd utilizing an in-house MS2 database (BiotreeDB, Shanghai) with a cutoff of 0.3 [16].</p>

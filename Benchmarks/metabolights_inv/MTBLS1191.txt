STUDY
Study Identifier	MTBLS1191
Study Title	Absence of tmRNA increases the persistence to cefotaxime by upregulating the metabolites GlcNAc in Aeromonas veronii
Study Description	<p>Bacterial persisters refer to a small proportion of phenotypically heterogeneous variants with transient capability for survival when exposing to high concentrations of antibiotic, which constitute the major cause for recurrent infections both in human and aquatic infections. In pathogenic bacteria Aeromonas veronii, tmRNA (transfer-messenger RNA), the core factor of trans-translation system, was identified as a determinant regulator mediating the persistence to β-lactams. Compared with the wild type, the deletion of tmRNA exhibited unchanged growth rate, sustained susceptibility to cefotaxime, but did increase persister cell formation. Transcriptomic and metabolomic analyses revealed that, the absence of tmRNA not only upreglated the expressions of metabolic genes especially in the metabolic flux of peptidoglycan biosynthesis, but also significantly elevated the intercellular level of metabolite GlcNAc, thereby intensifying the contents of peptidoglycans in the cell wall. Eventually, exogenous GlcNAc stimulated significantly the bacterial growth and persistence to cefotaxime in a concentration dependent manner. Taken together, these results uncover a novel mechanism of persister formation mediated by tmRNA against the β-lactam challenges.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The bacteria were cultured for 20 hrs then lysed using in 1 ml ice cold extraction solvent (2:2:1 methanol/acetonitrile/water). The extracts were homogenized in a ball mill for 4 min, and subjected to ultrasonic treatments in ice water 3 times, followed by incubation at -20 °C for 1 hr to precipitate the proteins. After centrifugation at 12000 rpm for 15 min at 4 °C, the supernatant was transferred into EP tubes and dried in a vacuum concentrator. Then, the extraction solvent (1:1 acetonitrile/water) was added for reconstitution. After vortex and ultrasonication for 10 min at 4 °C in water bath, the reconstituted extracts were spun down, and the supernatant was transferred into a LC/MS glass vial.</p><p><br></p><p>For pooled QC samples, 10 µl supernatant was combined from each sample.</p>	<p>The samples were homogenized in a ball mill for 4 min at 45 Hz, then ultrasound treated in an ice bath for 5 min. After homogenization x 3, samples were incubated for 1 h at -20 ℃ to precipitate proteins. They were then centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 15 min at 4 ℃. Supernatants (825 μl) were transfered into fresh EP tubes and extracts dryed in a vacuum concentrator without heating, before being reconstituted in 100 μl acetonitrile:water extraction solvent (1:1). Samples were vortexed x 30 sec, sonicated x 10 min in 4 ℃ water bath and centrifuged for 15 min at 12000 rpm, 4 ℃. Supernatants (60 μl) were transfered into fresh 2 ml LC/MS glass vials, 10 μl from each sample collected for pooled QC samples, and 60 μl for the UHPLC-QTOF-MS analysis.</p>	<p>LC-MS/MS analyses were performed using an UHPLC system (1290, Agilent Technologies) with a UPLC BEH Amide column (1.7μm 2.1 x 100mm, Waters) coupled to TripleTOF 6600 (Q-TOF, AB Sciex). The mobile phase consisted of 25 mM NH4OAc and 25 mM NH4OH in water pH 9.75 (A) and acetonitrile (B) with elution gradient as follows: 0 min, 95% B; 7min, 65% B; 9 min, 40% B; 9.1 min, 95% B; 12 min, 95% B, which was delivered at 0.5 ml/min. The injection volume was 2 μl.</p>	<p>The Triple TOF mass spectrometer was used for its ability to acquire MS/MS spectra on an information-dependent basis (IDA) during an LC/MS experiment. In this mode, the acquisition software (Analyst TF 1.7, AB Sciex) continuously evaluates the full scan survey MS data as it collects and triggers the acquisition of MS/MS spectra depending on preselected criteria. In each cycle, 12 precursor ions whose intensity greater than 100 were chosen for fragmentation at collision energy (CE) of 30 V (15 MS/MS events with product ion accumulation time of 50 msec each). ESI source conditions were set as following: ion source gas 1 as 60 psi, ion source gas 2 as 60 psi, curtain gas as 35 psi, source temperature 650 ℃, Ion Spray Voltage Floating (ISVF) 5000 V or -4000 V in positive or negative modes respectively.</p>	<p>MS raw data (.wiff) files were converted to the mzXML format using ProteoWizard and processed by R package XCMS (version 3.2). The preprocessing results generated a data matrix that consisted of the retention time (RT), massto-charge ratio (m/z) values, and peak intensity.</p>	<p>R package CAMERA was used for peak annotation after XCMS data processing. In-house MS2 database was applied in metabolites identification.</p>

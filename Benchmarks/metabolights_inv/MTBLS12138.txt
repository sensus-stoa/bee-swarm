Study Identifier	MTBLS12138
Study Title	Integrated transcriptomic and metabolomic analyses reveal the mechanism by which quercetin inhibits reflux esophagitis in rats
Study Description	<p>Quercetin relieves symptoms in mice with reflux esophagitis (RE), but the mechanism is unclear. Quercetin attenuates esophageal mucosal inflammation in RE mice by inhibiting the production of inflammatory factors IL-β and IL-6. In addition, using transcriptomic and metabolomic analyses, we found that metabolites related to bile acid metabolism, such as taurine, taurocholic acid and nicotinamide, were closely associated with RE mice. Quercetin reduced the expression of bile acid-related genes, such as Cd38, Sez6l2 and Nos2, who may be characteristic genes and therapeutic targets for reflux esophagitis.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The male Wistar rats, aged between 6-8 weeks and weighing between 240-260 g, were obtained from Beijing SPF Biotechnology Co., Ltd. The rats were acclimatized for 7 days and then randomly assigned numbers using a random number table, dividing them into 4 groups: control group, model group, omeprazole group and quercetin group. The control group underwent “laparotomy and suture surgery,” while the remaining groups underwent “modified partial cardia muscle incision and external pylorus partial ligation” to establish a rat model of reflux esophagitis (RE). After 7 days of modeling, each group was administered the corresponding medication dosage through gavage. The control group, model group,&nbsp;omeprazole group (30 mg/kg) and quercetin (100 mg/kg) group were given an equal volume of physiological saline for gavage. Prior to gavage, the refrigerated medications were warmed to an appropriate temperature and shaken well. The dosage was 2.5 mL per gavage, administered twice a day for a continuous intervention period of 14 days. The lower segment of the esophageal tissue was collected.</p>	<p>First, 50 mg solid sample was added to a 2 mL centrifuge tube and a 6 mm diameter grinding bead was added. Next 400 μLof extraction solution (methanol:water, 4:1, v:v) containing 0.02 mg/mL of internal standard (L-2-chlorophenylalanine) was used for metabolite extraction. Samples were ground by the Wonbio-96c (Shanghai wanbo biotechnology co., LTD) frozen tissue grinder for 6 min (-10 °C, 50 Hz), followed by low-temperature ultrasonic extraction for 30 min (5 °C, 40 kHz). The samples were left at -20 °C for 30 min, centrifuged for 15 min (4 °C, 13,000 x g), and the supernatant was transferred to the injection vial for LC-MS/MS analysis.</p><p><br></p><p>As a part of the system conditioning and quality control process, a pooled quality control sample (QC) was prepared by mixing equal volumes of all samples. The QC samples were disposed and tested in the same manner as the analytic samples. It helped to represent the whole sample set, which would be injected at regular intervals (every 5-15 samples) in order to monitor the stability of the analysis.</p>	<p>The LC-MS/MS analysis of sample was conducted on a Thermo Scientific Vanquish Flex UHPLC System equipped with an ACQUITY HSS T3 column (100 mm x 2.1 mm i.d., 1.8 μm; Waters, USA) at Majorbio Bio-Pharm Technology Co. Ltd (Shanghai, China). The mobile phases consisted of 0.1% formic acid in water:acetonitrile (95:5, v/v) (solvent A) and 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile:isopropanol:water (47.5:47.5, v/v) (solvent B). The flow rate was 0.40 mL/min and the column temperature was 40 °C.</p>	<p>The mass spectrometric data were collected using a Thermo UHPLC-Q Exactive HF-X Mass Spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source operating in positive mode and negative mode. The optimal conditions were set as followed: source temperature at 425 °C;sheath gas flow rate at 50 arb; Aux gas flow rate at 13 arb; ion-spray voltage floating (ISVF) at -3500 V in negative mode and 3500 V in positive mode, respectively; Normalized collision energy, 20-40-60 V rolling for MS/MS. Full MS resolution was 60000, and MS/MS resolution was 7500. Data acquisition was performed with the Data DependentAcquisition (DDA) mode. The detection was carried out over a mass range of 70-1050 m/z.</p>	<p>The pretreatment of LC-MS raw data was performed by Progenesis QI (Waters Corporation, Milford, USA) software, and a 3D data matrix in CSV format was exported.</p>	<p>At the same time, the metabolites were identified by&nbsp;searching database, and the main databases were the HMDB (http://www.hmdb.ca), Metlin (https://metlin.scripps.edu) and Majorbio Database.</p>

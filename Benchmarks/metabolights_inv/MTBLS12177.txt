Study Title	High-level de novo synthesis of medical plant derived flavonoid glycosides
Study Description	Pichia pastoris is a widely used eukaryotic host for recombinant protein expression in biopharmaceutical and biomanufacturing, offering advantages such as broad carbon source utilization, high cell density, a mature fermentation process, and genomic stability. Recent advances in genetic editing and regulatory tools have also enabled P. pastoris to synthesize various natural products. This study uses P. pastoris as a chassis cell to engineer biosynthetic pathways for specific flavonoid glycosides derived from Trollius chinensis Bunge. Through systematic metabolic engineering, we improved synthesis efficiency and validated production potential at a 3-L bioreactor scale.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Strain 2V-3 was fermented in a 3-L reactor using glucose and ethanol as carbon sources, and the cells fermented for 84 hours were taken as samples. The samples were stored at -80 °C until processing.</p>	<p>The cell pellets (about 10^7 cells) or bacteria pellets(about 10^7 bacteria) were taken, mixed with 1000 μL of extraction solution (MeOH:ACN:H2O, 2:2:1 (v/v)), the extraction solution contain deuterated internal standards, the mixed solution were vortexed for 30 s and incubated in liquid nitrogen for 1 min. The samples were then allowed to thaw at room temperature and vortexed for 30 s. This freeze–thaw cycle was repeated three times.Then the samples were sonicated for 10 min in 4&nbsp;°C&nbsp;water bath, and incubated for 1 h at -40&nbsp;°C&nbsp;to precipitate proteins. The samples were centrifuged at 12000 rpm (RCF=13800(×g),R= 8.6cm) for 15 min at 4&nbsp;°C. The supernatant was transferred to a fresh glass vial for analysis. The quality control (QC) sample was prepared by mixing an equal aliquot of the supernatant of samples.</p>	<p>LC-MS/MS analyses were performed using an UHPLC system (Vanquish, Thermo Fisher Scientific) with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide (2.1 mm × 50 mm, 1.7 μm) coupled to Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer (Orbitrap MS, Thermo). The mobile phase consisted of 25 mmol/L ammonium acetate and 25 mmol/L ammonia hydroxide in water(pH = 9.75)</p><p>(A) and acetonitrile (B). The auto-sampler temperature was 4&nbsp;°C, and the injection volume was 2 μL.&nbsp;</p>	<p>The Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer was used for its ability to acquire</p><p>MS/MS spectra on information-dependent acquisition (IDA) mode in the control of the acquisition software (Xcalibur, Thermo). In this mode, the acquisition software continuously evaluates the full scan MS spectrum. The ESI source conditions were set as following: sheath gas flow rate as 50 Arb, Aux gas flow rate as 15 Arb, capillary temperature 320&nbsp;°C, full MS resolution as 60000, MS/MS resolution as 15000, collision energy: SNCE 20/30/40, spray voltage as 3.8 kV (positive) or -3.4 kV (negative), respectively.</p>	<p>The raw data were converted to the mzXML format using ProteoWizard and processed with an in-house program. which was developed using R and based on XCMS, for peak detection, extraction, alignment, and integration. </p>	<p>The R package and the sangonDB(V3.0)&nbsp;were applied in metabolite identification.</p>

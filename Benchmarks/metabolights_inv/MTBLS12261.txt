Study Protocol Description	<p>Hemolymph was collected from 180 GF and R. nectarea-mono-colonized third-instar larvae by cutting one leg under a stereomicroscope, with 30 larvae pooled as one sample (n = 6). </p>	<p>Briefly, 100 μL liquid sample was added to a 1.5 mL centrifuge tube with 400 μL solution (acetonitrile:methanol, 1:1, v:v) containing 0.02 mg/mL internal standard (L-2-chlorophenylalanine) to extract metabolites. The samples were mixed by vortex for 30 s and low-temperature sonicated. The samples were placed at -20 °C to precipitate the proteins. Then the samples were centrifuged (4 °C, 13,000 x g). The supernatant was removed and blown dry under nitrogen. The sample was then re-solubilized with 100 µL solution (acetonitrile:water, 1:1) and extracted by low-temperature ultrasonication, followed by centrifugation at 13,000 x g and 4 °C. The supernatant was transferred to sample vials for LC-MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p>The LC-MS/MS analysis of sample was conducted on a Thermo UHPLC-Q Exactive HF-X system equipped with an ACQUITY HSS T3 column (100 mm x 2.1 mm i.d., 1.8 μm; Waters, USA) at Majorbio Bio-Pharm Technology Co. Ltd (Shanghai, China). The mobile phases consisted of 0.1% formic acid in water:acetonitrile (95:5, v/v) (solvent A) and 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile:isopropanol:water (47.5:47.5, v/v) (solvent B). The flow rate was 0.40 mL/min and the column temperature was 40 °C. The injection volume was 3 μL.</p>	<p>The mass spectrometric data were collected using a Thermo UHPLC-Q Exactive HF-X Mass Spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source operating in positive mode and negative mode. The optimal conditions were set as followed: source temperature at 425 °C; sheath gas flow rate at 50 arb; Aux gas flow rate at 13 arb; ion-spray voltage floating (ISVF) at -3500 V in negative mode and 3500 V in positive mode, respectively; Normalized collision energy, 20-40-60 V rolling for MS/MS. Full MS resolution was 60000, and MS/MS resolution was 7500. Data acquisition was performed with the Data Dependent Acquisition (DDA) mode. The detection was carried out over a mass range of 70-1050 m/z.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were processed using Progenesis QI software (Waters Corporation, Milford, USA). Data preprocessing included retention of features detected in ≥80% of samples, normalization using sum method and exclusion of QC samples with RSD &gt;30%. The normalized data were log10-transformed for subsequent analysis. Multivariate statistical analyses, including PCA and OPLS-DA, were conducted using R package “ropls” (Version 1.6.2) with 7-fold cross-validation.</p>	<p>After removing internal standards and false positive peaks, metabolites were identified through HMDB, Metlin and Majorbio databases.</p>

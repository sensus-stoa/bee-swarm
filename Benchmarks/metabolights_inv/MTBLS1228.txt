Study Identifier	MTBLS1228
Study Title	Hyperoxia induces glutamine-fuelled anaplerosis in retinal Müller cells
Study Description	Premature infants require oxygen supplementation to survive, but excess oxygen causes retinovascular growth suppression that underlies the leading cause of infant blindness known as retinopathy of prematurity (ROP). We analyzed changes in intermediary metabolism during hyperoxia in human retinal endothelial cells (RECs) and human retinal Müller glia, which coexist through glutamine consumption and production, respectively. Using a stable isotope labeling technique in human RECs and human Müller cells in culture, here we show that Müller cells in hyperoxia block entry of glycolytic carbon into the tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle and instead oxidize glutamine. In hyperoxia, catabolism of glutamine increased ammonium release by 2-fold. Hyperoxia induces glutamine-fueled anaplerosis that reverses basal Müller cell metabolism from production to consumption of glutamine.

Study Protocol Description	<p><strong>Cell culture</strong></p><p><br></p><p><strong><em>Immortal cell lines</em></strong></p><p>Immortalized Müller cell line MIO-M1 was a kind gift from Dr. Limb (UCL). Cells were cultured in DMEM (without glucose, glutamine and pyruvate) supplemented with 15 mM glucose, 5 mM glutamine, 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS) and 1% Penicillin/Streptomycin.</p><p><br></p><p><strong><em>Primary cells</em></strong></p><p><u>Retinal endothelial cells (REC)</u> were procured from Cell Systems (Kirkland, WA, USA; cat no. ACBRI 181).</p><p><br></p><p><u>Human cortical astrocytes</u> were procured from Sciencell (cat no.1801) and cultured in Astrocyte media. </p><p><em>All cells purchased from Sciencell have the following information listed regarding informed consent: “Human tissue used for the isolation of primary cells is derived from donors who have signed informed consent by the donor themselves or an authorized agent acting on the donor’s behalf.”</em></p><p><br></p><p><u>Müller glia cells</u> were isolated and cultured as follows. Enucleated eyes from P11-P12 mice were held in dissecting medium (EBSS + 1% Penicillin/Streptomycin) on ice. Dissected retinas were minced and transferred to a collection tube with minimal volume of dissecting medium, held on ice. Retinal tissue was dissociated using the Worthington Papain Dissociation System ( LK003150). Briefly, a vial of papain was reconstituted in EBSS equilibrated with sterile 95% O2 : 5% CO2 and incubated at 37 °C for 10 mins until fully dissolved. A vial of DNase I was reconstituted in equilibrated EBSS, and 250 μl was added to the activated papain. This papain/DNase solution was added to the retinal tissue and triturated gently with P1000 pipette. The tissues were equilibrated in sterile 95% O2 : 5% CO2 and incubated at 37 °C with 160 rpm agitation for a total of 35 mins, broken up into 15 min, 15 min, and 5 min incubations, triturating gently after each. The cell suspension was centrifuged at 550 x g for 5 mins at room temperature and pellet resuspended in ovomucoid inhibitor solution, prepared according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Cells were again pelleted at 550 x g for 5 mins at room temperature and passed through a 30 μm filter after resuspending in 1 ml primary Müller glia cell culture media: DMEM-high glucose (without L-glutamine) + sodium pyruvate + 1% GlutaMax, 1% Penicillin/Streptomycin, and 10% FBS. Cells obtained from the dissociation were counted, and a trypan blue dye exclusion assay was used to determine a final viability of about 83%. Müller cells were plated at a density of 1.1 x10^6 cells/ cm2 onto plates coated with 10 μg/cm2 laminin and maintained at 4 °C in primary Müller glia cell culture media. The first media change occurred after four days, followed by media changes every two days. Cells were harvested after 8 days in culture, at which point they are primarily comprised of Müller glia.</p><p><br></p><p><strong><em>Immortal and primary cell labelling</em></strong></p><p>Cells were cultured in 6-well plates to 90% confluence and then media was replaced with media containing labeled substrate(s). Following media change, cells were incubated for 24 h in normoxic cell culture incubator set at 5% CO2 and 37°C, to establish isotopic steady state. Once isotopic steady state was reached, cells were either incubated in normoxic or hyperoxia (75% O2 with 5% CO2) incubators for 8 or 24 h as indicated.</p><p>For MIO-M1, astrocytes and primary Müller cells, unlabelled glucose or glutamine was replaced with [13C6]glucose or [13C5]glutamine respectively in DMEM (without glucose, glutamine and pyruvate) supplemented with 15 mM glucose, 5 mM glutamine, 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS) and 1% Penicillin/Streptomycin.</p><p>For RECs, this media was diluted 50% with Classic Medium with Serum and CultureBoost™ (Cell systems, Kirkland, WA, USA). </p><p><br></p><p><strong><em>Retinal explant</em></strong></p><p>Mice were euthanised with lethal dose of ketamine/xylazine, eyes enucleated and retinas were dissected as described below for either acetate or glutamine labelling.</p><p>For acetate labeling, retinas were dissected into high glucose DMEM, briefly washed with room temperature normal saline and immediately added to 1 ml of culture media in 12- well plates. Media composition used for the tracer experiment was DMEM containing 5 mM glucose, 1 mM glutamine, 2 mM [1-13C]acetate, 10% FBS and 1% Penicillin/Streptomycin. Retinas were cultured for 30 min or 1 h, after which they were washed with room temperature normal saline and then snap frozen in liquid nitrogen.</p><p>For glutamine labeling, dissections were performed in KRB containing 5 mM glucose and 1% Penicillin/Streptomycin. Retinas were then incubated in KRB media containing 5 mM of [13C5] glutamine, 10% dialyzed FBS and 1% Penicillin/Streptomycin for 5 min. After 5 min of incubation in labeling media, retinas were removed and washed with 1ml of normal saline. After this step, 1ml of KRB media containing 10% FBS, 1% Penicillin/Streptomycin, 5 mM lactate and 5 mM glucose was added to each well of the 12-well plates containing retinal explants.</p>	<p><strong>Metabolite extraction</strong></p><p><br></p><p><strong><em>Immortal and primary cells</em></strong></p><p>To extract metabolites, media was aspirated from the 6-well plates with adherent cells and cells were quickly washed with 1 ml of room temperature normal saline. To each well, 400 μl of -20 °C cold methanol and 400 μl of cold water was added. Cells were scraped with cell scraper, while maintaining plates on ice. The resulting cell suspension was added to 400 μl of -20 °C cold chloroform. Tubes were agitated on thermomixer for 20 min at 4° C and 1400 rpm. Tubes were then centrifuged at 15000 x g for 5 min at 4 °C. The upper layer (300 μl) containing polar metabolites was dried under vacuum at -4 °C.</p><p><br></p><p>*<u>Glutamine labelled cells</u>; metabolites were extracted as described with the following additions. Before drying, 10 μl of 0.05 mg/ml [13C5]ribitol was added as an internal standard. Dried extracts were then derivatized with 25 μl of 40 mg/ml methoxylamine in pyridine for 30 min on a thermomixer set at 45 °C and 1000 rpm. These samples were further derivatized with 75 μl of MSTFA + 1% TMCS.</p><p><br></p><p>**<u>Primary Müller cell</u> experiments were preformed in 12-well plates and so metabolites were extracted as described except that the methanol, water and chloroform volumes were reduced by half. In this case 10 μl of 0.05 mg/ml of [13C5]ribitol was added to 200 μl of upper phase and dried under vacuum at -4 °C.</p><p><br></p><p><strong><em>Retinal explant</em></strong></p><p><u>Acetate labelled</u>; metabolites from retina were extracted using 500 μl of -20 °C cold 80% methanol, brief sonication and centrifugation at 15000 x g for 5 min at 4 °C. Supernatant (350 μl) together with 10 μl of 0.05 mg/ml of [13C5]ribitol were added to a fresh tube with and samples dried.</p><p><u>Glutamine labelled</u>; metabolites were extracted using the methanol/chloroform/water protocol described above for glutamine labelled immortal and primary cells. Supernatant (300 μl) together with 10 μl of 0.05 mg/ml of [13C5]ribitol were added to a fresh tube, samples dried under vacuum and derivatized as described.</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Ammonium extraction and sample derivatization for GC-MS measurement</strong></p><p>MIO-MI cells cultured in 6-well plates were either incubated in normoxia or hyperoxia. Ammonium was measured with the method described in Yang et al.[1] with slight modifications. After 48 h of incubation in hyperoxia, 400 μl of media sample was taken and added to 400 μl of (10)N NaOH in 1.5 ml tube. To this preparation, 400 μl of formaldehyde (36.5-38% in water) was added and left overnight at room temperature in the fume hood. Formaldehyde reacts with ammonium to produce a stable compound hexamethylenetetramine (HMT). To this HMT preparation, 200 μl of 10% iodine was added to form the extractible HMT-iodine adduct. Tubes were incubated on a thermomixer at 40 °C for 15 min with 1000 rpm agitation speed. Tubes were cooled down to room temperature and whole mixture was added to 5 ml of chloroform, to extract HMT-iodine from the solution. Tubes were centrifuged at 1000 x g at room temperature for 1 min to separate phases. Lower phase (100 μl) containing HMT-iodine was taken out into fresh tube and 1 μl of alkanes mix (C10-C40 50 mg/l, even chain alkanes) standard was added to the solution as internal standard. Each sample (1 μl) was then injected into the mass spec and m/z 140 corresponding to mass of HMT was used for quantitation and was normalized to one of the alkanes in the spiked alkanes internal standard with m/z 198, which corresponds to mass of C14H30.</p><p><br></p><p>References:</p><p>[1] Yang D, Puchowicz MA, David F, Powers L, Halperin ML, Brunengraber H. 15N enrichment of ammonium, glutamine-amide and urea, measured via mass isotopomer analysis of hexamethylenetetramine. J Mass Spectrom. 1999 Nov;34(11):1130-6. DOI:10.1002/(SICI)1096-9888(199911)34:11&lt;1130::AID-JMS871&gt;3.0.CO;2-Y</p>	<p>Samples were measured on a 7890B GC coupled to EI/CI 5977 mass selective detector Agilent mass spectrometer.</p><p>For labelling experiments, 1 μl of sample was injected. Front inlet heater was set to 250 °C, septum purge flow 3 ml/min. Injections were made in splitless mode onto HP-5ms inert 30 m x 250 μm x 0.25 μm column connected to the MSD. Samples were measured in a constant flow mode with flow of helium set to 1.1 ml/min. Oven was set to a starting temperature of 60 °C for 1 min and then ramped at 10 °C/min to a final temperature of 325 °C with a final hold time of 10 min. Column was re-equilibrated at 60 °C for 1 min.</p><p>Primary Müller and astrocyte metabolites were measured separately using the DB-5ms GC Column 30 m x 0.25 mm x 0.25 μm with DuraGuard 10 m. Scan method was similar with solvent delay of 6.6 min. For glutamine labelling assays, SIM method was used, also with solvent delay of 6.6 min. Ions used in SIM method were same&nbsp;with different time windows to account for differences in retention time on new column.</p>	<p>Samples were measured on an Agilent 5977 mass selective detector mass spectrometer with electron impact ionization. Full scan method was used for glucose labelling experiment, with scan widow from 50-800 m/z. </p><p><br></p><p>Selected ion monitoring (SIM) method was used for metabolite measurement for glutamine labeling assays.</p><p><strong>Time window : Metabolite Ions (m/z)</strong></p><p>6.0-6.16 : Pyruvate (173-178)</p><p>6.16-9.00 : Lactate (218-223)</p><p>9.0-9.65 : Proline (243-252)</p><p>9.65-10 : Succinate (246-252)</p><p>10-12.06 : Fumarate (244-250)</p><p>12.06-12.40 : Malate (334-340)</p><p>12.40-13.57 : Aspartate (333-339)</p><p>13.57-14.90 : Glutamate (347-354)</p><p>14.90-15.29 : U-13C Ribitol (323, 336-342)</p><p>15.29-15.80 : Glutamine (346-353)</p><p>15.80-16.60 : Citrate (464-474)</p><p>16.60-37.50 : Glucose (553-561)</p>	<p>Metabolite Detector and Mass Hunter software were used for deconvolution and annotation of the metabolites.</p><p><br></p><p>Files were converted from .D to .CDF using Chemstation, to upload them onto metabolights server.</p>	<p>IsoCor was used to correct for natural abundance of isotopes and to derive true mass isotopomer distribution for each metabolite analyzed. Unlabeled extracts from MIO-M1 cells and authentic metabolite standards were used to annotate metabolites in all the samples. NIST 2017 library was further used to confirm the compounds in the extracts from all cell lines and</p><p>retinal explants.</p><p>[13C5]ribitol was used to allow calculation and comparison of relative quantities of intracellular metabolites between retinal endothelial cells and Müller cells.</p><p>For ammonium extraction samples, HMT (m/z 140) was used for quantitation and was normalized to one of the alkanes in the spiked internal standard C14H30 (m/z 198).</p>

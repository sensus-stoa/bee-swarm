Study Identifier	MTBLS12286
Study Title	Adipocyte-specific Zeb1 confers breast cancer progression by triggering the tumor-associated adipose microenvironment remodeling
Study Description	<p>Upon penetrating the basement membrane, breast cancer cells directly interact with their surrounding adipose tissue, which forms a unique tumor-associated adipose microenvironment (TAME). However, the underlying mechanism involved in the regulation of lipid metabolic remodeling in the TAME remains elusive. Herein, we identified a Zeb1-orchestrated bidirectional communication between breast cancer cells and their adjacent cancer-associated adipocytes (CAAs). At the molecular level, breast cancer cells, through the secretion of adrenomedullin (AM), induce downregulation of Zeb1 expression to activate the Atgl/Hsl/Scd-depedent lipolysis in CAAs, which subsequently results in the release of palmitoleic acid (POA) into the TAME. In turn, accumulated POA triggers the malignant growth and metastasis of breast cancer cells and their production of AM via an ARA-PDG2-mediated membrane phospholipid replacement mechanism. Importantly, disruption of Zeb1-dependent lipolytic activity and/or membrane phospholipid remodeling within the TAME, severely diminishes the aggressiveness of breast cancer in vitro and in vivo</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>EO771 cells were seeded in 10-cm dishes, treated with POA (5&nbsp;μM) for 2 days, then washed 3x times with pre-chilled PBS and subsequently washed with 0.9% sodium chloride solution. After aspirating the sodium chloride solution, cells were scraped off in the presence of methanol/acetonitrile/water (2:2:1, v/v/v), rapidly frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -80 °C.</p>	<p>The samples were thawed at 4 °C, and 500&nbsp;μL of BHT protein precipitant was added, followed by the addition of 10&nbsp;μL of an internal standard at a concentration of 1&nbsp;μg/mL. The mixture was vortexed for 20 s and centrifuged at 14,000 rcf for 10 min at 4 °C. The 500&nbsp;μL of the supernatant was collected and combined with 1000&nbsp;μL of pure water. Sample purification was carried out using an Oasis HLB 96-well Plate. The plate wells were activated with 2 mL of methanol, added in two 1 mL portions. Next, the plate was balanced with 2 mL of pure water in two 1 mL steps. Sample loading was performed in several aliquots. Washing was done twice with 2 mL of Washing Solution A (H2O/BHT (2, 6-di-tert-butyl-p-cresol solution), 19:1 v/v), each time adding 1 mL, followed by two washes with 2 mL of Washing Solution B (Washing solution A/formic acid in a volume ratio of 10,000:1), also added in 1 mL increments. The samples were collected, eluted with 1 mL of pure methanol, dried under nitrogen, and then stored at -80 °C. For LC-MS analysis, the samples were re-dissolved in 50 μL 1% FA in acetonitrile/water (4:1, v/v) solution and adequately vortexed, then centrifuged (14,000 rcf, 4 °C, 15 min). The supernatants were collected for LC- MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p>Analyses were performed using an UHPLC (1290 Infinity LC, Agilent Technologies). Samples were kept in an autosampler at 4°C, with a column temperature of 35°C. Mobile phase A consisted of 0.1% formic acid in water, while mobile phase B was composed of 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile. The flow rate was maintained at 400 μL/min with an injection volume of 2 μL. The gradient profile for the liquid chromatography was as follows: from 0.0 to 1.0 min, phase B was held at 30%; from 1.0 to 9.0 min, phase B linearly increased to 90%; from 9.0 to 11.0 min, phase B was held at 90%; from 11.0 to 11.1 min, phase B decreased back to 20% linearly; and from 11.1 to 14.0 min, phase B was maintained at 20%. Quality control (QC) samples were interspersed within the sample sequence at regular intervals to monitor and assess the system's stability and reproducibility. A standard mix of target compounds was also included in the sample queue to calibrate retention times.</p>	<p>The ESI source settings for negative ions on the 5500 QTRAP were: source temperature at 500 °C; Ion Source Gas 1 (Gas1) at 50; Ion Source Gas 2 (Gas2) at 50; Curtain gas (CUR) at 30; and IonSpray Voltage Floating (ISVF) set to -4500 V. Target analyte transitions were detected in Multiple Reaction Monitoring (MRM) mode.</p>	<p>The chromatographic peak areas and retention times were extracted using the Multiquant 3.0.2 software. Retention time alignment was performed using standard compounds of the target metabolites for accurate identification.</p>	<p>The chromatographic peak areas and retention times were extracted using the Multiquant 3.0.2 software.

Study Title	Metabolomics highlights reduced glutamate and acetylcarnitine in the amniotic fluid of cytomegalovirus-infected pregnant women
Study Description	<p>Cytomegalovirus (CMV) is a ubiquitous infectious agent that causes significant morbidity in immunosuppressed hosts and is the most common cause of neonatal congenital deafness.An infection acquired during pregnancy can be vertically transmitted to the fetus and cause permanent sequelae in the newborn, even severe, with characteristic clinical manifestations such as intellectual disability and neurosensory deficits. To characterize the metabolic changes influenced by CMV, we set up a research study of amniotic fluid (AF) metabolites in pregnant women with primary CMV infection acquired in the first trimester of pregnancy, compared to a control group in the same gestational period. Weused a targeted metabolomics platform that allowed the quantification of 50 molecules including amino acids and acylcarnitines.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>AF samples were obtained from parturients who were found to have primary CMV infection in the first trimester of pregnancy. AF samples were aliquoted and stored at -80 °C until metabolomic analysis.</p>	<p>AF samples were centrifuged at 250 g, 5 min, 4 °C to remove floating cells. Then, 10 L of AF supernatants were spotted on a filter paper, and metabolites were extracted with 200 L of methanol containing stable isotope-labeled AA and AC standards. AF metabolites and internal standards were derivatized with 80 L of n-butanol/3 N HCl (30 min, 65 °C) and dried under nitrogen flow. A solution of acetonitrile/water (70/30) with 0.05% formic acid was added prior injection into the chromatographic system.</p>	<p>FIA analysis was performed using a LC-MS/MS platform consisting of a 1260 Infinity II HPLC (Agilent Technologies, Waldbronn, Germany) coupled with a 5500+ QTRAP mass spectrometer (SCIEX, Framingham, MA, USA). </p>	<p>The LC-MS/MS platform consisted of a 1260 Infinity II HPLC (Agilent Technologies, Waldbronn, Germany) coupled with a 5500+ QTRAP mass spectrometer (SCIEX, Framingham, MA, USA). Compounds were targeted by precursor ion scan, neutral loss scan or multiple reaction monitoring mode.</p>	<p>The identification and quantification of the analytes were attained using the Analyst and ChemoView v1.2 software (SCIEX) through comparison of analyte areas with those of stable isotope-labelled internal standards and finally expressed as M.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Compounds were targeted by precursor ion scan, neutral loss scan or multiple reaction monitoring mode.</p>

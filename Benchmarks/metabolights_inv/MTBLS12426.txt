Study Identifier	MTBLS12426
Study Title	Nano-functionalized probiotic treats atherosclerosis via inhibiting intestinal microbiota-TMA-TMAO axis
Study Description	<p>Many patients are suffering from atherosclerosis without typical risk factors, which&nbsp;can cause severe cardiovascular complications. Trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO), derived from gut microbes, is a key unconventional contributor&nbsp;to the development of atherosclerosis. Here we present a strategy&nbsp;performed by&nbsp;orally administered nano-functionalized probiotics (PDMF@LGG) to&nbsp;inhibit TMAO through the gut microbiota-trimethylamine (TMA)-TMAO axis. PDMF@LGG, composed of polydopamine-coated Lacticaseibacillus rhamnosus&nbsp;GG&nbsp;and nanoparticles based on a reactive oxygen species (ROS)-responsive polymeric prodrug of fluoromethylcholine (FMC), can promote the retention of probiotics and nanoparticles in the intestine to persistently scavenge elevated ROS and release drugs. This process suppresses TMA production and absorption, lowering plasma TMAO levels. The therapeutic effects on male ApoE-/-&nbsp;mice demonstrate that&nbsp;PDMF@LGG reduces TMAO levels, alleviates atherosclerotic plaque formation, and regulates gut microbial&nbsp;composition and various metabolites. Together, PDMF@LGG represents a potential&nbsp;candidate for atherosclerotic therapy caused by TMAO&nbsp;and broadens the range&nbsp;of treatable atherosclerosis.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>After 4 months of different treatments in high-choline diet induced atherosclerotic mice, mice were sacrificed. Serum samples were collected and stored at -80 °C refrigerator.</p>	<p>The sample stored at -80 °C refrigerator was thawed on ice and vortexed for 10 s. Next 50 μL of sample and 300 μL of extraction solution (ACN:methanol, 1:4, v/v) containing internal standards were added into a 2 mL microcentrifugetube. The sample was vortexed for 3 min and then centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 10 min (4 °C). Then 200 μL of the supernatant was collected and placed in -20 °C for 30 min, and then centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 3 min (4 °C). A 180 μL aliquots of supernatant were transferred for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>The sample extracts were analyzed using an LC-ESI-MS/MS system (UPLC, ExionLC AD, https://sciex.com.cn/;&nbsp;MS, QTRAP System, https://sciex.com).&nbsp;The analytical conditions were as follows, UPLC: column, Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 C18 (1.8 µm, 2.1 mm x 100 mm);&nbsp;column temperature, 40 °C;&nbsp;flow rate, 0.4 mL/min;&nbsp;injection volume, 2 μL;&nbsp;solvent system, water (0.1% formic acid): acetonitrile (0.1% formic acid);&nbsp;solvent B gradient program, 5-20% in 2 min, increased to 60% in the following 3 min, increased to 99% in 1 min and held for 1.5 min, then come back to 5% within 0.1 min, held for 2.4 min.</p>	<p>LIT and triple quadrupole (QQQ) scans were acquired on a triple quadrupole-linear ion trap mass spectrometer (QTRAP), QTRAP LC-MS/MS System, equipped with an ESI Turbo Ion-Spray interface, operating in positive and negative ion mode and controlled by Analyst 1.6.3 software (Sciex). The ESI source operation parameters were as follows: source temperature 500 °C; ion spray voltage (IS) 5500 V (positive), -4500 V (negative); ion source gas I (GSI), gas II (GSII) and curtain gas (CUR) were set at 55, 60 and 25 psi, respectively; the collision gas (CAD) was high. Instrument tuning and mass calibration were performed with 10 and 100 μmol/L polypropylene glycol solutions in QQQ and LIT modes, respectively. A specific set of MRM transitions were monitored for each period according to the metabolites eluted within this period.</p>	<p>The raw MS data were converted to mzXML files using ProteoWizard MsConvert before importing into freely available XCMS software. For peak picking, the folowing parameters were used: centWave, m/z = 25 ppm, peakwidth = c(10,60), preiter = c(10,100). For peak grouping, bw = 5, mzwid = 0.025, minfrac = 0.5 were used. In the extracted ion features, only the variables having more than 50% of the nonzero measurement values in at least one group were kept.</p>	<p>Compound identification of metabolites by MS/MS spectra with an in-house database established with available authentic standards.</p>

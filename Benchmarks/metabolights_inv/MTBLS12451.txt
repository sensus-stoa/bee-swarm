Study Title	ATG9A-PLA2G6 axis reprograms phospholipid metabolism to drive metabolic liver disease and hepatocellular carcinoma
Study Description	<p>The liver orchestrates systemic metabolism, and its dysfunction drives diseases including metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). ATG9A, an autophagy-related transmembrane protein and lipid scramblase, regulates lipid dynamics, yet its role in hepatic pathogenesis remains unclear. Using multi-model approaches, we demonstrate that liver-specific ATG9A overexpression in mice enhances autophagic flux but impairs degradation. ATG9A disrupted hepatic lipid metabolism: it reduced lipid droplet accumulation but exacerbated inflammation and fibrosis. Furthermore, we identified PLA2G6 as a fresh ATG9A binding protein. ATG9A-PLA2G6 interaction accelerated phosphatidylcholine degradation, perturbing fatty acid metabolism and causing mitochondrial dysfunction. Besides, ATG9A promoted tumor growth in vivo, independent of canonical autophagy. Our findings redefine ATG9A as a dual metabolic effector, driving liver disease progression through lipid remodeling and organelle stress. The ATG9A-PLA2G6 axis presents a therapeutic target for metabolic liver disorders and HCC.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>After injecting AAV-ATG9A virus into wild-type mice at 6 weeks, they were fed with regular feed and high-fat feed for 16 weeks. After sacrificing the mice from the normal and high-fat diet groups, the livers were quickly removed, rinsed with pre-cooled PBS and frozen in liquid nitrogen. The samples were transported on dry ice for further analysis.</p>	<p>Weigh 50 mg of each sample into the grinding tube of Jingxin, add 3 medium steel balls, and add 200 μL of pre cooling A methanol water solution was homogenized and crushed at low temperature in a tissue disruptor, and 825 mL was added for pre cooling Mix MTBE (Methyl tert butyl ether) well, extract at 4 °C and shake for 60 min; Then ice bath ultrasound 30 min, Add 200 μL of pre cooled ultrapure water, mix well and let it stand at room temperature for 10 min; 4 °C, 16,000 x g, away from Heart for 20 min, take the supernatant and evaporate it in a high-speed vacuum concentration centrifuge. Add during mass spectrometry analysis of each sample Dissolve 200 μL of dichloromethane methanol solution , at 4 °C, 20,000 x g, centrifuge for 15 min, Take the supernatant for injection analysis.</p>	<p>The samples were separated using a NexeraX2LC-30AD ultra-high pressure liquid chromatography system (Shimadzu). flow</p><p>Mobile phase: A solution is a 50% acetonitrile aqueous solution containing 10 mM ammonium acetate at pH 8.0, while B solution is 100% acetonitrile. The sample was dissolved in 200 L of dichloromethane:methanol (1:1) and placed in a 4 °C automatic sampler at a column temperature of 40 °C, The flow rate is 300 µL/min and the injection volume is 1.5 µL. The relevant liquid phase gradient is as follows: 0.0-0.1 min, and the B solution is maintained at 85%; From 0.1 to 7.5 min, liquid B undergoes a linear change from 85% to 65%; From 8.5 to 11.0 min, liquid B linearly changes from 65% to 5%; liquid B changes linearly from 5% to 85%; From 11.1 to 15.0 min, B solution maintained at 85%. Insert QC samples in the middle of the column for testing and evaluating the stability and repeatability of the system.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry analysis was performed using QTRAP5500 mass spectrometer (ABSCIEX) in both positive and negative ion modes. Negative ion mode: Source Temperature: 550 °C; Ion Source Gas1 (GAS1): 40; Ion Source Gas2 (GAS2): 50; Curtain Gas (CUR): 35; Ion Spray Voltage Floating (ISVF): -4500 V.

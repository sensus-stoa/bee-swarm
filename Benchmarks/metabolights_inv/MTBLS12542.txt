Study Protocol Description	<p>Canola cotyledon infected by L. maculans, samples were collected at 3,7,11 days after infection.</p>	<p>4 mL of methanol per gram of tissue and 1 mL of water per gram of tissue were added to the tubes containing pre-weighed plant samples. The sample tubes were then vortexed for 15 s. 700 μL of mixture from each sample was transferred to 2-mL Eppendorf tubes, which contain preloaded ceramic beads (2.8 mm). Each of the sample was then homogenized for 15 seconds using Bioprep-24 homogenizer (Allsheng, China). After incubated in -20 °C freezer for 10 min, the samples were finally centrifuged at 15,000 g for 10 min at 4 °C. The supernatants were transferred into new vials and were used for metabolomic analysis.</p>	<p>Instrument: Thermo Vanquish UHPLC System</p><p>Column: Agilent eclipse plus reversed-phase C18 column (150 x 2.1 mm, 1.8 μm particle size)</p><p>MPA: 0.1% (v/v) formic acid in water</p><p>MPB: 0.1% (v/v) formic acid in acetonitrile</p><p>Gradient: t = 0 min, 25% B; t = 10 min, 99% B; t = 13 min, 99% B; t = 13.1 min, 25% B; t=16 min, 25% B</p><p>Flow Rate: 400 μL/min</p><p>Column Oven Temperature: 40 °C</p><p>Mass Range: m/z 220-1000</p><p>Acquisition Rate: 1 Hz</p>	<p>Instrument: Thermo Q Exactive Orbitrap MS</p><p>Column: Agilent eclipse plus reversed-phase C18 column (150 x 2.1 mm, 1.8 μm particle size)</p><p>MPA: 0.1% (v/v) formic acid in water</p><p>MPB: 0.1% (v/v) formic acid in acetonitrile</p><p>Gradient: t = 0 min, 25% B; t = 10 min, 99% B; t = 13 min, 99% B; t = 13.1 min, 25% B; t=16 min, 25% B</p><p>Flow Rate: 400 μL/min</p><p>Column Oven Temperature: 40 °C</p><p>Mass Range: m/z 220-1000</p><p>Acquisition Rate: 1 Hz</p>	<p>LC-MS data (.raw file, in profile mode) were first exported to .txt file with Proteowizard MSConvert.

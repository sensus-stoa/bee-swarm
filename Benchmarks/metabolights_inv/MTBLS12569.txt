Study Identifier	MTBLS12569
Study Title	Histone serotonylation promotes pancreatic cancer development via lipid metabolism remodeling
Study Description	<p>Neurotransmitter serotonin (5-hydroxytryptamine [5-HT]) has emerged to play parallel roles in both neurobiology and oncology. Apart from receptor-mediated signaling transduction pattern, serotonin can be covalently integrated into histone (the post-translational modification known as histone serotonylation) and serve as an epigenetic mark associated with permissive gene expression. However, how histone serotonylation influences tumorigenesis is yet to be understood. In this study, we observe the higher levels of histone serotonylation (H3K4me3Q5ser) and transglutaminases 2 (TGM2, the enzyme catalyzing serotonylation) in both pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) tissues and cell lines in comparison with their normal counterparts, and inhibition of histone serotonylation suppresses PDAC development. Mechanistically, we demonstrate that TGM2-mediated histone serotonylation at promoter of the gene encoding stearoyl-CoA desaturase (SCD) up-regulates its expression and drives PDAC development by lipid metabolism remodeling. Collectively, this study reveals histone serotonylation as an important driver of PDAC tumorigenesis.Neurotransmitter serotonin (5-hydroxytryptamine [5-HT]) has emerged to play parallel roles in both neurobiology and oncology. Study Protocol Description	<p>The samples are PANC-1 and Capan-2 cells upon SCD knockdown or TGM2 knockdown and their control groups.</p>	<p>Collect cultured cells (1 x 107 cells per sample) into centrifuge tubes. After grinding and centrifugation of the cells, the supernatant was taken and vortexed with chloroform, centrifuged, and the lower layer liquid was taken and placed in a sample bottle. It was concentrated and dried, and dissolved in methanol.</p>	<p>The cellular fatty acids were detected by ultra high performance liquid chromatography ion mobility quadrupole time-of-flight mass spectrometer (Acquity I-class; Waters). The mobile phase A: containing 0.1% formic acid water; mobile phase B: containing 0.1% formic acid acetonitrile (1/1). The gradient was as follows: 95% A and 5% B (initial); 80% A and 20% B (5 min); 100% B (18 min); 95% A and 5% B (28 min) with a flow rate of 0.4 mL/min in column BEH C18 1.7 um, 2.1 x 100 mm, column temperature at 45 °C.</p>	<p>MS instrument is Waters Vion IMS QTof. MS conditions were as follows: the acquisition mode at MSE; ionization mode at ESI negative, capillary voltage at 2 KV (negative), cone voltage at 40 V, desolvation temperature at 450 °C, desolvation gas at 900 L/h, cone gas at 50 L/h, source temperature at 115 °C, acquisition range from 50 to 1000 m/z, scan rate at 0.2 s, collision energy at 6 eV / 20-45 eV; lockmass at 250 pg/μL of leucine enkephalin; flow rate at 10 μL/min; interval at 0.5 s; sample time at 0.5 s; collision energy at 6 eV.</p>	<p>Data acquisition and analysis were carried out by Waters UNIFI 1.9.4.</p>	<p>Identify the types of fatty acids in the sample compared with the standard (named FA series in this study).</p>

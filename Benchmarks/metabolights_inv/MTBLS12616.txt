Study Protocol Description	<p>Liver and skeletal muscle (plantarflexor) tissue was harvested from adult female C57BL/6 mice that were randomly assigned access to either a 100% water bottle (Control mice; n = 9) or a bottle containing 80% water + 20% alcohol (ethanol) (Alcohol mice; n = 14) for 34-40 weeks.</p>	<p>For Amide column: Tissue added to centrifuge tubes along with two magnetic beads; 800 µL of precooled extraction reagent added (methanol: acetonitrile: water (2:2:1, v/v/v)) then 50HZ grind for 5min; 10 μL of the prepared internal standard added to each sample; Ground samples placed at -20°C for 2h; Samples centrifuged (25000 g *4℃, 15min); 600µL of each sample added in split-new EP tubes , then freeze dried; 600 μL of Acetonitrile: pure water (7:3 v/v) added, shaking for 10 min; 25000 g *4℃ centrifuge for 15min, then take the supernatant and place it in a split-new EP tube; 10 μL of each sample taken and mixed into QC samples.</p><p><br></p><p>For BEH C18 column: Tissue added to centrifuge tubes along with two magnetic beads; 10 μL of prepared internal standard added to each sample; 800 µL of precooled extraction reagent (methanol: acetonitrile: water (2:2:1, v/v/v)) added; 50HZ grind for 5min; Ground samples placed at -20°C for 2h; Samples centrifuged (25000 g *4℃, 15min); 600µL of each sample added in split-new EP tubes , then freeze dried; 600 μL of 50% methanol added to the dried sample, and shaken until completely dissolved; 25000 g *4℃ centrifuge for 15min, then take the supernatant and place it in a split-new EP tube; 10 μL of each sample taken and mixed into QC samples.</p><p><br></p><p>For CSH C18 column: Tissue added to centrifuge tubes along with two magnetic beads; 800 µL of pre-chilled dichloromethane/methanol (3:1, v/v) precipitant added, along with 10 µL of the prepared internal standard to each sample; TissueLyser grinds for 5min, then ice bath ultrasound for 10min, then refrigerated overnight for -20℃; Samples centrifuged (25000 g *4℃, 15min); 600µL of each sample taken and drained in freezer dryer; 600µL lipid reconstituted solution (isopropanol :acetonitrile: water = 2:1:1), reconstituted, shaken for 10 min; Ice bath ultrasound for 10 min; 25000 g *4℃ centrifuge for 15min; 10 μL of each sample taken and mixed into QC samples.</p>	<p>For Amide column: Chromatographic separation was performed on BEH Amide column (1.7 μm 2.1*100 mm, Waters, USA). At positive ion mode with mobile phase A consisting 95% acetonitrile water + 10mM ammonium formate + 0.1% formic acid and mobile phase B consisting 50% acetonitrile in water + 10mM ammonium formate + 0.1% formic acid. At positive ion mode, with mobile phase A consisting 95% acetonitrile water + 10mM ammonium formate (adjust pH=9 with ammonia water) and mobile phase B consisting 50% acetonitrile water + 10mM ammonium formate (adjust pH=9 with ammonia water). The column temperature was maintained at 30 °C. The gradient conditions were as follows: 2% B over 0.0-0.5 min, 2-50% B over 0.5-12.0min, 50%~98% B over 12~14 min, held constant at 98% B over 14~16 min and washed with 2% B over 16.1~18min. The flow rate was 0.35 mL/min and the injection volume was 2 μL.</p><p><br></p><p>For BEH C18 column: Chromatographic separation was performed on a Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH C18 column (1.7 μm, 2.1 mm × 100 mm, Waters, USA), and the column temperature was maintained at 45 °C. The mobile phase consisted of 0.1% formic acid (A) and acetonitrile (B) in the positive mode, and in the negative mode, the mobile phase consisted of 10 mM ammonium formate (A) and acetonitrile (B). The gradient conditions were as follows: 0-1 min, 2% B; 1-9 min, 2%-98% B; 9-12 min, 98% B; 12-12.1 min, 98% B to 2% B; and 12.1-15min, 2% B. The flow rate was 0.35 mL/min and the injection volume was 5 μL.</p><p><br></p><p>For CSH C18 column: Chromatographic separation was performed on CSH C18 column(1.7 μm 2.1*100 mm, Waters, USA) at positive ion mode with mobile phase A consisting 60% acetonitrile in water + 10mM ammonium formate + 0.1% formic acid and mobile phase B consisting 90% isopropanol + 10% acetonitrile + 10mM ammonium formate + 0.1% formic acid. At positive ion mode, with mobile phase A consisting 60% acetonitrile in water + 10mM ammonium formate and mobile phase B consisting 90% isopropanol + 10% acetonitrile + 10 mM ammonium formate. The column temperature was maintained at 55 °C. The gradient conditions were as follows: 40% ~43% B over 0~2min,43%~50% B over 2~2.1min, 50%~54% B over 2.1~7 min,54% ~70% B over 7~7.1min,70% ~99% B over 7.1~13 min,99%~40% B over 13~13.1 min, held constant at 99%~40%B over 13.1~15 min and washed with 40% B over 13.1 15min. The flow rate was 0.4 mL/min and the injection volume was 5 μL.</p>	<p>For Amide column: Using Q Exactive (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA) perform primary and secondary mass spectrometry data acquisition. The full scan range was 70–1050 m/z with a resolution of 70000, and the automatic gain control (AGC) target for MS acquisitions was set to 3e6 with a maximum ion injection time of 100 ms. Top 3 precursors were selected for subsequent MSMS fragmentation with a maximum ion injection time of 50 ms and resolution of 17500, the AGC was 1e5. The stepped normalized collision energy was set to 15, 30 and 45 eV. ESI parameters were setting as : Sheath gas flow rate was 40, Aux gas flow rate was 10, positive-ion mode Spray voltage(|KV|) was 3.80, negative-ion mode Spray voltage(|KV|) was 3.20, Capillary temperature was 320°C, Aux gas heater temperature was 350°C.</p><p><br></p><p>For BEH C18 column: Using Q Exactive (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA) perform primary and secondary mass spectrometry data acquisition. The full scan range was 70–1050 m/z with a resolution of 70000, and the automatic gain control (AGC) target for MS acquisitions was set to 3e6 with a maximum ion injection time of 100 ms. Top 3 precursors were selected for subsequent MSMS fragmentation with a maximum ion injection time of 50 ms and resolution of 17500, the AGC was 1e5. The stepped normalized collision energy was set to 20, 40 and 60 eV. ESI parameters were setting as : Sheath gas flow rate was 40, Aux gas flow rate was 10, positive-ion mode Spray voltage(|KV|) was 3.80, negative-ion mode Spray voltage(|KV|) was 3.20, Capillary temperature was 320°C, Aux gas heater temperature was 350°C.</p><p><br></p><p>For CSH C18 column: Using Q Exactive (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA) perform primary and secondary mass spectrometry data acquisition. The full scan range was 70–1050 m/z with a resolution of 70000, and the automatic gain control (AGC) target for MS acquisitions was set to 3e6 with a maximum ion injection time of 100 ms. Top 3 precursors were selected for subsequent MSMS fragmentation with a maximum ion injection time of 50 ms and resolution of 17500, the AGC was 1e5. The stepped normalized collision energy was set to 15, 30 and 45 eV. ESI parameters were setting as : Sheath gas flow rate was 40, Aux gas flow rate was 10, positive-ion mode Spray voltage(|KV|) was 3.80, negative-ion mode Spray voltage(|KV|) was 3.20, Capillary temperature was 320°C, Aux gas heater temperature was 350°C.</p>	<p>For Amide column: Compound Discover output was subject to probabilistic quotient normalisation to obtain relative peak areas, quality control-based robust LOESS signal correction to correct batch effect, removal of features with a coefficient of variation larger than 30% based on relative peak area in QC samples (all using MetaX software).</p><p><br></p><p>For BEH C18 column: Compound Discover output was subject to probabilistic quotient normalisation to obtain relative peak areas, quality control-based robust LOESS signal correction to correct batch effect, removal of features with a coefficient of variation larger than 30% based on relative peak area in QC samples (all using MetaX software).</p><p><br></p><p>For CSH C18 column: LipidSearch output was subject to removal of lipids with &gt; 50% missing values in QC samples and &gt; 80% missing values in experimental samples, imputation of remaining empty values via the k-Nearest Neighbour Algorithm, probabilistic quotient normalisation to obtain relative peak areas, quality control-based robust LOESS signal correction to correct batch effect, filtering to remove features with a coefficient of variation larger than 30% based on relative peak area in QC samples (all using MetaX software).</p><p><br></p>	<p>For Amide column: Identification performed using Compound Discoverer 3.3 (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA) in combination with bmdb (BGI metabolome database), mzcloud database and chemspider online databases (parent ion mass deviation &lt; 5ppm; mass deviation of fragment ions &lt; 10ppm; retention time deviation: &lt; 0.2min).</p><p><br></p><p>For BEH C18 column: Identification performed using Compound Discoverer 3.3 (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA) in combination with bmdb (BGI metabolome database), mzcloud database and chemspider online databases (parent ion mass deviation &lt; 5ppm; mass deviation of fragment ions &lt; 10ppm; retention time deviation: &lt; 0.2min).</p><p><br></p><p>For CSH C18 column: Identification performed using LipidSearch 4.1 (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA) with following parameters: Identification type, product; Search quality deviation of parent ion and daughter ion, 5 ppm; Response threshold, relative response deviation of sub ions 5.0%; Peak lifting quality deviation, 5ppm; M-score, 5.0; c-score, 2.0; Identification level selection, 'A', 'B', 'C', 'D'; Positive ion mode adduct form, [M+H]+, [M+NH4]+, [M+Na]+; Negative ion mode adduct form, [M-H]-, [M-2H]-, [M+HCOO]-; Retention time deviation, 0.1min.</p>

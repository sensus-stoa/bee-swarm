Study Title	Small auxin-up RNA promotes GmMYB176 gene transcription to modulate soybean isoflavone content
Study Description	<p>Isoflavones have been widely studied because of their potential health benefits, including antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anticancer properties. Soybeans (Glycine max (L.) Merr) are an effective source of natural isoflavones in humans. Therefore, it is important to cultivate varieties showing increased isoflavone contents and elucidate the secondary metabolic pathways of soybean isoflavones. The small auxin-up RNA (SAUR) gene family is the largest family in the auxin response. However, few reports have described their roles in soybean flavonoid metabolism. In this study, the contents of 200 flavonoid target metabolites in GmSAUR:GmSAUR transgenic soybeans were determined. The results showed that the transgenic soybeans had higher daidzein and genistin contents. Chromatin immunoprecipitation sequencing identified potential targets, including GmMYB176. GmSAUR bound to the promoter of GmMYB176; activated the expression of cinnamate 4-hydroxylase, isoflavone-7-O-beta-glucoside 6′′-O-malonyltransferase, cytochrome P450 84A1-like, and cytochrome P450 84A1; and promoted the accumulation of soybean isoflavones. These results suggested that the GmSAUR gene promoted the accumulation of soybean isoflavones by regulating the expression of GmMYB176.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The sample was taken from soybean seeds under conditions in Harbin (45°75'N, 126°63'E) with a 16/8 h light/dark cycle. The soybean grains were placed in sampling tubes and stored in a low-temperature freezer.</p>	<p>20 mg samples were weighed in a 2 mL centrifuge tube ( see specific weight, add steel balls, add 1 mL methanol : aqueous solution ( 7:3, V/V) vortex for 30 s ; 45 Hz homogenizer for 15 min, and ultrasound for 30 min under ice water bath conditions ; the samples were centrifuged at 12000 r / min at 4 °C for 15 min. The supernatant 200 μL was filtered through 0.22 μm filter membrane and then injected for analysis. The corresponding amount of standard ( 10 mmol / L ) was accurately removed in a 10 mL volumetric flask to prepare a 10 umol / L mixed standard solution. A series of calibration solutions were obtained by successively diluting the standard solution.</p>	<p>In this experiment, the target compounds were separated by ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 ( 100 × 2.1 mm, 1.8 μm, Waters ) liquid chromatography column using Waters ACQUITY I-Class ultra-high performance liquid chromatograph. Liquid chromatography phase A was aqueous solution containing 0.1 % formic acid, and phase B was acetonitrile containing 0.1 % formic acid. The column oven temperature was 35 ° C, the sample tray was set to 10 ° C, and the injection volume was 2 μL.</p>	<p>In this experiment, SCIEX QTRAP 6500 + triple quadrupole mass spectrometer equipped with IonDrive Turbo V ESI ion source was used for mass spectrometry analysis in multiple reaction monitoring ( MRM ) mode. The ion source parameters are as follows : Curtain Gas = 35 psi, IonSpray Voltage = + 5500 V, -4500 V, Temperature = 550 ° C, Ion Source Gas 1 = 50 psi, Ion Source Gas 2 = 55 psi.&nbsp;</p><p>Before UHPLC-MS / MS analysis, the standard solution of the target compound was introduced into the mass spectrometry.</p><p>In this experiment, all mass spectrometry data acquisition and quantitative analysis of target compounds were completed by SCIEX Analyst Work Station Software ( Version 1.7.2 ) and Sciex OS 2.0.1.</p>	<p>The raw Ms data (wif.scan files) were converted to MzXMl files using ProteoWizard MsConvert before importing into freely available XCMs software.</p>	<p>The database of Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes http://www.genome.jp/kegg/</p>

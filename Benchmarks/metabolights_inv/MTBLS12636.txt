Study Protocol Description	<p>Participants were placed in the lithotomy position for sample collection, where vaginal fornix secretions were obtained using sterile speculums and sampling brushes without lubricant application. The brush was suspended in 5 mL of pre-chilled (4℃) 80% methanol immediately after collection. The methanol-preserved samples were vortexed for 15 seconds upon laboratory arrival and stored at -80℃ overnight for subsequent metabolomic analysis.</p>	<p>The methanol-preserved samples stored overnight were centrifuged at 12,000 rpm and 4℃ for 5 minutes. A 1.5 mL of the supernatant was collected, vacuum-dried to remove solvents, and reconstituted in 100 μL of a water:acetonitrile:methanol&nbsp;(2:1:1, v/v/v) solution. Following centrifugation at 15,000 rpm for 30 minutes at 4℃, the resulting supernatant was transferred to chromatographic vials for LC-MS/MS metabolomic analysis.</p>	<p>Data&nbsp;were&nbsp;acquired&nbsp;in both POSITIVE and NEGATIVE mode using an LC-20A HPLC system (Shimadzu, Tokyo, Japan) coupled to an ESI-Q-TOF mass spectrometer (SCIEX TripleTOF 4600, USA).&nbsp;Seven microliters of the extracted metabolite sample were loaded by an autosampler.&nbsp;Chromatographic separation was achieved using a Waters XBridge&nbsp;Amide&nbsp;column (2.1&nbsp;× 150&nbsp;mm, 3.5&nbsp;μm; Waters, Milford, MA, USA) maintained at 0.2 mL/min flow rate. For positive mode, the mobile phase consisted of:&nbsp;Solvent A,&nbsp;0.1% formic acid in water; Solvent B,&nbsp;0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile. For negative mode, the mobile phase consisted of: Solvent A, water; Solvent B,&nbsp;acetonitrile. The gradient program was as follows:&nbsp;0-3.5-13-18-23-23.01-28&nbsp;min,&nbsp;95%-75%-45%-30%-30%-95%-95%&nbsp;B.</p>	<p>Data&nbsp;were&nbsp;acquired&nbsp;in both POSITIVE and NEGATIVE mode using an LC-20A HPLC system (Shimadzu, Tokyo, Japan) coupled to an ESI-Q-TOF mass spectrometer (SCIEX TripleTOF 4600, USA).&nbsp;Mass spectrometry was performed in information-dependent acquisition (IDA) mode&nbsp;with these parameters:&nbsp;(1) MS acquisition: scan range = 50-1,000 Da; accumulation time&nbsp;= 0.25&nbsp;s; maximum number of candidate ions to monitor per cycle&nbsp;= 10 spectra; (2) MS/MS acquisition: scan range = 50-1,000 Da; accumulation time&nbsp;= 0.1 s; collision energy = 47&nbsp;eV&nbsp;(positive mode) or -30 eV (negative mode).</p>	<p>Raw data were converted to mzXML and mgf file by MSConvert (version 3.0.23222) followed by feature extraction by R package XCMS (version 4.0.2), with all parameters configured according to the official MetDNA documentation (http://metdna.zhulab.cn/metdna/help).</p>	<p>Metabolite structural identification was performed using MetDNA&nbsp;2, with all parameters configured according to the official MetDNA documentation (http://metdna.zhulab.cn/metdna/help).</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>After the tumor cells in logarithmic growth phase were digested with trypsin in a super-clean bench, the cells were resuspended in DMEM medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum and 1% double antibody, and the cell density was adjusted to 5×10^5 cells /mL. The cell suspension was inoculated into a cell culture dish and an appropriate volume, such as 5 mL, was inoculated into each bottle and gently shaken to evenly distribute the cells. The seeded cells were cultured in an incubator at 37℃ with 5% CO2 for 24 hours, and the cells were allowed to adhere to the wall. After 24h of culture, the cells were observed for adherence, and when the cells adhered well, the original medium was aspirated and the cells were gently washed 2-3 times with PBS buffer to remove non-adherent cells and impurities. Fresh DMEM complete medium containing 4g/L 13C labeled glucose (concentration determined according to literature) was added to the Petri dishes, while a control group was set up with the same amount of normal glucose medium. The cells were cultured in 5% CO2 incubator at 37℃ for 3 hours. The total number of cells per sample was not less than 5×10^7. At the end of the 3h incubation, the cell culture flask was quickly removed from the incubator and transferred to ice for operation. The cells in the cell culture flasks were digested and transferred to a 15 mL centrifuge tube and centrifuged at 3000 rpm for 10mins at 4℃ to remove cell debris and impurities. The centrifuged cell precipitate was transferred to a new 1.5 mL centrifuge tube, labeled with sample information, and immediately stored in a cryogenic freezer at -80 ° C for downstream detection.</p>	<p>Samples of stored metabolites were removed from the -80 ° C refrigerator and thawed on ice. 500μL of cold extraction buffer (methanol: acetonitrile: water = 2:2:1, v/v/v) was added to the collected cell sample, sonicated for 2mins, and then centrifuged at 14,000 g for 5mins at 4 ℃. The supernatant was completely lyophilized (using a FreeZone 6 Liter, Labconco, USA freeze dryer) and redissolved in 50 μL methanol-water (1:1, v/v) prior to measurements.</p>	<p>A SeQuant ZIC-HILIC column (100 mm×2.1 mm i.d., 3.5 µm, Merck, Germany) was used, the column temperature was maintained at 45 ° C, and the sample manager temperature was 4 ℃. Mobile phase A consisted of 50 mM aqueous ammonium formate and mobile phase B consisted of acetonitrile. Gradient conditions were 0-10 mins (90-50% B), 10-12 mins (50-90% B), and 12-15 mins (90% B), with a flow rate of 0.4 mL/min and an injection volume of 1 µL.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry conditions were as follows: analysis was carried out on a Thermo Q-Exactive plus hybrid quadrupole-orbital trap mass spectrometer. The capillary voltage was 2 Kv, the source temperature was 120 ° C, the desolvation temperature was 500 ° C, the desolvation gas flow rate was 1000 L/Hr, the cone gas flow rate was 50 L/h, the mass range was m/z 500-1000, the scanning time was 0.2 s, the collision energy (CE) was 6 eV, and the mass error was allowed to be 3 mDa. The adduct is expected to be -H. m/z 554.2620 was used as the reference mass for lock spray, with an interval of 0.5mins, a sampling time of 0.5mins, and a flow rate of 10 µL/min. Instruments were performed every 48h for performance optimization and routine maintenance</p>	<p>TraceFinder 5.0 (Thermo Fisher) and Xcalibur 4.0 (Thermo Fisher) were used for precise mass-based data processing and ion annotation.</p>	<p>An isotopically corrected matrix generated from a control group of unlabeled samples was used before meaningful interpretation. Metabolite mass isotope isomer distributions (MID, also known as mass distribution vector, MDV) are determined based on the ratio of the integrated peak areas of the selected isotope isomer to the sum of the integrated peak areas of all possible isotope isomers of a given metabolite.</p>	<p>For ¹³C-glucose flux analysis, cells were incubated with 4 g/L C13-labeld glucose (CIL) for 3 h. Metabolites were extracted with ice-cold methanol: acetonitrile: water (2:2:1), centrifuged (14,000×g, 10 min), lyophilized, and reconstituted in 50 μL methanol: water (1:1). Chromatographic separation was performed on a SeQuant ZIC-HILIC column (100 mm × 2.1 mm i.d., 3.5 µm) at 45°C, with mobile phase A as 50 mM ammonium formate and phase B as acetonitrile; the gradient elution was 90–50% B in 0–10 min, 50–90% B in 10–12 min, and 90% B in 12–15 min at 0.4 mL/min, with a 1 µL injection volume. Mass spectrometry was conducted using a Thermo Q-Exactive plus in negative ESI mode, with a capillary voltage of 2 kV, source temperature of 120°C, desolvation temperature of 500°C, and a mass range of m/z 500–1000 scanned at 0.2 s; HCD fragmentation at 6 eV collision energy was applied, and internal calibration used m/z 554.2620 every 0.5 min to maintain 3 mDa accuracy. Data were processed by TraceFinder 5.0 and Xcalibur 4.0, applying isotope correction from control samples and calculating mass isotopomer distribution as the ratio of selected isotopomer peak areas to the total for each metabolite, enabling accurate metabolite identification and isotope tracing analysis.</p>

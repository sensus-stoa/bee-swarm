Study Protocol Description	<p>Liquid chromatography mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) on plasma of young (aged 24-40) and aged (aged 65-90) human subjects.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Serum (100 μL) was extracted using methanol and methyl tert-butyl ether (MTBE), followed by water-induced phase separation. The organic phase was collected, purified with C18 SPE cartridges, dried under nitrogen, and reconstituted in 50% methanol.</p>	<p>Separation was performed on a reverse-phase ACQUITY UPLC BEH C18 column (2.1 × 100 mm, 1.7 μm) with water (0.1% formic acid) as mobile phase A and acetonitrile/isopropanol (70:30, 0.1% formic acid) as mobile phase B, at 0.3 mL/min and 40 °C.</p>	<p>MS analysis was performed using SCIEX TripleTOF 6600+ in high-resolution multiple-reaction monitoring mode. Electrospray ionization (positive mode) was used with a TOF-MS/MS method loop.</p>	<p>Quantification was performed using MultiQuant software (SCIEX). Calibration curves were constructed from 15 standard concentrations (0.01–1000 pg/μL). Internal standards were used for normalization.</p>	<p>Metabolites were identified and confirmed using PeakView software (SCIEX) based on accurate mass and MS/MS spectra.</p>

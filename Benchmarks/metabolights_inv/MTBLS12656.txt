STUDY
Study Identifier	MTBLS12656
Study Title	Exogenous glutamate potentiates gentamicin to kill multidrug- and carbapenem-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa through activating biosynthesis of unsaturated fatty acids
Study Description	<p>Antibiotic-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa is a major clinical challenge due to limited drug uptake. This study shows that exogenous glutamate restores gentamicin efficacy by reprogramming bacterial metabolism to enhance membrane permeability. The effect is mediated through increased biosynthesis of unsaturated fatty acids, which is further confirmed by oleic acid supplementation. These findings reveal a novel metabolic approach to overcome multidrug and carbapenem resistance, offering a promising adjunct strategy to improve antibiotic treatment outcomes.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>In brief, after being washed with physiological saline for 3 times, the overnight cultures were resuspended in M9 medium with corresponding treatment, and cultured at 37 °C, 200 rpm for 6 h. Then, the cultures were centrifuged at 8000 rpm for 5 min, washed 3 times and resuspended with physiological saline and adjusted OD600&nbsp;to 1.0. Next 10 mL these cultures were collected, and resuspend in 1 mL ?80&nbsp;pre-cooled methanol (Sigma). To normalize variations across samples, an internal standard (0.1 mg/mL ribitol, Sigma) was used. Crush with an ultrasonic disruptor for 10 min, power at 35%, on for 2 s, off for 3 s. Centrifuged at 12,000 rpm at 4 °C for 10 min. Transfer 900 L of the supernatant to a new centrifuge tube. Placed the centrifuge tube in a vacuum concentrator at 37&nbsp;°C until the sample is completely dried, which took approximately 180 min.</p>	<p>In brief, 80 L of a 20 mg/mL methoxylamine hydrochloride solution dissolved in pyridine, ultrasonicated until the precipitate dissolves, and incubated at 37 °C for 180 min. Then, added 80 L of N-methyl-N-(trimethylsilyl) trifluoroacetamide (MSTFA) and incubated at 37 °C for 30 min. Centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 10 min, then transferred 120 L of the supernatant into an insert vial for sample injection. The derivatized sample with 1? L was injected to DBS-MS column.</p>	<p>Initial temperature was 85 °C for 5 min, followed by an increase to 270 °C at a rate of 15 °C/min and held for 5 min.</p>	<p>Helium was used as carrier gas at constant flow with a rate of 1 mL/min. The MS scan range was at 50-600 m/z.</p>	<p>The GC-MS data were detected with an Agilent 7890A GC equipped with an Agilent 5975C VL MSD detector (Agilent Technologies). Initial peak detection and mass spectral deconvolution were carried out with Agilent software (Agilent 6.0).</p>	<p>Metabolites were identified according to spectral matching and retention time (RT) by searching in the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Mass Spectral Library. Data matrix was normalized using internal standard (ribitol) and the total intensity.</p>

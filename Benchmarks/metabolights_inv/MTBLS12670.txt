Study Protocol Description	<p>The experiment used L. angustifolia , a widely cultivated local variety, with seeds obtained from a commercial market in Yining, Xinjiang, China. Healthy, uniform seeds were surface-sterilized with 75% ethanol and germinated in a greenhouse. After 80 days of growth, seedlings were transferred to an artificial climate chamber (BIC-250, Shanghai Boxun Medical Biological Instruments Co., Ltd, China) for a 7-day acclimation period under controlled conditions (25 °C, 75% relative humidity, 16/8 h light/dark cycle). The acclimated seedlings were divided into two groups: a control group maintained under original conditions and treatment groups subjected to temperature stress at 38 °C for different durations—short-term (1, 3 and 5 h) and long-term cyclic stress (38/25 °C for 5/19 h daily over 3 and 5 days). After treatment, aboveground tissues were collected separately for metabolomic analyses, with 3 biological replicates per treatment.</p>	<p>Tissue samples (100 mg) were homogenized in liquid nitrogen and extracted with ice-cold 80% methanol (v/v). After vortexing and incubation on ice for 5 min, the homogenate was centrifuged (15,000 x g, 4 °C, 20 min). The supernatant was diluted with LC-MS grade water to a final ratio of&nbsp;53:47 methanol:water&nbsp;(v:v). Pooled quality control (QC) samples and solvent blanks were processed identically to monitor technical variability. Metabolites with a coefficient of variation (CV) &gt;30% in QCs were excluded from subsequent analysis.</p>	<p>Samples were analyzed using a Vanquish UHPLC system (Thermo Fisher Scientific) equipped with a Hypersil Gold C18 column (100 x 2.1 mm, 1.9 μm) maintained at 40 °C. The mobile phase consisted of (A) 0.1% formic acid in water and (B) methanol, delivered at 0.2 mL/min with the following gradient: 2% B (0.0-1.5 min), 2-85% B (1.5-4.5 min), 85-100% B (4.5-10.0 min), followed by column re-equilibration. A 2 μL injection volume was used with the autosampler temperature set to 4 °C.</p>	<p>Analysis was performed on an Orbitrap Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific) with electrospray ionization (ESI) operating in both positive and negative modes (spray voltage: ±3.5 kV). The mass range was set to 70-1050 m/z with a resolution of 120,000 (at m/z 200). Key source parameters included: capillary temperature 320 °C, sheath gas flow 35 arb, auxiliary gas flow 10 arb and auxiliary gas heater temperature 350 °C. Full MS scans were acquired at 12 Hz scan rate.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were processed using&nbsp;XCMS&nbsp;(version X.X) for peak detection, alignment and quantification with the following parameters: mass tolerance = 10 ppm, retention time window = 20 s and signal-to-noise threshold = 5. Data were then normalized to pooled quality control (QC) samples using the formula:&nbsp;Relative peak area = (Sample peak area)/(Median QC peak area). Metabolites with &gt;30% coefficient of variation (CV) in QC samples were excluded. Statistical analysis was performed in&nbsp;R (version X.X)&nbsp;using the&nbsp;metaX&nbsp;package for multivariate analysis (PCA, PLS-DA), with metabolites considered significant at&nbsp;*p*&nbsp;&lt; 0.05 and VIP &gt; 1.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was performed by matching MS/MS spectra against&nbsp;reference databases&nbsp;(MassBank, HMDB and LipidMaps) using&nbsp;MS-DIAL&nbsp;(version X.X) with a mass accuracy threshold of 10 ppm. Putative annotations were further validated by retention time alignment with authentic standards when available. For pathway analysis, identified metabolites were mapped to&nbsp;KEGG&nbsp;pathways using the&nbsp;MetaboAnalyst&nbsp;(version X.X) platform. Confidence levels for annotations followed&nbsp;Metabolomics Standards Initiative (MSI)&nbsp;guidelines: Level 1 (identified by reference standard), Level 2 (putatively annotated by spectral/library match) and Level 3 (putatively characterized by chemical class).</p>

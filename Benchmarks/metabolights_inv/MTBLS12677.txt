Study Identifier	MTBLS12677
Study Title	Metabolomic Profiling Reveals Key Metabolic Alterations in MCF7 Tamoxifen-Resistant Cells Following EPAS1 Inhibition
Study Description	<p>This study aims to investigate the metabolic alterations associated with acquired tamoxifen (TAM) resistance in luminal A breast cancer cells. Using untargeted LC-MS/MS metabolomics, we compared parental MCF7 and TAM-resistant MCF7/Tam1 cells to identify key metabolic pathways affected by EPAS1 (HIF-2α)-mediated hypoxia-driven reprogramming. The study further evaluates the impact of EPAS1 inhibition by PT2977 on the reversal of these metabolic changes and restoration of TAM sensitivity.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Cells were cultured under standard conditions and harvested from four experimental groups (MCF7, MCF7/Tam1, MCF7/Tam1 + PT2977 1 μM, and MCF7/Tam1 + PT2977 10 μM) for metabolite extraction.</p><p><br></p>	<p>Metabolite extraction was performed using 80% methanol at -20°C for 2 hours. After vortexing and centrifugation, supernatants were dried, reconstituted in 0.1% formic acid, and used for LC-MS/MS analysis.</p><p><br></p>	<p>Metabolites were separated on a PepMap RSLC C18 column using a gradient of 0.1% formic acid in water (solvent A) and 80% acetonitrile with 0.1% formic acid (solvent B) at 400 nL/min over 60 minutes.</p><p><br></p>	<p>MS analysis was performed using LC-MS/MS in the range of 100–1000 m/z. Conditions included positive ion mode and high-resolution acquisition to enable untargeted metabolomic profiling.</p><p><br></p>	<p>Raw data files were converted to mzML format and normalized prior to multivariate analysis using appropriate software packages for peak detection, alignment, and feature extraction.</p><p><br></p>	<p>Metabolite features were matched against reference databases using accurate mass, retention time, and MS/MS fragmentation patterns to identify compounds with high confidence.</p><p><br></p>

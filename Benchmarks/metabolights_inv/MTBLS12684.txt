Study Protocol Type Term Source REF	MTBLS	MTBLS	CHMO	CHMO	OBI	MI
Study Protocol Description	<p>Peripheral blood (5 mL) was drawn from fasting participants before gastroscopy or surgery into ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid (EDTA) tubes, delivered on ice, and processed within one hour at 4 °C. Samples were centrifuged at 1,800 g for 10 minutes at 4 °C, and the plasma layer was aliquoted and stored at –80 °C according to a uniform SOP (GB/T 38576-2020) .</p>	<p>Several recovery standards are added prior to the first step in the extraction process for QC purposes. Samples were homogenized and extracted using methanol in a ratio of 1:4. The mixtures were shaken for 3 min and precipitated by centrifugation at 4000 x g, 10 min at 20 °C. The resulting extract is divided into four fractions: two for analysis by two separate reverse phase (RP)/UPLC-MS/MS methods using positive ion mode electrospray ionization (ESI), one for analysis by RP/UPLC-MS/MS using negative ion mode ESI, one for analysis by HILIC/UPLC-MS/MS using negative ion mode ESI. Samples were transferred to sample plates and dried under blowing nitrogen, then re-dissolved in reconstitution</p><p>solutions for sample injection into UPLC-MS/MS systems.</p>	<p>All methods utilize a Waters ACQUITY ultra-performance liquid chromatography (UPLC). column temperature: 40 °C; injection volume: 5 µL; The LC separation was performed in a 3.4 min for positive (assay 1 and 2), 6.5 min for negative (assay 3 and 4).</p><p>Assay 1: UPLC-MS/MS method, QE was operated in positive ESI mode. In this method, the extract is gradient-eluted from a C18 column (Waters UPLC BEH C18-2.1x100 mm, 1.7 um) using water and methanol, containing 0.05% perfluoropentanoic acid (PFPA) and 0.1% formic acid (FA).</p><p>Assay 2: UPLC-MS/MS method had the QE operated in ESI positive mode , but is chromatographically optimized for more hydrophobic compounds. In this method, the extract is gradient eluted from the C18 reverse-phase (UPLC BEH C18, 2.1x100 mm, 1.7 um; Waters) using methanol, acetonitrile, water, 0.05% PFPA and 0.01% FA, and is operated at an overall higher organic content.&nbsp;</p><p>Assay 3: UPLC-MS/MS method, QE was operated in negative ESI mode, and the UPLC column was C18 reverse-phase (UPLC BEH C18, 2.1x100 mm, 1.7 um; Waters). The extracts are gradient-eluted from the column using methanol and water, however with 6.5 mM Ammonium Bicarbonate at pH 8.&nbsp;</p><p>Assay 4: QE was operated in negative ESI mode,and the UPLC column was HILIC (UPLC BEH Amide, 2.1x150 mm, 1.7 um; Waters). The extracts are gradient-eluted from the column using a gradient consisting of water and acetonitrile with 10 mM Ammonium Formate, pH 10.8.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Q Exactive (QE) hybrid Quadrupole-Orbitrap mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific, San Jose, USA) was operated at a mass resolution of 35000, the scan range was 70-1000 m/z, The AGC target was 1e6 in both positive and negative mode. MS data were recorded in electrospray ionization (ESI). MS/MS spectra of precursor ions was obtained in dd-MS2 mode, within an isolation window of 3.0 m/z at a peak resolution of 17500.</p>	<p>Raw data were extracted, peak-identified, and QC processed using Lab's hardware and software(e.g. Aird). These systems are built on a web-service platform utilizing Microsoft's .</p>	<p>For metabolite identification, a proprietary in-house librarycontaining retention times, molecular weight-to-charge ratios (m/z), and MS/MS spectraof pure reference compounds were used.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Sixty-two one-day-old specific-pathogen-free (SPF) White Leghorn chickens were randomly allocated into two groups (n = 31 per group) and housed in negative-pressure isolators with&nbsp;ad libitum&nbsp;access to food and water. At 32 days post-hatch, chickens in the challenge group were inoculated via the oculonasal route with 200 μL of allantoic fluid containing 10^5 EID50 of the IBV QXL strain. Control animals received 200 μL of sterile phosphate-buffered saline (PBS) via the same route. All chickens were euthanized by cervical dislocation following anesthesia at 39 days post-hatch. Oviduct tissues were pooled from three birds per biological replicate to ensure sufficient sample quantity; tissues from deceased birds were excluded. Six biological replicates per group were collected. All tissues were immediately snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at −70°C until processing.</p>	<p>&nbsp;50 mg of tissue was homogenized in liquid nitrogen and lysed in 500 µL precooled extraction solvent (methanol/water, 4:1 v/v, 0.1% formic acid). After vortexing (30 s) and ice-bath sonication (10 min), extracts were incubated at −20°C for 30 min. Following centrifugation (20,000 × g, 10 min, 4 ℃),&nbsp;the supernatant was centrifuged for 5 min again. The supernatant was transferred to a fresh vial for UPLC-HRMS analysis. Pooled quality control (QC) samples were generated by combining 10 µL aliquots of each extract.</p>	<p>Separation employed an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (100 mm × 2.1 mm, 1.8 µm; Waters) at 35 °C using a Waters I-CLASS UPLC system (Waters, USA). Mobile phases: 1) solvent A: 5 mmol/L ammonium acetate + 5 mmol/L acetic acid + water, and 2) solvent B: 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile. Gradient elution was performed at 0.4 mL/min.</p>	<p>A TripleTOF 6600 Plus high-resolution tandem mass spectrometer (AB SCIEX, UK) operated in IDA mode with these parameters: </p><p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Curtain gas: 30 psi</p><p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ion source gases: 60 psi (GS1 and GS2)</p><p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Interface temperature: 650°C</p><p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ion spray voltage: +5.0 kV (positive mode)/−4.5 kV (negative mode)</p><p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mass range: 60–1200&nbsp;Da</p><p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Survey scans: 150ms, up to 12 dependent scans (threshold: 100 counts/s, charge +1)</p><p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cycle time: 0.56s</p><p>•&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dynamic exclusion: 4s</p><p>Mass accuracy was calibrated every 20 samples; QC samples were injected every 10 injections.</p>	<p>Raw data (.wiff) were converted to mzXML format and processed using XCMS/CAMERA (R platform).</p>	<p>Peaks were aligned by retention time and&nbsp;m/z, generating a feature intensity matrix. Metabolite annotation matched experimental&nbsp;m/z&nbsp;(≤10 ppm tolerance) against HMDB and KEGG databases.</p>

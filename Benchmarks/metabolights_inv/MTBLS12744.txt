Study Protocol Description	<p>Sixty-two one-day-old specific-pathogen-free (SPF) White Leghorn chickens were randomly allocated into two groups (n = 31 per group) and housed in negative-pressure isolators with&nbsp;ad libitum&nbsp;access to food and water. At 32 days post-hatch, chickens in the challenge group were inoculated via the oculonasal route with 200 μL of allantoic fluid containing 10^5 EID50 of the IBV QXL strain. Control animals received 200 μL of sterile phosphate-buffered saline (PBS) via the same route. All chickens were euthanized by cervical dislocation following anesthesia at 39 days post-hatch. Oviduct tissues were pooled from three birds per biological replicate to ensure sufficient sample quantity; tissues from deceased birds were excluded. Six biological replicates per group were collected. All tissues were immediately snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at −70°C until processing.</p>	<p>50 mg samples were homogenized as above and extracted with precooled methanol/water solution (1:1 v/v). After vortexing and sonication (10 min, ice bath), 600 µL of precooled Methyl tert-Butyl Ether (MTBE) was added. Flowing centrifugation (3000 r/min, 10 min), the supernatant was freeze-dried and reconstituted in dichloromethane/methanol (1:1 v/v). The supernatant was transferred to a fresh vial for UPLC-HRMS analysis. Pooled QC samples were generated by combining 10 µL aliquots of each extract.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Separation employed an ACQUITY UPLC CSH C18 column (100 mm × 2.1 mm, 1.7 µm; Waters) at 40 °C using a Thermo Scientific Vanquish Flex UPLC system (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA). Mobile phases: 1) solvent A: Acetonitrile/water (6:4) + 10 mmol/L ammonium formate + 0.1% formic acid, and 2) solvent B: Isopropanol/acetonitrile (9:1) + 10 mmol/L ammonium formate + 0.1% formic acid. Gradient elution was performed at 0.35 mL/min.</p>	<p>A high-resolution tandem mass spectrometer Q-Exactive Plus (Thermo Fisher Scientific) was used to detect lipid eluted form the column. Each sample was operated in both positive and negative electrospray ionization mode. ESI</p><p>temperature is 350℃. The voltage is +3800 volts in positive ion mode and -3500 volts in negative ion mode. The sweep gas pressure of the ion source is 0 Arb, Gas 1 (Auxiliary gas) pressures set to 12 Arb, Gas 2 (Sheath gas) pressures set to 45 Arb. The mass spectrometric data were obtained with full scan and data-dependent acquisition (DDA) modes. In one acquisition cycle, the full scan acquisition range is 100-1500 Da, and the resolution is 70k, AGC target is set to 3E6, Maximum IT is set to 100 ms. Then, the top 6 signal ions with a signal accumulation intensity of more than 100000 were selected from the full scan for DDA scanning, the DDA resolution is 17.5k, Maximum IT is set to 50 ms. Dynamic exclusion is set to 6 s.</p>	<p>LC−MS raw data files were converted into mzXML format and then processed by the XCMS, CAMERA and metaX toolbox implemented with the R software.</p>	<p>The online KEGG, HMDB database was used to annotate the metabolites by matching the exact molecular mass data (m/z) of samples with those from database. If a mass difference between observed and the database value was less than 10 ppm, the metabolite would be annotated and the molecular formula of metabolites would further be identified and validated by the isotopic distribution measurements. We also used a in-house fragment spectrum library of metabolites to validate the metabolite identification.</p>

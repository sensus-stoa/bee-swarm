Study Protocol Description	<p>Each participant provided a fresh stool sample in the morning. At least 2 g of each fecal sample were collected using sterile tubes for metabolomic analyses. The samples were then stored at -80 °C within 3 h after collection.</p>	<p>A 100 mg solid sample was added to a 2 mL centrifuge tube, and a 6 mm diameter grinding bead was added. Next 800 μL of extraction solution (methanol:water, 4:1, v:v) containing 4 internal standards) was used for metabolite extraction. Samples were ground by the Wonbio-96c frozen tissue grinder for 6 min (-10 °C, 50 Hz), followed by low-temperature ultrasonic extraction for 30 min (5 °C, 40 kHz). The samples were left at -20 °C for 30 min, centrifuged for 15 min (4 °C, 13,000 x g), and the supernatant was transferred to the injection vial for LC-MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p>LC-MS/MS analysis was performed on a Thermo Scientific UHPLC-Orbitrap Exploris 240 system fitted with an ACQUITY HSS T3 column (100 x 2.1 mm, 1.8 μm). Separation was achieved using 0.1% formic acid in water/acetonitrile (2:98, v/v) (solvent A) and 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile (solvent B) at 0.4 mL/min, 40 °C, with a 5 μL injection.</p>	<p>The ESI source operated in both positive (3.5 kV) and negative (-2.8 kV) modes at 400 °C, with sheath and auxiliary gas flows of 40 and 10 arb, respectively. MS/MS data were acquired in data-dependent acquisition mode using normalized collision energies of 20, 40 and 60 eV over an m/z range of 70-1050.</p>	<p>The&nbsp;Proqenesis QI software&nbsp;(Waters Corporation, Milford, USA) was used to collect and analyze the LC-MS raw information for feature alignment, signal integration and normalization to construct a matrix containing retention time-m/z pairs, peak intensities and sample information.</p>	<p>Metabolites were identified with Progenesis QI against HMDB, Lipid Maps and an in-house library. OPLS-DA (VIP &gt; 1) and Student’s t-test (P &lt; 0.05) in ropls defined differential metabolites between day 0 and day 180. KEGG enrichment of these metabolites was carried out in clusterProfiler (P &lt; 0.05), and the top 20 pathways were plotted.</p>

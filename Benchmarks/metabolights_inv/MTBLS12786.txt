Study Protocol Description	<p>Patients with a clinical diagnosis of BCC were recruited at Bispebjerg Hospital, Department of Dermatology between 2021 and 2023. Inclusion criteria were patients presenting with BCC and age over 18 years. Immunosuppressed patients were excluded in the study and participants were not reimbursed. Written informed consent for the present study was obtained from all participants. This study was conducted in accordance with the Declaration of Helsinki. This study was approved by the Danish Research Ethics Committee (H-20009236), the Danish Knowledge Center for Data Reviews (P-2020-390), and it was registered on ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT06227416).</p>	<p>Moh’s micrographic surgery (MMS) was performed. Excised tissue was immediately snap-frozen, embedded in a mixture of hydroxypropyl methylcellulose and polyvinylpyrrolidone (7.5% : 2.5%) and sectioned at 10 µm on microscope glass slides (Dako Denmark, Glostrup, Denmark)&nbsp;(Dannhorn et al., 2020).</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry data acquisition was carried out on a Waters Xevo G2-XS QToF mass spectrometer equipped with an Ambimass Direct inlet REIMS ion-source prototype. MassLynx v4.1 was used for MS instrument control and High Definition Imaging (HDI) v1.4 was used for data structuring and coordination between the laser and the mass spectrometer. An Opotek Opolette HE2940 tunable laser operated at a wavelength of 2940 nm, pulse width of 5-7 nm, pulse energy of 70 µJ. The beam was focused using an f = 5.95 mm Geltech aspheric lens (Thorlabs) and a spot size of 25-30 µm was achieved on tissue sections.&nbsp;Spectra were acquired in negative ion mode in a&nbsp;m/z&nbsp;range of 50-1200, 0.1 sec scan time, 50 µm pixel sizes. A custom built 2D imaging stage (Prosolia, Thorlabs) was used to perform actuation with a repeat accuracy of 2 µm for the linear stages to ensure robust and reliable imaging performance.</p>	<p>The&nbsp;central section from each block&nbsp;was mounted for H&amp;E staining and an adjacent section was used for LA-REIMS imaging. Section slides were stored at −80 °C.</p>	<p>Waters Raw files were converted to MZ5 format using ProteoWizard msConvert v3.0.24066-8d99481 [1].</p><p><br></p><p>[1] A cross-platform toolkit for mass spectrometry and proteomics. Chambers, M.C., MacLean, B., ... Mallick, P. Nature Biotechnology 30, 918-920 (2012)</p>	<p>The m/z values were matched to entries found in LIPID MAPS and METASPACE. A maximum deviation of ±30 ppm was allowed. Deprotonated and chlorate adducts were considered. As assignment is based only on accurate mass, no differentiation between isobaric/isomeric species is capable.</p>

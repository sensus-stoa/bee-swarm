Study Protocol Description	<p>Fecal samples were collected from C57BL/6J mice at the end of a 14-day study. The experiment involved inducing colitis with DSS and treating a subset of these mice with Fu brick tea polysaccharides. All samples were immediately stored at -80°C for non-targeted metabolomics analysis.</p>	<p>Fecal samples were extracted using a methanol:acetonitrile:water (2:2:1) solvent mixture. The supernatant was collected after centrifugation, vacuum-dried, and then reconstituted for LC-MS analysis. Pooled quality control (QC) samples, created by mixing aliquots from all experimental samples, were analyzed periodically throughout the run to monitor system stability and ensure data quality.</p>	<p>The analysis was performed on a UHPLC system coupled to a TripleTOF 5600 high-resolution mass spectrometer (AB Sciex, USA). Chromatographic separation was achieved using a Waters BEH C18 column (2.1 mm × 100 mm, 1.7 μm). Data was acquired in both positive and negative ion modes using an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Specific details on the mobile phase, gradient, flow rate, and injection volume were not provided in the method description.</p>	<p>The analysis was conducted on a TripleTOF 5600 mass spectrometer (AB Sciex) with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Data was acquired in both positive and negative ion modes over a mass range of m/z 100–1200. Key parameters included an ion spray voltage of +5500 V (positive) / -4500 V (negative), a source temperature of 550°C (positive) / 600°C (negative), and gas pressures set to 35 psi (curtain gas) and 55 psi (gas 1 and 2).</p>	<p>Raw data files were processed using Progenesis QI software. The workflow included automatic retention time alignment, peak picking, and normalization to all compounds to correct for analytical variance. To ensure data quality, features were filtered, retaining only those with a relative standard deviation (RSD) below 30% in the pooled QC samples, resulting in a final data matrix for statistical analysis.</p>	<p>Metabolite annotation was performed using the Progenesis QI software by searching the experimental MS/MS fragmentation spectra against public databases, including the Human Metabolome Database (HMDB) and METLIN. Identifications were confirmed based on three criteria: 1) precursor mass accuracy within 10 ppm, 2) a fragmentation score matching the reference spectra, and 3) an isotope distribution score.</p>

Study Identifier	MTBLS12827
Study Title	Prophylactic and therapeutic itaconate treatment alleviates COVID-19-associated lung injury
Study Description	<p>Although itaconate demonstrates anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties as an immunomodulatory metabolite, the therapeutic potential and mechanistic basis of vaccine-induced or prophylactic itaconate administration in COVID-19 intervention remains underexplored. Our studies reveal that intranasal delivery of dNS1-RBD significantly upregulates endogenous itaconate production. Subsequent research reals that both systemic (intraperitoneal) and pulmonary-targeted (intranasal) itaconate administration mitigate acute lung injury by augmenting efferocytosis and reprogramming macrophage lipid metabolism. Metabolomic analyses further uncover that exogenous itaconate modulates amino acid flux, with parallel protection observed through ornithine/arginine supplementation, suggesting a universal metabolic preconditioning mechanism. This conserved tolerance pathway, validated in human BALF scRNA-seq datasets, establishes metabolic intervention as a strategic approach to harmonize antiviral responses and inflammation control during viral challenge.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Serum samples were collected from SARS-CoV-2-infected hamsters at&nbsp;3 days post-infection (3 dpi). Three experimental groups were analyzed:</p><p>Therapeutic group: Intraperitoneal injection of itaconate (100 mg/kg) at 6, 24, and 48 hours post-infection.</p><p>Prophylactic group: Intranasal pretreatment with itaconate (20 mg/kg) on days 9, 6, and 3 pre-infection.</p><p>Control group: PBS or saline treatment.</p><p>All samples were&nbsp;immediately frozen at -80°C&nbsp;and shipped to OE Biotechnology (Shanghai) for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>Serum proteins were precipitated using&nbsp;methanol/acetonitrile (70:30, v/v). Supernatants were concentrated via nitrogen drying and reconstituted in&nbsp;water-acetonitrile (95:5). Quality controls included:</p><p>Solvent blanks&nbsp;(water-acetonitrile) to monitor background interference.</p><p>Pooled QC samples&nbsp;(equal mixtures of all serum samples) injected every 10 runs to assess instrumental stability.</p><p>Standard compounds&nbsp;(e.g., itaconate, ornithine) for retention time calibration.</p>	<p>Analysis was performed on a Thermo Scientific UHPLC system with a Hypersil GOLD C18 column (2.1 × 100 mm, 1.9 μm). Key parameters:</p><p><br></p><p>Mobile phase:</p><p>A: 0.1% formic acid in water</p><p>B: 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile</p><p><br></p><p>Gradient: 5% B (0–2 min) - 95% B (12 min) - 95% B (15 min) - 5% B (15.1–20 min).</p><p><br></p><p>Flow rate: 0.3 mL/min; Column temperature: 40°C; Injection volume: 5 μL.</p>	<p>Data acquisition used a Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher) with HESI-II ion source:</p><p>Ionization mode: Positive/negative switching (specific batch configuration undefined).</p><p>m/z range: 70–1050</p><p>Source parameters:</p><p>Spray voltage: ±3.5 kV (positive) / ±2.8 kV (negative)</p><p>Capillary temperature: 320°C</p><p>Sheath/aux gas flow: 40/10 arb units</p><p>Scan modes:</p><p>Full MS (120,000 resolution) - dd-MS² (15,000 resolution, stepped NCE 20/40/60 eV).</p>	<p>.raw files were processed using Compound Discoverer 3.1 (Thermo Fisher):</p><p>Peak detection: 5 ppm mass tolerance, S/N threshold &gt;3.</p><p>Alignment and gap filling: Features with RSD &lt;30% in pooled QCs were retained; missing values filled with group minima.</p><p>Normalization: LOESS correction based on QC signals to correct for instrumental drift.</p>	<p>Annotation combined multi-tiered approaches:</p><p>Primary annotation:</p><p>mzCloud: MS/MS spectral match (&gt;70% similarity).</p><p>HMDB: Mass error &lt;5 ppm (priority for immunometabolites like itaconate and ornithine.</p><p>Biological validation:</p><p>Retention time matching with authentic standards.</p><p>Pathway enrichment via&nbsp;MetaboAnalyst 5.0&nbsp;(e.g., arginine/proline metabolism).</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Four host plants of <em>Hyphantria cunea</em> were selected for experiment, including happy tree <em>Camptotheca acuminata</em>, poplar <em>Populus deltoides</em>, mountain cherry <em>Prunus serrulata</em>, and a new host (bald cypress, <em>Taxodium distichum</em>). In addition, a typical non-host in the habitat of <em>H. cunea</em> (camphor tree, <em>Camphora officinarum</em>) was also used in our study. Leaves were collected from middle of the outer canopy of all tested plants in the campus of Nanjing Forestry University, Jiangsu, China (118° 49' 22'E, 32° 5' 2'N). Samples of leaves for metabolomic profiling were collected in May 2024 (peak period of larval emergence), then immediately frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at -80℃ until further analysis.&nbsp;</p>	<p>A mass of 60 mg frozen leaf sample was mixed with 600 μL of methanol/water mixture (7: 3, v/v) containing the mixture of four 4 μg/mL internal standards (see below). After precooling at -40℃ for 2 min, the samples were pulverized, and sonicated for 30 min in ice water bath. The mixtures were left at -40℃ for 24 h, then centrifuged at 13000 rpm at 4℃ for 20 min. Finally, 150 μL of the supernatant was filtered using 0.22 μm syringe filters into an autosampler vial prior to LC-MS analysis. Meanwhile, 150 μL of the supernatant was transferred into a mini vial for concentration through volatilization. Then, 80 μL of 15 mg/mL pyridine solution of methoxylamine hydrochloride was added into the concentrate for oximation reaction. The reactant was mixed with 50 μL N,O-bis(trimethylsilyl)trifluoroacetamide (BSTFA), 20 μL hexane and ten 10 μL chloroform-based internal standards (see below). The mixture was allowed to react for 1 h, then left to stand at room temperature for 30 min before GC-MS analysis. Additionally, the quality control (QC) sample was prepared by mixing the aliquot of all the individual samples.</p><p><strong>Internal standards:</strong></p><p><strong>For LC-MS: </strong> L-2-Chlorophenylalanine, succinic-d4 acid, L-Valine-d8, cholic acid-d4, D-Luciferin</p><p><strong>For GC-MS: </strong>Methyl octanoate, methyl nonoate, methyl caprate, methyl dodecanoate, methyl myristate, methyl hexadecanoate, methyl stearate, methyl icosanoate, methyl docosanoate, methyl tetracosanoate</p><p> </p>	<p>LC-MS analysis was performed by an ACQUITY UPLC I-Class plus system (Chromatographic column: ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3, 100 mm × 2.1 mm × 1.8 μm, Waters Corp., Milford, MA, USA) coupled with a QE high resolution mass spectrometer System (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, USA) in both positive and negative ionisation modes. A volume of 3 μL sample was injected and the column temperature was maintained at 45 °C with a gradient elution programme (see below). Ultrapure water with 0.1% formic acid (A) and acetonitrile (B) were employed as the mobile phases, and the flow rate was 0.35 mL/min. </p><p><strong><em>Gradient:</em></strong></p><p><strong><em>A: </em></strong><em>0 min 95 °C, 2 min 95 °C, 4 min 70 °C, 8 min 50 °C, 10 min 20 °C, 14 min 0 °C, 15 min, 0 °C, 15.1 min 95 °C, 16 min, 95 °C.</em></p><p><strong><em>B: </em></strong> <em>0 min 5 °C, 2 min 5 °C, 4 min 30 °C, 8 min 50 °C, 10 min 80 °C, 14 min 100 °C, 15 min, 100 °C, 15.1 min 5 °C, 16 min, 5 °C.</em></p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;GC-MS analysis was performed by Gas chromatography tandem mass spectrometry (8890-5977B, Agilent Technologies Inc., Palo Alto, CA, USA) equipped with a DB-5MS capillary column (30 m × 0.25mm × 0.25 μm, Agilent J&amp;W Scientific, Folsom, CA, USA). The injection volume is 1 μL through a splitless injection mode and the solvent delay is 5 min. The temperature of the injection port is 260°C. The carrier gas is high-purity helium (purity not less than 99.999%) and the flow rate is 1.0 mL/min. </p>	<p><strong>LCMS-pos:</strong></p><p>Spray Voltage: 3800 V</p><p>Capillary Temperature: 320 °C</p><p>Aux gas heater temperature: 350 °C</p><p>Sheath Gas Flow Rate: 35 Arb</p><p>Aux gas flow rate: 8 Arb</p><p>S-lens RF level: 50</p><p>Mass range: 70-1050 m/z</p><p>Full MS resolution: 70000</p><p>MS/MS resolution: 17500</p><p>NCE/stepped NCE: 10, 20, 40</p><p><strong>LCMS-neg:</strong></p><p>Spray Voltage: -3000 V</p><p>Capillary Temperature: 320 °C</p><p>Aux gas heater temperature: 350 °C</p><p>Sheath Gas Flow Rate: 35 Arb</p><p>Aux gas flow rate: 8 Arb</p><p>S-lens RF level: 50</p><p>Mass range: 70-1050 m/z</p><p>Full MS resolution: 70000</p><p>MS/MS resolution: 17500</p><p>NCE/stepped NCE: 10, 20, 40</p><p><strong>GC-MS:</strong></p><p>Ion source temperature: 230 ℃</p><p>Quadrupole temperature: 150 ℃</p><p>Electron energy: 70 eV</p><p>Scanning mode: Full scan</p><p>Quality scan range: 50-500 m/z</p>	<p>AnalysisBaseFileConverter (Reifycs, Tokyo, Japan) was employed for file conversion of the raw data of GC-MS. Subsequently, raw peaks identification, peaks filtration, peaks alignment and internal standard normalization were processed by MS-DIAL v4.24(Tsugawa et al. 2020). As to LC-MS platform, the raw data was subjected to baseline filtering, peak identification, integration, retention time correction, peak alignment, and normalization through Progenesis QI v3.0 (Nonlinear Dynamics, Newcastle, UK).&nbsp;</p><p><br></p>	<p>The metabolites were identified using the metabolome database of LuMet-Plant 3.0 and LuMet-GC 5.0 (Lumingbio, Shanghai, China).</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Human colorectal cancer tissue was prospectively collected from 10 patients undergoing surgical resection at Kingston Health Sciences Centre (2020–2021), under HSREB approval Queen’s University File No. 6018391; informed consent obtained. Tissue was sampled immediately after receipt in pathology, keeping cold ischemic time &lt; 20 min. For this dataset we include one resection tissue section per patient (no biopsy data).&nbsp;</p>	<p> Tissues were embedded in an optimal cutting temperature (OCT) medium, flash-frozen, and cryosectioned to a 10 µm thickness on uncharged slides. The slides were stored at −80 °C until DESI analysis.</p>	<p>MSI was performed in negative ion mode on a Waters Xevo G2-XS ToF fitted with a Prosolia 2D DESI source (high-performance sprayer, heated inlet). Solvent: 95% (v/v) methanol/water containing 0.05 ng/µL leucine-enkephalin delivered by syringe pump at 2.25 µL/min. Acquisition range:m/z 50–1500. Spatial resolution:100 × 100 µm. Stage speed:175 µm/s. Source temp:100 °C; capillary voltage:0.57 kV; N2 nebulizing gas ~15 psi.</p>	<p>Histology images corresponding to these DESI-MSI data are available separately a</p>	<p>Raw DESI-MSI data acquired in Waters .raw format were deposited without additional processing. No normalization, peak picking, or other transformations were applied prior to submission.&nbsp;</p>	<p>No targeted metabolite identification was performed in this study. Instead, analysis focused on spectral and image-level features using MassVision, an end-to-end MSI analysis platform. Ion signals were processed in their centroided form and used directly for classification and visualization. </p><p>MassVision: <a href='https://slicermassvision.readthedocs.io/en/latest/' rel='noopener noreferrer' target='_blank'>https://slicermassvision.readthedocs.io/en/latest/</a></p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>A mixture of&nbsp;Chinese herbal medicines—including Astragalus membranaceus, Citri Reticulatae Pericarpium, Citri Reticulatae Pericarpium Viride, Hordeum vulgare, Atractylodes macrocephala, Massa Fermentata, and Berberis julianae—was sourced from local markets and uniformly blended in a fixed ratio of 2:2:2:3:1:2:3</p>	<p>Weigh 50 mg of sample into a centrifuge tube, and add 700 μL of pre-cooled (-40 ℃) extraction solution (methanol:water = 3:1, containing internal standards);Vortex for 30 seconds, homogenize at 40 Hz for 4 minutes, and sonicate in an ice-water bath for 5 minutes; repeat the homogenization-sonication steps 3 times;Incubate overnight at 4 ℃ on a shaker;Centrifuge at 12,000 rpm (relative centrifugal force 13,800 × g, radius 8.6 cm) at 4 ℃ for 15 minutes;Filter the supernatant through a 0.22 μm microporous membrane;Dilute the supernatant 20-fold with extraction solution and store at -80 ℃ until instrumental analysis.</p>	<p>Instrument model: Ultra-high performance liquid chromatograph (ExionLC AD, Sciex);Column: Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 (1.8 μm, 2.1×100 mm);Mobile phase: Phase A: 0.1% formic acid in water; Phase B: acetonitrile (both LC-MS grade, brand: CNW Technologies);</p><p>Column temperature: 40 ℃;Autosampler temperature: 4 ℃;Injection volume: 2 μL;Flow rate: 400 μL/min;</p>	<p>Instrument model: High-sensitivity mass spectrometer (QTrap 6500+, Sciex);Ion source: IonDrive Turbo V ESI;Scan mode: Multiple reaction monitoring (MRM);</p>	<p>Raw data (.wiff format) was converted to TXT format using MSconventer software;</p>	<p>SCIEX Analyst Work Station Software (Version 1.6.3) was employed for MRM data acquisition and processing.</p>

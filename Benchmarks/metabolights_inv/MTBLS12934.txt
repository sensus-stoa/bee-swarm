Study Protocol Description	<p>After anesthesia, the mice were fixed on the surgical table. The thoracic cavity was opened, and the hearts were rapidly removed. The hearts were then placed in a pre-cooled RNase-free preservation solution to remove blood and other impurities. The left ventricle from the infarct border zone or from the sham group was dissected. The isolated tissue samples were quickly placed in liquid nitrogen for snap-freezing and subsequently transferred to a -80°C freezer for storage.</p>	<p>(1) Remove the samples from the -80°C freezer and place them on ice to thaw until they can be cut (all subsequent operations must be performed on ice).</p><p>(2) Cut the samples into small pieces and mix thoroughly. Collect multiple points to weigh 20 mg (±1 mg) into correspondingly labeled centrifuge tubes.</p><p>(3) Add one steel bead using tweezers, and homogenize the sample with a ball mill (30 Hz) for 20 seconds. Centrifuge the sample at 3000 r/min for 30 seconds at 4°C to pellet the sample (the duration may be extended appropriately depending on the actual homogenization conditions).</p><p>(4) After centrifugation, add 400 μL of 70% methanol aqueous internal standard extraction solution, and shake at 2500 r/min for 5 minutes. Let it stand on ice for 15 minutes.</p><p>(5) Centrifuge at 12000 r/min for 10 minutes at 4°C. Transfer 300 μL of the supernatant to another correspondingly labeled centrifuge tube, and let it stand in a -20°C freezer for 30 minutes.</p><p>(6) Centrifuge again at 12000 r/min for 3 minutes at 4°C. Transfer 200 μL of the supernatant to the corresponding insert vial of the injection bottle for instrumental analysis.</p>	<p>T3 Chromatographic Conditions</p><p>(1) Chromatographic column: Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 C18, 1.8 µm, 2.1 mm * 100 mm;</p><p>(2) Mobile phase: Phase A - ultrapure water (0.1% formic acid), Phase B - acetonitrile (0.1% formic acid);</p><p>(3) Flow rate: 0.4 ml/min; Column temperature: 40°C; Injection volume: 2 µl (for broadly targeted detection), 5 µl (for non-targeted detection).</p><p>HILIC Chromatographic Conditions</p><p>(1) Chromatographic column: Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH HILIC Column, 1.7 µm, 1 mm * 100 mm;</p><p>(2) Mobile phase A: 20 mM ammonium formate, 10% water, 10% methanol, 80% acetonitrile, pH adjusted to 10.6 with ammonia water; Mobile phase B: 20 mM ammonium formate, 60% water, 40% acetonitrile, pH adjusted to 10.6 with ammonia water;</p><p>(3) Instrument column temperature: 40°C; Flow rate: 0.4 mL/min; Injection volume: 2 µl (for broadly targeted detection), 5 µl (for non-targeted detection).</p>	<p>The data acquisition instrument system primarily consists of an Ultra Performance Liquid Chromatography (UPLC) (ExionLC AD,&nbsp;https://sciex.com.cn/) and a Tandem Mass Spectrometry (MS/MS) system (QTRAP®,&nbsp;https://sciex.com/).</p>	<p>The raw data files generated by UHPLC-MS/MS were processed using the Compound</p><p>Discoverer 3.3 (CD3.3, ThermoFisher) to perform peak alignment, peak picking, and</p><p>quantitation for each metabolite. The main parameterswere set as follows: peak area</p><p>was corrected with the first QC; actual mass tolerance, 5ppm; signal intensity</p><p>tolerance, 30%; and minimum intensity, et al.After that, peak intensities were</p><p>normalized to the total spectral intensity. The normalized data was used to predict the</p><p>molecular formula based on additive ions,molecular ion peaks and fragment ions.And</p><p>then peaks were matched with the mzCloud (https://www.mzcloud.org/), mzVaultand</p><p>MassListdatabase to obtain the accuratequalitative and relative quantitative results.</p>	<p>Statistical analyses were performed using the statistical software R (R version</p><p>R-3.4.3), Python (Python 2.7.6 version) and CentOS(CentOS release 6.6) , When data</p><p>were not normally distributed, standardize according to the formula: sample raw</p><p>quantitation value / (The sum of sample metabolite quantitation value / The sum of</p><p>QC1 sample metabolite quantitation value ) to obtain relative peak areas; Andcompounds whose CVs of relative peak areas in QC samples were greater than 30%</p><p>were removed, and finally the metabolites' identification and relative quantification</p><p>results were obtained.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>We isolated pulmonary ILC2s and treated them with or without rmCD5L for 3 days. Subsequently, we performed metabolomics analysis to quantify the metabolite landscape.</p>	<p>Add 1000 μ L extraction solution (methanol: water=3:1, excluding internal standard);</p><p>Vortex for 30 seconds; Ultrasound for 10 minutes under ice water bath conditions, let stand Centrifuge the sample at 4 , 12000 rpm, for 15 minutes;Centrifuge and dry the supernatant, adding 50% μ L extraction solution (methanol: water=3:1, including internal standard) re solution. Centrifuge the sample at 4 , 12000 rpm, for 15 minutes; Take the supernatant into the injection vial.</p>	<p>LC-MS/MS analyses were performed using an UHPLC system (Vanquish, Thermo Fisher</p><p>Scientific) with a UPLC HSS T3 column (2.1 mm × 100 mm, 1.8 μm) coupled to Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer (Orbitrap MS, Thermo). The mobile phase consisted of 5 mmol/L ammonium acetate and 5 mmol/L acetic acid in water (A) and acetonitrile (B). The auto-sampler temperature was 4 , and the injection volume was 2 μL.</p>	<p>The Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer was used for its ability to acquire MS/MS</p><p>spectra on information-dependent acquisition (IDA) mode in the control of the acquisition software (Xcalibur, Thermo). In this mode, the acquisition software continuously evaluates the full scan MS spectrum. The ESI source conditions were set as following: sheath gas flow rate as 50 Arb, Aux gas flow rate as 15 Arb, capillary temperature 320 , full MS resolution as 60000, MS/MS resolution as 15000 collision energy as 10/30/60 in NCE mode, spray Voltage as 3.8 kV (positive) or -3.4 kV (negative), respectively.</p>	<p>The raw data were converted to the mzXML format using ProteoWizard and processed with an in-house program. The program was developed using R and based on XCMS, for peak detection, extraction, alignment, and integration.</p>	<p>Then an in-house MS2 database (BiotreeDB) was applied in metabolite annotation. The cutoff for annotation was set at 0.3.</p>

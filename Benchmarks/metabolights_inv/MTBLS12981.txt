Study Title	Explore changes of riboflavin metabolism in acute pancreatitis
Study Description	<p> Gut barrier dysfunction is critical in the pathogenesis of acute pancreatitis (AP), yet the underlying mechanisms remain unclear. Mucin 2 (MUC2), the primary component of the intestinal mucus layer, is essential for gut homeostasis and microbial eubiosis. This study aimed to elucidate the protective mechanism of intestinal MUC2 in AP.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p> In our study, we firstly verified intestinal MUC2 changes in three classic AP mice models. Mice were randomly assigned into four groups (n = 6 per group): control (CON), Cae-AP, Larg-AP and NaTCA-AP. Mice in Cae-AP group were injected intraperitoneally with 100 mg/kg cerulein (MedChemExpress, China) 10 times at an hour intervals[10]. L-arginine (4.5 g/kg, Sigma-Aldrich, USA) was injected intraperitoneally twice at an hour interval to establish Larg-AP mice[10]. As previously described[46], mice in NaTCA-AP group were anesthetized with Zoletil 50 (Virbac, France), and 50 µL 2% sodium taurocholate solution (Sigma-Aldrich, USA) was infused into the biliopancreatic duct at a rate of 5 µL/min. Following AP induction, mice were sacrificed for the collection of pancreas which were then stored at -80 °C or in 4% paraformaldehyde for subsequent analysis.</p>	<p>Tissue samples were thawed at 4 ℃ in a refrigerator, and the same group of samples were mixed well. An appropriate amount of sample (the exact weight is shown in the result table) was placed in a 10 mL centrifuge tube and 5 mL of extraction solution (methanol: water: formic acid; 15:4:1 with 0.5% BHT) </p><p>was added. The mixture was vortexed for 1 minute, ultrasonicated for 30 minutes, and allowed to stand at -40℃ for 60 minutes, then centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 10 minutes. The supernatant was then removed for solid-phase extraction.</p>	<p>Activation: 3 mL water, 3 mL methanol; Adsorption: the supernatant into the SPE solid phase extraction column (flow rate ≤ 1 mL/min); rinsing: 3 mL water, 10% methanol water; Elution: 1 mL methanol.The above eluent was concentrated to dryness in a concentrator, re-dissolved in 0.60 mL of 80% methanol water, vortexed and mixed for 1 min, centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 10 min, and then the supernatant was taken into the machine for LC-MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p>The liquid chromatography system was a Waters Acquity UPLC, coupled with a mass spectrometer from AB SCIEX 5500 QQQ-MS. The chromatographic columns employed were Acquity UPLC BEH C18 (1.7 µm, 2.1 mm × 100 mm) and Acquity UPLC HSS T3 (1.8 µm, 2.1 mm × 100 mm). The chromatographic separation conditions included a column temperature of 35 °C and a flow rate of 0.30mL/min. The mobile phase composition consisted of component A, which was water containing 10 mM ammonium formate, and component B, which was methanol. The total run time was 8 minutes, with an injection volume of 6 µL. The mass spectrometric conditions were as follows: ion source, ESI; curtain gas, 35 arb; collision gas, 7 arb; ion spray voltage, 4500V; ion source temperature, 450 °C; ion source gases, 55 arb each for Ion Source Gas1 and IonSource Gas2. The MRM acquisition parameters were established based on the aforementioned chromatographic and mass spectrometric conditions, and standard solution preparations were injected into the sample vials for analysis.</p>	<p>Integration was carried out using MultiQuant software, and content calculation was performed according to the internal standard one-point method.The internal standard method is calculated as shown below: The internal standard method is to add a certain weight of pure substance as an internal standard to a certain amount of the analyzed sample mixture, and calculate the content of the measured component according to the mass ratio of the test sample and the internal standard, the ratio of its corresponding chromatographic peak area and the relative correction factor.Where As and Ar are the peak areas or peak heights of the internal standard and the control, respectively, and ms and mr are the amounts of the internal standard and the control added, respectively. Then take the solution of the component to be tested containing the internal standard into the sample, record the chromatogram, and then calculate the content (mi) according to the peak response value of the solution of the component to be tested containing the internal standard:where Ai and As are the peak areas or peak heights of the to-be-measured and internal standard, respectively, and ms is the amount of internal standard added.</p>	<p>The MultiQuant software was utilized for integration and the standard curve was utilized for content calculation. 1) Preparation of standard solution Accurately take the standard solution (1.00 µg/mL) 0.20 mL of the standard series solution. The standard solution wasprepared ready to use. 2) Standard curve drawing The standard curve is drawn according to the peak area of the standard at different concentrations and corresponding concentrations, as can be seen in the figure, the R2 is greater than 0.99, which indicates that the standard curve has a better linear relationship, and can be used for the calculation of the concentration in the sample.</p>

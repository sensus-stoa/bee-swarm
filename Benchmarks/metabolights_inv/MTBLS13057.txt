Study Protocol Description	<p>Liver tissues from DNASE1L3 knockout (KO) and wild-type (WT) mice were collected at 8 weeks of age. Mice were anesthetized, and livers were quickly excised, rinsed in cold PBS, snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at −80 °C until metabolite extraction.</p>	<p>Approximately 50 mg of liver tissue was homogenized in 1 mL methanol:acetonitrile:water (2:2:1, v/v/v) on ice. Samples were vortexed, sonicated, and centrifuged at 14,000 × g for 15 min at 4 °C. The supernatant was transferred to a fresh tube, dried under vacuum, and reconstituted in 100 μL 50% acetonitrile for LC–MS analysis. Quality control (QC) samples were prepared by pooling equal aliquots from each extract.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed on a Waters ACQUITY UPLC system using a HILIC column (e.g., ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide, 2.1 × 100 mm, 1.7 μm). The mobile phases consisted of solvent A (25 mM ammonium acetate and 25 mM ammonium hydroxide in water) and solvent B (acetonitrile). The gradient elution started from 95% B, decreased to 65% B over 7 min, then to 40% B over 1 min, maintained for 1 min, and returned to 95% B over 1 min. Flow rate was 0.4 mL/min; column temperature 25 °C; injection volume 2 μL.</p>	<p>Metabolites were detected using a Thermo Q Exactive Orbitrap mass spectrometer equipped with an ESI source, operated in negative ion mode. The mass range was m/z 70–1000. Source parameters: sheath gas 40 arb units, auxiliary gas 10 arb units, spray voltage −3.0 kV, capillary temperature 320 °C. Data acquisition was performed at a resolution of 70,000 (m/z 200).</p>	<p>Raw LC–MS data were converted to mzXML format using ProteoWizard (MSConvert). Peak detection, alignment, and normalization were performed using XCMS (R package) and in-house scripts. Features with &gt;50% missing values were removed; remaining missing values were imputed using k-nearest neighbor (KNN).</p>	<p>Metabolites were annotated by matching accurate mass and MS/MS fragmentation patterns against public databases (HMDB, KEGG, METLIN) and in-house spectral libraries, with a mass tolerance of 10 ppm. Identification levels were assigned according to the Metabolomics Standards Initiative (MSI) guidelines.</p>

Study Identifier	MTBLS13200
Study Title	The Cd-tolerance of pepper (Capsicum annuum L.) is linked with rhizosphere bacterial community composition and rhizosphere soil metabolites profiles
Study Description	<p>Cadmium (Cd) poses a serious threat to plant growth, so the selection and breeding of Cd-tolerant pepper cultivars is an effective method to reduce Cd hazards. This study identified C. annuum G03 as a Cd-tolerant cultivar, evidenced by its superior leaf physiological activity under Cd stress compared to the sensitive C. annuum C07. 16S rDNA sequencing revealed a more complex bacterial symbiotic network in the bulk soil, rhizosphere, and root endophyte of C. annuum G03, enhancing its buffering capacity against Cd stress. Furthermore, correlation analysis between rhizosphere bacteria and metabolites indicated that Armatimonadota and Bdellovibrionota were the key functional bacteria of C. annuum G03. They can influence the levels of key metabolites such as nerolidol, glutathionylspermine, alpha-linolenic acid, thereby reducing antioxidant damage and Cd ion uptake in plant roots. These findings provide valuable insights into the microbial mechanisms underpinning pepper tolerance to Cd stress.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The soil was collected from Hongqi Village, Wudang District, Guiyang City (26°49′N, 160°56′E). The Capsicum annuum C07 (was purchased from Changsha Chunrun Seed Company) and Capsicum annuum G03 (was purchased from Guiyang Jiefeng Agricultural Seeds Limited company) were used in this experiment. Pepper seeds were germinated with reference to Xin et al. (2014). The uniform seedlings were placed into pots (19 cm in height×19 cm in upper aperture×11 cm in lower aperture). The experimental design consisted of four treatments (0 mM CdCl2+C. annuum C07, 1 mM CdCl2+C. annuum C07, 0 mM CdCl2+C. annuum G03 and 1 mM CdCl2+C. annuum G03) with five replicates. After 30 d of pepper seedlings growth, the Cd stress was carried out. Each pot was applied 200 mL 1 mM CdCl2 every 4 days for 5 times, and control group was treated with ddH2O. After 10 weeks,&nbsp;rhizosphere soil was collected for&nbsp;LC-MS.</p>	<p>Ultraperformance liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry (LC/MS) was used for&nbsp;rhizosphere metabolomic analysis . Rhizosphere soil sample of 50 mg was accurately weighed and transferred into a 2 mL EP tube containing 1000 µL of extraction solution [methanol: acetonitrile: water = 2:2:1, internal standard (2-Chloro-L-phenylalanine) concentration 20 mg/L], followed by vortexing and mixing for 30 s. Then steel beads were added, and grinding was performed for 10 min at 40 Hz. subsequently sonicated in an ice-water bath for 10 min. After sonication, the extracts were allowed to stand for 1 h at -20°C, then was centrifuged for 15 min (12000 rpm, 4°C) and 500 µL of supernatant was taken for vacuum drying. After drying, 160 μL of extract was added to dissolve the metabolites, vortexed and mixed for 30 s, sonicated for 10 min in an ice-water bath, and centrifuged again for 15 min (12000 rpm, 4 ). Next, 120 μL of supernatant was aspirated as the prepared samples for LC-MS. Take 10 μL of each sample to prepare a quality control (QC) sample.</p>	<p>The column used is purchased from Waters Acquity UPLC HSS T3 column (1.8um 2.1*100mm)</p><p>Positive ion mode: mobile phase A: 0.1% formic acid aqueous solution; mobile phase B: 0.1% formic acid acetonitrile; Negative ion mode: mobile phase A: 0.1% formic acid aqueous solution; mobile phase B: 0.1% formic acid acetonitrile. Injection volume 1μL</p>	<p>Waters Xevo G2-XS QTOF high resolution mass spectrometer can collect primary and secondary mass spectrometry data in MSe mode under the control of the acquisition software (MassLynx V4.2, Waters). In each data acquisition cycle, dual-channel data acquisition can be performed on both low collision energy and high collision energy at the same time. The low collision energy is 2V, the high collision energy range is 10~40V, and the scanning frequency is 0.2 seconds for a mass spectrum. The parameters of the ESI ion source are as follows: Capillary voltage: 2000V (positive ion mode) or -1500V (negative ion mode); cone voltage: 30V; ion source temperature: 150°C; desolvent gas temperature 500°C; backflush gas flow rate: 50L/ h; Desolventizing gas flow rate: 800L/h.</p>	<p>The raw data collected using MassLynx V4.2 is processed by Progenesis QI software for peak extraction, peak alignment and other data processing operations, based on the Progenesis QI software online METLIN database and Biomark’s self-built library for identification, and at the same time, theoretical fragment identification and mass deviation All are within 100ppm.</p>	<p>After normalizing the original peak area information with the total peak area, the follow-up analysis was performed. Principal component analysis and Spearman correlation analysis were used to judge the repeatability of the samples within group and the quanlity control samples. The identified compounds are searched for classification and pathway information in KEGG, HMDB and lipidmaps databases.</p>

Study Identifier	MTBLS13212
Study Title	The effect and mechanism of gut flora metabolite deaminotyrosine on chronic inflammatory pain
Study Description	<p>This study focuses on deaminotyrosine DAT, a metabolite of gut flora, and systematically investigates its effect on chronic inflammatory pain as well as the underlying mechanism. The research takes high-fat diet HFD as a key entry point for the background, as HFD often leads to an imbalance in the structure of gut flora. This imbalance may affect the production of intestinal metabolites including DAT, and thereby be associated with the occurrence and development of chronic inflammatory pain.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The brain tissue of mice was washed with PBS and frozen in liquid nitrogen for 30min, then transferred to -80 for storage</p>	<p>50 mg solid sample was added to a 2 mL centrifuge tube and a 6 mm diameter grinding bead </p><p>was added. 400 μL of extraction solution methanol: water = 4:1 (v:v) containing 0.02 mg/mL of </p><p>internal standard</p>	<p>100 μL liquid sample was added to a 1.5 mL centrifuge tube with 400 μL solution </p><p>(acetonitrile: methanol = 1:1(v:v)) containing 0.02 mg/mL internal standard </p><p>L-2-chlorophenylalanine to extract metabolites.The samples were mixed by vortex for 30 s and </p><p>low-temperature sonicated for 30 min </p>	<p>The LC-MS/MS analysis of sample was conducted on a SCIEX UPLC-Triple TOF 6600 </p><p>system equipped with an ACQUITY HSS T3 column (100 mm × 2.1 mm i.d., 1.8 μm; Waters, </p><p>USA) at Majorbio Bio-Pharm Technology Co. Ltd. (Shanghai, China).The mobile phases </p><p>consisted of 0.1% formic acid in water:acetonitrile (95:5, v/v) </p>	<p>The UPLC system was coupled to a quadrupole-time-of-flight mass spectrometer Triple </p><p>TOF TM5600+, Sciex, equipped with a electrospray ionization source operating in </p><p>positive mode and negative mode. The optimal conditions were set as followed: source </p><p>temperature at 550 oC ; curtain gas at 30 psi; both Ion Source Gas1 and Gas2 at 50 psi; </p><p>ion-spray voltage floating ISVF at -4000V in negative mode and 5000V in positive mode, </p><p>respectively; declustering potential at 80V; collision energy</p>	<p>The UHPLC-MS raw data were converted into the common format by Progenesis QI </p><p>software through baseline filtering, peak identification, peak integral, </p><p>retention time correction, and peak alignment.

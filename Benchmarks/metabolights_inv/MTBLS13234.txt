Study Title	Comprehensive Evaluation of Anti-hyperuricemic Effect of Red Kidney Bean Anthocyanins and Molecular Screening of the Lead Candidate
Study Description	<p>Hyperuricemia (HUA) has emerged as an increasingly prevalent metabolic disorder worldwide. This study aimed to investigate the effects of red kidney bean (Phaseolus vulgaris L.) anthocyanins (RKBA) on alleviating HUA and screen the lead candidate. RKBA effectively inhibited XOD in vitro. In vivo results showed that RKBA significantly reduced serum uric acid levels (p &lt; 0.05), protected kidney function, alleviated inflammation and tissue damage in vivo. Mechanistically, RKBA down-regulated xanthine oxidase, adenosine deaminase and 5′-nucleotidase while modulating urate transporters URAT1, GLUT9 and OAT3, thereby rebalancing uric-acid metabolism. Additionally, it reshaped the gut microbiome (particularly increasing Ligilactobacillus and Dubosiella), and elevated short-chain fatty acids. Subsequently, UPLC-ESI-MS/MS-based anthocyanin-targeted omics identified and quantified 42 anthocyanins, which is the most comprehensive RKBA profiling to date. Ultimately, integrating molecular docking and dynamics simulation, pelargonidin-3,5-diglucoside was selected as the lead candidate owing to its high abundance and strong affinity for xanthine oxidase, which was further confirmed by in vitro experiment. Pelargonidin-3,5-diglucoside has not been reported as an anti-hyperuricemic nutraceutical before, hence this study lays a foundation for future in vivo validation.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Red kidney beans (Liyin No.1) were purchased from a local supplier in Kunming (Yunnan, China), which were harvested in July, 2024.</p>	<p>The skins of red kidney beans were dried, and ground. RKBA were isolated from skins powder using acidified ethanol with the following conditions: ethanol concentration of 65.0%, solvent-to-material ratio of 20:1, extraction time of 55 min, extraction temperature of 55 °C (room temperature of 25-30 °C), hydrochloric acid concentration of 1.0%, and extraction by magnetic stirrer. After adjusting the extract to neutral pH with NaOH solution, it was centrifuged at 2050 g for 15 min, and the supernatant was collected. Finally, the extract was concentrated by rotary evaporation and freeze-dried. The content of anthocyanins was measured by kit following protocol provided by the manufacturer.</p>	<p>RKBA were extracted with 70% methanol (0.1% formic acid) by ultrasonication, centrifuged, and the supernatant was filtered through a 0.22 µm membrane. Separation was achieved on an ACQUITY I-Class UPLC system (Waters) with an HSS T3 column (100×2.1 mm, 1.8 µm). Mobile phases were 0.1% formic acid in water (A) and in acetonitrile (B); the column was maintained at 35°C with a 2 µL injection volume. Detection used a QTRAP 6500+ triple quadrupole (SCIEX) in ESI-MRM mode.</p>	<p>Source settings were: curtain gas 35 psi, GS1 50 psi, GS2 55 psi, source temperature 550°C, and ion-spray voltage +5500/-4500 V. Compound-specific precursor/product ion pairs and collision energies were optimized prior to analysis.</p>	<p>The experiment was conducted by Biomarker Technologies Co.,Ltd.

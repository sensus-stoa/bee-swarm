Study Protocol Description	<p>A549 cells were infected with NDV at a multiplicity of infection (MOI) of 3 at 37°C, and the cells were collected 24 hours post-infection.</p>	<p>Lipids were extracted according to MTBE method. Briefly, a 200-uL volume of waterwas added to 30 mg sample and vortexed for 5s. Subsequently, 240 uL of precoolingmethanol was added and the mixture vortexed for 30 s. After that, 800 uL of MTBE wasadded and the mixture was ultrasound 20 min at 4'C followed by sitting still for 30 min atroom temperature. The solution was centrifuged at 14000g for 15min at 10'C and the upperorganic solvent layer was obtained and dried under nitrogen.</p>	<p _msttexthash='32059222' _msthash='1606'>The sample was separated using the UHPLC Nexera LC-30A ultra-highl performanceliquid chromatography system.Cl8 chromatographic column was used with a column temperature of 45°C and a flow rate of 300 uL/min. The mobile phase consisted of solvent A.acetonitrile-water solution (acetonitrile:water-6:4,v/v) and solvent B: acetonitrile-isopropanolsolution (acetonitrile:isopropanol = 1:9, v/v). The gradient elution program was as follows:0-3.5 min,B was maintained at 40%:3.5-13 min,B linearly increased from 40% to 75%.13-19 min, B linearly increased from 75% to 99%;19-24 min, B was maintained at 40%.Throughout the entire analysis process, the samples were kept in an autosampler at 10°C toprevent any impact from fluctuations in the instrument detection signal. To avoid anysystematic errors, the samples were analyzed in a random order.</p>	<p>Mass spectra was acquired by Q-Exactive Plus in positive and negative mode, respectively. ESI&nbsp;parameters were optimized and preset for all measurements as follows: Source temperature, 300 °C;&nbsp;Capillary Temp, 350 °C, the ion spray voltage was set at 3000V, S-Lens RF Level was set at 50% and&nbsp;the scan range of the instruments was set at m/z 200– 1800.</p>	<p>Raw data were transformed by ProteoWizard. MzML format, and then the XCMS program was used for peak alignment, retention time correction, and peak area extraction. The data extracted by XCMS were first treated with metabolite structure identification, data preprocessing (null filtering: deleting ion peaks with missing values &gt;50%; null filling: KNN filling: data filtering: filtering features with RSD&gt;50%), and finally data analysis. Using XCMS software parameters set as follows: For peak picking , centWave m/z = 25ppm, peakwidth = c (10, 60), prefilter = c (10, 100). For peak grouping, bw = 5,&nbsp;mzwid = 0.025, minfrac = 0.5 were used.</p>	<p>1. Raw data were obtained by LipidSearch software (*.raw format). Preprocessing steps such as peak&nbsp;alignment, peak identification, and peak extraction are performed on lipid molecules and internal&nbsp;standard lipid molecules to get data matrix of mass to charge ratio (m/z) , retention time (rt) and&nbsp;peak intensity et al.</p><p>2. All peak intensities were batch normalized to the total spectral intensity for comparing the&nbsp;data of different orders of magnitude.</p><p>3. Using LipidSearch software to perform lipid identification. The main parameters were set as&nbsp;follows: precursor tolerance: 5 ppm, product tolerance: 5 ppm, product ion threshold: 5%.</p><p><br></p>

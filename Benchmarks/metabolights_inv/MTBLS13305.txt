Study Title	Comparative evaluation of nutritional quality and flavor characteristics for Acrossocheilus fasciatus muscle in different aquaculture systems
Study Description	<p>Abstract:This study comparatively evaluated the effects of four aquaculture systems pond (PD), rice field (RF), round bucket (RB) and water trough (WT) on the flesh quality, gut microbiota and metabolites of Acrossocheilus fasciatus. Compared with the other three groups, texture analysis revealed that the RF group exhibited significantly higher hardness and chewiness (P &lt; 0.05), the highest content of umami amino acids, relatively high levels of flavor compounds (alcohols and esters), and significantly higher polyunsaturated fatty acid (PUFA) content , particularly rich in eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA) and docosahexaenoic acid (DHA). Additionally, metabolomics and gut microbiota analyses showed that compared with the other three groups, the RF group had significantly higher contents of beneficial metabolites and bacterial genera, enhancing flavor and nutritional value by regulating amino acids, fatty acids, and volatile flavor compounds.These findings highlight the potential of RF in improving fish quality and elucidating metabolomic and microbial mechanisms, providing theoretical support for ecological aquaculture.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Samples of intestinal contents were collected immediately after dissection, placed in 2 mL cryopreservation tubes, and quickly frozen at -80°C for storage to maintain the stability of metabolites.</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted from intestinal content samples using 400 μL of a methanol:water (4:1) solution, followed by centrifugation to collect the supernatant for subsequent LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>An ACQUITY HSS T3 chromatographic column (1.8 μm, 100 mm × 2.1 mm) was used, with the column temperature set at 40°C. Mobile phase A was water:acetonitrile (95:5) containing 0.1% formic acid, and mobile phase B was acetonitrile:isopropanol:water (47.5:47.5:5) containing 0.1% formic acid. The flow rate was 0.4 mL/min, and the total running time was set according to the gradient program.</p>	<p>A Thermo UHPLC-Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer was used, equipped with an electrospray ion source (ESI), with switching between positive and negative ion modes. The ion source temperature was 425°C, the sheath gas flow rate was 50 arb, the auxiliary gas flow rate was 13 arb, the ion spray voltage was ±3500 V, and the collision energy was rolling from 20 to 60 V.</p>	<p>The raw data were processed using Progenesis QI software to extract peak information, perform peak alignment, and normalization. Metabolite identification was based on the HMDB, Metlin, and Majorbio databases, with matching performed by combining retention time and mass spectrometric fragment information.</p>	<p>Data preprocessing was performed using Progenesis QI, metabolite annotation was conducted based on databases such as HMDB, Metlin, and Majorbio, and in-depth statistical analysis and biological function interpretation were carried out using the Majorbio Cloud Platform. The identification results were divided into different confidence levels according to the presence or absence of standard validation or the matching degree of MS/MS spectra, ensuring the reliability of the results.</p>

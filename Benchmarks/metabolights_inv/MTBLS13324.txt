Study Protocol Description	<p>The salivary glands were separately collected from&nbsp;Aedes aegypti&nbsp;mosquitoes in the following four groups: those injected with AaaaNAT1 dsRNA, those injected with GUS dsRNA, those after blood-feeding, and those without any treatment. All collected samples were immediately flash-frozen in liquid nitrogen and subsequently shipped to the company for analysis on dry ice.</p>	<p>The sample stored at -80 °C refrigerator was thawed on ice. The thawed sample was homogenized by a grinder (30 HZ) for 20 s. A 400 μL solution (Methanol : Water = 7:3, V/V) containing internal standard was added in to 20 mg grinded sample, and shaked at 2500 rpm for 5 min. After placing on ice for 15 min, the sample was centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 10 min (4 °C). A 300 μL of supernatant was collected and placed in -20 °C for 30 min. The sample was then centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 3 min (4 °C). A 200 μL aliquots of supernatant were transferred for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>T3 UPLC Conditions : The sample extracts were analyzed using an LC-ESI-MS/MS system (UPLC, ExionLC AD, https://sciex.com.cn/; MS, QTRAP® System,&nbsp;https://sciex.com/). The analytical conditions were as follows, UPLC: column, Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 C18 (1.8μm, 2.1 mm*100 mm); column temperature, 40°C; flow rate, 0.4 mL/min; injection volume, 2μL or 5μL; solvent system, water (0.1% formic acid): acetonitrile (0.1% formic acid); gradient program, 95:5 V/V at 0 min, 10:90 V/V at 10.0 min, 10:90 V/V at 11.0 min, 95:5 V/V at 11.1 min, 95:5 V/V at 14.0 min.</p>	<p>The Triple TOF mass spectrometer was used for its ability to acquire MS/MS spectra on an information-dependent basis (IDA) during an LC/MS experiment. In this mode, the acquisition software (TripleTOF 6600, AB SCIEX) continuously evaluates the full scan survey MS data as it collects and triggers the acquisition of MS/MS spectra depending on preselected criteria. In each cycle, 12 precursor ions whose intensity greater than 100 were chosen for fragmentation at collision energy (CE) of 30 V (12 MS/MS events with product ion accumulation time of 50 msec each). ESI source conditions were set as following: Ion source gas 1 as 50 Psi, Ion source gas 2 as 50 Psi, Curtain gas as 25 Psi, source temperature 500°C, Ion Spray Voltage Floating (ISVF) 5500 V or -4500 V in positive or negative modes, respectively.</p><p><br></p>	<p>All sample extracts were mixed in equal volumes to form a quality control (QC) sample, which was subjected to untargeted detection on an LC-QTOF-MS/MS platform. Based on the self-built standard compound database MWDB (including MS/MS spectra and retention time, RT), the integrated public database DB-all (including Metlin, HMDB, KEGG, etc.), AI-predicted libraries, and MetDNA, compounds were accurately identified. The multiple ion pairs and retention time (RT) information of the identified metabolites were extracted and combined with Metware's self-built targeted database to create a project-specific new library.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Finally, using the Q-Trap instrument platform, MRM-based precise quantification of metabolites in the new library was performed across all samples.</p>

Study Identifier	MTBLS13345
Study Title	Co-analysis of the metabolomics and transcriptomics reveals regulation network of mint pigment and flavone
Study Description	<p>Mint is considered to have a greater number of phenolic acids, flavonoids, antioxidants and other bioactive components, and is widely used as food, medicine, spices, and flavoring agents. Thus, metabolites composition is an important parameter for assessing the quality of mint. In this study, two mint cultivars (Mentha canadensis L.)&nbsp;were sampled, purple mint and green mint. The purple mint had much higher anthocyanin and total flavone content compared with green mint. Transcriptomics and metabolomics&nbsp;technique were employed to elucidate the regulation network of mint pigment and flavone. A total of 167,901 unigenes were obtained by high-throughput RNA-Seq and 34,608 genes were differentially expressed. The differentially expressed genes (DEGs) were mainly involved in lignin metabolic process and flavonoid biosynthetic process. A total of 143 differentially expressed metabolites (DEMs) were enriched in isoflavonoid, flavonoid biosynthesis, flavone and flavonol biosynthesis, and anthocyanin biosynthesis pathways.&nbsp;The co-analysis results of DEGs and DEMs showed that the flavone and flavonol biosynthesis pathway (ko00944) contained the most DEMs, followed by flavonoid biosynthesis pathway (ko00941) and anthocyanin biosynthesis pathway (ko00942). Furthermore, the most important nine genes and metabolites were screened using O2PLS model. The results will provide a theoretical basis for revealing pigment and flavone biosynthesis network of mint.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The mint materials were planted in the field station of Tangshan Normal University, Tangshan, China in 2022. Tangshan is located in the region with a temperate monsoon climate, characterized by four distinct seasons. The average annual temperature is about 11-12℃, the annual precipitation is 500-700 mm, and the frost-free period is about 180-200 days. The spring growth temperature of mint is 20-30℃, the night is not less than 10℃, and the relative humidity of the air is 40%-60%. Get 8-10 hours of light a day. Stem at 20 days after planting&nbsp;were collected from both purple mint (Z,Mentha canadensis L.) and green mint (&nbsp;L, Mentha canadensis L.). The samples from 6 individuals are mixed as a biological replicate.&nbsp;Three biological replicates are used for metabolomics analysis.</p>	<p>For&nbsp;the&nbsp;metabolomic&nbsp;analysis&nbsp;of&nbsp;flavonoids&nbsp;in&nbsp;green&nbsp;and&nbsp;purple&nbsp;mint&nbsp;stems,&nbsp;the&nbsp;samples&nbsp;were&nbsp;first&nbsp;freeze-dried&nbsp;and&nbsp;ground&nbsp;into&nbsp;a&nbsp;fine&nbsp;powder.&nbsp;Approximately&nbsp;50&nbsp;mg&nbsp;of&nbsp;each&nbsp;sample&nbsp;was&nbsp;extracted&nbsp;with&nbsp;1&nbsp;mL&nbsp;of&nbsp;70%&nbsp;methanol&nbsp;(v/v)&nbsp;by&nbsp;ultrasonic&nbsp;treatment&nbsp;for&nbsp;30&nbsp;min&nbsp;at&nbsp;room&nbsp;temperature,&nbsp;followed&nbsp;by&nbsp;centrifugation&nbsp;at&nbsp;12,000&nbsp;rpm&nbsp;for&nbsp;10&nbsp;min&nbsp;to&nbsp;collect&nbsp;the&nbsp;supernatant.&nbsp;The&nbsp;supernatant&nbsp;was&nbsp;filtered&nbsp;through&nbsp;a&nbsp;0.22&nbsp;μm&nbsp;organic&nbsp;phase&nbsp;filter&nbsp;membrane&nbsp;prior&nbsp;to&nbsp;injection.&nbsp;Control&nbsp;samples&nbsp;included&nbsp;flavonoid&nbsp;standard&nbsp;solutions&nbsp;(e.g.,&nbsp;rutin,&nbsp;quercetin)&nbsp;for&nbsp;qualitative&nbsp;and&nbsp;quantitative&nbsp;calibration,&nbsp;pooled&nbsp;quality&nbsp;control&nbsp;(QC)&nbsp;samples&nbsp;prepared&nbsp;by&nbsp;mixing&nbsp;equal&nbsp;volumes&nbsp;of&nbsp;all&nbsp;sample&nbsp;extracts&nbsp;to&nbsp;monitor&nbsp;system&nbsp;stability,&nbsp;and&nbsp;a&nbsp;solvent&nbsp;blank&nbsp;(70%&nbsp;methanol)&nbsp;to&nbsp;eliminate&nbsp;background&nbsp;interference.</p>	<p>For&nbsp;the&nbsp;metabolomic&nbsp;analysis&nbsp;of&nbsp;flavonoids&nbsp;in&nbsp;mint&nbsp;stems,&nbsp;the&nbsp;chromatography&nbsp;setup&nbsp;involves&nbsp;the&nbsp;Chromatography&nbsp;Instrument&nbsp;as&nbsp;SHIMADZU&nbsp;Nexera&nbsp;X2&nbsp;(Ultra&nbsp;Performance&nbsp;Liquid&nbsp;Chromatography,&nbsp;UPLC).&nbsp;The&nbsp;Column&nbsp;model&nbsp;is&nbsp;Agilent&nbsp;SB-C18&nbsp;(1.8&nbsp;μm,&nbsp;2.1&nbsp;mm&nbsp;*&nbsp;100&nbsp;mm),&nbsp;which&nbsp;belongs&nbsp;to&nbsp;a&nbsp;reversed-phase&nbsp;column&nbsp;(Column&nbsp;type).&nbsp;The&nbsp;Autosampler&nbsp;model&nbsp;is&nbsp;the&nbsp;one&nbsp;integrated&nbsp;with&nbsp;the&nbsp;SHIMADZU&nbsp;Nexera&nbsp;X2&nbsp;system&nbsp;(not&nbsp;specified&nbsp;in&nbsp;the&nbsp;provided&nbsp;info).&nbsp;A&nbsp;commonly&nbsp;used&nbsp;Guard&nbsp;column&nbsp;matching&nbsp;this&nbsp;setup&nbsp;is&nbsp;Agilent&nbsp;SB-C18&nbsp;guard&nbsp;column&nbsp;(1.8&nbsp;μm,&nbsp;2.1&nbsp;mm&nbsp;*&nbsp;10&nbsp;mm).</p>	<p>For&nbsp;the&nbsp;flavonoid&nbsp;metabolomic&nbsp;analysis&nbsp;of&nbsp;mint&nbsp;stems,&nbsp;the&nbsp;mass&nbsp;spectrometry&nbsp;parameters&nbsp;involve&nbsp;an&nbsp;Ion&nbsp;source&nbsp;of&nbsp;electrospray&nbsp;ionization&nbsp;(ESI),&nbsp;with&nbsp;the&nbsp;Mass&nbsp;analyzer&nbsp;configured&nbsp;in&nbsp;QQQ&nbsp;(triple&nbsp;quadrupole)&nbsp;and&nbsp;LIT&nbsp;(linear&nbsp;ion&nbsp;trap)&nbsp;modes&nbsp;(as&nbsp;part&nbsp;of&nbsp;the&nbsp;Instrument:&nbsp;Applied&nbsp;Biosystems&nbsp;4500&nbsp;QTRAP&nbsp;tandem&nbsp;mass&nbsp;spectrometer).&nbsp;A&nbsp;commonly&nbsp;used&nbsp;Scan&nbsp;m/z&nbsp;range&nbsp;for&nbsp;flavonoids&nbsp;(covering&nbsp;both&nbsp;positive&nbsp;and&nbsp;negative&nbsp;ion&nbsp;modes)&nbsp;is&nbsp;50–1000&nbsp;m/z.</p>	<p>Post-annotation,&nbsp;QC&nbsp;filtering&nbsp;(removing&nbsp;features&nbsp;with&nbsp;RSD&nbsp;&gt;30%&nbsp;in&nbsp;pooled&nbsp;QC&nbsp;samples)&nbsp;and&nbsp;median&nbsp;normalization&nbsp;were&nbsp;applied,&nbsp;followed&nbsp;by&nbsp;multivariate&nbsp;analysis&nbsp;(PCA,&nbsp;OPLS-DA)&nbsp;in&nbsp;SIMCA-P&nbsp;to&nbsp;compare&nbsp;flavonoid&nbsp;profiles&nbsp;between&nbsp;green&nbsp;and&nbsp;purple&nbsp;mint&nbsp;stems.</p>	<p>Flavonoid&nbsp;features&nbsp;were&nbsp;annotated&nbsp;by&nbsp;matching&nbsp;MRM&nbsp;transitions,&nbsp;retention&nbsp;times,&nbsp;and&nbsp;MS&nbsp;fragments&nbsp;against&nbsp;in-house&nbsp;standards&nbsp;(e.g.,&nbsp;rutin,&nbsp;quercetin)&nbsp;and&nbsp;databases&nbsp;(HMDB,&nbsp;Metlin)&nbsp;via&nbsp;MS-DIAL.</p>

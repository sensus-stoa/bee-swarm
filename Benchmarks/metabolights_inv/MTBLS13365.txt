Study Protocol Description	<p>Chlorella is kept in a dark environment with 12 hours of light/12 hours. Subsequently, the supernatant was centrifuged at 5000g for 10 minutes, and the supernatant was centrifuged at 10000g for 30 minutes. After passing through the 0.22 micron filter screen, 15000g was separated for 90 minutes to obtain Chlorella exosomes.</p>	<p>Suck 100 μ l of liquid sample into a 1.5 ml centrifuge tube, add 400 μ l of extraction solution (acetonitrile: methanol=1:1) containing four internal standards (l-2-chlorophenylalanine (0.02 mg/ml), etc.), vortex and mix well for 30 s, extract for 30 min (5 , 40 kHz) with low temperature ultrasound, and place the sample at -20&nbsp;for 30 min. At 4 , 13000 g was centrifuged for 15 min, the supernatant was transferred, dried with nitrogen, redissolved with 100 µ l of complex solution (acetonitrile: water=1:1), extracted with low-temperature ultrasound for 5 min (5 , 40KHz), and centrifuged at 4 , 13000 g for 10 min. the supernatant was transferred to the injection vial with internal cannula for analysis.</p>	<p>The 3 μ l sample was separated by HSS T3 column (100 mm × 2.1 mm I.D., 1.8 µ m) and detected by mass spectrometry. Mobile phase a was water/acetonitrile (95/5, V/V) solution containing 0.1% formic acid, and mobile phase B was acetonitrile/isopropanol/water (47.5/47.5/5, V/V/V) solution containing 0.1% formic acid. The flow rate was 0.40 ml/min and the column temperature was 40 .</p>	<p>The mass spectrum signal of the sample was collected by positive and negative ion scanning mode, and the mass scanning range was m/z:70-1050.

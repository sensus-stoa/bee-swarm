Study Title	Untargeted LC–MS serum metabolomics in a colitis mouse model treated with the microbiota-regulating nanomedicine MRA@RP
Study Description	<p>This study applies untargeted liquid chromatography–mass spectrometry (LC–MS)–based serum metabolomics to characterize metabolic alterations in a dextran sulfate sodium–induced colitis mouse model and to evaluate the therapeutic effects of the microbiota-regulating nanomedicine MRA_RP. Serum samples from healthy, colitis control and MRA_RP-treated mice were analyzed, and multivariate statistics (including PCA and volcano plots) were used to identify differentially abundant metabolites. MRA_RP treatment shifted the metabolic profile of colitis mice toward that of healthy controls. These data support a role for MRA_RP in comprehensive metabolic reprogramming linked to gut microbiota modulation, contributing to the amelioration of ulcerative colitis and related neuropsychiatric symptoms.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Male C57BL/6 mice were housed under pathogen-free conditions and acute colitis was induced by dextran sulfate sodium (DSS). Mice were divided into three groups: healthy, control (acute colitis mice treated with PBS), and MRA@RP (acute colitis mice treated with the nanoagent MRA@RP). At the end of treatments, mice were sacrificed and whole blood was collected for metabolomics analysis. The blood was allowed to stand in centrifuge tubes for 1 h at 4 °C to allow coagulation and layering, followed by centrifugation at 5000 g for 10 min at 4 °C. The upper serum was collected for subsequent non-targeted LC–MS metabolomic profiling.</p>	<p>The samples (100 μL) were placed in the EP tubes and resuspended with prechilled 80%</p><p>methanol by well vortex. Then the samples were incubated on ice for 5 min and</p><p>centrifuged at 15,000 g, 4°C for 20 min. Some of supernatant was diluted to final</p><p>concentration containing 53% methanol by LC-MS grade water.The samples were</p><p>subsequently transferred to a fresh Eppendorf tube and then were centrifuged at</p><p>15000 g, 4°C for 20 min. Finally, the supernatant was injected into the LC-MS/MS</p><p>system analysis</p>	<p>UHPLC-MS/MS analyses were performed using a Vanquish UHPLC system</p><p>(ThermoFisher, Germany) coupled with an Orbitrap Q ExactiveTM HF mass</p><p>spectrometer, Orbitrap Q ExactiveTM HF-X mass spectrometer, Orbitrap Exploris™</p><p>120 mass spectrometer or Orbitrap Exploris™ 480 mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher,</p><p>Germany) in Novogene Co., Ltd. (Beijing, China). Samples were injected onto a</p><p>Hypersil Goldcolumn (100×2.1 mm, 1.9μm) using a 12-min linear gradient at a flow</p><p>rate of 0.2 mL/min. The eluents for the positive and negative polarity modes were</p><p>eluent A (0.1% FA in Water) and eluent B (Methanol).The solvent gradient was set as</p><p>follows: 2% B, 1.5 min; 2-85% B, 3 min; 85-100% B, 10 min; 100-2% B, 10.1 min; </p><p>2% B, 12 min.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Q ExactiveTM HF mass spectrometer was operated in positive/negative</p><p>polarity mode with spray voltage of 3.5 kV, capillary temperature of 320°C, sheath</p><p>gas flow rate of 35 psi and aux gas flow rate of 10 L/min, S-lens RF level of 60, Aux</p><p>gas heater temperature of 350°C.</p>	<p>The data files generated by UHPLC-MS/MS were processed by XCMS to perform</p><p>peak alignment, peak picking, and quantitation for each metabolite. Then, based on</p><p>adduct ions and setting mass deviation to 10 ppm, a comparison was made between</p><p>these data and the self-built high-quality secondary spectrum database (NovoMetDB)</p><p>to obtain results for metabolite identification. After eliminating background ions</p><p>according to blank samples, the original quantitative results were normalized by the</p><p>following formula to obtain relative peak areas: Relative peak areas = Raw</p><p>quantitative value of samples/ (The sum of quantitative value of samples/ The sum of</p><p>quantitative value of QC1); Compounds with a coefficient of variation (CV) of</p><p>relative peak areas in QC samples greater than 30% were removed.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Finally, the</p><p>identification and relative quantitation results of metabolites were obtained. Data</p><p>processing is based on the Linux operating system (CentOS version 6.6), using R and</p><p>Python. For details on specific packages and software versions used, please refer to</p><p>the README file included in the results.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>KCs (2×106 cells) from both Sed and PEx mice were harvested for Metabolomics. 3 weeks after tumor injection, both mice bearing MC38 tumors were sacrificed. The livers were harvested and processed into single-cell suspensions, and KCs were isolated&nbsp;</p>	<p>&nbsp;The cells were vortexed for 30 seconds, then sonicated for 30 minutes at 4°C. Subsequently, each sample was kept at -20°C for 1 hour, and then the sample was&nbsp;centrifuged at 12,000 rpm and 4°C for 15 minutes. Finally, 200 μL of the supernatant and 5 μL of DL-o-Chlorophenylalanine (0.2 mg/mL) were transferred to vials for LC-MS analysis</p>	<p>Separation is performed by ACQUITY UPLC (Waters) combined with Q Exactive MS (Thermo) and screened with ESI-MS. The LC system is comprised of ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 (100×2.1 mm×1.8 μm) with ACQUITY UPLC (Waters). The mobile phase is composed of solvent A (0.05% formic acid water) and solvent B (acetonitrile) with a gradient elution (0-1 min, 5% B; 1-12.5 min, 5%-95% B; 12.5-13.5 min, 95% B; 13.5-13.6 min, 95%-5% B; 13.6-16 min, 5% B). The flow rate of the mobile phase is 0.3 mL/min. The column temperature is maintained at 40°C, and the sample manager temperature is set at 4°C.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry parameters in ESI+ and ESI- mode are listed as follows:</p><p>ESI+: Heater Temp 300°C; Sheath Gas Flow rate, 45 arb; Aux Gas Flow Rate, 15 arb; Sweep Gas Flow Rate, 1 arb; spray voltage, 3.0 KV; Capillary Temp, 350°C; S-Lens RF Level, 30%.</p><p>ESI-: Heater Temp 300°C, Sheath Gas Flow rate, 45 arb; Aux Gas Flow Rate, 15 arb; Sweep Gas Flow Rate, 1 arb; spray voltage, 3.2 KV; Capillary Temp,350°C; S-Lens RF Level,60%.</p>	<p>Metabolomics raw data processing utilizes XCMS to transform raw spectral data into a normalized feature table. </p>	<p>Metabolites are then annotated by matching acquired MS/MS spectra against reference spectral libraries MassBank and by comparing accurate mass and fragmentation pattern</p><p><br></p><p><br></p><p><br></p>

Study Protocol Description	Cells were washed with ammonium acetate solution on ice and extracted with eighty percent methanol.	Metabolites were extracted with cold eighty percent methanol and supernatants were dried and reconstituted.	Metabolites were separated by reverse phase liquid chromatography on a Luna NH2 one hundred angstrom column.	Samples were analyzed on a Q Exactive Plus mass spectrometer in full scan mode.	Raw data were processed in TraceFinder and abundances were normalized to cell number and to the median intensity.	Metabolites were identified by matching retention time and accurate mass to verified standards.

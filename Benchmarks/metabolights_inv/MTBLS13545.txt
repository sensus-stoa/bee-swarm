Study Identifier	MTBLS13545
Study Title	Released palmitic acid–mediated TLR4/NF-κB activation enhances the virulence of Bordetella pertussis MT28 lineage
Study Description	<p>Whooping cough resurged globally despite the widespread vaccination. A macrolide-resistant (MR) lineage carrying the high-virulence ptxP3 allele, ptxP3 MR-MT28 (MT28), has imposed a substantial burden on pertussis epidemics, while the underlying factors driving its prevalence remain unclear. Thus, we performed an epidemiological surveillance and bacterial genomic, phenotypic, in vitro and in vivo pathogenicity analyses. We found MT28 lineage exhibits enhanced abilities of colonization and pro-inflammatory. Transcriptomic analysis revealed significant upregulation of key virulence genes (ptxA, fhaB, tcfA and bvgA), suggesting a molecular basis for its transmission and pathogenicity. Further, lipid-targeted metabolomics and liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry (LC-MS) identified B. pertussis-derived palmitic acid (PA) as a crucial pro-inflammatory mediator that promotes MT28 pathogenicity via the TLR4/NF-κB signaling pathway. These findings elucidate the mechanisms underlying the prevalence and increased pathogenicity of the MT28 lineage, and propose a novel pathogenic mechanism of B. pertussis involving PA-mediated activation of the TLR4/NF-κB inflammatory signaling pathway.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Bacterial suspensions were collected from freshly cultured B. pertussis at 60 hours and centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 15 minutes at 4 °C.&nbsp;</p>	<p>20 μl of filtered bacterial supernatant or blank medium was mixed with 200 μl mass spectrometry-grade ethanol and 20 μl ultrapure water, vortexed, and centrifuged at 14,000 rpm for 5 min at 4 °C</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was achieved on a Synergi™ 4 μm Fusion-RP 80 Å column (50 × 2 mm) using a Waters ACQUITY UPLC I-Class PLUS system coupled to a SCIEX X500B QTOF LC-MS/MS. The mobile phase consisted of water (A) and acetonitrile (B) at a flow rate of 0.5 mL/min, with a linear gradient from 95% A/5% B to 0% A/100% B over 6 min, followed by re-equilibration to initial conditions</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry was operated in negative ion mode with ion source gas at 45 psi, curtain gas at 25 psi, and a source temperature of 500 °C. Data were acquired in full-scan TOF-MS mode with a spray voltage of -4500 V, a declustering potential of -80 V, a collision energy of -10 V, and an m/z range of 100–1000</p>	<p>Data acquisition and analysis were performed using SCIEX OS software (version 3.1.6).</p>	<p>Calibration curves were constructed by spiking known concentrations of palmitic acid standards together with a fixed amount of internal standard, covering the concentration range observed in the samples. palmitic acid (palmitic acid-d4-1) as an internal standard.&nbsp;</p>

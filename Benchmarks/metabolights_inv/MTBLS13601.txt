Study Protocol Description	<p>24 samples of metabolites from the&nbsp;of mice</p>	<p>For each sample, 25mg was taken and added to the thickened centrifuge tube, along with 2 small magnetic beads.At the same time, 10μL of the prepared internal standard 1 was added to each sample.800µL of the precipitation agent (methanol: acetonitrile: water = 2:2:1, V/V/V) was added to the sample, and it was ground at 50Hz for 5 minutes.The ground sample was placed at -20°C for precipitation for 2 hours.The sample was placed in the centrifuge (25000 g * 4 °C, 15 minutes), and 600µL of the corresponding sample was taken from each sample and added to a new EP tube, and frozen and dried. 600μL of 50% methanol was added to the dried sample, and shaken until completely dissolved.Centrifuge at 25000 g * 4 °C for 15 minutes, and the supernatant was placed in a new EP tube. 10μL of each sample was mixed to form the QC sample, and the prepared supernatant was handed over to the personnel of the upper unit.</p>	<p>The chromatographic column used was the BEH C18 column (1.7 μm, 2.1*100 mm, Waters, USA).&nbsp;In the positive ion mode, the mobile phase was a water solution containing 0.1% formic acid (A solution) and methanol containing 0.1% formic acid (B solution).&nbsp;In the negative ion mode, the mobile phase was a water solution containing 10 mM ammonium formate (A solution) and 95% methanol (B solution). The elution gradient was as follows: 0-1 min, 2% B solution; 1-9 min, 2% - 98% B solution; 9-12 min, 98% B solution; 12-12.1 min, 98% B solution - 2% B solution; 12.1-15 min, 2% B solution. The flow rate was 0.35 mL/min, the column temperature was 45 °C, and the injection volume was 5 μL.</p>	<p>The primary and secondary mass spectrometry data were collected using the Q Exactive HF mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA). The mass-to-charged particle ratio range for the mass spectrometry scan was 70 to 1050. The primary resolution was 120,000, the AGC was 3e6, and the maximum injection time (IT, injection time) was 100ms. Based on the intensity of the parent ion, the top 3 ions were selected for fragmentation, and the secondary information was collected. The secondary resolution was 30,000, the AGC was 1e5, and the maximum injection time (IT, injection time) was 50ms. The fragmentation energy (stepped nce) was set to 20, 40, and 60 eV. The parameters of the ion source (ESI) were set as follows: the sheath gas flow rate was 40, the auxiliary gas flow rate was 10, the spray voltage (|KV|) was 3.80 in the positive ion mode, 3.20 in the negative ion mode, the ion transmission tube temperature was 320°C, and the auxiliary gas heating temperature was 350°C.</p>	<p>The offline data from the mass spectrometry was imported into the Compound Discoverer 3.3 software (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA). After conducting mass spectrometry data analysis by integrating BMDB (BGI Metabolome Database), mzCloud database and ChemSpider online database, a data matrix containing information such as peak areas of metabolites and identification results would be obtained. Subsequently, further information analysis and processing would be carried out on this table.</p>	<p>Name: Compound Discoverer Version v.3.3,Molecular ion mass deviation: &lt; 5 ppm,Fragment ion mass deviation: &lt; 10 ppm,Retention time deviation: &lt; 0.2 min</p>

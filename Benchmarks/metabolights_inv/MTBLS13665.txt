Study Title	Untargeted metabolomics analysis of scallop coelomic fluids
Study Description	<p>Ascidian is an invasive fouling species within marine ecosystem. The fouling ascidians usually attach on bivalve aquaculture facilities, which is likely related to the influence of certain chemical factors potentially released from scallops. To investigate whether certain chemicals can be indeed released from tissues of scallops, researchers conducted untargeted metabolomic analysis of scallop coelomic fluids.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The coelomic fluids were collected from healthy scallops by using a syringe and immediately stored at -80°C until analysis. The samples were thawed on ice and vortexed for 10 s before further treatment.</p>	<p>The 200 μL of samples were mixed with 1 mL of the extraction solvent (MTBE: MeOH =3:1, v/v) containing internal standard mixture, followed by a whirling for 15 min. After that, 100 μL of ultrapure water was added into the sample mixture. Subsequently, the sample mixture was vortexed for 1 min and centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 10 min. The volume of 500 μL of the upper organic layer was collected and evaporated using a vacuum concentrator.</p>	<p>The sample extracts were analyzed using an LC-ESI-MS/MS system (UPLC, ExionLC AD, https://sciex.com.cn/; MS, QTRAP®6500+ System, https://sciex.com/). UPLC: column, Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 C18 (1.8 µm, 2.1 mm*100 mm) for hydrophilic compounds. UPLC: column, Thermo Accucore™ C30 (2.6 μm, 2.1 mm*100 mm i.d.) for hydrophobic compounds.</p>	<p>LIT and triple quadrupole (QQQ) scans were acquired on a triple quadrupole-linear ion trap mass spectrometer (QTRAP), QTRAP® LC-MS/MS System, equipped with an ESI Turbo Ion-Spray interface, operating in positive and negative ion mode and controlled by Analyst 1.6.3 software (Sciex).</p>	<p>The raw data were converted to MzXML files using ProteoWizard MSCovert before importing into freely available XCMS software.</p>	<p>The metabolites were detected by MetWare (http://www.metware.cn/) based on the AB Sciex QTRAP 6500 LC-MS/MS platform.</p>

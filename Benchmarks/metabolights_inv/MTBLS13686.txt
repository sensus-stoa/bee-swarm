Study Title	Regulation of flavonoid accumulation during tuber development of 'Tiegun' yam (Dioscorea polystachya Turcz.)
Study Description	<p> ‘Tiegun’ yam (Dioscorea polystachya Turcz.) is regarded as a medicinal and edible crop with extremely health-preserving values. However, the types and accumulation of bioactive substances in ‘Tiegun’ are incompletely elucidated. We firstly explored the bioactive substance accumulation across the process of ‘Tiegun’ tuber growth. Some well-recognized functional substances such as isoflavones and flavanols continuously accumulated significantly in the mature tuber. The flavonoid biosynthesis pathway was analyzed through transcriptome sequencing on the different tuber growth stage. DpERF1-4 expression levels and the contents of ethylene and gibberellin were significantly positively correlated with flavonoid accumulation. Exogenous application of 0.5 mM ethephon at the early stage could increase flavonoid content in tubers by 31-fold, and simultaneously upregulating the expression levels of DpERF1-4 and key flavonoid biosynthesis genes. This is the first report about DpERF response to ethylene signal and its promotion the key flavonoid biosynthesis genes expression.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>In April 2024, ‘Tiegun’ yams (D. polystachya Turcz.) were planted in the test field located in the College of Life Sciences, Henan Normal University (Xinxiang City, China; 35.19°N, 113.52°E). The sampling time for ‘Tiegun’ were 90 days after planting (DAP) (named Dp1), 120 DAP (Dp2), 150 DAP (Dp3), and 180 DAP (Dp4).&nbsp;All samples of yam tubers were harvested, and the middle and lower parts of tubers were selected for sliced and frozen at -80 ℃, immediately.&nbsp;</p>	<p>&nbsp;‘Tiegun’ tubers during different development stages (Dp1~Dp4) were collected, followed by metabolomic analysis with six biological repetitions for each stage. In brief, 100 mg of fresh tuber powder preserved at -80 ℃ was added into 800 μL of extraction buffer (CH3OH: H2O = 4:1, V/V), and mixed fully in a tissue grinder (-10 ℃, 6 min). Then, the extraction mixture was placed in an ultrasonic instrument at 5 ℃, with 40 kHz for 30 min. These samples were stored at -20 ℃ for 30 min and then centrifuged at 13,000 rpm for 15 min.&nbsp;</p>	<p>The components in the supernatant were analyzed by LC-MS/MS (UHPLC-Triple TOF 6600, AB SCIEX, USA).&nbsp;The column was ACQUTIY UPLC BEH C18 (100 mm × 2.1 mm i.d., 1.7 μm; Waters, USA). The mobile phase contained solvent A, containing 0.1% formic acid in water: acetonitrile (2:98, V/V), and solvent B, containing 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile. The protocol of gradient condition as followed: 2% solvent B maintained at 0- 0.5 min; increasing the concentration of solvent B from 2% to 35% at 0.5- 7.5 min; further increasing the concentration of solvent B from 35% to 95% at 7.5- 13 min; 95% solvent B maintained at 13- 14.4 min; decreasing the concentration of solvent B from 95% to 2% at 14.4- 14.5 min; the final step, 2% solvent B maintained at 14.5- 16 min. The flow rate was 0.4 mL/min and the column temperature was 40 ℃.&nbsp;</p>	<p>The signals of mass were collected as follows: an electrospray ionization was used as a source (heated to 500 ℃) and the detection mass range was set at 50- 1200 m/z; the voltage in positive mode was set 5500 V while -4500 V in negative mode.</p>	<p>The raw data was treated by Progenesis QI (Waters, USA) </p>	<p>Identified by Human metabolome (HMDB), Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes (KEGG), and Self-built plant-specific metabolic database (MJDBPM, Majorbio, China).</p>

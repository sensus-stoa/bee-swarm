Study Title	Multi-omics reveals the protective mechanisms of Gastrodia elata ethyl acetate extract in vascular dementia
Study Description	<p>Objective: This study aimed to investigate the ameliorative effect of the ethyl acetate extract of Gastrodia elata (EEGE) on vascular dementia (VD) and its underlying mechanisms.&nbsp;</p><p>Methods: A VD rat model was established using the two-vessel occlusion method, while an in vitro cerebral ischemia injury model was constructed by subjecting HT22 cells to oxygen-glucose deprivation. The mechanisms were systematically explored through behavioral tests, ELISA, integrated network analysis, and combined metabolomic and transcriptomic techniques. Key targets were further validated by Western blot.&nbsp;</p><p>Results: EEGE significantly improved cognitive function in VD rats. Integrated multi-omics and network analysis predicted that its effects involved two key targets, TNF and IGF1, and identified Parishin A and p-hydroxybenzaldehyde as prioritized drug metabolites for assessment. Subsequent experiments confirmed that EEGE effectively downregulated serum levels of IL-6, TNF-α, and IL-1β by modulating the IGF1-TREM2 signaling axis and the AMPK-SIRT1-FoxO1- NF-κB pathway.&nbsp;</p><p>Conclusion: The improvement of cognitive dysfunction in vascular dementia by EEGE is closely associated with its regulation of the IGF1-TREM2 axis and the AMPK-SIRT1-FoxO1-NF-κB pathway, thereby mitigating neuroinflammation. This study provides experimental evidence and a potential mechanistic basis for further exploration of EEGE in VD intervention.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Take 100 mg of brain tissue from each group, grind and homogenize it. Centrifuge at 3000 rpm for 30 s at 4 °C. After centrifugation, add 400 μL of 70% methanol-water internal standard extraction solution, oscillate at 1500 rpm for 5 min, and let it stand on ice for 15 min. Then, centrifuge at 12,000 rpm for 10 min, and transfer 300 μL of the supernatant to a new centrifuge tube for instrumental analysis. Quality control (QC) samples are prepared by mixing sample extracts, which are used to analyze the repeatability of samples under the same treatment method.</p>	<p>Take 100 mg of brain tissue from each group, grind and homogenize it. Centrifuge at 3000 rpm for 30 s at 4 °C. After centrifugation, add 400 μL of 70% methanol-water internal standard extraction solution, oscillate at 1500 rpm for 5 min, and let it stand on ice for 15 min. Then, centrifuge at 12,000 rpm for 10 min, and transfer 300 μL of the supernatant to a new centrifuge tube for instrumental analysis. Quality control (QC) samples are prepared by mixing sample extracts, which are used to analyze the repeatability of samples under the same treatment method.</p>	<p>The chromatographic conditions are as follows: Chromatographic column: Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 C18 1.8 μm, 21 mm x 100 mm; Mobile phase: Phase A is ultrapure water (0.1% formic acid), and phase B is acetonitrile (0.1% formic acid); Elution gradient: At 0 min, water/acetonitrile (95:5), at 11.0 min it is 10:90, at 12.0 min it remains 10:90, at 12.1 min it is 95:5, and at 14.0 min it remains 95:5; Flow rate: 0.4 mL/min; Column temperature: 40 °C; Injection volume: 2 μL.</p>	<p>The mass spectrometry conditions are as follows: The temperature of the electrospray ionization (ESI) source is 500 °C, the mass spectrometry voltage is 5500 V (positive) and-4500 V (negative), the ion source gas I is 55 psi, gas II is 60 psi, and the collision-activated dissociation (CAD) parameter is set to high.</p>	<p>For the analysis of the model group and the drug-administered group, differential metabolites are screened with the criteria of variable importance in projection (VIP)&gt;1 and P&lt;0.05. Extract the VIP values from the orthogonal partial least-squares discriminant analysis (OPLS-DA) results, which also include score plots and permutation plots. Combine the p-value&lt;0.05 and VIP&gt;1 to identify the metabolites with the highest potential as biomarkers. Perform partial least-squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA), principal component analysis (PCA), and conduct pathway analysis using MetaboAnalyst 5.0.</p>	<p>Finally, the final screening is conducted by calculating the P-values and VIP values. When the P-value &lt; 0.05 and the VIP value &gt; 1, the metabolite molecules are considered to be statistically significant. Pathway analysis is mainly performed using the MetaboAnalyst software package. This is used for the enrichment analysis of the obtained differential metabolites. Additionally, the pathway maps in the KEGG Mapper tool are used to browse the differential metabolites.</p>

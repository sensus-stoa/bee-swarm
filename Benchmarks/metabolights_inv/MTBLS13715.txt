Study Protocol Description	<p><em>Candida albican treated with PGaDH</em> were collected, immediately frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at −80 °C until analysis.Untargeted metabolomics analysis was performed on Candida albican samples.</p>	<p>1. Accurately weigh an appropriate amount of sample into a 2 mL centrifuge tube,add 10 0 mg</p><p>glass bead;</p><p>2. Add 1000 µL 50% methanol water, vortex for 30 s;</p><p>3. Put the centrifuge tube containing the sample into the 2 mL adapter matched with the</p><p>instrument, immerse it in liquid nitrogen for rapid freezing for 5 min, take out the centrifuge tube</p><p>and thaw at room temperature, put the centrifuge tube into the 2 mL adapter again, install it into</p><p>the tissue grinder and grind it at 55 Hz for 2 min;</p><p>4. Repeat step 3 twice;</p><p>5. Take out the centrifuge tube, centrifuge for 10 min at 12,000 rpm and 4 ℃℃, take all the</p><p>s upernatant, transfer it to a new 2 mL centrifuge tube, concentrate and dry it;</p><p>6. Accurately add 300 µL of 2 Amino 3 --(2 chloro phenyl) propionic acid (4 ppm) solution</p><p>prepared with 50% methanol water to redissolve the sample, filter the supernatant by 0.22 μ m</p><p>membrane and transfer into the detection bottle for LC MS detection.</p>	<p>The LC analysis was performed on a Vanquish UHPLC System (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA).</p><p>Chromatography was carried out with an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 Column (2.1 × 100 mm, 1.8 μm)</p><p>(Waters, Milford, MA, USA). The column maintained at 40 ℃. The flow rate and injection volume</p><p>were set at 0.3 mL/min and 5 μL, respectively.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection of metabolites was performed on Orbitrap Exploris 120 (Thermo</p><p>Fisher Scientific, USA) with ESI ion source. Simultaneous MS1 and MS/MS (Full MS-ddMS2 mode,</p><p>data-dependent MS/MS) acquisition was used. The parameters were as follows: sheath gas</p><p>pressure, 40 arb; aux gas flow, 10 arb; spray voltage, 3.50 kV and -2.50 kV for ESI(+) and ESI(-),</p><p>respectively; capillary temperature, 325 ℃ ; MS1 range, m/z 100-1000; MS1 resolving power,</p><p>60000 FWHM; number of data dependant scans per cycle, 4; MS/MS resolving power, 15000FWHM; normalized collision energy, 30%; dynamic exclusion time, automatic</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted and profiled by LC-MS. Differential metabolites were identified using VIP &gt; 1.0 and p &lt; 0.05 and analyzed for pathway enrichment through the Suzhou Panomix Biomedical Technology Co., Ltd’s cloud-based platform.</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted and profiled by LC-MS. Differential metabolites were identified using VIP &gt; 1.0 and p &lt; 0.05 and analyzed for pathway enrichment through the Suzhou Panomix Biomedical Technology Co., Ltd’s cloud-based platform.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Fecal samples were collected from non-drinkers and drinkers, immediately snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen after collection to avoid metabolite degradation, and then stored at -80 °C until subsequent pretreatment and detection.</p>	<p>For polar metabolites detection, chromatographic analysis was performed on a Vanquish ultra-high performance liquid chromatography system (Thermo Fisher Scientific). Separation was achieved using a reverse phase Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH C18 column (2.1mm × 50mm, 1.7μm) — a typical reverse phase column suitable for polar metabolites separation, with mobile phase A (25mmol/L ammonium acetate and 25mmol/L ammonia aqueous solution) and mobile phase B (acetonitrile). The sample plate temperature was maintained at 4 ℃, and the injection volume was 2 μL.</p>	<p>Raw mass spectrometric data were processed using Xcalibur software and metabolomic analysis tools for peak extraction, alignment, deconvolution and normalization. Metabolites with more than 50% missing values were removed, and multivariate and univariate statistical analyses were performed to screen differential metabolites.</p>	<p>Fecal samples were accurately weighed (50 mg per sample), homogenized with 500 μL pre-cooled extraction solvent (methanol:acetonitrile:water = 2:2:1, v/v/v) containing internal standard, vortexed vigorously for 3 min, and sonicated on ice for 15 min to fully release polar metabolites. After incubation at -20 °C for 30 min to precipitate proteins, the samples were centrifuged at 12,000 × g and 4 °C for 15 min. The supernatants were collected and filtered through 0.22 μm organic phase filters, and the filtrates were used for ultra-high performance liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry (UHPLC-MS/MS) analysis (combined chromatographic and mass spectrometric detection).</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was conducted on an Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific) controlled by Xcalibur software (version 4.4), equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source, and operated in both positive and negative ion modes. Key parameters included sheath gas flow rate 50 Arb, auxiliary gas flow rate 15 Arb, capillary temperature 320 ℃, full ms resolution 60,000, MS/MS resolution 15,000, collision energy SNCE 20/30/40, and spray voltage 3.8 kV (positive) or -3.4 kV (negative).</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was achieved by matching the retention time, accurate molecular weight, and MS/MS fragmentation patterns of detected metabolites with those in standard databases, combined with the mass spectrometric parameters and chromatographic separation characteristics.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The sample extracts were analyzed using an LC-ESI-MS/MS system (UPLC, ExionLC AD, https://sciex.com.cn&nbsp;/; MS, QTRAP® 6500+ System,&nbsp;https://sciex.com&nbsp;/).The analytical conditions were as follows.</p><p>T3 method: HPLC: column, Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 C18 (100 mm×2.1 mm i.d.1.8 µm); solvent system, water with 0.05% formic acid (A), acetonitrile with 0.05% formic acid (B). The gradient was started at 5% B (0 min), increased to 95% B (8-12.5 min), finaly ramped back to 5% B (12.6-15 min); flow rate, 0.35 mL/min; temperature, 40°C; injection volume: 2 μL.</p><p>Amide method: HPLC: column, ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide (i.d.2.1×100 mm, 1.7 μm); solvent system, water with 10mM Ammonium acetate and 0.3% Ammonium hydroxide (A), 90% acetonitrile/water (V/V)(B); The gradient was started at 95% B (0-1.2 min), decreased to 70% B (8 min),50% B (9-11 min), finaly ramped back to 95% B (11.1-15 min); flow rate, 0.4 mL/min; temperature, 40°C; injection volume: 2 μL.</p>	<p>The mass spectrometry data were processed using Analyst 1.6.3 and MultiQuant 3.0.3 software.</p>	<p>The sample was thawed on ice, 100 μL of ultrapure water extract was added to resuspend the cell pellet. Divide 50 μL cell suspension and add 200 µL of methanol (precooled at -20°C) and vortexed for 2 min under the condition of 2500 r/min. The sample was frozen in liquid nitrogen for 5 min, removed on ice for 5 min, after that, the sample was vortexed for 2 min.The previous step was repeated for 3 times. The sample was centrifuged at 12000 r/min for 10 min at 4°C. Take 200 μL of supernatant into a new centrifuge tube and place the supernatant in -20°C refrigerator for 30 min. Then the supernatant was centrifuged at 12000 r/min for 10 min at 4°C. After centrifugation, transfer 180 μL of supernatant through Protein Precipitation Plate for further LC-MS analysis. The left 50 μL cell suspension was frozen and thawed for 3 times, centrifuged at 12,000 r/min for 10 min, and the supernatant was taken to determine the protein concentration by BCA Protein Assay kit.</p>	<p>AB 6500+ QTRAP® LC-MS/MS System, equipped with an ESI Turbo Ion-Spray interface, operating in both positive and negative ion modes and controlled by Analyst 1.6 software (AB Sciex). The ESI source operation parameters were as follows: ion source, turbo spray; source temperature 550°C; ion spray voltage (IS) 5500 V(Positive),-4500 V(Neagtive); curtain gas (CUR) were set at 35.0 psi; DP and CE for individual MRM transitions was done with further DP and CE optimization. A specific set of MRM transitions were monitored for each period according to those metabolites eluted within this period.</p>	<p>Based on standard samples, the MWDB (Metware Database) is constructed to perform qualitative analysis on mass spectrometry detection data.</p>	<p>THLE-2 cells were seeded into 100 mm culture dishes at a density of 3×1000000 cells per dish in a final culture volume of 6 mL. The cells were divided into three groups: a blank control group, a CCl4 group, and a CCl4 + exosome group (containing 8.4×100000000000 exosome particles). The treatments for the CCl4 group and the CCl4 + exosome group were identical to those described above, with a final CCl4 concentration of 15 mM in both groups. On the fourth day, cells were harvested using a cell scraper and sent for analysis (Metware Metabolomics Biotechnology Co., Ltd., Wuhan, Hubei Province, China).&nbsp;</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>UHPLC-MS/MS analyses were performed using a Vanquish UHPLC system</p><p>(ThermoFisher, Germany) coupled with an Orbitrap Q ExactiveTMHF-X mass</p><p>spectrometer (Thermo Fisher, Germany)in Novogene Co., Ltd. (Beijing, China).</p><p>Samples were injected onto a HypersilGoldcolumn (100×2.1 mm, 1.9μm) using a</p><p>12-min linear gradient at a flow rate of 0.2mL/min. The eluents for the positive and</p><p>negative polarity modes were eluent A (0.1% FA in Water) and eluent B</p><p>(Methanol).The solvent gradient was set as follows: 2% B, 1.5 min; 2-85% B, 3 min;</p><p>85-100% B, 10 min; 100-2% B, 10.1 min; 2% B, 12 min.&nbsp;</p>	<p>The raw data files generated by UHPLC-MS/MS were processed using the Compound</p><p>Discoverer 3.3 (CD3.3, ThermoFisher) to perform peak alignment, peak picking, and</p><p>quantitation for each metabolite. The main parameterswere set as follows: peak area</p><p>was corrected with the first sample; actual mass tolerance, 5ppm; signal intensity</p><p>tolerance, 30%; and minimum intensity, et al.After that, peak intensities were</p><p>normalized to the total spectral intensity.</p>	<p>The samples (1 mL) were freeze-dried and resuspended with prechilled 80% methanol</p><p>by well vortex. Then the sampleswere incubated on ice for 5 min and centrifuged at</p><p>15,000 g, 4°C for 15 min. Some of supernatant was diluted to final concentration</p><p>containing 53% methanol by LC-MS grade water.The samples were subsequently transferred to a fresh Eppendorf tube and thenwere centrifuged at 15000 g, 4°C for 15</p><p>min. Finally, the supernatant was injected into the LC-MS/MS system analysis.</p>	<p>Q ExactiveTM HF mass</p><p>spectrometer was operated in positive/negative polarity mode with spray voltage of</p><p>3.5 kV, capillary temperature of 320°C, sheath gas flow rate of 35 psi and aux gas</p><p>flow rate of 10 L/min, S-lens RF level of 60, Aux gas heater temperature of 350°C.</p>	<p>The normalized data was used to predict the</p><p>molecular formula based on additive ions,molecular ion peaks and fragment ions.And</p><p>then peaks were matched with the mzCloud(https://www.mzcloud.org/), mzVaultand</p><p>MassListdatabase to obtain the accuratequalitative and relative quantitative</p><p>results.Statistical analyses were performed using the statistical software R (R version</p><p>R-3.4.3),Python (Python 2.7.6 version) and CentOS(CentOS release 6.6),When data</p><p>were not normally distributed, normal transformations were attempted using of area</p><p>normalization method.</p>	<p>The bacterial culture and medium were mixed, centrifuged, and the supernatant was collected and stored at -80°C.</p>

Study Identifier	MTBLS13907
Study Title	Metabolomic profiling of colon tissue in loperamide-induced constipation mice treated with Rothia mucilaginosa
Study Description	<p>Untargeted metabolomics was performed on colon tissue from 26 C57BL/6 mice to investigate metabolic mechanisms underlying Rothia mucilaginosa-mediated constipation alleviation. Mice were divided into normal control (NC, n=8), loperamide-induced constipation model control (MC, n=8), and R. mucilaginosa gavage-treated (RG, n=10) groups. The experimental protocol consisted of adaptation (days 0-7), loperamide modeling (days 8-21), and R. mucilaginosa intervention (days 22-40, BNCC363031, 2x10^8 CFU/day). Colon tissue metabolomics was performed using UHPLC-MS/MS (Vanquish UHPLC coupled with Orbitrap Exploris 120, Thermo Fisher) in both positive and negative ionization modes. This dataset supports the identification of differential metabolites and metabolic pathways associated with R. mucilaginosa intervention in constipation, with particular focus on tryptophan metabolism, bile acid metabolism, and neuroactive signaling.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Colon tissues were collected from C57BL/6 mice in the loperamide-induced constipation experiment (NC, MC, RG groups). Samples were snap-frozen and stored at -80°C until analysis. QC samples were prepared by pooling equal volumes from all experimental samples; QC was injected before, during, and after the sample sequence for system monitoring and data quality control.</p>	<p>&nbsp;UHPLC-MS/MS analyses were performed using a Vanquish UHPLC system (Thermo Fisher, Germany) coupled with an Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher, Germany) at Novogene Co., Ltd. (Beijing, China). Samples were injected onto a Hypersil Gold column (100×2.1 mm, 1.9 μm) using a 12-min linear gradient at a flow rate of 0.2 mL/min. The eluents for the positive and negative polarity modes were eluent A (0.1% FA in Water) and eluent B (Methanol). The solvent gradient was set as follows: 2% B, 1.5 min; 2-85% B, 3 min; 85-100% B, 10 min; 100-2% B, 10.1 min; 2% B, 12 min.</p>	<p>Raw data were converted to mzXML format using ProteoWizard. XCMS was used for peak extraction, alignment, retention time correction, and quantitation. Metabolites with &gt;50% missing values were filtered; remaining missing values were imputed using KNN. Background ions were eliminated based on blank samples. Raw quantitative results were normalized and metabolites with CV&gt;30% in QC samples were filtered. Relative peak areas = Raw value / (Sum of sample values / Sum of QC1 values).</p>	<p>&nbsp;Tissues (100 mg) were individually grounded with liquid nitrogen and the homogenate was resuspended with prechilled 80% methanol by vortex. The samples were incubated on ice for 5 min and centrifuged at 15,000 g, 4°C for 20 min. Supernatant was diluted to 53% methanol with LC-MS grade water, transferred to a fresh tube, and centrifuged again at 15,000 g, 4°C for 20 min. The supernatant was injected into the LC-MS/MS system (Want et al., Nature Protocols 2012).</p><p><br></p>	<p>Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher, Germany). Operated in positive/negative polarity mode with ESI source. Scan range: m/z 100-1500. MS/MS: data-dependent scans. Ion source parameters: spray voltage 3.5 kV, capillary temperature 320°C, sheath gas flow rate 35 psi, aux gas flow rate 10 L/min, S-lens RF level 60, aux gas heater temperature 350°C.</p>	<p>&nbsp;Identification was performed by matching against the NovoMetDB database (Novogene in-house high-quality secondary spectrum database) with adduct ions and mass deviation set to 10 ppm. Annotation used KEGG, HMDB, and LIPID MAPS databases.</p><p><br></p>

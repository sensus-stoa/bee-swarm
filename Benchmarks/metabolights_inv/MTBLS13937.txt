Study Title	Mechanism of Organic Plant Protection Technology in Controlling Apple Bitter Pit: Insights from Integrated Transcriptomic and Metabolomic Analyses
Study Description	<p>Apple bitter pit (ABP), a severe physiological disorder, severely impairs the quality and yield of the susceptible 'Qingcui' apple. This study investigated the control efficacy and mechanism of a three-in-one Organic Plant Protection Technology (OPPT) against ABP, with treatments set as control (CK), untreated diseased (UD), and OPPT-sprayed healthy groups. Results showed OPPT significantly reduced ABP incidence to 14.84%, improved fruit quality, enhanced antioxidant defense systems, mitigated oxidative damage, repaired fruit cell structural damage, and restored fruit calcium levels to the healthy level. Integrated transcriptomic and metabolomic analyses revealed OPPT reshaped fruit gene and metabolite profiles, with the flavonoid biosynthesis pathway as the core regulatory node, upregulating key genes (CHS, FLS, etc.) and promoting flavonoid metabolite accumulation. In conclusion, OPPT controls ABP via multi-dimensional regulation of phenotype, physiology, cell structure and molecular metabolism, providing a novel paradigm for the green control of apple physiological disorders and supporting sustainable apple industry development.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>UHPLC-MS/MS analyses were performed using a Vanquish UHPLC system (ThermoFisher, Germany) coupled with an Orbitrap Q ExactiveTM HF mass spectrometer or Orbitrap Q ExactiveTMHF-X mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher, Germany) in Novogene Co., Ltd. (Beijing, China). Samples were injected onto a Hypersil Goldcolumn (100×2.1 mm, 1.9μm) using a 12-min linear gradient at a flow rate of 0.2 mL/min. The eluents for the positive and negative polarity modes were eluent A (0.1% FA in Water) and eluent B (Methanol).The solvent gradient was set as follows: 2% B, 1.5 min; 2-85% B, 3 min; 85-100% B, 10 min; 100-2% B, 10.1 min; 2% B, 12 min.</p>	<p>The data files generated by UHPLC-MS/MS were processed by XCMS to perform peak alignment, peak picking, and quantitation for each metabolite. Then, based on adduct ions and setting mass deviation to 10 ppm, a comparison was made between these data and the high-quality secondary spectrum database to obtain results for metabolite identification. After eliminating background ions according to blank samples, the original quantitative results were normalized by the following formula to obtain relative peak areas: Relative peak areas = Raw quantitative value of samples/ (The sum of quantitative value of samples/ The sum of quantitative value of QC1); Compounds with a coefficient of variation (CV) of relative peak areas in QC samples greater than 30% were removed. Finally, the identification and relative quantitation results of metabolites were obtained. Data processing is based on the Linux operating system (CentOS version 6.6), using R and Python. For details on specific packages and software versions used, please refer to the README file included in the results.</p>	<p>Tissues (100 mg) were individually grounded with liquid nitrogen and the homogenate wasresuspended with prechilled 80% methanol by well vortex. The sampleswere incubated on ice for 5 min and then were centrifuged at 15,000 g, 4°C for 20 min. Someof supernatant was diluted to final concentration containing 53% methanol by LC-MS grade water.Thesamples were subsequently transferred to a fresh Eppendorf tube and thenwere centrifuged at 15000 g, 4°C for 20 min. Finally, the supernatant was injected into the LC-MS/MSsystem analysis.</p>	<p>Q ExactiveTM HF mass spectrometer was operated in positive/negative polarity mode with spray voltage of 3.5 kV, capillary temperature of 320°C, sheath gas flow rate of 35 psi and aux gas flow rate of 10 L/min, S-lens RF level of 60, Aux gas heater temperature of 350°C.</p>	<p>These metabolites were annotated using the KEGG database (https://www.genome.jp/kegg/pathway.html) , HMDB database(https://hmdb.ca/ metabolites)and LIPIDMaps database (http://www.lipidmaps.org/).</p>	<p>Sample differences</p><p>Significant variations exist in apple bitter pit incidence, fruit quality, ROS metabolism status, cell structural integrity, calcium content, and transcriptomic/metabolomic profiles among the three groups (CK, UD, OPPT). UD (untreated diseased group) shows the highest bitter pit incidence (≈70.99%), severe fruit quality deterioration, intense ROS accumulation and oxidative damage, disordered cell arrangement with organelle damage, significantly reduced calcium content, and abnormal gene expression and metabolite accumulation in key pathways. OPPT (OPPT-sprayed healthy group) has the incidence sharply reduced to 14.84%, recovered and even improved fruit quality (higher total flavonoids and Vc content), alleviated oxidative damage by enhancing antioxidant defense system, repaired cell structural damage with normal organelle morphology, restored calcium content to the healthy level, and reshaped fruit transcriptomic and metabolomic profiles by activating core pathways such as flavonoid biosynthesis. CK (untreated healthy control group) has no bitter pit incidence, normal fruit quality and physiological status, intact cell structure, normal calcium content, and stable gene/metabolite expression in metabolic pathways.</p><p>Controlled variables</p><p>Uniform experimental materials (5-year-old Malus domestica 'Qingcui' apple trees), consistent planting environment (State Farms Apple Demonstration Base and farmers' orchards in Haibian Village, Yongfeng Town, Zhaoyang District, Zhaotong City, Yunnan Province (103.42° E, 27.19° N) with wide-row and narrow-plant trellis high-density cultivation system (1.5 m × 4 m plant spacing × row spacing, 192 plants/hm²)), identical field cultivation management measures (fertilization, irrigation, pruning, etc.), the same spraying tool (motorized sprayer) and equal-volume spraying method, unified detection methods, instruments and kit specifications for all indicators, and consistent biological/technical replicates for each experiment. The only variables are the spraying treatment type (clean water/OPPT mixture) and the fruit health status (healthy/naturally diseased) of the apple trees.</p>

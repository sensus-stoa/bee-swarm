Study Title	Elucidating the molecular mechanisms of Daifu decoction in ulcerative colitis treatment through a multi-omics framework and experimental verification
Study Description	<p>Background: Ulcerative colitis (UC) is a significant and challenging condition in the digestive system, necessitating the development of effective therapeutic interventions. The Daifu decoction (DFD) is derived from the Lizhong decoction, a classic traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) formula used to treat digestive diseases. Previous studies have found that DFD has a clear therapeutic effect on UC. This study aimed to investigate the underlying mechanisms of DFD in UC&nbsp;treatment.</p><p>Methods:&nbsp;A mouse model of dextran sulfate sodium (DSS)-induced UC was established, and mice were treated with DFD using the concentrations 1.6 g/kg/d, 3.2 g/kg/d, and 6.4 g/kg/d for 7 days. H&amp;E and AB-PAS staining, ELISA, qPCR, and immunofluorescence were selected to assess the role of DFD in alleviating colitis and improving intestinal barrier damage. In addition, untargeted metabolomics and transcriptomics helped investigate the potential mechanism of DFD against UC, 16S rRNA sequencing evaluated the characteristics of the gut microbiota. The key target and downstream molecules were verified by molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulation, qPCR, western blotting, and immunohistochemistry.</p><p>Results: DFD prevented goblet cell loss, downregulated proinflammatory factors in serum, and improved tight junction mRNA and protein expression in colon tissue. Furthermore, DFD decreased the proportion of pathogenic microbes, while increasing the microbiota diversity and the proportion of beneficial bacterial. The integrated multi-omics analysis revealed that PRKCG was the key target of DFD to improve UC. Molecular docking and molecular dynamics simulations further confirmed that the active components of DFD could bind to PRKCG stably. The results of qPCR, western blotting, and immunohistochemistry revealed that DFD could regulate PKCγ/ERK/NF-κB pathway.</p><p>Conclusion:&nbsp;DFD can exert therapeutic effects on UC by regulating the PKCγ/ERK/NF-κB signaling pathway and the gut microbiota to restore the intestinal barrier and decrease the secretion of proinflammatory cytokines.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>C57BL/6 male mice that weighed 20 to 22 g and were 6 to 8 weeks old. After 1 week of adaptive feeding, the mice (n=10) were randomly assigned to normal control (NC), model (DSS), Daifu decoction (DFD). In addition to the NC group, the other groups were given a 2.5% DSS solution for 7 days to induce colitis. From Day 4, the mice in theDFD groups were given DFD, the other groups received distilled water of the same volume. On day 11,1% pentobarbital sodium solution helped sedate the mice by intraperitoneal injection (200 mg/kg), the colon tissue and intestinal contents were gathered and stored at -80℃.</p>	<p>All samples were for two LC/MS methods. One aliquot was analyzed using positive ion conditions and was eluted from T3 column (Waters ACQUITY Premier HSS T3 Column 1.8 µm, 2.1 mm * 100 mm) using 0.1 % formic acid in water as solvent A and 0.1 % formic acid in acetonitrile as solvent B in the following gradient: 5 to 20 % in 2 min, increased to 60 % in the following 3 mins, increased to 99 % in 1 min and held for 1.5 min, then come back to 5 % mobile phase B witnin 0.1 min, held for 2.4 min The analytical conditions were as follows, column temperature, 40 °C; flow rate, 0.4 mL/min; injection volume, 4 μL; Another aliquot was using negative ion conditions and was the same as the elution gradient of positive mode.</p>	<p>The raw data of mass spectrometry was converted into mzXML format by ProteoWizard, and the peak extraction, alignment and retention time correction were performed by XCMS program. The peaks with missing rate &gt;50% in each group were filtered, and the blank values were filled by KNN, and the peak areas were corrected by SVR method. After correcting the selected peaks, the metabolites were identified by searching the laboratory's self-built database, integrating the public library, prediction library and metDNA method. Finally, the substances with a comprehensive score of more than 0.5 and CV value of QC samples less than 0.3 were extracted and identified, and then positive and negative mode were combined (the substances with the highest qualitative grade and the lowest CV value were retained) to obtain the all_sample_data.xlsx file. R software was used for analysis.</p>	<p>The thawed sample was homogenized by a grinder (30 HZ) for 20 s. A 400 μL solution (Methanol : Water = 7:3, V/V) containing internal standard was added in to 20 mg grinded sample, and shaked at 1500 rpm for 5 min. After placing on ice for 15 min, the sample was centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 10 min (4 °C). A 300 μL of supernatant was collected and placed in -20 °C for 30 min. The sample was then centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 3 min (4 °C). A 200 μL aliquots of supernatant were transferred for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>The&nbsp;data&nbsp;acquisition&nbsp;was&nbsp;operated&nbsp;using&nbsp;the&nbsp;informationdependent&nbsp;acquisition&nbsp;(IDA)&nbsp;mode&nbsp;using&nbsp;Analyst&nbsp;TF&nbsp;1.7.1&nbsp;Software&nbsp;(Sciex,&nbsp;Concord,&nbsp;ON,&nbsp;Canada).&nbsp;The&nbsp;source&nbsp;parameters&nbsp;were&nbsp;set&nbsp;as&nbsp;follows:&nbsp;ion&nbsp;source&nbsp;gas&nbsp;1&nbsp;(GAS1),50&nbsp;psi;&nbsp;ion&nbsp;source&nbsp;gas&nbsp;2&nbsp;(GAS2),&nbsp;50&nbsp;psi;&nbsp;curtain&nbsp;gas&nbsp;(CUR),&nbsp;25&nbsp;psi;&nbsp;temperature(TEM),&nbsp;550&nbsp;°C;&nbsp;declustering&nbsp;potential&nbsp;(DP),&nbsp;60&nbsp;V,&nbsp;or−60&nbsp;V&nbsp;in&nbsp;positive&nbsp;or&nbsp;negative&nbsp;modes,&nbsp;respectively;&nbsp;and&nbsp;ion&nbsp;spray&nbsp;voltagefloating&nbsp;(ISVF),&nbsp;5000&nbsp;V&nbsp;or−4000&nbsp;V&nbsp;in&nbsp;positive&nbsp;or&nbsp;negative&nbsp;modes,&nbsp;respectively.&nbsp;The&nbsp;TOF&nbsp;MS&nbsp;scan&nbsp;parameters&nbsp;were&nbsp;set&nbsp;as&nbsp;follows:&nbsp;mass&nbsp;range,&nbsp;50–1000&nbsp;Da;&nbsp;accumulation&nbsp;time,&nbsp;200&nbsp;ms;&nbsp;and&nbsp;dynamic&nbsp;background&nbsp;subtract,&nbsp;on.&nbsp;The&nbsp;product&nbsp;ion&nbsp;scan&nbsp;parameters&nbsp;were&nbsp;set&nbsp;as&nbsp;follows:mass&nbsp;range,&nbsp;25–1000&nbsp;Da;&nbsp;accumulation&nbsp;time,&nbsp;40&nbsp;ms;&nbsp;collision&nbsp;energy,&nbsp;30&nbsp;or−30&nbsp;V&nbsp;in&nbsp;positive&nbsp;or&nbsp;negative&nbsp;modes,&nbsp;respectively;&nbsp;collision&nbsp;energy&nbsp;spread,&nbsp;15;&nbsp;resolution,&nbsp;UNIT;&nbsp;charge&nbsp;state,&nbsp;1&nbsp;to&nbsp;1;&nbsp;intensity,&nbsp;100&nbsp;cps;&nbsp;exclude&nbsp;isotopes&nbsp;within&nbsp;4&nbsp;Da;&nbsp;mass&nbsp;tolerance,&nbsp;50&nbsp;ppm;&nbsp;maximum&nbsp;number&nbsp;of&nbsp;candidate&nbsp;ions&nbsp;to&nbsp;monitor&nbsp;per&nbsp;cycle,&nbsp;18</p>	<p>After

Study Protocol Description	<p>The GC-MS analysis was performed using a Shimadzu GC 2010 gas chromatograph coupled with a Shimadzu TQ-8050 tandem mass spectrometer (Shimadzu, Kyoto, Japan). The GC analysis was performed on a Restek Rxi-5Sil column (length 30 m, id 0.25 mm, film thickness 0.25 µm) with the following settings: carrier gas helium (minimum purity 99.9995%) in constant linear velocity mode at 30 cm/sec, SSL Injector at 250 °C, septum purge at 6 mL/ min, and an injection volume of 1 µL. The GC oven temperature program for formic, acetic, propionic, isobutyric, butyric, 2-Methylbutyric, isovaleric, valeric, hexanoic acid was as follows: 50 °C for 3.27 min, ramp 20 °C/min to 162 °C, and then 40 °C/min until 290 °C (held for 3 min) with a split ratio of 1:60, whereas the GC oven temperature program for heptanoic, octanoic, lactic, nonanoic, succinic, decanoic, undecanoic and dodecanoic acid was set to the following: 100 °C for 1.00 min, ramp 15 °C/min to 197 °C, then 30 °C/min until 252 °C, then 40 °C/min until 290 °C (held for 3 min) with a split ratio of 1:3.</p>	<p>The raw data files were analyzed using GCMS Postrun analysis by Shimadzu.</p>	<p>For 250 mg of sample, 16.68 µL of a 300 ppm solution (Pyridine:Water = 3:7) of the labelled forms of the organic acids are added to correct for the recoveries, and the sample is let it stand for 20 minutes. Then, the sample is the vortexed for 20 seconds and, extracted for 15 minutes using an overhead shaker. After centrifugation, to an aliquot of 400 µL the supernatant, 20 μL of 1 N NaOH solution, 80 µL of Isobutanol, 100 µL of Pyridine, ultra-pure water to 650 µL and one boiling chip are added. At this point, the first aliquot of derivatization reagent (25 µL of Isobutyl chloroformate) is added and after 3 minutes of waiting time a second aliquot of derivatization reagent (75 µL of Isobutyl chloroformate) is added. After 10 minutes, the solution is vortexed for 5 seconds and the isobutyl esters of the organic acids are extracted with 400 µL of hexane, for 5 minutes by vortexing. This solution is then centrifuged for 2 minutes at 3000 rcf and the hexane/isobutanol upper layer is analyzed by GC-MS.</p>	<p>The GC-MS analysis was performed using a Shimadzu GC 2010 gas chromatograph coupled with a Shimadzu TQ-8050 tandem mass spectrometer (Shimadzu, Kyoto, Japan). </p><p>The mass spectrometer was operated in SIM mode with electron impact ionization at 70 eV, MS transfer line temperature at 250 °C and, MS source temperature at 200 °C.</p>	<p>Metabolites were identified by targeted analysis based on matching GC retention time to derivatized authentic standards, and detection of the expected SIM ions (m/z) for each derivatized compound.</p>	<p>Colon and ileum digesta samples were collected from piglets at day 16 and day 22 post-weaning. Immediately after collection, digesta aliquots were transferred into pre-labelled tubes, snap-frozen and stored at −80 °C until analysis to minimize metabolic activity and degradation of volatile short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs). Samples were thawed on ice prior to processing.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>For monocultures, bacteria were inoculated to an OD600 of 0.01 in Chopped Meat Broths (Solarbio, LA4610). For co-cultures, Fusobacterium and Prevotella were inoculated in an equal ratio (OD600 of 0.005 each) in base media. The cultures were incubated at 37 °C anaerobically. Six biological replicates were performed for each sample. At specific time points (6, 12, or 24 hrs), the cultures were centrifuged at 4,000 g for 10 min, and the supernatants were filter-sterilized.</p>	<p>UHPLC-MS/MS analyses were performed using a Vanquish UHPLC system (ThermoFisher, Germany) coupled with an Orbitrap Q ExactiveTM HF mass spectrometer or Orbitrap Q ExactiveTMHF-X mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher, Germany) in NuoBiotech (Guangzhou)&nbsp;Co., Ltd, China.&nbsp;</p>	<p>The data files generated by UHPLC-MS/MS were processed by XCMS to perform peak alignment, peak picking, and quantitation for each metabolite.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Samples were injected onto a Hypersil Goldcolumn (100×2.1 mm, 1.9μm) using a 12-min linear gradient at a flow rate of 0.2 mL/min. The eluents for the positive and negative polarity modes were eluent A (0.1% FA in Water) and eluent B (Methanol).</p>	<p>ExactiveTM HF mass spectrometer was operated in positive/negative polarity mode with spray voltage of 3.5 kV, capillary temperature of 320°C, sheath gas flow rate of 35 psi and aux gas flow rate of 10 L/min, S-lens RF level of 60, Aux gas heater temperature of 350°C.</p>	<p>Then, based on adduct ions and setting mass deviation to 10 ppm, a comparison was made between these data and the high-quality secondary spectrum database to obtain results for metabolite identification. After eliminating background ions according to blank samples, the original quantitative results were normalized by the following formula to obtain relative peak areas: Relative peak areas = Raw quantitative value of samples/ (The sum of quantitative value of samples/ The sum of quantitative value of QC1); Compounds with a coefficient of variation (CV) of relative peak areas in QC samples greater than 30% were removed. Finally, the identification and relative quantitation results of metabolites were obtained. Data processing is based on the Linux operating system (CentOS version 6.6), using R and Python.</p>

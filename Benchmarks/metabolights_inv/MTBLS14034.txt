Study Protocol Description	<p>After 5 days of incubation, nematodes from different treatment groups were collected.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation used helium carrier gas (1.0 mL/min) with the following temperature program: 100°C (1 min), increased to 180°C at 8°C/min, and held for 1 min.</p>	<p>After tested for the series concentrations of standard solution, the calibration curve </p><p>was constructed by using the concentration of standard solution as the abscissa, and the </p><p>ratio of peak area between it and internal standard as the ordinate. The obtained linear </p><p>regression equation of each substance was shown in Table 1 (correlation coefficient </p><p>R &gt;0.99).</p>	<p>The worms were mixed with 50 µL of 15% phosphoric acid, 100 µL of internal standard (isohexanoic acid, 125 µg/mL), and 400 µL of diethyl ether.</p>	<p>After vortexing and centrifugation, the supernatant was analyzed on a Thermo ScientificTM&nbsp;Trace 1310 GC-ISQ LT MS system (Waltham, MA, USA) equipped with Agilent DB-FFAP capillary column (30 m × 0.25 mm × 0.25 μm).</p>	<p>Quantitation was performed using external calibration curves based on the peak area ratio of each SCFA to the internal standard.</p>

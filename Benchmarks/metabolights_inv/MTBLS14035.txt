STUDY
Study Identifier	MTBLS14035
Study Title	Tumor-derived L-arginine acts as a metabolic checkpoint to sustain CAR T cell anti-tumor memory
Study Description	<p>Metabolic competition within the tumor microenvironment (TME) severely impairs the efficacy and persistence of chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T cells in solid tumors. While targeting tumor metabolism is a promising therapeutic avenue, its precise implementation remains a challenge. Here, we discover that pharmacological glutamine blockade unexpectedly reprograms tumor cell metabolism, triggering a compensatory biosynthetic pathway for L-arginine (L-Arg) via upregulation of ASS1 and suppression of ARG2. To exploit this finding therapeutically, we developed a tumor-targeted delivery platform using nanoengineered, DON-conjugated CAR T cells (DNP-CAR T). This approach locally inhibits glutaminolysis, which in turn induces ASS1-driven L-Arg biosynthesis to replenish the TCA cycle via anaplerosis, elevating intratumoral L-Arg by 2.74-fold. The enriched L-Arg pool functions as a metabolic checkpoint messenger, instructing a shift in CAR T cell metabolism toward oxidative phosphorylation (OXPHOS) and promoting a memory-like phenotype, which enhances their infiltration and cytotoxicity. Consequently, DNP-CAR T cells achieve sustained tumor control and significantly prolong survival in vivo. Our work defines L-arginine as a critical immunometabolic node and provides a translatable, combined cell therapy strategy to overcome metabolic immunosuppression in solid tumors.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The UHPLC separation was carried out using an ExionLC System, equipped with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 (100 × 2.1 mm, 1.8 μm). The mobile phase A was 0.1% formic acid and 1mM/L ammonium acetate in water, and the mobile phase B was acetonitrile. The column temperature was set at 40 ℃. The auto-sampler temperature was set at 4 ℃ and the injection volume was 1 μL.</p>	<p>Skyline Software was employed for MRM data processing.</p>	<p> An aliquot of each individual sample was precise weighed and transferred to an Eppendorf tube. After the addition of 80 μL of extract solvent (precooled at -20 oC, acetonitrile, within 0.1% FA) and 20 μL of H2O, and the samples were vortexed for 30 s, homogenized at 45 Hz for 4 min, and sonicated for 5 min in ice-water bath. The homogenate and sonicate circle was repeated for 3 times, followed by subsiding at -40 ℃ overnight and centrifuging at 12000 rpm and 4 ℃ for 15 min. An 80 µL of the supernatants were transferred to the EP tube, incubated for 30 min after adding 40 µL 100 mmol/L sodium carbonate solution and 40 µL 2% benzoyl chloride acetonitrile solution. The samples were centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 15 min at 4 ℃ after addition of 10 µL internal standard. The 40 μL of the supernatants were added in 20 µL H2O and then transferred to an auto-sampler vial for UHPLC-MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p>AB Sciex QTrap 6500+ mass spectrometer was applied for assay development. Typical ion source parameters were: IonSpray Voltage: +5000V, Curtain Gas: 35 psi, Temperature: 400 ℃, Ion Source Gas 1: 60 psi, Ion Source Gas 2: 60 psi.</p>	<p>Skyline Software was employed for MRM data processing.</p>	<p>The tumors were collected after the treatment.&nbsp;</p>

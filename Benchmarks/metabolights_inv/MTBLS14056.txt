Study Title	Non-targeted LC–MS metabolomics of human left atrial appendage tissue with high vs low myocardial micro(nano)plastic burden
Study Description	<p>Non-targeted metabolomics profiling was performed on human left atrial appendage tissue obtained from cardiac surgery patients. Samples were grouped by myocardial nanoplastic (NP) burden (High vs Low). Polar metabolites were analyzed by UHPLC–Orbitrap MS in both positive and negative ionization modes. Derived spectral data are provided as mzXML files for each sample and polarity.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Left atrial appendage was obtained as specimens before initiation of cardiopulmonary bypass and surgical ablation. Sample collection was performed by trained research staff wearing cotton surgical scrub suits and rubber surgical gloves near the operating table. The tissue was frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -80°C for downstream molecular analyses. All samples were collected following standardized surgical procedures, ensuring patients were not exposed to any additional harm.</p>	<p>Polar metabolites were separated using a Vanquish UHPLC system (Thermo Fisher Scientific) equipped with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide column (2.1 mm × 50 mm, 1.7 µm; HILIC). Mobile phase A was 25 mM ammonium acetate and 25 mM ammonia hydroxide in water (pH 9.75), and mobile phase B was acetonitrile. The autosampler temperature was maintained at 4 °C and the injection volume was 2 µL. (Gradient program and flow rate followed the service provider’s standard method.)</p>	<p>Raw LC–MS data were converted to mzXML format using ProteoWizard. Converted mzXML files were processed using an in-house R-based pipeline incorporating XCMS for peak detection, retention-time alignment, and peak integration to generate the feature intensity matrix. Features were filtered by relative standard deviation (RSD) de-noising. Missing values were imputed with half of the minimum detected value, and peak intensities were normalized using the internal standard method. The processed data matrix was used for downstream statistical analyses.</p>	<p>Approximately 25±1 mg human left atrial appendage tissue was extracted with 500 µL methanol:acetonitrile:water (2:2:1, v/v) containing deuterated internal standards. Samples were vortexed (30 s), homogenized (35 Hz, 4 min) and sonicated (4°C water bath, 5 min) for three cycles, then incubated at −40°C for 1 h and centrifuged.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry was performed on an Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific) with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source, operating in positive and negative ion modes (separate acquisitions). Data were acquired under Xcalibur software in information-dependent acquisition (IDA) mode (full-scan MS with data-dependent MS/MS). Source conditions were: sheath gas 50 Arb, auxiliary gas 15 Arb, capillary temperature 320 °C, spray voltage +3.8 kV (positive) and −3.4 kV (negative). Full-scan MS resolution was 60,000 and MS/MS resolution was 15,000, with normalized collision energy (NCE) 20/30/40.</p>	<p>Metabolite annotation was performed using an R package combined with the BiotreeDB database (version 3.0), based on accurate mass and, where available, MS/MS fragmentation matching. Annotated metabolite lists and feature tables were exported for downstream analysis and interpretation.</p>

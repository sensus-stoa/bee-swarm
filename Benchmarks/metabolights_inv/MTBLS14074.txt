Study Protocol Description	<p>Single CaCo02 and HT29_MTX cell samples were isolated using the Yokogawa SS2000 single cellome. </p>	<p>Both Caco2 and HT29-MTX cells were culture to form a a monolayer, 2-3 hours prior to single cell isolation using capillary sampling through a Yokogawa SS2000 the cell media was replaced with serum-free Dulbecco’s Modified Eagle Medium. A limited Trypsin treatment was applied to the to aid in disruption of cellular tight junction to improve single cell isolation efficiency. After isolation into capillary sampling, capillary tips were placed onto dry ice before being stored at -80 C until analysis.</p><p><br></p><p>Samples were prepared for LCMS by eluting cells from the capillary tips using 5 microlitres of 70:30 mobile phase A (60:40 ACN:H2O + 0.1% formic acid + 10 mM Ammonium Formate) and mobile phase B (85:10:5 IPA:H2O:ACN + 0.1% formic acid + 10 mM Ammonium Formate) spiked with 16ng/mL Avanti EquiPLASH lipid mix. Cells were eluted into 10 uL of 70:30 mobile phase A and B to make a final volume of 15 uL. Samples were vortexed and placed into the LC autosampler which was set to 4 C. </p>	<p>Samples were then injected onto Ultimate 3000 UHPLC (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA) attached to a Q-Exactive Plus Orbitrap mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA). The HPLC was performed using an Accucore C30 column (2.6 µm, 150 × 2.1nm, Thermo Fisher Scientific, UK), using a flow rate of 0.4 mL/min. Lipids were eluted with a LC gradient was set to start with 30% of mobile phase B (85:10:5 (v/v) isopropanol: H2O : acetonitrile with 10 mM ammonium formate and 0.1% (v/v) formic acid) which was increased to 43% of mobile phase B after 5 min. The proportion of mobile phase B was further increased to 50% at 5.1 min, and raised to 70% at 14 min; it was then increased to 99% from 21 to 24 min, before being decreased back to 30% at 24.1 min and held for 4 min.&nbsp;</p>	<p>The data was acquired in full scan in positive ionisation mode, with a heated ESI (HESI) probe at 320 °C with a spray voltage at 3 kV. The full scan automatic gain control (AGC) target was of 1×106 with the mass range of 150- 1200 m/z, and the detection resolution was set at 140,000.</p>	<p>The raw data fils were initially processed using MS-Dial (ver. 5.3.240719) using a mass tolerance of 0.01 Da as previously described 9.&nbsp;Lipid identification was performed using LipidBlast database with a mass tolerance of 0.01 Da for MS1 and 0.025 Da for MS2, with an 80% identification score cutoff with [M + H]+, [M + NH4]+, and [M + H − H2O]+ as allowable adducts. The statistical analysis such as PCA and heatmap was performed through MetaboAnalyst 6.022. Lipid metabolic pathway analysis was carried out using BioPAN (www.lipidmaps.org/biopan). The Z-scores were calculated to assess regulation of the reaction pathway by lipid subclass. Statistical significance was taken &lt; 0.05, correspond to Z &gt; 1.645.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Lipid identifications were achieved using a in-house lipid library constructed using MS2 lipid data from PANC-1 cells in conjunction with the internal MS-Dial (ver. 5.3.240719) lipid library.</p>

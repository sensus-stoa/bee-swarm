Study Title	Study on root metabolism of cassava TRV-MeCOMT8
Study Description	<p>This project is based on non targeted metabolomics analysis of cassava (Manihot esculenta) root samples. LC-MS/MS is used to obtain metabolite characteristic peaks and perform peak extraction, alignment, and quantification. Differential metabolites are screened, with a focus on flavonoid and phenylpropane related metabolic changes. To improve data repeatability and reusability, we upload raw mass spectrometry data and processed feature peak tables/metabolite annotation results, and provide sample grouping information and analysis process instructions for subsequent researchers to conduct reanalysis, comparison, and integration studies.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Chromatographic separation was conducted on an UltiMate 3000 UHPLC system (Thermo Fisher Scientific) equipped with an UltiMate 3000 autosampler. Separation was achieved using an ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide analytical column (100 mm × 2.1 mm, 1.7 μm) coupled with a matched ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide guard column (5 mm × 2.1 mm, 1.7 μm), following a hydrophilic interaction liquid chromatography (HILIC) gradient elution program optimized for broad-spectrum plant metabolite profiling. The system was operated with a controlled column temperature and low-temperature autosampler to maintain metabolite stability during analysis.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were processed using commercial metabolomics data analysis software (Thermo Scientific Compound Discoverer 3.3 or XCMS Online) for peak alignment, peak picking, noise reduction, and normalization to obtain high-quality metabolite peak matrices. </p>	<p>Non-targeted metabolite extraction using 80% methanol-water (v/v) extraction solution (mainstream for plant tissue metabolomics); frozen root samples were ground to powder in liquid nitrogen, extracted via ultrasonic-assisted extraction at low temperature (4℃), centrifuged to obtain supernatant, filtered through 0.22 μm hydrophilic PTFE membrane for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was performed on a Q Exactive Orbitrap MS/MS platform (Thermo Fisher Scientific) equipped with a Heated Electrospray Ionization (HESI-II) source, operating in alternating positive and negative ion modes for comprehensive non-targeted metabolite profiling. The mass analyzer was a high-resolution Quadrupole-Orbitrap hybrid system, with a full-scan m/z range set to 70 - 1050 to cover most primary and secondary metabolites in cassava root tissues. High-resolution scanning mode was adopted to ensure accurate mass measurement and reliable metabolite structure inference.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was carried out by matching accurate mass-to-charge ratio (m/z), retention time, and MS/MS fragmentation spectra against mainstream public metabolomics databases including HMDB (Human Metabolome Database), Metlin, and a plant-specific metabolite database, combined with accurate mass deviation (≤5 ppm) and MS/MS spectral similarity scoring. Only metabolites with reliable database matching and high spectral similarity were annotated and subjected to subsequent statistical and biological function analysis.</p>	<p>Organism: Manihot esculenta Crantz (Cassava)</p><p>Sample Type: Root tissue samples</p><p>Experimental Treatment: Roots collected from cassava plants with MeCOMT8 gene silencing, for non-targeted metabolic profiling analysis</p>

Study Identifier	MTBLS14112
Study Title	Untargeted metabolomics profiling of esophageal tissues from a rat model of reflux esophagitis treated with Shunwei Jiangni Decoction
Study Description	<p>This dataset contains raw untargeted metabolomics data generated from esophageal tissue samples of rats in a reflux esophagitis (RE) model. The study aimed to characterize metabolic alterations associated with RE pathogenesis and to evaluate the regulatory effects of Shunwei Jiangni Decoction (SWJN), a modified traditional Chinese medicine formula derived from Banxia Xiexin Decoction. Male Wistar rats were divided into four groups: sham control (Control), RE model (Mod), SWJN low-dose (SWJN_L), and SWJN high-dose (SWJN_H). After 4 weeks of treatment, esophageal tissues were collected and subjected to untargeted metabolomics analysis using ultra-high performance liquid chromatography coupled with quadrupole time-of-flight mass spectrometry (UPLC-Q/TOF-MS) in both positive and negative ion modes. Metabolite features were extracted and annotated using the XCMS package and public databases (HMDB, Metlin, and self-built libraries).&nbsp;</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Organism: Rattus norvegicus (SD rat)</p><p>Sample type: Esophageal tissue</p><p>Groups: Control, Mod, SWJN_L, SWJN_H, Quality Control (QC)</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed on a Thermo Scientific Vanquish UHPLC system equipped with a Vanquish autosampler, using a Waters ACQUITY Premier HSS T3 analytical column (1.8 μm, 2.1 mm × 100 mm) protected by a matching guard column. The column temperature was maintained at 40 °C, with a flow rate of 0.4 mL/min and injection volume of 3 μL. A gradient elution program was applied using 0.1% formic acid in water (mobile phase A) and 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile (mobile phase B), optimized for non-targeted metabolomic profiling of rat esophageal tissue.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were converted to mzML format using ProteoWizard, then processed with XCMS for peak detection, alignment, and retention time correction. Missing values were filtered and imputed (KNN for &lt;50% blank values, 1/5 minimum for &gt;50% blank values), and peak areas were corrected using SVR. QC samples were used to monitor data stability, with only features showing CV &lt;0.3 in QC samples retained.</p>	<p>Frozen esophageal tissue samples were thawed on ice, then minced and homogenized. A 20 mg (±1 mg) aliquot was extracted with 400 μL of 70% methanol (containing internal standards) using bead milling (30 Hz, 20 s), followed by vortexing and centrifugation steps. The final supernatant was filtered and prepared for UHPLC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was carried out using a Thermo Scientific Q Exactive HF-X Orbitrap mass spectrometer coupled to the Vanquish UHPLC system, equipped with an Electrospray Ionization (ESI) source operating in both positive and negative ion modes. The quadrupole-Orbitrap hybrid mass analyzer was set to scan over an m/z range of 70–1000 Da for both full MS and data-dependent MS2 acquisition, with optimized resolution and collision energy settings to ensure high-resolution accurate mass measurement and comprehensive metabolite fragmentation analysis.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was performed by matching accurate mass-to-charge ratio (m/z), retention time, and MS/MS fragmentation spectra against an in-house database, public databases, and predictive libraries, with a combined score ≥0.5. Isotopes and adducts were annotated, and the final dataset was merged across positive and negative ion modes (retaining the highest confidence annotation and lowest CV value) for downstream statistical analysis.</p>

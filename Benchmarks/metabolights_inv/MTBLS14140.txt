Study Protocol Description	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed on a Vanquish UHPLC system (Thermo Fisher, Germany) equipped with a Hypersil Gold C18 column (100 × 2.1 mm, 1.9 μm, Thermo Fisher, USA). The column temperature was maintained at 40°C, and the flow rate was set to 0.2 mL/min. Mobile phase A consisted of 0.1% formic acid in water, and mobile phase B was methanol. The gradient elution program was as follows: 0 min, 98% A; 1.5 min, 98% A; 3 min, 15% A; 10 min, 0% A; 10.1 min, 98% A; 12 min, 98% A.</p>	<p>Raw data files were processed using Compound Discoverer 3.3 software. Peak alignment, extraction, and integration were performed with a mass tolerance of 5 ppm and signal intensity tolerance of 30%. Peak areas were normalized using the first QC sample. Background ions were removed using blank samples. Data were further normalized using the formula: raw value / (total metabolite sum in sample / total metabolite sum in QC1). Metabolites with relative peak area CV &gt; 30% in QC samples were excluded.</p>	<p>For tissue samples: 100 mg of tissue was homogenized in liquid nitrogen and mixed with 500 µL of 80% methanol in water. After vortexing and ice-bath incubation for 5 min, samples were centrifuged at 15,000 g for 20 min at 4°C. The supernatant was diluted with LC-MS grade water to achieve a final methanol concentration of 53%, followed by another centrifugation step (15,000 g, 4°C, 20 min). The final supernatant was used for LC-MS analysis. QC samples were prepared by pooling equal volumes of all experimental samples. Blank samples (53% methanol in water) were processed identically to experimental samples.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry was performed using a Q Exactive HF or Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher, Germany) coupled with a Vanquish UHPLC system. Electrospray ionization (ESI) was used in both positive and negative ion modes. The scan range was m/z 100–1500. ESI source parameters were as follows: spray voltage, 3.5 kV; sheath gas flow rate, 35 psi; auxiliary gas flow rate, 10 L/min; capillary temperature, 320°C; S-lens RF level, 60; auxiliary gas heater temperature, 350°C. MS/MS acquisition was performed using data-dependent scans.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was performed by matching molecular ions and fragmentation patterns against the mzCloud (https://www.mzcloud.org/), mzVault, and Masslist databases. Retention time, m/z, and adduct information were considered. Background ions were removed using blank samples. The identification process was carried out using Compound Discoverer 3.3 software. Annotations were further supported by KEGG, HMDB, and LIPIDMaps databases.</p>	<p>Biological samples were collected and stored at -80°C until analysis. Samples were shipped on dry ice to the testing facility. All samples were kept at -80°C prior to extraction to ensure metabolic stability.</p>

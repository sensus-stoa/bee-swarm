Study Title	Metabolic and gut microbial remodeling in captive Amur tigers under uniform feeding
Study Description	<p _msttexthash='64160037318' _msthash='272'>Ultra-high-performance liquid chromatography (UHPLC) was conducted using a Vanquish UHPLC system (Thermo Fisher), coupled with a Q Exactive™ HF/Q Exactive™ HF-X mass spectrometer for liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry (LC-MS) analysis.&nbsp;To ensure high resolution and effective analyte retention, a Hypesil Gold chromatographic column (100 ×&nbsp;2.1 mm, 1.9 µm) was used to separate the samples by hydrophilic interaction chromatography (HILIC). In both positive and negative electrospray ionization (ESI) modes, the chromatographic parameters were as follows: flow rate: 0.2 mL/min, column temperature: 40℃, mobile phase A = 0.1% formic acid, B = methanol. The separated metabolites were then analyzed via mass spectrometry, with a scanning range of m/z&nbsp;100–1500. The MS/MS secondary scan was performed using a data-dependent acquisition mode,&nbsp;and QC samples were used to correct instrument drift and signal fluctuation.&nbsp;Subsequently, the raw LC-MS data files were processed using Compound Discoverer 3.3 (CD3.3; Thermo Fisher Scientific)&nbsp;(Cooper and Yang, 2024).&nbsp;Initially, each metabolite was screened, and the peak area was normalized using the first QC sample. In the subsequent steps, high-resolution molecular ion peaks and characteristic fragment ions were used to predict molecular formulas, and compared with the mzCloud, mzVault, and MassList databases to achieve accurate metabolite identification and relative quantification.</p>

All the individuals involved in the study mainly consumed chicken, beef, and pork in their diet. According to age differences, we divided the subjects into three developmental stages: subadults (2-3 years old, N = 10), adults (4-9 years old, N = 10), and old&nbsp;individuals (&gt; 10 years old, N = 10) (refer to Zhu et al., 2021; Zhao et al, 2025). The collected fecal samples were transferred to an ultra-low temperature refrigerator at&nbsp;-80℃&nbsp;for unified storage for subsequent non-targeted metabolomics and 16S rRNA sequencing analysis.</p>

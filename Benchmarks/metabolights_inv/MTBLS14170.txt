Study Protocol Description	<p>Freshly isolated mitochondria were resuspended in 500 μL of incubation buffer, comprising 0.25 M sucrose, 50 mM Tris-HCl (pH 7.5), 25 mM KCl, 5 mM MgCl2, 2 mM DTT, aprotinin (1 μg/mL), leupeptin (0.5 μg/mL), 1 mM adenosine 5′-triphosphate (ATP), 1 mM adenosine 5′-diphosphate (ADP), 1 mM flavin adenine dinucleotide (FAD), 1 mM nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+), and one of the following substrates at a final concentration of 10 mM,&nbsp;13C6&nbsp;glucose or 1,2-13C2&nbsp;glucose.</p>	<p>In this experiment, we employed the Waters UPLC/MS/MS system (ACQUITY-I UPLC/Xevo TQS) from the United States for metabolic flux detection. The instrument parameters and method settings are listed in the table below. System optimization and maintenance were performed every 48 hours.</p>	<p>After data collection by analytical instruments, project data is immediately transferred to the internal data storage server, which is accessible only to authorized Metabo-Profile personnel. Without client consent, we pledge not to disclose project details to third parties. Backups of data stored on Metabo-Profile servers are retained for up to two years, after which the data will be automatically deleted. For clients requiring extended storage solutions, Metabo-Profile may provide appropriate extensions based on assessment.</p>	<p>Prior to sample processing, samples should be thawed on an ice bath to minimize degradation. Add 400 μL of 80% methanol solution to each pipette of mitochondrial samples, followed by ultrasonic cell disruption (JY92-IIN, NingBo Scientz Biotechnology Co. Ltd.). Centrifuge at 18,000 g for 15 minutes at 4°C (Microfuge 20R, Beckman Coulter Inc., Indianapolis, IN, USA). Collect all supernatants, centrifuge to concentrate (Labconco, Kansas City, MO, USA), and resuspend with 100 μL of 80% methanol solution before injection for analysis.</p>	<p>In this experiment, we employed the Waters UPLC/MS/MS system (ACQUITY-I UPLC/Xevo TQS) from the United States for metabolic flux detection. The instrument parameters and method settings are listed in the table below. System optimization and maintenance were performed every 48 hours.</p>	<p>The raw data generated by UPLC-TQMS were processed using Waters MassLynx software (v4.1, Waters, Milford, MA, USA) for peak extraction, integration, identification, and quantitative analysis of each metabolite. Free open-source R language (v4.1.1) was employed for subsequent statistical analysis.</p>

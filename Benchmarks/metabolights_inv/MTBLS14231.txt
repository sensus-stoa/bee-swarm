Study Protocol Description	<p>All the hybrid groupers were siblings bred under identical conditions by Fujian Yiyuan Aquatic Technology Co., Ltd., located in Zhangzhou City, Fujian Province, with an average length of 32.8 ± 2.2 cm. These individuals were transported to the laboratory and maintained under identical conditions. Skin from the same anatomical position in different groups were excised and stored at -80°C for subsequent omics-level analysis. All samples were collected from anesthetized animals using MS-222 (tricaine methanesulfonate).</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Vanquish ultra-high-performance liquid chromatography (UHPLC) system (Thermo Fisher Scientific) equipped with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide column (2.1 mm × 50 mm, 1.7 μm). The mobile phase consisted of solvent A, which was water containing 25 mmol/L ammonium acetate and 25 mmol/L ammonium hydroxide, and solvent B, which was acetonitrile. The autosampler temperature was maintained at 4 °C, and the injection volume was 2 μL.</p>	<p>The raw data were converted to the mzXML format using ProteoWizard and processed with an in-house program, which was developed using R and based on XCMS, for peak detection, extraction, alignment, and integration. </p>	<p> Epidermis tissue samples (25 mg each) from N, W, and Y fish (6 replicates/group)&nbsp;were homogenized in 500 μL of MeOH:ACN:H2O (2:2:1, v/v) at 35 Hz for 4 min. The homogenate was incubated at -40°C for 1 h, centrifuged at 12,000 rpm (4°C, 15 min), and 400 μL of supernatant was collected for LC-MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was carried out on an Orbitrap Exploris 120 high-resolution mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific) operated under the control of Xcalibur software (version 4.4). Data acquisition was performed in both positive and negative electrospray ionization modes, with full MS scans acquired at a resolution of 60,000 and MS/MS scans at a resolution of 15,000. Data-dependent acquisition was employed, using stepped normalized collision energies of 20, 30, and 40. The ion source conditions included a sheath gas flow rate of 50 Arb, an auxiliary gas flow rate of 15 Arb, a capillary temperature of 320 °C, and spray voltages set to 3.8 kV in positive ion mode and −3.4 kV in negative ion mode.</p>	<p>An in-house MS2 database (BiotreeDB) was applied in metabolite annotation. The cutoff for annotation was set at 0.3.</p>

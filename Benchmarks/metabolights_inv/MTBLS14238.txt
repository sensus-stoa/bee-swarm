Study Identifier	MTBLS14238
Study Title	Non-targeted metabolomics analysis of synovial fluid from TMD patients
Study Description	This study performed non-targeted metabolomics detection on 62 human joint cavity lavage fluid samples using UHPLC-QE-MS. Metabolites were extracted with methanol:acetonitrile (1:1), separated on a Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide column, and detected by Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer in both positive and negative ion modes. Data were processed using XCMS-based in-house R package and annotated against BiotreeDB (V3.0).

Study Protocol Description	<p>During Arthrography, 1 mL of sterile saline was injected into the superior joint compartment. Patients performed repeated opening and closing movements to mix the saline with synovial fluid. The mixture was aspirated and re-injected 3–5 times before final collection. Samples were centrifuged at 3,000 rpm for 5 minutes at 4 °C. The supernatant was aliquoted, snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen for 15 minutes, and stored at −80°C.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed on a Vanquish UHPLC system (Thermo Fisher Scientific) equipped with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide column (2.1 mm × 50 mm, 1.7 μm particle size). The column temperature was maintained at 40°C. Mobile phase A consisted of 25 mmol/L ammonium acetate and 25 mmol/L ammonia hydroxide in water (pH = 9.75). Mobile phase B was acetonitrile. The flow rate was set to 0.3 mL/min (typical value for this column; exact flow rate as per standard protocol). The injection volume was 2 μL, and the autosampler temperature was kept at 4°C. The gradient program was as follows: 0–2 min, 95% B; 2–11 min, 95–65% B; 11–13 min, 65–40% B; 13–15 min, 40% B; 15–15.1 min, 40–95% B; 15.1–18 min, re-equilibration at 95% B. Total run time was 18 min.</p>	<p>Raw mass spectrometry data (.raw files) were converted to mzXML format using ProteoWizard (version 3.0). Peak detection, alignment, and integration were performed using an in-house R package based on XCMS. The resulting data matrix contained peak intensities (area) for all features across all samples. No further normalization was applied at this stage.</p>	<p>Joint cavity lavage fluid samples (62 experimental samples) were collected and stored at -80°C until processing. For metabolite extraction, 100 μL of each sample was mixed with 400 μL of extraction solution (methanol:acetonitrile = 1:1, v/v) containing deuterated internal standards. The mixture was vortexed for 30 s, sonicated for 10 min in an ice-water bath, and then incubated at -40°C for 1 h to precipitate proteins. After centrifugation at 12,000 rpm (13,800 × g) for 15 min at 4°C, 400 μL of supernatant was transferred to a new tube and dried under vacuum. The dried residue was reconstituted in 100 μL of reconstitution solution (methanol:acetonitrile:water = 2:2:1, v/v/v) containing internal standards, sonicated for 10 min, and centrifuged. The final supernatant was transferred to an autosampler vial for LC-MS analysis. A pooled quality control (QC) sample was prepared by mixing equal aliquots of all experimental sample supernatants. Solvent blanks were also prepared and analyzed throughout the run to monitor carryover.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry was performed using an Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific) equipped with a heated electrospray ion source (HESI). Data were acquired in both positive and negative ion modes in separate runs. For positive ion mode, the spray voltage was set to 3.8 kV; for negative ion mode, spray voltage was set to -3.4 kV. Sheath gas flow rate was 50 Arb, auxiliary gas flow rate was 15 Arb, and capillary temperature was 320°C. Full MS scans were acquired at a resolution of 60,000 (FWHM) over an m/z range of 100–1500. Data-dependent MS/MS scans were performed at a resolution of 15,000 with stepped normalized collision energy (SNCE) of 20/30/40. The acquisition software was Xcalibur (version 4.4, Thermo Fisher Scientific).</p>	<p>Metabolite annotation was performed using the BiotreeDB (V3.0) in-house database, which contains over 20,000 metabolite standards with MS1, MS2, and retention time information. The identification pipeline was implemented in R. Metabolites were matched based on precursor m/z (mass tolerance 15 ppm), retention time (tolerance 0.2 min), and MS/MS fragmentation patterns. Annotation confidence levels followed the Metabolomics Standards Initiative (MSI): Level 1 (authentic standard matching), Level 2 (public library matching), Level 3 (putative annotation), and Level 4 (unknown). The majority of reported metabolites were annotated at MSI Level 2 or higher.</p>

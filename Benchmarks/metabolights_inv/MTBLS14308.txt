Study Protocol Description	<p>For gut microbiota and metabolite analysis, fresh fecal samples were collected immediately after defecation, snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen within 30 seconds, and stored at -80°C until further processing.&nbsp;</p>	<p>All samples were for two LC/MS methods. One aliquot was analyzed using positive ion conditions and was eluted from T3 column (Waters ACQUITY Premier HSS T3 Column 1.8 µm, 2.1 mm * 100 mm) using 0.1 % formic acid in water as solvent A and 0.1 % formic acid in acetonitrile as solvent B in the following gradient: 5 to 20 % in 2 min, increased to 60 % in the following 3 mins, increased to 99 % in 1 min and held for 1.5 min, then come back to 5 % mobile phase B witnin 0.1 min, held for 2.4 min. The analytical conditions were as follows, column temperature, 40 °C; flow rate, 0.4 mL/min; injection volume, 4 μL; Another aliquot was using negative ion conditions and was the same as the elution gradient of positive mode.</p>	<p>The raw mass spectrometry data were converted to mzML format using ProteoWizard, followed by peak detection, alignment, and retention time correction using the XCMS program. Peaks with &gt;50% missing values across any group were filtered out. Missing values were imputed using a hybrid approach: KNN imputation combined with 1/5 of the minimum value (if missing rate ≤50%, KNN imputation was applied; if missing rate &gt;50%, imputation with 1/5 of the minimum value was used). Peak areas were further normalized using a support vector regression (SVR)-based method.&nbsp;</p>	<p>The sample stored at -80 °C refrigerator was thawed on ice. A 400 μL solution (Methanol : Water = 7:3, V/V) containing internal standard was added into 20 mg sample, and vortexed for 3 min. The sample was sonicated in an ice bath for 10 min and vortexed for 1 min, and then placed in -20 °C for 30 min. The sample was then centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 10 min (4 °C). And the sediment was removed, then centrifuged the supernatant at 12000 rpm for 3 min (4 °C). A 200 μL aliquots of supernatant were transferred for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>All the methods alternated between full scan MS and data dependent MSn scans using dynamic exclusion. MS analyses were carried out using electrospray ionization in the positive ion mode and negative ion mode using full scan analysis over m/z 75-1000 at 35000 resolution. Additional MS settings are: ion spray voltage, 3.5 KV or 3.2 KV in positive or negative modes, respevtively; Sheath gas (Arb), 30; Aux gas, 5; Ion transfer tube temperature, 320 °C; Vaporizer temperature, 300 °C; Collision energy, 30,40,50 V; Signal Intensity Threshold, 1*e6 cps; Top N vs Top speed, 10; Exclusion duration, 3s.</p>	<p>The filtered and normalized peaks were annotated by searching against an in-house laboratory database, integrating public databases, a prediction library, and the metDNA method. Only metabolites with a comprehensive identification score ≥0.5 and a coefficient of variation (CV) in QC samples &lt;0.3 were retained. Finally, positive and negative mode data were merged (keeping the metabolite with the highest confidence level and smallest CV), resulting in the file 'all_sample_data.xlsx'.</p>

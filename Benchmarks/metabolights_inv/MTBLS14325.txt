Study Protocol Description	<p>Lamb serum collected at Tianjin Agricultural University</p>	<p>An ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (100 A, 1.8 µm, 2.1 mm x 100 mm) was used at a flow rate of 0.4 mL/min, a column temperature of 40 °C, an autosampler temperature of 8 °C, and an injection volume of 2 μL. The mobile phases for both positive and negative ion modes were as follows: mobile phase A was 0.1% formic acid in water, and mobile phase B was acetonitrile containing 0.1% formic acid.</p>	<p>The acquired .raw data were imported into the MS-DIAL software (version 4.9.221218). Peak extraction, alignment, filtering, and metabolite identification were performed using this software. Peaks not detected in more than 50% of the QC samples were filtered out. For the remaining missing peaks, missing values were filled using the software’s gap-filling algorithm, followed by normalization.</p>	<p>A volume of 50 μL of serum sample was transferred to a 2 mL centrifuge tube, and 200 μL of pre-chilled methanol/acetonitrile solution (1:1, v/v) was added. After thorough mixing, the mixture was stored at 20°C for 30 minutes. Subsequently, centrifugation was performed at 12,000 rpm and 4°C for 10 minutes. A 200 μL aliquot of the supernatant was collected, mixed with 150 μL of 50% methanol for reconstitution, and centrifuged again under the same conditions. The resulting supernatant was filtered through a 0.22 μm membrane, and the filtrate was transferred to an injection vial for analysis. To evaluate instrument stability and data reliability, a quality control (QC) sample was prepared by pooling 20 μL of filtrate from each individual sample.</p>	<p>The Thermo Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer, controlled by Xcalibur software (version 4.7, Thermo Fisher Scientific), was used for DDA (data-dependent acquisition) mass spectrometry data acquisition in positive and negative ion modes, respectively. A HESI (heated electrospray ionization) source was employed with spray voltages of +3.5 kV and –3.0 kV, sheath gas at 40 arb, auxiliary gas at 10 arb, capillary temperature at 320 °C, and auxiliary gas temperature at 300 °C. The MS1 resolution was set to 60,000, with a scan range of 70–1000 m/z, AGC target set to Standard, and maximum injection time (Max IT) of 100 ms. The top 4 most intense precursor ions were selected for MS/MS fragmentation with a dynamic exclusion time of 4 s. The MS2 resolution was 15,000, HCD collision energy was set to 30%, AGC target was Standard, and Max IT was set to Auto.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was performed based on the Personalbio PSNGM (PerSonalbio Next-Generation Metabolomics Database), which includes an in-house built database of standards, the mzCloud library (https://www.mzcloud.org/), LIPID MAPS (https://www.lipidmaps.org/), HMDB (https://hmdb.ca/), MoNA (https://mona.fiehnlab.ucdavis.edu/), NIST_2020_MSMS, and an AI-predicted MS/MS spectral library. The main parameters for library searching were as follows: MS1 tolerance for identification 0.01, MS2 tolerance for identification 0.05, Smoothing level 3, Minimum peak height 10000, Minimum peak width 5, Mass slice width 0.05, Identification score cut-off 70.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Semen was collected from healthy 35-week-old Gushi roosters by abdominal massage. Qualified ejaculates with motility&nbsp;0.7, normal appearance, and no fecal or urinary contamination were pooled, diluted 1:1 with pre-warmed extender, aliquoted, and stored at 4 °C in the dark. Seminal plasma was collected at 0 h, 24 h, 72 h, and 120 h by centrifugation at 2,000 × g for 5 min at 4 °C, followed by centrifugation at 12,000 × g for 20 min at 4 °C twice. Samples were checked microscopically to confirm the absence of spermatozoa, frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at -80 °C until metabolomic analysis.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed on an Agilent 1290 Infinity II UHPLC system using a HILIC column. The mobile phase consisted of 25 mM ammonium acetate/ammonium hydroxide in water as solvent A and acetonitrile as solvent B. Samples were analyzed using gradient elution under both positive and negative ionization modes. QC samples were injected periodically to monitor chromatographic reproducibility and analytical stability.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS/MS data were converted using ProteoWizard and processed using XCMS Online for peak detection, retention time correction, peak alignment, and peak integration. Background signals and features with high missing values were filtered, and peak intensities were normalized before statistical analysis. Multivariate analyses were performed using SIMCA-P 16.1. Differential metabolites were selected using VIP &gt; 1, P &lt; 0.05, and fold change&nbsp;2 or&nbsp;0.5.</p>	<p>Seminal plasma samples were extracted with pre-cooled methanol/acetonitrile/water (2:2:1, v/v/v). Briefly, 100 μL of seminal plasma was mixed with 400 μL of extraction solvent, vortexed for 30 s, sonicated in an ice-water bath for 30 min for two cycles, and incubated at -20 °C for 1 h for protein precipitation. Samples were centrifuged at 13,000 × g for 15 min at 4 °C, and the supernatants were dried under vacuum. Dried extracts were reconstituted in 100 μL acetonitrile/water (1:1, v/v) before LC-MS/MS analysis. Pooled QC samples and solvent blanks were included.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric analysis was performed on an AB Sciex TripleTOF 6600 mass spectrometer coupled to an Agilent 1290 Infinity II UHPLC system. Data were acquired in both positive and negative electrospray ionization modes. Full-scan MS and MS/MS spectra were collected for untargeted metabolomic profiling. Ion source and acquisition parameters were optimized for the analytical method, and QC samples were analyzed throughout the run to monitor signal stability and mass accuracy.</p>	<p>Metabolites were putatively annotated by matching accurate mass, adduct information, retention time, and MS/MS fragmentation spectra against public and in-house databases, including HMDB, KEGG, LipidMaps, METLIN, and available MS/MS spectral libraries. Unless confirmed using authentic standards, metabolite identities were considered putative annotations. KEGG was used for pathway annotation and enrichment analysis.</p>

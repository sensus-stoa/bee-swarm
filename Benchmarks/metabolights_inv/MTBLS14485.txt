Study Protocol Description	<p>Fresh human feces were diluted 1:100 in YCFA medium containing 10 mg/mL of fucosyllactose or lactose, or without carbohydrate (control). Anaerobic fermentation was carried out at 37 °C for 60 h. Samples were collected at multiple time points, centrifuged, and the supernatants stored at -80 °C until analysis.</p>	<p>The 10 μL of the samples were injected into the SCIEX ExionLC AD System (AB SCIEX, USA), which was fitted with an HSS T3 column (1.8 μm, 100 mm × 2.1 mm; Waters, Milford, USA). The flow rate was 0.4 mL/min, and the column temperature was maintained at 40 °C. Mobile phase A consisted of a water solution containing 0.1 % (v/v) formic acid, while mobile phase B was a mixture of acetonitrile and isopropanol (1:1, v/v) which containing 0.1 % (v/v) formic acid. The solvent gradient was adjusted as follows: 95 % A (0 min), 80 % A (3 min), 5 % A (9 min), 5 % A (13.0 min), 95 % A (13.1 min), and 95 % A (16 min).</p>	<p>XCMS online (https://xcmsonline.scripps.edu/) and MetaboAnalyst 5.0 (https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/) were used for data processing and bioinformatic analyses. Volcanic plot and partial least squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA) were conducted to profile metabolic changes. Differential metabolites were identified based on the fold change (FC) method, PLS-DA method, and t-test methods.</p>	<p>Metabolites in F-2'-FL and F-Non-O were extracted using a methanol/water (4:1, v/v) solution and vortexed for 1 min, followed by vortexing for 1 min and protein precipitation at −20°C for 1 h. After centrifugation (13,000 × g, 4°C, 15 min), the supernatant was filtered through a 0.22 μm membrane filter. A quality control (QC) sample was made by combining equal volumes (10 μL) of each fermentation sample and was injected repeatedly every six samples throughout the analysis sequence.</p>	<p>A high-resolution tandem mass spectrometer TripleTOF 5600+ (SCIEX, Framingham, MA, USA) was used to detect metabolites eluted form the column. The Q-TOF was operated in both positive and negative ion modes. The curtain gas was set 30 PSI, Ion source gas1 was set 60 PSI, Ion source gas2 was set 60 PSI, and an interface heater temperature was 650 ℃.For positive ion mode, the Ionspray voltage floating were set at 5000 V, respectively. For negative ion mode, the Ionspray voltage floating were set at -4500V, respectively. The mass spectrometry data were acquired in IDA mode. The TOF mass range was from 60 to 1200 Da. The survey scans were acquired in 150 ms and as many as 12 product ion scans were collected if exceeding a threshold of 100 counts per second (counts/s) and with a 1+ charge-state. Total cycle time was fixed to 0.56 s.Four time bins were summed for each scan at a pulser frequency value of 11 kHz through monitoring of the 40 GHz multichannel TDC detector with four-anode/channel detection. Dynamic exclusion was set for 4 s.During the acquisition, the mass accuracy was calibrated every 20 samples. Furthermore, in order to evaluate the stability of the LC-MS during the whole acquisition, a quality control sample (Pool of all samples) was acquired after every 10 samples.</p>	<p> Volcanic plot, principal components analysis (PCA), and Projections to Latent Structures Discriminant Analysis (PLS-DA) was conducted to profile metabolic changes between two groups. Two supervised models were validated using R² (goodness-of-fit) and Q² (predictive ability) scores, along with permutation test to confirm the reliability and avoid overfitting. Differential metabolites were selected based on VIP value &gt; 1 and P &lt; 0.05. Identified metabolites were annotated and subsequently mapped to KEGG database (http://www.genome.jp/kegg/) for pathway enrichment analysis.</p>

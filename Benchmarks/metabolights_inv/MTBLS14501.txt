Study Protocol Description	<p>Each strain was grown separately in liquid TSB at 27 ?C with shaking for 3 d, with six independent biological replicates per strain. Culture purity was checked by streaking an aliquot from each replicate onto TSA plates. Only uncontaminated replicates were used for metabolite extraction. Cultures were centrifuged at 4,000 ? g for 15 min, and the resulting cell-free supernatants were subjected to organic extraction for LC-MS/MS analysis.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was carried out on an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (100 mm ? 2.1 mm i.d., 1.8 ?m; Waters, Milford, MA, USA). Mobile phase A consisted of water/acetonitrile (95:5, v/v) containing 0.1% formic acid, and mobile phase B consisted of acetonitrile/isopropanol/water (47.5:47.5:5, v/v/v) containing 0.1% formic acid. The injection volume was 3 ?L, and the column temperature was maintained at 40?C.</p>	<p>The LC??S raw data were preprocessed using Progenesis QI software, and a three-dimensional data matrix was exported in CSV format. The matrix contained sample information, metabolite names, and mass spectral response intensities. Internal standard peaks and known false-positive peaks, including noise peaks, column bleed peaks, and derivatization reagent-related peaks, were removed from the data matrix. Subsequently, peak de-redundancy and peak merging were performed to obtain the final processed dataset.</p>	<p>Precisely, 200 ?L of each sample was transferred into a 1.5 mL centrifuge tube. Then, 800 ?L of extraction solvent consisting of methanol/acetonitrile (1:1, v/v) was added. The extraction solvent contained four internal standards, including L-2-chlorophenylalanine at a concentration of 0.02 mg/mL. The mixture was vortexed for 30 s and then subjected to low-temperature ultrasonic extraction for 30 min at 5?C and 40 kHz. Subsequently, the samples were incubated at ??0?C for 30 min and centrifuged at 13,000 ? g for 15 min at 4?C. The supernatant was collected and dried under a gentle stream of nitrogen.</p><p>The dried residue was reconstituted in 120 ?L of acetonitrile/water (1:1, v/v). After vortexing for 30 s, the samples were further subjected to low-temperature ultrasonic extraction for 5 min at 5?C and 40 kHz. The samples were then centrifuged at 13,000 ? g for 10 min at 4?C, and the supernatant was transferred into autosampler vials with inserts for instrumental analysis. In addition, 20 ?L of supernatant from each sample was pooled to prepare the quality control sample.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was performed using electrospray ionization (ESI) in both positive and negative ion modes. The scan range was set to m/z 70??050. The sheath gas flow rate and auxiliary gas flow rate were set to 60 and 20 arb, respectively. The heater temperature was maintained at 350?C, and the capillary temperature was set to 320?C. The spray voltage was 3400 V in positive ion mode and ??000 V in negative ion mode. The S-lens RF level was set to 70. The normalized collision energies were 20%, 40%, and 60%. The resolution was set to 60,000 for full MS scans and 15,000 for MS/MS scans.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was performed by matching the detected features against public and in-house databases, including the Human Metabolome Database (HMDB; http://www.hmdb.ca/), METLIN (https://metlin.scripps.edu/), and an in-house database.</p>

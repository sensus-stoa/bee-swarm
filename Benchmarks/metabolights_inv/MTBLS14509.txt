Study Identifier	MTBLS14509
Study Title	Metabolomic responses of Solanum lycopersicum to Tuta absoluta infestation and TaCRVP effector expression
Study Description	This study presents the metabolomic profiling of Solanum lycopersicum in response to feeding by the herbivorous insect Tuta absoluta and the overexpression of its salivary effector, TaCRVP. To elucidate how this effector manipulates host immunity at the metabolic level, we performed untargeted LC-MS analysis.The study aimed to investigate the metabolic reprogramming induced by the insect salivary effector TaCRVP. The metabolomic results demonstrate a significant downregulation of precursor pathways for jasmonate (JA) synthesis in TaCRVP-OE lines. These metabolic signatures strongly indicate that the TaCRVP effector actively subverts plant oxidative defenses and effectively dismantles the downstream JA-mediated defense network to facilitate insect adaptation.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Fresh leaf tissues of Solanum lycopersicum were rapidly collected and cleaned to remove surface impurities. All leaf samples were immediately frozen in liquid nitrogen to terminate metabolic reactions, then preserved at −80 °C in a refrigerator until untargeted metabolomics analysis.</p>	<p>Parameter Value[Chromatography Instrument]: Agilent 1290 Infinity Ultra High Performance Liquid ChromatographyParameter Value[Autosampler model]: Agilent 1290 Standard AutosamplerParameter Value[Column model]: Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 Column (1.8 μm, 2.1 mm × 100 mm)Parameter Value[Guard column]: Waters HSS T3 VanGuard Pre-column (1.8 μm, 2.1 mm × 5 mm)</p>	<p>Original mass spectrometry raw data were imported into Progenesis QI software for peak matching, retention time alignment, peak identification and data normalization. Unreliable low-quality peaks were eliminated. PCA and OPLS-DA were performed to screen differentially expressed metabolites among experimental groups of tomato leaves.</p>	<p>The frozen tomato leaf samples were ground thoroughly under low temperature. Metabolites were extracted using ice-cold organic extraction solution via vortex mixing and ultrasonic-assisted extraction. After high-speed refrigerated centrifugation, the supernatant was collected, concentrated and redissolved. The resulting solution was centrifuged again and filtered through microporous membrane for subsequent UHPLC-MS/MS detection.</p>	<p>Parameter Value[Scan m/z range]: 60–1000 m/zParameter Value[Instrument]: AB Sciex TripleTOF 6600 Mass SpectrometerParameter Value[Ion source]: Electrospray Ionization (ESI), positive and negative ion modesParameter Value[Mass analyzer]: Time-of-Flight (TOF)</p>	<p>Metabolite annotation was accomplished by comparing precise molecular weight, secondary fragment spectra and characteristic retention time with multiple public databases including HMDB, METLIN, KEGG and self-built plant metabolite library. Qualitative identification was confirmed according to mass error tolerance and fragment ion matching degree, followed by metabolic pathway enrichment analysis.</p>

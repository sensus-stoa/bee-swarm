Study Protocol Description	<p>MC3T3-E1 osteoblast cells were transfected with siRNA targeting Connexin 43 (si-Cx43) or negative control siRNA (si-NC). After transfection, cells were incubated under standard culture conditions. Samples were collected at the endpoint of treatment, followed by metabolite extraction using methanol/acetonitrile/water solution. The supernatant was stored and subjected to LC–MS-based untargeted metabolomic analysis.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Thermo Scientific Vanquish UHPLC system equipped with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide column (1.7 µm, 2.1 × 50 mm). The mobile phase consisted of solvent A (water containing 25 mmol/L ammonium acetate and 25 mmol/L ammonia) and solvent B (acetonitrile). A gradient elution was applied at a constant flow rate, and samples were analyzed under controlled column temperature with a fixed injection volume for LC–MS-based untargeted metabolomics.</p>	<p>Raw LC–MS data were processed for peak detection, alignment, and feature extraction using high-resolution mass spectrometry data processing workflows. The resulting peak intensity tables were normalized prior to statistical analysis. Multivariate analysis including orthogonal partial least squares discriminant analysis (OPLS-DA) was performed using the ropls package in R. Differential metabolites were identified based on VIP &gt; 1 and P &lt; 0.05, followed by KEGG pathway enrichment analysis.</p>	<p>Samples were extracted using a methanol/acetonitrile/water (2:2:1, v/v/v) solution. The mixture was subjected to liquid nitrogen freezing and thawing cycles, vortex mixing, and centrifugation. The supernatant was collected for LC–MS analysis. Pooled quality control (QC) samples and solvent blanks were prepared for instrument conditioning and data quality monitoring.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric data were acquired using a Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer coupled with a Vanquish UHPLC system. Electrospray ionization (ESI) was used as the ion source in both positive and negative ion modes. Full scan MS data were acquired over an m/z range of 100–1000. Typical instrument settings, including spray voltage, gas flow, and ion transfer parameters, were optimized according to manufacturer recommendations for untargeted metabolomics analysis.</p>	<p>Metabolite features were annotated based on accurate mass measurements and MS/MS spectral matching. Putative metabolite identification was performed by searching against public databases including KEGG and HMDB. Annotation was supported by high-resolution mass accuracy and fragmentation patterns. Identified metabolites were used for downstream pathway enrichment analysis.</p>

Study Identifier	MTBLS14599
Study Title	Identification of a specialized lipid barrier for Drosophila metamorphosis (MS Imaging)
Study Description	<p>Many terrestrial insects transition from humid to dry environments at metamorphosis yet how they avoid dehydration is unclear. In <em>Drosophila melanogaster</em>, we identify a lipid barrier to water loss in the pupa that is anatomically distinct from that of the adult. It comprises unisex hydrocarbons that are longer and less volatile than their sex-specific adult counterparts. These hyper-long hydrocarbons are produced by larval oenocytes and stored in the fat body for subsequent transport to the cuticle at pupariation. A fatty acid elongase, <em>EloHL</em>, is required for hyper-long hydrocarbon biosynthesis and for a functional barrier to dehydration during early metamorphosis. Hyper-long barrier hydrocarbons are widespread across the <em>Drosophila</em> genus and typically transition to shorter sex-specific versions during adult maturation. In the desert species <em>D. mojavensis</em>, however, adults retain hyper-long hydrocarbons. This study reveals how insect lipid barriers are tuned to environmental variations in aridity and temperature during development and evolution.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Pupae were collected with a paintbrush from the side of the vial at 48 hr APF. They were then attached to double-sided sticky tape and removed from the larval cuticle (puparium) with watchmakers forceps. Pupae were then transferred to PBS and a small incision was made at the anterior end of the pupal cuticle followed by a longitudinal cut with watchmakers scissors and the removal of all internal tissues. Empty pupal cuticles were then washed twice with PBS and twice with 150 mM ammonium formate. For cryo-ToF-SIMS, the empty pupal cuticles were mounted with either the internal or external surface facing uppermost on ITO-coated glass coverslips (SPI Supplies 6462-AB, 18 mm², resistivity 70-100 Ω) cut to size with a diamond knife. For cryo-OrbiSIMS, empty pupal cuticles were mounted on cleaned silicon wafers (Agar Scientific AGG3390-10).</p><p><br></p>	<p>Peaks were picked manually, and area under the curve generated by SurfaceLab 7.3. These values were normalised to the total ion count.</p>	<p>N/A, no histology was performed on these samples.</p>	<p>Cryo-ToF-SIMS measurements were performed on a ToF-SIMS 5 instrument (IONTOF, GmbH) as previously described (Newell et al., 2020). Samples on the ITO-coated glass slides were mounted on a cooling stage. The loadlock was flooded with nitrogen gas before cooling during pump-down. During the analysis, the sample was cooled with a copper cooling finger to approximately -110ºC. Analyses were conducted using a 30 keV Bi3+ beam with a spot size of approximately 1 μm and a current of 0.04 pA at a cycle time of 200 μs. Secondary ions were extracted with an extraction voltage of 1 kV, with delayed extraction mode activated. For measurements used to generate graphs, data was obtained from a field of view of 250 x 250 μm at a resolution of 128 x 128 pixels. The measurements shown are the sum of 20 scans. To compensate for sample charging, an electron flood gun was applied during analysis with an energy of 21 eV and a current of -10 μA. The total ion dose was approximately 3.27 x 10^9 ions/cm². The mass resolution of the resulting spectra was approximately 5,000 at m/z 200. Spectral mass calibration was performed with reference to the known ions [C]+, [CH]+, [CH2]+, and [CH3]+. For measurements used to plot mass spectra, a total of 50 scans was acquired, with a total ion dose of approximately 2.05 x 10^8 ions/cm². For quantification, the [M]+. ion from hydrocarbons ranging in length from C29-C37 were summed. For these hydrocarbons, presence and positive identification has been confirmed in pupal samples with GC-MS.</p><p><br></p><p>Cryo-OrbiSIMS measurements were performed on a Hybrid-SIMS instrument (IONTOF, GmbH) as previously described (Newell et al., 2020). Samples were pre-cooled in liquid nitrogen in a Leica VCM and loaded into the pre-cooled loadlock with a Leica VCT. During the analysis, samples were kept at approximately -160oC. Analysis was performed with a 20 keV Ar3500+ GCIB with a spot size of approximately 3 μm, current of 13 pA and a duty cycle of 10.15%. Secondary ions were extracted with an extraction voltage of 2 kV. Data were obtained from a field of view of 250 x 250 μm at a resolution of 70 x 70 pixels. The measurements shown are the sum of 50 scans. To compensate for sample charging, an electron flood gun was applied during analysis with an energy of 21 eV and a current of -20 μA. The total ion dose was approximately 6 x 1012 ions/cm². The mass resolution of the resulting spectra was approximately 240,000 at m/z 200. Before data acquisition, the Orbitrap mass analyzer was calibrated against silver clusters using a silver standard.</p>	<p>For cryo-ToF-SIMS quantification, the [M]+. ion from hydrocarbons ranging in length from C29-C37 were summed. For these hydrocarbons, presence and positive identification has been confirmed in pupal samples with GC-MS.</p><p><br></p><p>For cryo-OrbiSIMS hydrocarbon signal quantification, the [M+N]+ ion was used as previously described (Newell et al., 2020).</p>	<p>N/A, no additional preparation was performed on these samples above the dissection detailed in the sample collection section.</p>

Study Identifier	MTBLS14666
Study Title	Untargeted Metabolomics Data of Rectal Contents from Diarrheic and Healthy Saanen Dairy Goats (MS (MTBLS))
Study Description	This dataset aims to investigate alterations in intestinal metabolites associated with diarrhea in Saanen dairy goats. A total of 14 goats reared under the same intensive housing conditions were selected, including 7 healthy individuals and 7 diarrheic individuals. Diarrhea was defined based on clinical signs, including wet tail and the presence of pasty fecal residues around the perianal area. Untargeted metabolomics analysis was performed on rectal content samples. The results indicated that organic acids and related metabolites were significantly upregulated in diarrheic goats, suggesting substantial alterations in the intestinal metabolic environment. To further elucidate the interactions between gut microbiota and host metabolism, this study also integrates multi-omics datasets, including: (1) metagenomic sequencing data of rectal contents; (2) 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing data of colonic contents from fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) mice; (3) transcriptomic data from an inflammatory model of goat small intestinal organoids. These multi-omics data provide a comprehensive resource for understanding the microbiota–metabolite interactions and the underlying mechanisms of diarrhea in Saanen dairy goats.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Rectal contents were collected from diarrheic with obvious wet tail symptoms and healthy Saanen dairy goats. All samples were immediately frozen in liquid nitrogen after collection and stored at -80 °C until further processing.</p>	<p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Thermo Fisher Vanquish UHPLC system.</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'><br></p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>For the reversed-phase (C18) separation:</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Column: Thermo Fisher Hypersil Gold C18 (1.9 μm, 2.1 × 100 mm)</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Column temperature: 40 °C</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Flow rate: 0.2 mL/min</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Mobile phase A: 0.1% formic acid in water</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Mobile phase B: methanol</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Gradient: 0-1.5 min, 2% B; 1.5-3 min, 2-85% B; 3-10 min, 85-100% B; 10-10.1 min, 100-2% B; 10.1-12 min, 2% B.</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'><br></p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>For the HILIC separation (if applicable):</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Column: Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide (1.7 μm, 2.1 × 100 mm)</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Column temperature: 40 °C</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Flow rate: 0.2 mL/min</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Mobile phase A (positive mode): 0.1% formic acid, 10 mM ammonium acetate in 95% acetonitrile</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Mobile phase B (positive mode): 0.1% formic acid, 10 mM ammonium acetate in 50% acetonitrile</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Mobile phase A (negative mode): 10 mM ammonium acetate in 95% acetonitrile</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Mobile phase B (negative mode): 10 mM ammonium acetate in 50% acetonitrile</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1532'>- Gradient: 0-1.5 min, 2% B; 1.5-7 min, 2-100% B; 7-9 min, 100% B; 9-9.1 min, 100-2% B; 9.1-12 min, 2% B.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data files were converted to mzXML format using ProteoWizard software. Peak picking, alignment, and integration were performed with the XCMS package. The first sample was used for retention time correction. Background ions were removed using solvent blank samples. Peak intensities were normalized using the formula: `Sample intensity / (Sample total intensity / First sample total intensity)` to obtain relative peak areas.</p>	<p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1454'>For metabolite extraction, 100 μL of each rectal content sample was mixed with 400 μL of 80% methanol aqueous solution, vortexed, and incubated on ice for 5 min. The mixture was then centrifuged at 15,000 g for 20 min at 4 °C. The supernatant was diluted with LC-MS grade water to a final methanol concentration of 53%, and centrifuged again at 15,000 g for 20 min at 4 °C. The resulting supernatant was collected for LC-MS analysis.</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1454'><br></p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1454'>Quality control (QC) samples were prepared by pooling equal volumes from all experimental samples. Solvent blank samples (53% methanol in water) were processed in parallel with the experimental samples to assess background contamination.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry analysis was performed using a Thermo Fisher Q Exactive HF / Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. The instrument was operated in both positive and negative ion modes.</p><p>- Spray voltage: 3.5 kV</p><p>- Sheath gas flow rate: 35 psi</p><p>- Auxiliary gas flow rate: 10 L/min</p><p>- Capillary temperature: 320 °C</p><p>- Auxiliary gas heater temperature: 350 °C</p><p>- S-lens RF level: 60</p><p>- Full scan range: m/z 100–1500</p><p>- MS/MS acquisition: data-dependent scans (DDA)</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was performed by matching the accurate mass (with a mass tolerance of 10 ppm) and MS/MS spectra against multiple databases, including the Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes (KEGG), the Human Metabolome Database (HMDB), and LIPID MAPS.</p>

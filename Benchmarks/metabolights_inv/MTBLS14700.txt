Study Protocol Description	<p>Canine osteosarcoma McKinley cells and normal canine osteoblast PEKCC2382 cells were cultured at Northeast Agricultural University. Cells were harvested in logarithmic growth phase, washed with ice-cold PBS, and immediately snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen, then stored at -80°C until metabolite extraction. Three biological replicates were prepared for each cell line.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed on a Thermo Fisher Vanquish UHPLC system equipped with a Waters Acquity HSS T3 column (1.8 μm, 2.1 × 100 mm). Mobile phase A was 0.1% formic acid in water, and mobile phase B was acetonitrile with 0.1% formic acid. The gradient elution program was: 0–1 min, 5% B; 1–9 min, 5–95% B; 9–12 min, 95% B; 12–12.1 min, 95–5% B; 12.1–15 min, 5% B. The flow rate was 0.3 mL/min, column temperature was 40°C, and injection volume was 2 μL.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were processed using Progenesis QI software (Waters) for peak picking, alignment, normalization, and feature detection. Retention time alignment was performed using the default settings. The peak intensities were normalized to the total ion count (TIC) of each sample.</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted using ice-cold methanol-water solution (4:1, v/v). Briefly, cell pellets were homogenized in extraction solvent, vortexed, and sonicated in an ice bath. After centrifugation at 12,000 g for 15 min at 4°C, supernatants were collected and dried under vacuum. The dried extracts were reconstituted with methanol-water (1:1, v/v) before analysis. A pooled quality control (QC) sample was prepared by mixing equal volumes of all samples and injected at regular intervals throughout the run.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry analysis was performed on a Thermo Fisher Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. The instrument was operated in both positive and negative ionization modes. The spray voltage was set to 3.5 kV (positive) and 3.0 kV (negative). The capillary temperature was 320°C, and the auxiliary gas heater temperature was 350°C. Sheath gas flow rate was 40 arb, auxiliary gas flow rate was 10 arb, and sweep gas flow rate was 1 arb. Full scan MS spectra were acquired with a mass range of m/z 100–1000 at a resolution of 60,000.</p>	<p>Metabolite annotation was performed by matching accurate mass and MS/MS spectra against public databases including HMDB, Metlin, and KEGG. The mass tolerance was set to 5 ppm for precursor ions and 0.02 Da for fragment ions. Identifications were confirmed by comparison with authentic standards where available.</p>

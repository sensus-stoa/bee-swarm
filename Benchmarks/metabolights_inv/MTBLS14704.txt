Study Protocol Type Term Source REF		CHMO	OBI	EFO	CHMO	MI
Study Protocol Description	<p>Culture supernatants of two different strains of Lactobacillus salivarius were collected after fermentation. The obtained supernatants were used as test samples for subsequent metabolomic analysis.</p>	<p>Liquid chromatographic separation was performed using ultra-high performance liquid chromatography.</p><p>Parameter Value[Chromatography Instrument]: Agilent 1290 Infinity LC, Vanquish UHPLC</p><p>Parameter Value[Autosampler model]: Agilent 1290 Autosampler, Vanquish Autosampler</p><p>Parameter Value[Column model]: C18 column (conventional analytical column for metabolomics)</p><p>Parameter Value[Guard column]: Corresponding C18 guard column</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were imported into professional metabolomics software for peak picking, peak alignment, retention time correction, noise filtering and normalization. Feature intensities were extracted for subsequent statistical analysis.</p>	<p>An appropriate volume of fermentation supernatant was mixed with ice-cold acetonitrile at a fixed ratio for protein precipitation. The mixture was vortexed thoroughly and centrifuged at high speed. The resulting supernatant was collected, filtered through a 0.22 μm organic membrane, and subjected to LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry detection was carried out on two high-resolution mass spectrometers respectively.</p><p>Parameter Value[Scan m/z range]: 50–1200 m/z</p><p>Parameter Value[Instrument]: AB Triple TOF 6600, Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Exploris 480</p><p>Parameter Value[Ion source]: Electrospray ionization (ESI)</p><p>Parameter Value[Mass analyzer]: Time-of-Flight (TOF), Orbitrap</p>	<p>Metabolites were identified by matching the acquired MS and MS/MS spectra with public metabolite databases. Accurate molecular weight, fragment ion information and retention time were comprehensively compared to annotate candidate metabolites. Differential metabolites between two groups of supernatants were further screened.</p>

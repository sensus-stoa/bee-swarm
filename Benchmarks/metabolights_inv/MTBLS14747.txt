Study Protocol Description	<p>Rhizosphere soil samples collected from split-root experiment at 30 d post-inoculation; tightly adhering soil (&lt; 1 mm) was flash-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at –80 °C.</p><p><br></p><p>Bacterial culture samples obtained from S. pactum Act12 grown in Gause’s No. 1 medium for 72 h; cell-free supernatant was filtered (0.22 μm), flash-frozen, and stored at –80 °C.</p>	<p>Instrument: Ultra-performance liquid chromatography (ExionLC AD, Sciex)</p><p><br></p><p>Column: Agilent SB-C18 (1.8 μm, 2.1 × 100 mm)</p><p><br></p><p>Column temperature: 40°C</p><p><br></p><p>Mobile phase: A: water with 0.1% formic acid; B: acetonitrile with 0.1% formic acid</p><p><br></p><p>Flow rate: 0.35 mL/min</p><p><br></p><p>Injection volume: 2 μL</p><p><br></p><p>Gradient program: 0–9 min, B increased linearly from 5% to 95%; 9–10 min, held at 95% B; 10–11.1 min, B decreased to 5%; 11.1–14 min, held at 5% B.</p>	<p>Raw data processing: Mass spectrometry data were processed using Analyst 1.6.3 software for peak detection and integration.</p><p><br></p><p>Peak integration and calibration: Peak areas of each metabolite were integrated and calibrated using MultiQuant software, and the peak area was used as the relative abundance.</p><p><br></p><p>Data normalization: Peak area data were log2-transformed and then mean-centered (Ctr) or unit variance scaled (UV scaling, i.e., Z-score normalization) prior to multivariate statistical analysis.</p><p><br></p><p>Quality control: Data stability was assessed by the coefficient of variation (CV) of QC samples. CV &lt; 0.3 for &gt;75% of the metabolites indicates highly stable data.</p>	<p>Rhizosphere soil samples: 2 g of fresh soil was extracted with 10 mL of ice-cold 80% methanol containing internal standards, vortexed for 1 min, and ultrasonicated in an ice bath for 30 min. After centrifugation (12,000 ×g, 10 min, 4°C), the supernatant was filtered through a 0.22 μm membrane.</p><p><br></p><p>Bacterial culture samples: 1 mL of cell-free supernatant was mixed with 3 volumes of ice-cold methanol containing internal standards, vortexed, and kept at –20°C for 1 h. The mixture was centrifuged (12,000 ×g, 15 min, 4°C), and the supernatant was dried under nitrogen. The residue was reconstituted in 200 μL of 80% methanol and filtered (0.22 μm).</p><p><br></p><p>Quality control (QC): Equal aliquots of supernatant from each treatment were pooled to prepare QC samples. A QC sample was injected after every 10 analytical samples to monitor instrument stability and reproducibility.</p><p><br></p><p>Solvent blank: 80% methanol was used as a blank control to subtract background signals.</p>	<p>ESI-QTRAP (Sciex) in positive/negative ion modes. Source temperature 550°C; ion spray voltage ±5500 V; GSI 50 psi, GSII 60 psi, curtain gas 25 psi; collision gas medium (N2); MRM mode with optimized transitions for each metabolite.</p>	<p>Metabolites were identified using the in-house MWDB database based on retention time, precursor and fragment ions. Confidence levels 1–3 were assigned according to MS² spectrum match scores. MultiQuant (Sciex) and Analyst 1.6.3 were used for data processing. Additional databases including MHK (Metlin, HMDB, KEGG) were also referenced.</p>

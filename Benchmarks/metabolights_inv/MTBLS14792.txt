Study Identifier	MTBLS14792
Study Title	Untargeted LC-MS metabolomics of in vitro cultured Rothia mucilaginosa supernatant and pellet samples
Study Description	This study reports untargeted LC-MS metabolomics data from in vitro culture of Rothia mucilaginosa (BNCC363031). A total of 13 biological samples were analyzed, including culture supernatant samples (S1-S5), bacterial pellet samples (B1-B5), and uninoculated medium controls (M1-M3). Data were acquired in both positive and negative ion modes, resulting in 34 raw mzXML files (17 POS and 17 NEG). UHPLC-MS/MS analysis was performed on a Vanquish UHPLC system coupled with an Orbitrap platform (Q Exactive HF-X/Exploris series), followed by standard processing including peak detection, alignment, annotation, and QC-based filtering. This dataset supports characterization of extracellular and intracellular metabolite profiles associated with Rothia culture conditions.

Study Protocol Description	Biological samples were prepared by the customer, transported on dry ice, and stored at -80 C before metabolomics processing. Quality control (QC) samples were prepared by pooling equal volumes of all experimental samples, and blank samples used 53% methanol in water.	UHPLC-MS/MS analyses were performed using a Vanquish UHPLC system coupled with an Orbitrap Q Exactive HF, Q Exactive HF-X, Orbitrap Exploris 120, or Orbitrap Exploris 480 mass spectrometer. Samples were injected onto a Hypersil Gold column (100 x 2.1 mm, 1.9 um) using a 12-min linear gradient at a flow rate of 0.2 mL/min. Eluent A was 0.1% formic acid in water and eluent B was methanol. The gradient was: 2% B at 1.5 min, 2-85% B at 3 min, 85-100% B at 10 min, 100-2% B at 10.1 min, and 2% B at 12 min.	The data files generated by UHPLC-MS/MS were processed by XCMS for peak alignment, peak picking, and quantitation. Metabolites with more than 50% missing values were filtered and remaining missing values were imputed using KNN. Background ions were removed using blank samples, and compounds with QC coefficient of variation greater than 30% were filtered.	For cell or bacteria samples, samples were resuspended with prechilled 80% methanol, sonicated in a water bath for 15 min, centrifuged at 15,000 rpm for 20 min, and supernatants were freeze-dried and dissolved with 10% methanol before LC-MS/MS analysis. For cell or bacteria culture medium samples, 1 mL samples were freeze-dried, resuspended with prechilled 80% methanol, incubated on ice for 5 min, centrifuged at 15,000 rpm for 20 min, diluted to 53% methanol, centrifuged again, and supernatants were injected. Blank samples used 53% methanol in water with the same preprocessing workflow.	Q Exactive HF mass spectrometer was operated in positive and negative polarity mode with spray voltage 3.5 kV, capillary temperature 320 C, sheath gas flow rate 35 psi, aux gas flow rate 10 L/min, S-lens RF level 60, and aux gas heater temperature 350 C. The scan range was m/z 100-1500 and MS/MS acquisition used data-dependent scans.	Based on adduct ions and a mass deviation of 10 ppm, metabolite identification was performed by matching to the self-built high-quality secondary spectrum database (NovoMetDB). Identified metabolites were further annotated using KEGG, HMDB, and LIPIDMaps databases.

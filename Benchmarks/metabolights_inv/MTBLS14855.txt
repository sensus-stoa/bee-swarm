Study Identifier	MTBLS14855
Study Title	Metabolomic profiling of zebrafish DSS-induced colitis intervened by Akkermansia muciniphila-derived extracellular vesicles (AmEVs)
Study Description	This study performs untargeted metabolomics analysis on intestinal tissues of DSS-induced colitis zebrafish models to uncover the metabolic regulatory mechanisms of AmEVs. We compared intestinal metabolite profiles between two experimental groups: DSS-induced colitis model group and AmEV intervention group. Differential metabolites were screened to explore how AmEVs alleviate DSS-triggered intestinal inflammation and restore damaged intestinal barrier function in zebrafish.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Zebrafish intestinal content samples were collected from DSS-induced colitis model fish and DSS-induced colitis fish treated with Akkermansia muciniphila-derived extracellular vesicles (AmEVs). The experimental groups included the DSS-treated group (DD) and the DSS + AmEVs intervention group (DA), with biological replicates for each group. Intestinal contents were collected under clean conditions and pooled or mixed as required for metabolomic analysis. Quality control (QC) samples were prepared by pooling equal aliquots from study samples to monitor analytical stability and reproducibility.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Thermo Vanquish ultra-high-performance liquid chromatography system. Metabolites were separated on a Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (1.8 μm, 2.1 mm × 100 mm) under reverse-phase liquid chromatography conditions. The autosampler was used for sample injection prior to mass spectrometry analysis.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were processed for peak detection, peak alignment, retention time correction, peak area extraction and metabolite abundance matrix generation. Positive and negative ion mode data were processed for untargeted metabolomics analysis. Quality control samples were used to evaluate the stability and reproducibility of the analytical workflow. The processed metabolite abundance table was used for downstream statistical analyses, including PCA, PLS-DA/OPLS-DA, differential metabolite screening, clustering analysis and pathway enrichment analysis.</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted from zebrafish intestinal content samples before LC-MS analysis. Samples were processed using an organic solvent-based extraction procedure, followed by centrifugation to remove insoluble material. The supernatant was collected for untargeted metabolomics analysis. No derivatization was performed. Pooled QC samples were prepared and analyzed together with the experimental samples to evaluate the stability and reproducibility of the LC-MS workflow.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry was performed using a Thermo Orbitrap Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher). The ion source was electrospray ionization (ESI). Data were acquired for untargeted LC-MS metabolomic profiling in both positive and negative ionization modes. The scan polarity was recorded as alternating, and the scan m/z range was set to 70–1050. The mass analyzer was Orbitrap.</p>	<p>Metabolite features were annotated based on accurate mass-to-charge ratio, retention time, chemical formula, annotation score and database matching. Metabolite identifiers from public databases, including HMDB, PubChem and KEGG Compound IDs, were used where available. The metabolite annotation results included metabolite name, chemical formula, m/z value, retention time, database identifier, annotation score and sample abundance values. The annotated metabolite table was used as the metabolite assignment file for downstream metabolomics analysis.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Biopsy samples were collected from infraspinatus muscle of sheep before&nbsp;and after 2 weeks of tendon release from the operated and contralateral muscle. One group of animals were treated with oral doses of L-carnitine. Collected biopsies were frozen air-tight at -80 °C until being further processed.</p>	<p><strong>Metabolite isolation</strong></p><p>Polar compounds were extracted from 10 mm3 biopsy material from infraspinatus muscle in cold Methanol : Methyl-tert-butylether: H20 = 360: 1,200: 348 using a full glass Potter type homogenizer and subjected to ultrahigh performance liquid chromatographytandem mass spectrometry (UPLC-MS). The polar phase containing the majority of metabolites was stored at -30 °C until being dried down and reconstituted for separation via UPLC-MS.</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Lipid isolation</strong></p><p>Biopsy material (10 mm3) from infraspinatus muscle in cold Methanol was extracted: Methyl-tert-butylether: H20 = 360: 1,200: 348 using a full glass Potter type homogenizer and subjected to ultrahigh performance liquid chromatographytandem mass spectrometry (UPLC-MS) essentially as described in Fluck et al [1]. The non-polar phase containing the majority of lipids were stored at -30 °C until being dried down and reconstituted for separation via UPLC-MS.</p><p><br></p><p>References:</p><p>[1] Fluck M, Ruoss S, Mohl CB, Valdivieso P, Benn MC, von Rechenberg B, Laczko E, Hu J, Wieser K, Meyer DC, Gerber C: Genomic and lipidomic actions of nandrolone on detached rotator cuff muscle in sheep. J Steroid Biochem Mol Biol. 2017;165:382-395</p>	<p>Lipids were separated with a C18 reversed phase column (HSS T3 1.7 mm, 0.2 mm x 150 mm) by a nanoAquity UPLC (Waters) applying a gradient of 5 uM ammoniumacetate in acetonitrile/water 40:60 v/v (A) and 5uM ammoniumacetate in acetonitrile - isopropanol 10:90. Metabolites were separated on nanoAquity UPLC (Waters) equipped with a BEH-Amide capillary column (200 mm x 150 mm, 1.7 µm particle size, Waters), applying a gradient of 0.5 mM ammoniumacetate in acetonitril (A) and 0.5 mM ammoniumacetate in water (B) from 90 to 50% A.</p>	<p>Eluates from the separation of lipids were injected in six-second-intervals into a Q ExactiveTM Hybrid Quadrupole-Orbitrap Mass Spectrometer (Thermo Fisher) by a nanoESI source that was operated in positive polarization mode.</p><p>Eluates from the separation of metabolites were separated on nanoAquity UPLC (Waters) equipped with a BEH-Amide capillary column (200 mm x 150 mm, 1.7 mm particle size, Waters), applying a gradient of 0.5 mM ammoniumacetate in acetonitrile (A) and 0.5 mM ammoniumacetate in water (B) from 90 to 50% A and injected into Q Exactive‚ Hybrid Quadrupole-Orbitrap Mass Spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Reinach, Switzerland). MS data was acquired using negative polarization and all ion fragmentation.</p><p>All solvents used were of quality HPLC grade (Chromasolv, Sigma-Aldrich, Buchs, Switzerland).</p>	<p>Data were processes from the output of Progenesis QI. The raw numbers for thesSignals of the individual compounds were normalised to the total signal per sample.</p>	<p>Detected ions were identified using the Progenesis QI software (Nonlinear Dynamics, A Waters Company) based on accurate mass, detected adduct patterns and isotope patterns by comparing the observed values with entries in the LipidMaps database (LM). A mass accuracy of 5mDalton was set for the searches. Each identified compounds was allocated its respective Pubchem substance identifier (SID). For multiple hits the compound with the highest score was accepted.</p>

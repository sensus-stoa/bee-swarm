Study Protocol Description	<p>After seeding neural cells onto a culture plate, Rg5 pretreatment was performed and placed in an anaerobic environment.</p>	<p>Samples were separated using a Vanquish ultra-high performance liquid chromatography (UHPLC) system with a HILIC column. The column temperature was maintained at 25 °C, the flow rate was 0.3 mL/min, and the injection volume was 2 μL. The mobile phase consisted of solvent A (water containing 25 mM ammonium acetate and 25 mM ammonia solution) and solvent B (acetonitrile).</p><p>The gradient elution program was set as follows: 0–1.5 min, 98% B; 1.5–12 min, linear decrease from 98% to 2% B; 12–14 min, maintained at 2% B; 14–14.1 min, linear increase from 2% to 98% B; 14.1–17 min, maintained at 98% B. During the entire analysis, all samples were stored in an autosampler at 4 °C.</p><p>To minimize systematic signal fluctuations, all samples were analyzed continuously in a randomized order. Quality control (QC) samples were interspersed throughout the sample sequence to monitor instrument stability and ensure the reliability of analytical data.</p>	<p>Raw data were converted to .mzXML format via ProteoWizard, followed by peak alignment, retention time correction, and peak area integration using XCMS software. </p>	<p>Samples were thawed slowly at 4 °C. An appropriate amount of each sample was mixed with pre-cooled methanol/acetonitrile/water solution (2:2:1, v/v), followed by vortex mixing and low-temperature sonication for 30 min. The mixture was stood at −20 °C for 10 min and then centrifuged at 14000 g for 20 min at 4 °C. The supernatant was collected and vacuum-dried.</p><p>Prior to mass spectrometry analysis, the dried residue was reconstituted in 100 μL acetonitrile aqueous solution (acetonitrile: water = 1:1, v/v), vortexed, and centrifuged at 14000 g for 15 min at 4 °C. The resulting supernatant was subjected to injection and analysis.</p>	<p>Samples were separated using a Vanquish LC ultra-high-performance liquid chromatography (UHPLC) system and then analyzed on a Q Exactive series mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific) in both positive and negative electrospray ionization (ESI) modes.</p><p>The ESI source and MS parameters were set as follows: nebulizer gas (Gas1): 60; auxiliary heating gas (Gas2): 60; curtain gas (CUR): 30 psi; ion source temperature: 600 °C; ion spray voltage (ISVF): ±5500 V (for positive and negative modes). For MS1 full-scan acquisition, the mass-to-charge ratio range was 80–1200 Da, with a resolution of 60 000 and an accumulation time of 100 ms. For MS2, a segmented acquisition method was employed, with a scan range of 70–1200 Da, a resolution of 30 000, an accumulation time of 50 ms, and a dynamic exclusion time of 4 s.</p>	<p>The resulting dataset underwent metabolite identification and preprocessing, after which data quality was assessed prior to further statistical analysis.</p>

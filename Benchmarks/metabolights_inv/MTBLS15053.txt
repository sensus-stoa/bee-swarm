Study Identifier	MTBLS15053
Study Title	Untargeted metabolomics of fecal samples from patients with bullous pemphigoid and healthy controls
Study Description	This study applied untargeted metabolomics to characterize the fecal metabolic profiles of bullous pemphigoid (BP) patients and healthy controls. Fecal metabolites were extracted, separated by ultra-high performance liquid chromatography, and detected by high-resolution mass spectrometry (UHPLC-QTOF-MS) in both positive and negative ionization modes. Raw data were processed for peak picking and alignment, and metabolites were identified against the METLIN and other public spectral databases. The study aims to identify differential metabolites and disrupted pathways associated with BP.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Fecal samples from patients with bullous pemphigoid and healthy controls were collected in sterile containers and stored at −80°C.</p>	<p>Metabolites were separated on an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (2.1 × 100 mm, 1.8 μm). Mobile phase A was water with 0.1% formic acid, mobile phase B was acetonitrile with 0.1% formic acid. The column temperature was maintained at 40 °C, flow rate 0.4 mL/min, injection volume 2 μL. Detection was performed on a Waters Xevo G2-XS QTOF mass spectrometer.</p>	<p>Raw data collected by MassLynx V4.2 was processed with Progenesis QI software to complete peak extraction, peak alignment and other preprocessing operations.</p>	<p>Fecal samples were extracted using bead-beating and ultrasonication with extraction solvent, incubated at −20°C for 1 hour, centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 15 minutes at 4°C, and the supernatant was vacuum-dried and reconstituted prior to analysis. Pooled QC samples were prepared from equal aliquots of all samples and run periodically to ensure data quality.</p>	<p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1519'>Mass spectrometry detection was carried out on a Waters Xevo G2-XS QTOF high-resolution mass spectrometer under MSe mode controlled by MassLynx V4.2 software. Dual-channel data acquisition was conducted with simultaneous low and high collision energy scanning (collision energy range: 10–40 V) in each acquisition cycle, with a scanning frequency of 0.2 seconds per mass spectrum. The ESI ion source parameters were set as follows: cone voltage 30 V, source temperature 100 °C, desolvation gas temperature 500 °C, cone gas flow rate 50 L/h, desolventizing gas flow rate 800 L/h. Capillary voltage was set to 2500 V in positive ion mode and 2000 V in negative ion mode.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was conducted by matching accurate mass and fragment spectra against the online METLIN database embedded in Progenesis QI and an in-house Biomark library; compound classification and pathway information were further annotated from KEGG, HMDB and LipidMaps databases.</p>

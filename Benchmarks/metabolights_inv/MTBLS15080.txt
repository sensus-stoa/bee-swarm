Study Identifier	MTBLS15080
Study Title	Seminal lipid depletion and gut microbiome–metabolome alterations in men with gonadotropin-treated idiopathic hypogonadotropic hypogonadism: an integrated multi-omics study
Study Description	This study investigated seminal plasma lipid alterations and gut microbiome–metabolome changes in men with gonadotropin-treated idiopathic hypogonadotropic hypogonadism. Seminal plasma metabolomics, fecal metagenomic sequencing, and fecal metabolomics were performed in patients and healthy controls. The resulting multi-omics data were analyzed to characterize metabolic and microbial differences and their associations with semen parameters.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Fecal and seminal plasma samples were collected from men with gonadotropin-treated idiopathic hypogonadotropic hypogonadism and healthy controls, and stored at low temperature before metabolomics analysis.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using reverse-phase liquid chromatography prior to mass spectrometry analysis.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were processed for peak detection, alignment, normalization, and metabolite feature annotation to generate metabolomics data matrices for downstream analysis.</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted from fecal and seminal plasma samples using an organic solvent-based extraction procedure before LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>Untargeted metabolomics was performed using LC-MS in both positive and negative ion modes to profile metabolite features in fecal and seminal plasma samples.</p>	<p>Metabolite features were annotated based on LC-MS data using peak information including retention time, mass-to-charge ratio, and MS/MS spectral information when available. Metabolite identification was performed by matching detected features against reference databases and/or an in-house spectral library. The resulting metabolite annotation table was used for downstream statistical analysis and biological interpretation.</p>

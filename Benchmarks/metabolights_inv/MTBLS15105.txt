Study Protocol Description	<p>After 5 days of incubation, nematodes from different treatment groups were collected.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Thermo Vanquish UHPLC system&nbsp;(Waltham, MA, USA) equipped with a HSS T3 column (1.7 µm, 2.1 mm × 150 mm). A 12-min gradient elution was applied at a flow rate of 0.3 mL/min, with quality control (QC) samples interspersed throughout the queue.</p>	<p>Raw data were processed via Compound Discoverer 3.3 (Thermo Scientific, Waltham, MA, USA)&nbsp;for peak alignment and annotation against multiple databases.</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted from approximately 100 µL of sample using 400 µL of pre-cooled methanol:acetonitrile (3:1, v/v), followed by vortexing, sonication, and incubation at 4 °C. After centrifugation, supernatants were dried, reconstituted in 50%&nbsp;(v/v) methanol, and centrifuged again prior to analysis.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry data were acquired in both positive and negative ion modes using a Thermo QE HF-X instrument&nbsp;(Waltham, MA, USA).</p>	<p>Raw data were processed via Compound Discoverer 3.3 (Thermo Scientific, Waltham, MA, USA)&nbsp;for peak alignment and annotation against multiple databases.</p>

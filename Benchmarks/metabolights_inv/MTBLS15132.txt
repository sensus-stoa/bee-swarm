INVESTIGATION
Investigation Identifier	MTBLS15132
Investigation Title	Time-Resolved Microbiome–Metabolome Remodeling Reveals Biotransformation Patterns in Liquid-Fermented Dendrobium officinale Flowers
Investigation Description	This study investigated temporal metabolomic changes during a 60-day mixed-culture liquid fermentation of Dendrobium officinale flowers. Fermentation supernatants were collected at days 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60, with six biological replicates at each time point. Untargeted LC-MS/MS analysis was performed in positive and negative electrospray ionization modes using a UHPLC-Orbitrap Exploris 240 system. The deposited dataset contains raw mass spectrometry files for 36 biological samples and four pooled quality-control samples and supports the analysis of time-dependent metabolite remodeling during fermentation.

Study Protocol Description	Fermentation supernatants were collected at days 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60 during a 60-day mixed-culture liquid fermentation of Dendrobium officinale flowers. Six biological replicates were collected at each time point. Supernatants were separated from fermentation pellets by centrifugation and stored at -20 degrees C until untargeted LC-MS/MS analysis. Four pooled quality-control samples were included for analytical quality control.	Reversed-phase liquid chromatography was performed using a Thermo Scientific UHPLC system coupled to an Orbitrap Exploris 240 mass spectrometer and an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (100 mm x 2.1 mm, 1.8 micrometre; Waters). The flow rate was 0.40 mL/min and the column temperature was 40 degrees C.	LC-MS raw data were processed using Progenesis QI for baseline filtering, peak detection, integration, retention-time correction, and peak alignment. Features detected in at least 80% of samples in any group were retained. Missing values were imputed with the minimum value, peak intensities were sum-normalized, variables with QC RSD greater than 30% were excluded, and data were log10-transformed.	Fermentation supernatants were extracted using acetonitrile:methanol (1:1, v/v) containing four internal standards, including L-2-chlorophenylalanine (0.02 mg/mL). Samples were vortexed, sonicated at 5 degrees C, incubated at -20 degrees C, centrifuged at 13000 x g, dried under nitrogen, reconstituted in acetonitrile:water (1:1, v/v), sonicated, and centrifuged before LC-MS/MS analysis.	Mass spectrometry data were acquired on a Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Exploris 240 mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization source in separate positive and negative ion modes. The mass scan range was m/z 70-1050. Data-dependent MS/MS acquisition used stepped normalized collision energies of 20, 40, and 60.	MS and MS/MS spectra were matched against HMDB, METLIN, KEGG, and the Majorbio self-compiled database (MJDB). Internal-standard peaks and known false-positive features, including noise and column-bleed signals, were removed, and redundant features were pooled. Metabolite annotations were reported with metabolite name, mass-to-charge ratio, retention time, molecular formula, database identifier, and identification confidence.

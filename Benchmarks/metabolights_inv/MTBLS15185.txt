Study Protocol Description	<p>Gonad samples of Cherax quadricarinatus were analyzed. The dataset comprises 20 biological samples assigned to four experimental groups n=5 per group and three pooled quality-control samples. Animal sampling gonad dissection collection time preservation transport and storage conditions were not provided in the supplied materials.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed by ultra-high-performance liquid chromatography UHPLC. The UHPLC make and model autosampler analytical and guard columns mobile phases additives gradient program flow rate injection volume and temperature settings were not provided in the supplied materials.</p>	<p>Spectral data were converted to centroided mzXML using ProteoWizard 3.0.24054. Processing of the existing feature matrices included total-ion-current TIC normalization filtering of features with RSD greater than 30% or detection rate below 50% and half-minimum imputation of missing values. Software and settings for feature detection deconvolution retention-time alignment and integration were not provided.</p>	<p>Extraction preceded untargeted LC-MS analysis. The supplied materials do not specify sample mass extraction solvent or volume internal standards homogenization or sonication incubation centrifugation drying reconstitution or the procedure used to prepare the pooled quality-control samples.</p>	<p>Mass spectra were acquired on a Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Exploris 120 with electrospray ionization in separate positive- and negative-ion assays. The submitted mzXML files contain centroided full-scan MS1 data over m/z 70-1200 no MS2 scans are present. Resolution AGC target injection time source temperatures gas flows spray voltage and other acquisition settings were not provided.</p>	<p>Metabolite annotations were taken from the existing feature and annotation matrices. The submitted mzXML files contain centroided full-scan MS1 data only. Annotation software database or spectral-library versions matching tolerances scoring thresholds confidence levels and authentic-standard confirmation were not provided. Annotations are reported as supplied without assigning an unsupported identification confidence level.</p>

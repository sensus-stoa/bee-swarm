Study Description	<p>This study investigates metabolomic changes associated with personalized cancer therapy responses in patient-derived organoid chips containing a reconstructed tumor microenvironment. The dataset includes de-identified sample metadata, raw mass spectrometry files, processed metabolite abundance matrices, and metabolite annotations from different treatment conditions. These data support independent evaluation and reproduction of the metabolomics analyses reported in the manuscript.</p>

Study Protocol Type Term Source REF		CHMO	OBI	EFO	CHMO	MI
Study Protocol Description	<p>Culture supernatant samples were collected from patient-derived cancer organoid cultures following the indicated treatment conditions. Samples were immediately frozen and stored at −80°C until metabolomics analysis. Pooled quality-control samples were prepared by mixing equal volumes from all experimental samples. A blank sample was prepared to monitor and remove background ions.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Thermo Scientific Vanquish UHPLC system equipped with a Hypersil Gold C18 column (100 × 2.1 mm, 1.9 μm). The column temperature was maintained at 40°C and the flow rate was 0.2 mL/min. Mobile phase A was 0.1% formic acid in water, and mobile phase B was methanol. The gradient was: 0–1.5 min, 2% B; 1.5–3 min, 2–85% B; 3–10 min, 85–100% B; 10–10.1 min, 100–2% B; and 10.1–12 min, 2% B.</p>	<p>Instrument data files were converted to mzXML format using ProteoWizard. Peak detection, peak quantification, retention-time alignment, and feature alignment across samples were performed using XCMS. Blank samples were used to remove background features. Peak areas were normalized relative to the total metabolite abundance of QC1, and features with a coefficient of variation greater than 30% in QC samples were excluded.</p>	<p>One milliliter of each culture supernatant sample was lyophilized and reconstituted in 100 μL of 80% methanol in water. The samples were vortexed, incubated on ice for 5 min, and centrifuged at 15,000 × g for 15 min at 4°C. The supernatant was diluted with LC-MS-grade water to a final methanol concentration of 53%, followed by centrifugation at 15,000 × g for 15 min at 4°C. The resulting supernatant was collected for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry data were acquired using a Thermo Scientific Q Exactive HF-X Hybrid Quadrupole-Orbitrap mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization source. Data were acquired separately in positive and negative ion modes over an m/z range of 100–1500. The spray voltage was 3.5 kV, sheath gas flow rate was 35 psi, auxiliary gas flow rate was 10 L/min, capillary temperature was 320°C, S-lens RF level was 60, and auxiliary gas heater temperature was 350°C. Data-dependent MS/MS acquisition was used.</p>	<p>Metabolite features were identified by matching accurate mass, retention time, adduct information, and MS/MS spectra against the NovoMetDB in-house spectral database using a mass tolerance of 10 ppm. Identified metabolites were further annotated using the KEGG, HMDB, and LIPID MAPS databases.</p>

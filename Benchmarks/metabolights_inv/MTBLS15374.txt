Study Identifier	MTBLS15374
Study Title	Epigenomic and transcriptomic profiling of FaCMT3-dependent strawberry fruit development
Study Description	This project investigates how FaCMT3-dependent CHG methylation regulates fruit development in cultivated strawberry (Fragaria × ananassa Benihoppe). RNA sequencing and whole-genome bisulfite sequencing were performed across selected strawberry genotypes and fruit stages. Integrated methylome–transcriptome analyses identified loci associated with FaCMT3-dependent methylation and transcriptional regulation, including a restricted subset of SLEEPER-like genes. These datasets provide a resource for studying the epigenetic regulation of strawberry fruit development.

Study Protocol Type Term Source REF		CHMO	OBI	OBI	CHMO	MI
Study Protocol Description	<p>A total of 24 plant samples were collected, divided into 8 groups with 3 biological replicates per group (Facmt3-19F, Facmt3-19W, Facmt3-21F, Facmt3-22F, Facmt3-22W, FawtF, Fvcmt3-12, Fvwt). All fresh tissues were rapidly frozen and preserved at low temperature to block metabolic activity. QC samples were prepared by pooling equal volumes of extraction liquid from all test samples for system stability monitoring.</p>	<p>Ultra-high performance liquid chromatography (UPLC) was applied for metabolite separation. The separated components were sequentially introduced into tandem time-of-flight mass spectrometry. Both positive ion and negative ion electrospray modes were operated to capture full-spectrum chromatographic signals of metabolites. Total ion chromatogram (TIC) overlaps of QC samples were used to evaluate chromatographic reproducibility.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were processed to extract ion peak intensity information. All metabolite abundance values were normalized and standardized for downstream statistical analysis:</p><p>Multivariate modeling: PCA, PLS-DA, OPLS-DA for intergroup variation evaluation;</p><p>Differential screening: log2 fold change, P-value and VIP value transformation;</p><p>Z-score normalization for clustering heatmaps and trend clustering (Mfuzz fuzzy c-means);</p><p>Statistical permutation tests to avoid supervised model overfitting.</p>	<p>All experimental samples and mixed QC samples underwent unified metabolite extraction. Metabolites were extracted from plant tissue matrix to obtain crude extraction solutions, which were later used for subsequent LC-MS/MS instrumental analysis.</p>	<p>A UHPLC-tandem time-of-flight mass spectrometer was used for untargeted metabolome detection. Mass spectrum parameters including molecular weight, secondary fragmentation patterns and collision energy were recorded for each ion feature. Instrument stability was validated via QC correlation analysis, Hotelling’s T2 test, multivariate control charts and RSD statistics of ion peaks.</p>	<p>Metabolites were annotated against an in-house standard database with strict matching criteria: mass error &lt;10 ppm, matched retention time, secondary fragment spectra and collision energy. All annotation results were manually double-checked, and all identified metabolites reached Level 2 identification standard. A total of 1402 metabolites were annotated, which were further categorized by SuperClass, Class and SubClass chemical taxonomy for statistical analysis.</p>

Study Protocol Type Term Source REF	EFO	CHMO	OBI	EFO	CHMO	MI
Study Protocol Description	Rice plants (Oryza sativa L. Zhonghua 11; wild type and PpPEPC1b-overexpression lines OE8, OE15, OE33) were grown hydroponically under low-Pi (5 uM KH2PO4) for 40 days. Roots were rinsed and immersed in 50 mL ultrapure water for 24 h in the dark; the root exudate collection solution was measured for pH and filtered through 0.45 um membranes. Pooled QC samples were prepared by mixing equal aliquots of all samples.	Shimadzu LC-30A UPLC system with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (1.8 um, 2.1 mm x 100 mm) at 40 C. Mobile phase A: water with 0.1% formic acid; mobile phase B: acetonitrile with 0.1% formic acid. Linear gradient of 5%-95% B over 9 min, 1 min hold at 95% B, then re-equilibration at 5% B. Flow rate 0.35 mL/min; injection volume 2 uL.	Raw data were converted to mzXML format with ProteoWizard. Peaks were detected, aligned and retention-time corrected with XCMS in R; missing values were imputed with the K-nearest neighbour method.	Samples were thawed and vortexed for 30 s, frozen overnight at -80 C and vacuum freeze-dried. Lyophilized material was reconstituted in 70% methanol containing internal standard at a 30-fold concentration ratio (e.g. 9 mL sample + 300 uL extraction solvent; internal standard stock 1000 ug/mL in 70% methanol, diluted to 250 ug/mL). Mixtures were vortexed for 15 min, sonicated in an ice-water bath for 10 min, centrifuged at 12000 r/min at 4 C for 3 min, and the supernatant was filtered through a 0.22 um membrane into autosampler vials.	AB SCIEX TripleTOF 6600+ mass spectrometer in positive and negative electrospray ionisation (ESI) modes. Ion source gas 1 (GS1) 50 psi; ion source gas 2 (GS2) 60 psi; curtain gas (CUR) 25 psi; source temperature 550 C; ion spray voltage floating (ISVF) 5500 V (positive) and -4500 V (negative).	Metabolites were identified using metDNA (Metabolite Identification and Dysregulated Network Analysis) against an in-house reference database at Metware Biotechnology (Wuhan, China). Differentially accumulated metabolites were screened by variable importance in projection (VIP) > 1 and |log2 fold change| >= 1.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Plasma samples were collected from experimental animals following the established study protocol. Blood samples were collected into anticoagulant-containing tubes and centrifuged to isolate plasma. Plasma samples were immediately stored at −80°C until metabolomic analysis. Before extraction, plasma samples were thawed on ice and processed under standardized conditions.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Waters ACQUITY UPLC I-Class Plus system (Waters, USA). Plasma extracts were analyzed by ultra-performance liquid chromatography coupled with mass spectrometry (UPLC-MS). Chromatographic separation was achieved using a reversed-phase C18 column. The mobile phase composition, gradient program, column temperature, and injection volume were set according to the manufacturer's recommended metabolomics analysis workflow. The flow rate and chromatographic parameters were optimized for untargeted plasma metabolomic profiling.</p>	<p>Raw MS data were processed using Compound Discoverer software (version 3.3, Thermo Fisher Scientific) for peak detection, retention time alignment, and metabolite annotation. Metabolite identification was performed by matching against public databases including HMDB, mzCloud, and ChemSpider. The processed metabolite abundance matrix was normalized using probabilistic quotient normalization (PQN) and corrected for signal drift using QC-based robust LOESS signal correction (QC-RLSC). The normalized data were log10-transformed, median-centered, and Pareto-scaled for downstream statistical analyses.</p>	<p>Plasma samples were thawed at 4°C and extracted using a methanol/acetonitrile/water solvent mixture (4:2:1, v/v) containing internal standards for normalization. Samples were incubated at −20°C for 2 h to facilitate protein precipitation and metabolite extraction. Extracts were centrifuged at 25,000 × g for 15 min at 4°C, and the supernatants were collected. The extracts were dried under vacuum, reconstituted in methanol, and centrifuged again to remove particulates before UPLC-MS analysis. Pooled quality control (QC) samples were prepared by mixing aliquots from representative samples and were analyzed throughout the sequence to monitor analytical stability and reproducibility.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric analysis was performed using a SCIEX QTRAP 6500 Plus Triple Quadrupole Linear Ion Trap Mass Spectrometer coupled with the Waters ACQUITY UPLC I-Class Plus system. Metabolites were ionized using electrospray ionization (ESI) in both positive and negative ion modes. Untargeted MS data were acquired using full-scan MS and MS/MS acquisition. The mass range was set according to the instrument acquisition method. Quality control samples were analyzed throughout the sequence to monitor instrument stability and analytical reproducibility.</p>	<p>Raw MS data were processed using Compound Discoverer software (version 3.3, Thermo Fisher Scientific) for peak detection, retention time alignment, and feature grouping. Metabolite annotation was performed by matching detected features against multiple public databases, including the Human Metabolome Database (HMDB), mzCloud, and ChemSpider. Putative metabolite identification was based on accurate mass matching, retention time information, and MS/MS spectral similarity when available.</p>

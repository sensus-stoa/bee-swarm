Study Protocol Description	<p>Blood samples were collected from Malayan pangolins into EDTA tubes. Plasma was separated by centrifugation at 1,500 × g for 10 min at 4 °C, immediately frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at −80 °C until metabolomic analysis.</p>	<p>Untargeted metabolomics was performed using a Thermo Scientific Vanquish UHPLC system with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide column. Targeted metabolomics was performed using an Agilent 1290 Infinity II UHPLC system with RP and HILIC separation.</p>	<p>Raw untargeted LC-MS data were converted to mzXML format using ProteoWizard and processed for peak detection, alignment and quantification. Features with excessive missing values or poor reproducibility were removed, and the remaining data were normalized by total ion current.</p>	<p>For untargeted metabolomics and isotope tracing, 50 μL plasma was mixed with 200 μL extraction solvent containing isotope-labeled internal standards. For targeted metabolomics, 50 μL plasma was extracted with 200 μL precooled methanol containing internal standards. Pooled QC samples were prepared by mixing equal aliquots from all samples.</p>	<p>Untargeted metabolomics and isotope tracing were performed using an Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer in positive and negative ion modes. Targeted metabolite quantification was performed using a SCIEX Triple Quad 6500+ mass spectrometer with an ESI source in multiple reaction monitoring mode.</p>	<p>Metabolites were annotated using accurate mass, retention behavior and MS/MS information against the BiotreeDB database (version 4.0). For targeted analysis, metabolites were identified using authentic standards and quantified using internal standard calibration.</p>

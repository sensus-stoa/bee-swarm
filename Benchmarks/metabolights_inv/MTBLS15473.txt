Study Protocol Description	<p>A total of 104 human fecal samples were collected in China in 2024 from individuals classified as low or high responders to metformin. Each sample was assigned a unique identifier before untargeted LC-MS analysis. The available study documentation did not specify the collection device, detailed collection procedure, transport conditions, or storage temperature.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Thermo Scientific Vanquish Horizon UHPLC system equipped with an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (100 mm × 2.1 mm, 1.8 µm; Waters). Mobile phase A consisted of 95% water and 5% acetonitrile containing 0.1% formic acid, while mobile phase B consisted of 47.5% acetonitrile, 47.5% isopropanol, and 5% water containing 0.1% formic acid. The column temperature was maintained at 40°C, and the injection volume was 3 µL. The mobile-phase flow rate and gradient program were not specified in the experimental report.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were processed using Progenesis QI v3.0 (Waters Corporation, Milford, USA). Processing included baseline filtering, feature detection, peak integration, retention-time correction, and peak alignment across samples. The processed data were exported as a feature matrix containing retention time, m/z, and peak-intensity values for downstream statistical analysis.</p>	<p>Approximately 50 ± 5 mg of each sample was transferred into a 2 mL centrifuge tube containing a 6 mm grinding bead. A total of 400 µL of extraction solvent (methanol/water, 4:1, v/v), containing four internal standards including L-2-chlorophenylalanine (0.02 mg/mL), was added. Samples were homogenized for 6 min at −10°C and 50 Hz, followed by ultrasonic extraction for 30 min at 5°C and 40 kHz. After incubation at −20°C for 30 min, the samples were centrifuged at 13,000 × g for 15 min at 4°C. The supernatants were transferred into autosampler vials for LC-MS analysis. A pooled quality-control (QC) sample was prepared by combining 20 µL of supernatant from each sample and was analyzed periodically throughout the analytical sequence.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric analysis was performed using a Thermo Scientific Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Data were acquired separately in positive- and negative-ion modes over an m/z range of 70-1050. The sheath gas flow rate was 50 arbitrary units, the auxiliary gas flow rate was 13 arbitrary units, the heater temperature was 425°C, and the capillary temperature was 325°C. The spray voltage was set to 3500 V in positive-ion mode and -3500 V in negative-ion mode, and the S-lens RF level was 50. The normalized collision energies were 20%, 40%, and 60%. The resolutions were 60000 for full-MS scans and 7500 for MS/MS scans.</p>	<p>Metabolite features were annotated using Progenesis QI v3.0 by matching the acquired MS and MS/MS spectra against public metabolite databases, including the Human Metabolome Database (HMDB; https://www.hmdb.ca/) and METLIN (https://metlin.scripps.edu/), as well as an in-house spectral library. The precursor-ion mass tolerance was set to less than 10 ppm, and MS/MS fragmentation matching scores were used to support metabolite annotations. Where available, HMDB, LipidMaps, KEGG compound, CAS, molecular formula, adduct, retention-time, and mass-error information was recorded.</p>

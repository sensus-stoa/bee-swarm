Study Description	This study investigated metabolic alterations in an AOM/DSS-induced mouse colorectal tumor model following bacterial intervention with Faecalibacterium prausnitzii and Peptostreptococcus stomatis. Targeted bile acid profiling and untargeted metabolomics were performed using LC-MS on colorectal tumor tissues and fecal samples from control and intervention groups to characterize changes in metabolic profiles associated with bacterial intervention.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Colorectal tumor tissues and fecal samples were collected from female C57BL/6J mice subjected to AOM/DSS-induced colorectal tumorigenesis. Mice were assigned to control, Faecalibacterium prausnitzii, Peptostreptococcus stomatis, and combined Faecalibacterium prausnitzii plus Peptostreptococcus stomatis intervention groups. Samples were collected for targeted bile acid profiling and untargeted metabolomic analysis.</p>	<p>Liquid chromatography was performed using a Thermo Vanquish UHPLC system equipped with an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (2.1 × 100 mm, 1.8 µm; Waters). The column temperature was maintained at 40°C, with a flow rate of 0.3 mL/min and an injection volume of 2 µL. For positive ion mode, mobile phases were 0.1% formic acid in water (A) and 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile (B). The gradient was: 0–1 min, 10% B; 1–5 min, 10–98% B; 5–6.5 min, 98% B; 6.5–6.6 min, 98–10% B; and 6.6–8 min, 10% B. For negative ion mode, mobile phases were 5 mM ammonium formate in water (A) and acetonitrile (B), using the same gradient program.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were acquired in positive and negative ion modes for subsequent untargeted metabolomic data processing and analysis.</p>	<p>An appropriate amount of colorectal tumor tissue was transferred into a 2 mL centrifuge tube and extracted with 1000 µL tissue extraction solution consisting of 75% (9:1 methanol:chloroform) and 25% water. Samples were homogenized with steel balls at 50 Hz for 60 s twice, sonicated at room temperature for 30 min, and incubated on ice for 30 min. Samples were centrifuged at 12,000 rpm and 4°C for 10 min. The supernatant was collected, concentrated to dryness, and reconstituted in 200 µL of 50% acetonitrile containing 4 ppm 2-chloro-L-phenylalanine. The reconstituted samples were filtered through a 0.22 µm membrane before LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was performed using a Thermo Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Data were acquired separately in positive and negative ion modes using Full MS-ddMS2 acquisition. Spray voltages were 3.50 kV for ESI positive mode and −2.50 kV for ESI negative mode. The sheath gas and auxiliary gas were set to 40 arb and 10 arb, respectively, and the capillary temperature was 325°C. Full MS scans were acquired over m/z 100–1000 at a resolution of 60,000 FWHM. Data-dependent MS/MS was performed at a resolution of 15,000 FWHM with a normalized collision energy of 30%, selecting the top four ions per cycle with dynamic exclusion.</p>	<p>Metabolite features detected by untargeted LC-MS analysis were subjected to subsequent metabolite annotation and identification.</p>

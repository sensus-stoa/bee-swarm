Study Identifier	MTBLS15725
Study Title	Metabolomic profiling of seedless oil palm mesocarp during fruit development
Study Description	This study investigated developmental changes in the mesocarp metabolome of seedless oil palm (Elaeis guineensis) at 95, 125, and 185 days after pollination. Mesocarp samples from three developmental stages (FL1, FL2, and FL3) were analyzed by untargeted HILIC LC-MS/MS using two biological replicates per stage. The dataset was generated to characterize developmental metabolite abundance patterns, with particular attention to fatty-acid- and lipid-metabolism-related features associated with the late-stage increase in the oleic-acid-associated LC-MS/MS signal.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Seedless oil palm mesocarp samples were collected at three developmental stages corresponding to FL1 (95 DAP), FL2 (125 DAP), and FL3 (185 DAP). For each stage, fruits were collected from independent biological samples. The mesocarp tissues were carefully separated, immediately frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at −80°C prior to metabolomic extraction and LC-MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p> Chromatographic separation was performed using an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 C18 column (2.1 × 100 mm, 1.8 μm; Waters). The mobile phase consisted of water containing 0.1% formic acid (A) and acetonitrile containing 0.1% formic acid (B). The gradient program was as follows: 0–5 min, 5%–65% B; 5–6 min, 65%–99% B; 6–7.5 min, 99% B; 7.5–7.6 min, 99%–5% B; 7.6–10 min, 5% B. The flow rate was 0.4 mL/min, the column temperature was maintained at 40°C, and the injection volume was 4 μL.</p>	<p> dRaw LC-MS/MS data were processed using a metabolomics data analysis pipeline for peak detection, alignment, and feature extraction. The resulting data were subjected to quality control procedures and exported for downstream statistical analysis and metabolite annotation.</p><p><br></p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted from seedless oil palm mesocarp samples using a methanol-based extraction method. The samples were homogenized, extracted with organic solvent, centrifuged, and the collected supernatants were prepared for LC-MS/MS analysis. Quality control samples were prepared by pooling aliquots of individual samples and analyzed together with experimental samples.</p>	<p> dMass spectrometry analysis was performed using a high-resolution LC-MS/MS system operated in alternating positive and negative ion modes. The mass range was set from m/z 100 to 1000. The ion source parameters and instrument settings were optimized according to the manufacturer's recommendations for metabolomic profiling.</p><p>escription</p>	<p>Metabolite features were annotated based on accurate mass, retention time, and MS/MS fragmentation information. Metabolite identification was performed by matching experimental data against public databases, including HMDB and KEGG databases, combined with MS/MS spectral information.</p>

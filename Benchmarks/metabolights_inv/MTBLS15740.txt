Study Protocol Description	<p>Mouse fecal, intestinal-content and serum specimens were included in this untargeted LC-MS study. Biological samples, pooled quality-control samples and analytical blanks are identified in the sample metadata. The analytical laboratory reports do not specify the animal sampling procedure, sampling time points or pre-extraction storage conditions.</p>	<p>Chromatography was performed on a Thermo Fisher Scientific Vanquish UHPLC system with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (2.1 x 100 mm, 1.8 um). The column temperature was 40 degrees C, flow rate was 0.3 mL/min, and injection volume was 2 uL. For positive-ion analysis, mobile phase A was water with 0.1% formic acid and B was acetonitrile with 0.1% formic acid. For negative-ion analysis, A was 5 mM aqueous ammonium formate and B was acetonitrile. The gradient was 8% B at 0-1 min, 8-98% B at 1-8 min, 98% B at 8-10 min, 98-8% B at 10-10.1 min, and 8% B at 10.1-12 min.</p>	<p>Raw mass-spectrometry files were converted to mzXML with MSConvert in ProteoWizard v3.0.8789. Features were detected, retention times corrected, and peaks aligned using XCMS v3.12.0 in R with the centWave method (ppm=15, peakwidth=5-30 s, bw=2, mzwid=0.015, mzdiff=0.01). QC-based support vector regression was used to correct systematic variation. Features with a relative standard deviation greater than 30% in QC samples were excluded from subsequent analysis.</p>	<p>Fecal and intestinal-content samples were extracted with 600 uL methanol containing 4 ppm 2-chloro-L-phenylalanine. Samples were vortexed for 30 s, bead-milled at 50 Hz for 120 s, sonicated at room temperature for 10 min, and centrifuged at 12,000 rpm and 4 degrees C for 10 min. Supernatants were filtered through 0.22 um membranes. Serum samples were thawed at 4 degrees C and vortexed for 1 min. An aliquot was mixed with 400 uL methanol, vortexed for 1 min, and centrifuged at 12,000 rpm and 4 degrees C for 10 min. The supernatant was dried, reconstituted in 150 uL of 80% aqueous methanol containing 20 ppm 2-chloro-L-phenylalanine, and filtered through a 0.22 um membrane. Pooled QC samples were prepared by combining aliquots of processed study samples.</p>	<p>Mass spectra were acquired on a Thermo Fisher Scientific Q Exactive mass spectrometer with an electrospray ionization source in separate positive- and negative-ion runs. Spray voltage was 3.50 kV in positive mode and -2.50 kV in negative mode. Sheath gas and auxiliary gas settings were 40 and 10 arbitrary units, respectively, and capillary temperature was 325 degrees C. Full-scan MS1 data were acquired over m/z 100-1000 at a resolution of 70,000. Data-dependent MS/MS selected the top 10 ions per cycle, using HCD at 30 eV and an MS/MS resolution of 17,500; dynamic exclusion was automatic.</p>	<p>Putative metabolite annotations were assigned using accurate precursor masses, adduct information and available MS/MS fragment spectra. Spectra were compared with HMDB, MassBank, LIPID MAPS, mzCloud, KEGG and the analytical laboratory's in-house reference database. A mass-error threshold of less than 30 ppm was used for database matching; MS/MS fragments were compared with reference spectra where available.</p>

Study Title	13(S)-HODE Generated by Rhizopus microsporus var. rhizopodiformis Aggravates MASLD via the Elane-PAR2-SREBP-1c Axis
Study Description	Abstract Objective:Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease (MASLD) poses a major public health challenge. Increasing data suggest a role of gut fungi in liver disease, yet their diversity and biological function in MASLD are still poorly understood. Methods:Internal transcribed spacer (ITS) sequencing was conducted on fecal samples from 107 individuals with MASLD and 120 matched healthy controls (HC). A high-fat diet (HFD)-induced MASLD mouse model was established, and multi-omics analyses, including liver transcriptomic sequencing, qPCR, WB, and immunofluorescence, were performed to elucidate the molecular mechanisms underlying key gut fungi associated with MASLD. Microbial-derived metabolites involved in MASLD were investigated through multi-omics analyses in vitro and in vivo. Results:MASLD patients showed significantly reduced fungal diversity and richness compared with HC. The gut mycobiota of MASLD patients exhibited a marked increase in the pathogenic genus Rhizopus, particularly Rhizopus microsporus var. rhizopodiformis and R. microsporus var. chinensis, which were positively associated with hepatic steatosis and body mass index (BMI). Fecal microbiota transplantation from MASLD patients into antibiotic-treated mice induced MASLD-like phenotypes, while amphotericin B treatment mitigated disease progression. Oral administration of R. microsporus var. rhizopodiformis in HFD-fed mice aggravated gut dysbiosis, disrupted intestinal barrier integrity, and activated hepatic SREBP-1c signaling, leading to upregulation of key lipogenic enzymes ACC1 and FASN. These changes were mediated through a pro-inflammatory cascade involving the hepatic LPS–TLR4–NF-κB axis and enhanced neutrophil degranulation. 13(S)-HODE, potentially identified as a key metabolite produced by R. microsporus var. rhizopodiformis, aggravated MASLD. 13(S)-HODE promotes neutrophil trafficking to the liver, where released elastase (Elane) directly cleaves and activates the master transcription factor SREBP-1c via a non-canonical PAR2 signaling pathway, leading to uncontrolled hepatic de novo lipogenesis. Conclusion:Our findings suggest that Rhizopus microsporus var. rhizopodiformis and its candidate metabolite 13(S)-HODE are associated with MASLD progression, potentially via the Elane-PAR2-SREBP-1c axis. These observations provide a preliminary basis for further investigating the role of gut fungi in MASLD pathogenesis.

Study Protocol Description	<p>All sample pretreatment procedures were performed on ice.</p>	<p>For the polar system, chromatographic separation of the target compounds was performed on an Agilent 1290 Infinity II ultra-high performance liquid chromatography (UHPLC) system equipped with an </p><p>Atlantis Premier BEH Z-HILIC Column (1.7 µm, 2.1 mm × 150 mm). The mobile phase A consisted of ultrapure water/acetonitrile (10:90, v/v) containing 10 mmol/L ammonium acetate, and mobile phase B was ultrapure water/acetonitrile (90:10, v/v) containing 10 mmol/L ammonium acetate. The temperature of the sample tray was set at 6 °C with an injection volume of 2 μL (Note: the original volume value was garbled). </p><p>For the non-polar system, the target compounds were subjected to chromatographic separation on the same Agilent 1290 Infinity II UHPLC system with an ACQUITY UPLC BEH C18 Column (1.7 µm, 2.1 mm × 150 mm). The mobile phase A was ultrapure water with 0.1% formic acid, and mobile phase B was methanol/ultrapure water (95:5, v/v) containing 10 mmol/L ammonium formate. The temperature of the sample tray was maintained at 6 °C, with an injection volume of 1 μL (Note: the original volume value was garbled).</p>	<p>The raw data were converted to the mzXML format using ProteoWizard and processed with an in-house program.</p>	<p>The frozen samples were thawed on ice and vortexed for 30 s to homogenize thoroughly; 100 μL of each liquid sample was aliquoted into 1.5 mL Eppendorf (EP) tubes, followed by the addition of 400 μL of methanol as the extraction solution with subsequent vortexing for complete mixing. The mixtures were sonicated in an ice-water bath for 15 min and then incubated at -40 °C for 1 h, prior to centrifugation at 12,000 rpm and 4 °C for 15 min. The resulting supernatants were carefully transferred into injection vials for instrumental analysis. In addition, equal volumes of supernatants from all individual samples were pooled to prepare quality control (QC) samples, which were analyzed under the same instrumental conditions for quality assessment.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric data acquisition for full scan MS and MS/MS spectra was conducted on an Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer under the control of Xcalibur software (Version 4.4, Thermo Fisher Scientific). The detailed mass spectrometry parameters were set as follows: Sheath gas flow rate: 50 Arb; Aux gas flow rate: 15 Arb; Capillary temperature: 320 °C; Full MS resolution: 60000; MS/MS resolution: 15000; Collision energy: SNCE 20/30/40; Spray Voltage: 3.8 kV (positive ion mode) or -3.4 kV (negative ion mode).</p>	<p>which was developed using R and based on XCMS, for feature detection, extraction, alignment, and integration.</p>

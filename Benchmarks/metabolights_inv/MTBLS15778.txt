Study Protocol Description	<p>Human podocytes were stimulated for 24 h with immune complexes isolated from patients with systemic lupus erythematosus or with IgG from healthy donors as a control. Cells were collected after treatment and stored at -80 °C until metabolite extraction.</p>	<p>Samples were separated on a Waters ACQUITY Premier HSS T3 column (1.8 μm, 2.1 × 100 mm) at 40 °C with a flow rate of 0.4 mL/min and an injection volume of 4 μL. Mobile phase A was 0.1% formic acid in water and mobile phase B was 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile. The gradient was 5-20% B from 0 to 2 min, 20–60% B from 2 to 5 min, 60-99% B from 5 to 6 min, 99% B from 6 to 7.5 min, followed by a return to 5% B at 7.6 min and a hold until 10 min.</p>	<p>The LC-MS data were acquired using Analyst TF 1.7.1 and converted to the mzML format using ProteoWizard. Feature extraction, peak alignment, normalization and metabolite annotation were performed using the Metware data-processing workflow and MetaboAnalystR.</p>	<p>A 500 μL volume of methanol (4:1, v/v) containing internal standard was added to each sample. Samples were vortexed, subjected to three freeze–thaw cycles, and centrifuged at 12,000 × g for 10 min at 4 °C. A 200 μL aliquot of the supernatant was transferred for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>LC-MS/MS data were acquired using a SCIEX TripleTOF 6600+ mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Data were acquired in both positive and negative ion modes using information-dependent acquisition (IDA), with an m/z scan range of 50-1000. Data acquisition was performed using Analyst TF 1.7.1.</p>	<p>Metabolite annotation was performed using the Metware data-processing workflow. The processed result tables contained metabolite names, molecular formulas, mass-to-charge ratios, retention times, adduct and charge information, and database identifiers where available. HMDB identifiers were retained when provided, while features without HMDB identifiers were retained using their original feature IDs. SMILES and InChI identifiers were left blank when not available in the source data.</p>

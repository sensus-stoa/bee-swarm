Study Title	Integrated multi-omics reveals gut microbiota–metabolic mechanisms underlying the neuroprotective effects of Xijiao Dihuang Decoction against cerebral ischemia–reperfusion injury
Study Description	This study investigated the effects of Xijiao Dihuang Decoction (XDD) on cerebral ischemia–reperfusion injury using a middle cerebral artery occlusion/reperfusion rat model. Untargeted LC–MS metabolomics was performed on fecal samples to characterize metabolic alterations associated with MCAO/R and XDD treatment. Metabolomic profiles were acquired in both positive- and negative-ion modes and integrated with gut microbiome data to explore microbiota–metabolite associations.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Fecal samples were collected under sterile conditions and immediately stored at −80 °C until untargeted metabolomics analysis.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Thermo Scientific Vanquish UHPLC system equipped with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide column (2.1 × 50 mm, 1.7 μm). Mobile phase A consisted of water containing 25 mmol/L ammonium acetate and 25 mmol/L ammonium hydroxide, and mobile phase B was acetonitrile. The autosampler temperature was maintained at 4 °C and the injection volume was 2 μL.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Raw LC–MS data were converted to mzXML format using ProteoWizard v3.0.24054 and processed using an in-house R-based pipeline. Features with an RSD &gt;30% or a missing rate &gt;50% were excluded. Missing values were imputed using one-half of the minimum observed value, followed by total ion current normalization. </p>	<p>Approximately 25 ± 1 mg of each fecal sample was mixed with 500 μL of extraction solvent containing isotope-labeled internal standards (methanol/acetonitrile/water, 2:2:1, v/v/v). Samples were vortexed for 30 s, homogenized at 35 Hz for 4 min, and sonicated in an ice-water bath for 5 min. The homogenization–sonication procedure was repeated three times. Samples were then incubated at −40 °C for 1 h. Subsequently, 200 μL of the extract was filtered through a 0.22-μm filter plate under positive pressure (6 psi for 180 s). Pooled quality-control samples were prepared by mixing equal aliquots from all experimental samples.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric data were acquired using a Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer in both positive- and negative-ion modes. The sheath gas and auxiliary gas flow rates were set to 50 and 15 Arb, respectively, and the capillary temperature was 320 °C. Full-MS and MS/MS resolutions were 60,000 and 15,000, respectively. Stepped normalized collision energies were set at 20, 30, and 40. The spray voltage was 3.8 kV in positive-ion mode and −3.4 kV in negative-ion mode. The scan range was m/z 70–1200.</p>	<p>Metabolites were annotated against BiotreeDB v3.0 using retention time, precursor-ion MS1 information, and MS/MS fragmentation spectra. Identification confidence was classified according to the Metabolomics Standards Initiative: Level 1, matching of MS1, MS2, and retention time to authentic standards; Level 2, matching of MS1 and MS2 to public databases; Level 3, matching of MS1, MS2, and predicted retention time to theoretical databases; and Level 4, unknown features.</p>

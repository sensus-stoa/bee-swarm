Study Title	Metabolomic analysis of inhibited pollen tube growth in unilateral crosses of Clematis
Study Description	<p>This study utilized broad-target metabolomics to analyze the metabolic changes underlying the inhibition of pollen tube growth in Clematis one-way hybrid crosses with low compatibility. It identified differentially accumulated metabolites and elucidated the key metabolic pathways involved in one-way hybridization barriers, thereby providing a metabolic basis for interspecific hybrid breeding in Clematis.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Pistil tissue samples from clematis were collected at various time points after pollination. Immediately after removal, the samples were rapidly frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored in a -80°C ultra-low temperature freezer for future use.</p>	<p>An ultra-high-performance liquid chromatography (UHPLC) system was used, with a C18 column for component separation; the liquid-phase separation was performed using the appropriate mobile phase system and elution program.</p>	<p>Perform preprocessing operations on raw mass spectrometry data, including peak alignment, peak identification, signal filtering, and data normalization.</p>	<p>Grind and pulverize the frozen pistil samples, extract metabolites using a methanol-water solution, vortex thoroughly, centrifuge, and then aspirate the supernatant for subsequent instrumental analysis.</p>	<p>Using a triple quadrupole mass spectrometer, collect mass spectrometry signals of metabolites in both positive and negative ion modes.</p>	<p>Using the project’s in-house metabolite database and comparing it with the mass spectrometry data of reference standards, complete the qualitative annotation of metabolites.</p>

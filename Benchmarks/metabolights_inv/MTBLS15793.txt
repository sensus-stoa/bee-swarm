Study Protocol Description	<p>The experimental materials were two-year-old healthy Bougainvillea ‘Imperial Thai Delight’ plants, cultivated in a greenhouse with unified water and fertilizer management. Four groups were set according to soil water content: control (C, &gt;40%), light (L, 30%–40%), moderate (M, 20%–30%), and severe (S, 10%–20%) water deficit stress.</p><p><br></p><p>Samples were collected every 3 days until flower bud differentiation was completed. Mature functional leaves from the middle of branches were selected as materials. For metabolome analysis, collected samples were immediately snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -80℃ ultra-low temperature freezer until analysis.</p>	<p>An ultra-high-performance liquid chromatography (UHPLC) system was used, with a C18 column for component separation; the liquid-phase separation was performed using the appropriate mobile phase system and elution program.</p>	<p>Perform preprocessing operations on raw mass spectrometry data, including peak alignment, peak identification, signal filtering, and data normalization.</p>	<p>Grind and pulverize the frozen leaf samples, extract metabolites using a methanol-water solution, vortex thoroughly, centrifuge, and then aspirate the supernatant for subsequent instrumental analysis.</p>	<p>Using a triple quadrupole mass spectrometer, collect mass spectrometry signals of metabolites in both positive and negative ion modes.</p>	<p>Using the project’s in-house metabolite database and comparing it with the mass spectrometry data of reference standards, complete the qualitative annotation of metabolites.</p>

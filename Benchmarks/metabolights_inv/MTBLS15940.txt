Study Protocol Description	Mice received four drinking water types for 5 months. At the endpoint, colonic contents were collected aseptically, snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at -80°C for metabolomic profiling.	For solid colonic contents, 100 mg sample was placed in a 2 mL centrifuge tube with one 6 mm diameter grinding bead. Metabolites were extracted with 800 uL of methanol/water (4:1, v/v) containing four internal standards including L-2-chlorophenylalanine (0.02 mg/mL). Samples were ground in a frozen tissue grinder for 6 min (-10°C, 50 Hz), followed by low-temperature ultrasonic extraction for 30 min (5°C, 40 kHz). Samples were left at -20°C for 30 min and centrifuged at 4°C, 13000 g for 15 min. The supernatant was transferred to an injection vial for LC-MS/MS analysis. No derivatization was performed.	Metabolites were separated on a Thermo Scientific UHPLC-Orbitrap Exploris 240 system equipped with an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (1.7 µm, 2.1 mm x 100 mm, Waters). Mobile phase A was 0.1% formic acid in water/acetonitrile (95:5, v/v). Mobile phase B was 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile/isopropanol/water (47.5:47.5:5, v/v/v). The flow rate was 0.40 mL/min and the column temperature was 40°C. The injection volume was 3 uL. The LC gradient was the standard Majorbio untargeted metabolomics gradient; the exact time/%B program should be confirmed from the raw data acquisition method.	Untargeted metabolomic profiling was performed on a Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Exploris 240 mass spectrometer with an electrospray ionization source operating in positive and negative modes. The scan range was m/z 70-1050. Positive ion spray voltage was 3500 V and negative ion spray voltage was -3000 V. Sheath gas was 50 arb and auxiliary heating gas was 13 arb. The ion source heater temperature was 450°C. Normalized collision energy was 20-40-60 V for MS/MS. Data were acquired in data-dependent acquisition (DDA) mode.	Raw LC-MS data were processed with Progenesis QI (Waters Corporation, Milford, USA) for baseline filtering, peak picking, integration, retention time correction, and peak alignment. A data matrix containing retention time, m/z, and peak intensity was generated. MS and MS/MS spectra were matched against HMDB, Metlin, and the Majorbio in-house database. The data matrix was uploaded to the Majorbio Cloud Platform (cloud.majorbio.com). Features were retained if detected in at least 80% of samples in any group. Missing values were filled with the minimum value. Total sum normalization was applied. Features with QC RSD >30% were removed, followed by log10 transformation. PCA and OPLS-DA were performed using the R ropls package (Version 1.6.2) with 7-cycle cross-validation.	Metabolites were annotated against HMDB, Metlin, KEGG, LipidMaps, and the Majorbio in-house database. Differential metabolites were identified with VIP >1 from the OPLS-DA model and p <0.05 from Student's t-test. KEGG pathway annotation and enrichment analysis were performed, and Fisher's exact test was used to obtain the most relevant biological pathways. Key bile acids and tryptophan metabolites were targeted for validation.

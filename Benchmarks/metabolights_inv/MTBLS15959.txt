Study Protocol Description	<p>Male KM mice (5 weeks old) were fed a high-purine diet with potassium oxonate (HPD/PO) for 2 weeks to induce hyperuricemia. Subsequently, treatments (CON, HPD/PO, 11C, ALLO, or 11C-MVs) were administered for an additional 5 weeks. At the end of the experiment, cecal contents were collected, immediately snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at -80°C until untargeted metabolomics analysis. Two independent animal experiments were performed as describ</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed on a Thermo Scientific Vanquish UHPLC System equipped with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (1.8 µm, 2.1 × 100 mm). The mobile phase consisted of 0.1% formic acid in water (A) and 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile (B) with a gradient elution. The flow rate and column temperature were maintained at 0.3 mL/min and 40 °C, respectively.</p>	<p>Raw data were processed using the Majorbio Cloud Platform. Peak detection, alignment, and retention time correction were performed. Data matrices were normalized and used for subsequent multivariate statistical analysis.</p>	<p>Cecal contents were mixed with precooled extraction solvent (MeOH/H2O, 70:30, v/v) containing internal standards. The mixture was vortexed, sonicated, and centrifuged. The supernatant was collected and filtered through a 0.22 µm membrane for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was performed using a Thermo Scientific Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Data were acquired in both positive and negative ion modes with a scan m/z range of 100-1500.</p>	<p>Metabolites were identified by matching the accurate mass and MS/MS fragmentation patterns against public databases including HMDB, KEGG, and LipidMaps.</p>

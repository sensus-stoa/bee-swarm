Study Title	Targeted metabolomics analysis of 146 central carbon metabolites in J2 macrophages infected with CPXV
Study Description	<p>This study contains targeted metabolomics data from mouse J2 macrophages that were uninfected or infected with CPXV (moi 0.5) for 24 h. Samples were analysed using UHPLC-MRM-MS with HILIC and reversed-phase chromatography on a SCIEX Triple Quad 6500+ mass spectrometer. The dataset includes mzML files and quantitative metabolite result tables.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Samples originated from mouse J2 macrophages. The study included two sample groups, CPXV24h and UI, with six samples in each group. Sample identifiers were CPXV24h_1 to CPXV24h_6 and U2_1 to U2_6, respectively.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using an Agilent 1290 Infinity II UHPLC system.</p><p><br></p><p>For reversed-phase (RP) analysis, an ACQUITY UPLC BEH C18 column (1.7 µm, 2.1 × 150 mm; Waters) was used. Mobile phase A was ultrapure water containing 0.1% formic acid. Mobile phase B was methanol/ultrapure water (95:5) containing 10 mmol/L ammonium formate.</p><p><br></p><p>For HILIC analysis, an Atlantis Premier BEH Z-HILIC column (1.7 µm, 2.1 × 150 mm; Waters) was used. Mobile phase A was ultrapure water/acetonitrile (90:10) containing 10 mmol/L ammonium acetate. Mobile phase B was ultrapure water/acetonitrile (10:90) containing 10 mmol/L ammonium acetate.</p><p><br></p><p>The sample tray temperature was maintained at 6°C, and the injection volume was 2 µL.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric data acquisition and quantitative analysis were performed using SCIEX Analyst Work Station Software (version 1.7.3) and BioBud (version 2.0.4).</p>	<p>Cell samples were extracted with 500 µL of methanol/water (4:1, v/v) containing internal standards and pre-cooled to −40°C, and transferred to fresh microcentrifuge tubes. Samples underwent three freeze–thaw cycles using liquid nitrogen, followed by sonication in an ice-water bath for 20 min and incubation at −40°C for 1 h. Samples were centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 15 min at 4°C, and the supernatants were transferred to sample vials for analysis. Sample preparation was performed on ice.</p><p><br></p><p>Individual standard stock solutions were prepared at 10 mmol/L in 10 mL volumetric flasks. Aliquots of the stock solutions were combined to prepare a mixed standard solution, which was serially diluted to obtain calibration solutions. The calibration solutions contained an isotopically labelled internal standard mixture at the same concentration as in the samples.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric analysis was performed using a SCIEX Triple Quad 6500+ triple quadrupole mass spectrometer equipped with an IonDrive Turbo V electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Data were acquired in multiple reaction monitoring (MRM) mode with positive and negative ionization.</p><p><br></p><p>The ion spray voltage was +5500 V in positive mode and −4500 V in negative mode. The source temperature was 400°C. Curtain gas pressure was 35 psi, and ion source gas 1 and ion source gas 2 were both set to 50 psi.</p>	<p>Targeted LC-MS/MS analysis was performed using multiple reaction monitoring (MRM). Compound names, molecular formulae and database identifiers were obtained from the supplied Targeted_Analyte_Information.xlsx. The supplied MRM_Parameters.xlsx contains precursor ions, product ions, ion polarity, and quantifier/qualifier transitions. Retention times and identification confidence levels were not included in the supplied result tables.</p>

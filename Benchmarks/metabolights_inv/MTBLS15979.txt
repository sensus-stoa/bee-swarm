Study Identifier	MTBLS15979
Study Title	CRISPR-Cas9 knockout of WS1A/WS1B alleviates postharvest green vein defect via tissue-specific chloroplast degradation in flue-cured tobacco
Study Description	This dataset contains tissue-resolved untargeted metabolome data from flue-cured tobacco cultivar Honghuadajinyuan (wild-type) and transgene-free ws1 mutants generated by CRISPR-Cas9 knockout of homeologous WS1A/WS1B. Multiple tobacco tissues were profiled to capture metabolite alterations triggered by gene knockout. The dataset covers metabolites belonging to alkaloid, phenylpropanoid, flavonoid and jasmonate-related pathways. Raw LC-MS spectra and processed metabolite abundance tables are provided for comparative metabolic analysis between wild-type and ws1 mutant plants.

Study Protocol Type Term Source REF		CHMO	OBI	EFO	CHMO	MI
Study Protocol Description	<p>Tobacco tissues (leaf lamina, main vein, stem) were harvested from Nicotiana tabacum cv. Honghuadajinyuan wild-type and transgene-free ws1 CRISPR knockout mutants. Three biological replicates were collected for each genotype × tissue group. Fresh samples were immediately frozen in liquid nitrogen after dissection, then stored at -80°C until metabolite extraction.</p>	<p>The chromatographic separation was performed on a C18 column. Mobile phase A was water with 0.1% formic acid, mobile phase B was acetonitrile with 0.1% formic acid. The gradient elution program: 0-1 min, 5% B; 1-8.5 min, 5-95% B; 8.5-10 min, 95% B; 10.1-12 min, 5% B. The flow rate was set at 0.3 mL/min, column temperature was 40 °C.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was performed by matching accurate mass, retention time and MS/MS fragments against metabolite databases. Metabolite annotation confidence was assigned according to metabolite identification standards.</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted with pre-cooled methanol and water solvent, and internal standard solution was added. After extraction and centrifugation, the supernatant was prepared for LC-MS injection.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry detection was performed in both positive and negative ion modes with auxiliary gas heater. Full scan acquisition was applied. The m/z range was 50-1050 for both positive and negative ion modes. Full-scan resolution was set for data collection.</p>	<p>Metabolite annotation was achieved by matching accurate mass, experimental retention time and MS/MS fragmentation spectra against the BGI Metabolome Database, mzCloud spectral library and ChemSpider database for molecular formula verification. Annotated metabolites were mapped to the KEGG database for pathway enrichment analysis using hypergeometric test.</p>

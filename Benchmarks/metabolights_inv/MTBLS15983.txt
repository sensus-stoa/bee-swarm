Study Identifier	MTBLS15983
Study Title	Collection and Analysis of Root Exudates
Study Description	Exposed peach tree roots were selected and gently rinsed with tap water to avoid mechanical damage to the root tissue structure. The treated roots were completely immersed in a 50 mL centrifuge tube filled with ultrapure water. The tube mouth was sealed with Parafilm, and the tube was wrapped in aluminum foil for light protection. The centrifuge tube was then returned to the original soil environment and covered with soil for in situ incubation. After 24 h, root exudate samples were collected and cryopreserved at −80 °C until further use.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Peach roots were gently rinsed with tap water to remove soil without damaging the root tissues. The roots were immersed in 50 mL centrifuge tubes filled with ultrapure water, sealed with Parafilm, wrapped in aluminum foil, and returned to the original soil environment for in situ incubation. After 24 h, root exudates were collected.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Thermo Scientific Vanquish UHPLC System equipped with an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 C18 column (1.8 μm, 2.1 mm × 100 mm; Waters) in reverse-phase mode. Mobile-phase and gradient conditions followed the standard method of the metabolomics service provider.</p>	<p>Raw data were processed using Progenesis QI (Waters Corporation). Features with more than 20% missing values were removed, remaining missing values were imputed, and data were normalized and log10-transformed. Features with a QC relative standard deviation greater than 30% were removed. Metabolite annotation was performed using HMDB, Metlin and KEGG databases.</p>	<p>Root exudate samples were prepared for non-targeted LC-MS/MS analysis according to the standard sample preparation protocol provided by the metabolomics service provider. Pooled quality-control samples were prepared and analyzed for quality assessment.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was performed using a Thermo Scientific Exploris 120 mass spectrometer with electrospray ionization. Data were acquired in both positive and negative ion modes over an m/z range of 70–1050. Positive- and negative-ion data were recorded as separate assays.</p>	<p>Metabolite features were detected and annotated using Progenesis QI (Waters Corporation). Metabolite identification was based on MS/MS spectral matching against the HMDB and Metlin databases, together with an in-house compound library. KEGG was used for pathway annotation and metabolite assignment.</p>

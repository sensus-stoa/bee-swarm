Study Protocol Description	<p>The pathogen was activated on CPDA plates at 28 °C for 7 days. A 4-mm-diameter agar plug containing a pathogen colony was inoculated on the left side of each plate. Streptomyces KH13 was streaked 5 cm away from the pathogen to establish a head-to-head culture. Plates inoculated with KH13 alone served as blank controls. Each group consisted of three biological replicates and was incubated at 28 °C. After 6 days, when clear and stable antagonistic zones had formed, KH13 cells were scraped from the confrontation and control plates. Approximately 0.1 g of each sample was collected, flash-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at −80 °C.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using an ExionLC™ AD UPLC system equipped with an Agilent SB-C18 column (1.8 μm, 2.1 mm × 100 mm). The mobile phases were water containing 0.1% formic acid and acetonitrile containing 0.1% formic acid. The acetonitrile proportion was increased linearly from 5% to 95% over 9 min, maintained at 95% for 1 min, decreased to 5% from 10.00 to 11.10 min, and equilibrated at 5% until 14 min. The flow rate was 0.35 mL/min, the column temperature was 40 °C, and the injection volume was 2 μL.</p>	<p>Raw mass spectrometry data were processed using Analyst 1.6.3 and MultiQuant software. Metabolite signals were integrated and corrected based on chromatographic peak areas. Missing values were imputed as one-fifth of the minimum detected value for each metabolite. Quality-control coefficients of variation were calculated, and metabolites with QC CV values below 0.5 were retained for downstream analysis.</p>	<p>Samples were vacuum freeze-dried for 63 h using a Scientz-100F freeze dryer and ground at 30 Hz for 1.5 min using an MM 400 grinder (Retsch). Approximately 30 mg of dried sample powder was extracted with 1500 μL of pre-cooled 70% methanol-water containing internal standards. Samples were vortexed for 30 s every 30 min for a total of six cycles, centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 3 min, and the supernatants were filtered through 0.22 μm membrane filters before UPLC-MS/MS analysis. Pooled quality-control samples were prepared from sample extracts.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was performed using a SCIEX triple-quadrupole tandem mass spectrometer with electrospray ionization. Data were acquired in multiple reaction monitoring (MRM) mode in separate positive- and negative-ion assays. The ion spray voltage was 5500 V in positive mode and −4500 V in negative mode. The source temperature was 500 °C. Ion source gas 1, ion source gas 2, and curtain gas were set to 50, 60, and 25 psi, respectively. Collision gas was nitrogen at a medium setting. Declustering potentials and collision energies were optimized for individual MRM transitions.</p>	<p>Metabolites were identified using MS/MS information against the MWDB (Metware Database) library. Isotope signals, duplicate signals containing K+, Na+, or NH4+ adducts, and fragment ions derived from larger molecules were removed during annotation. Metabolite abundances were quantified using MRM chromatographic peak areas.</p>

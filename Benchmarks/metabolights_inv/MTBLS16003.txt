Study Protocol Description	<p>Placentae were collected from pigs (Sus scrofa). The experimental group received thiamine supplementation. Collected placental tissues were immediately snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at -80°C until LC-MS metabolomics analysis.</p>	<p>Liquid chromatography was performed on a Vanquish UHPLC system using an ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide column (1.7 μm, 2.1 mm × 100 mm) at 25°C. The mobile phase consisted of water containing 25 mM ammonium acetate and 25 mM ammonium hydroxide (A) and acetonitrile (B). The gradient program was as follows: 0–0.5 min, 95% B; 0.5–5 min, linear decrease from 95% to 65% B; 5–5.5 min, linear decrease from 65% to 40% B; 5.5–6 min, linear increase from 40% to 95% B; 6–8 min, held at 95% B. The flow rate was 0.5 mL/min, injection volume was 2 μL, and the autosampler was maintained at 4°C. Samples were analyzed in randomized order with QC injections interspersed throughout the run.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data files (.raw) were converted to mzXML format using ProteoWizard. Peak alignment, retention time correction, and peak area extraction were performed using XCMS software. Metabolite identification was conducted based on local standard databases. Data preprocessing included filtering of ions with missing values &gt;50%, imputation of missing values using K-nearest neighbors (KNN), and removal of features with relative standard deviation (RSD) &gt;50%. Quality evaluation was performed using six pooled QC samples, followed by statistical analysis.</p>	<p>Frozen placental tissues were crushed in liquid nitrogen. Metabolites were extracted using a pre-cooled mixture of methanol/acetonitrile/water (2:2:1, v/v/v). After vortexing, the mixture was sonicated at low temperature for 30 min, incubated at -20°C for 10 min, and centrifuged at 14,000 g for 20 min at 4°C. The supernatant was vacuum-dried and reconstituted in 100 μL of acetonitrile/water (1:1, v/v). The solution was vortexed and centrifuged at 14,000 g for 15 min at 4°C. The supernatant was used for LC-MS analysis. Pooled QC samples were prepared by mixing equal aliquots of all individual extracts.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry data were acquired on a Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Exploris 480 mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Samples were analyzed in separate runs for positive and negative ionization modes. ESI source parameters: sheath gas (Gas1): 50 arbitrary units; auxiliary gas (Gas2): 2 arbitrary units; ion transfer tube temperature: 350°C; spray voltage: 3500 V (positive mode) and 2800 V (negative mode). Full MS scans were acquired over an m/z range of 70–1200 Da at a resolution of 60,000 (at m/z 200), with a scan accumulation time of 100 ms. Data-dependent MS/MS acquisition was performed using segmented isolation windows over an m/z range of 70–1200 Da at a resolution of 60,000, with a scan accumulation time of 100 ms and dynamic exclusion of 4 s. Pooled QC samples were injected periodically throughout the analytical sequence.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was performed based on a local standard database. Putative annotation of metabolites was conducted by matching accurate mass and MS/MS spectra with reference databases including HMDB, KEGG, and LipidMaps. The XCMS software was used for feature detection and alignment.</p>

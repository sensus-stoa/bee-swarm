Study Protocol Description	<p>Leaf samples were collected from peanut plants (Arachis hypogaea) representing the H and DA experimental groups. Immediately after collection, the leaf samples were frozen in liquid nitrogen and maintained under frozen conditions until homogenization and subsequent metabolomic sample preparation.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using an ultra-high performance liquid chromatography (UHPLC) system. Samples were injected onto an HSS T3 column (100 mm × 2.1 mm i.d., 1.8 µm particle size). Mobile phase A consisted of 0.1% formic acid in water/acetonitrile (95:5, v/v), and mobile phase B consisted of 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile/isopropanol/water (47.5:47.5:5, v/v/v). The gradient was as follows: 0–0.1 min, 0% B; 0.1–2 min, 0–5% B; 2–9 min, 5–25% B; 9–13 min, 25–100% B; 13–13.1 min, 100% B; and 13.1–16 min, 100–0% B. The flow rate was 0.1 mL/min and the column temperature was maintained at 40°C.</p>	<p>Raw mass spectrometry data were pre-processed using Progenesis QI software. Data processing included peak detection, retention-time alignment, feature extraction, and normalization of peak intensities for downstream metabolomic analysis.</p>	<p>Frozen leaf samples were homogenized with 5-mm zirconia beads using a Precellys-24 homogenizer (Bertin Technologies, France). Approximately 50 mg of homogenized tissue was extracted with 400 µL methanol/water (4:1, v/v) containing L-2-chlorophenylalanine at 0.02 mg/mL as an internal standard. Samples were vortexed for 1 min and incubated at -20°C for 30 min for protein precipitation. After centrifugation at 14,000 × g for 20 min at 4°C, the resulting supernatants were collected for LC-MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric data were acquired using a Thermo Fisher Scientific Q Exactive mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Data were acquired separately in positive and negative ion modes over an m/z range of 75–1050. The heater temperature was set to 400°C and the capillary temperature to 320°C. The sheath gas flow rate was 40 arbitrary units and the auxiliary gas flow rate was 10 arbitrary units. The spray voltage was 3500 V in positive ion mode and -2800 V in negative ion mode. Tandem mass spectra were acquired using normalized collision energies of 20, 40 and 60.</p>	<p>Metabolites were annotated by matching detected features against the Human Metabolome Database (HMDB) and the METLIN metabolite database. Differentially accumulated metabolites were identified using variable importance in projection (VIP) scores from orthogonal partial least squares-discriminant analysis (OPLS-DA) together with two-tailed Student's t-tests. Metabolites with VIP &gt; 1 and P &lt; 0.05 were considered differential. OPLS-DA was performed using the R package ropls (version 1.6.2). Metabolic pathway annotation of differential metabolites was performed using the KEGG database.</p>

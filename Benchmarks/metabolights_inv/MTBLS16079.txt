Study Identifier	MTBLS16079
Study Title	Spatial metabolomics data of seed development in the soybean cultivar QiHuang No. 34. (MS Imaging)
Study Description	Spatial metabolomics profiling was performed on materials collected from four stages of seed development (including the early maturation stage and three time points of the mid-maturation stage) of the soybean cultivar QH34 grown in a field environment. For spatial metabolomics, tissues were cryosectioned at 12 µm on a Leica CM1950 cryostat, thaw-mounted onto pre-chilled ITO conductive glass slides, and vacuum-dried for 30 min. The sections were then sprayed with 15 mg/mL DHB matrix (acetonitrile:water = 90:10) using a TM-Sprayer (60 °C, 0.12 mL/min, 8 psi, 25 cycles, with 5 s of drying between cycles), and acquired on a timsTOF fleX mass spectrometer in DataImaging mode with an imaging resolution of 20 µm and a mass range of m/z 50–1,300. Raw data were imported into SCiLS Lab and normalized by RMS to generate ion images.

Study Protocol Description	Pods were collected at five stages of seed development scored on the Fehr and Caviness scale: EMS (early seed enlargement, ~5 mm), MMS-1 (beginning seed fill, ~8 mm), MMS-2 (~10 mm), MMS-3 (full seed, ~12 mm, pods beginning to yellow) and LMS (beginning maturity, ~12 mm, pods brown), with approximately 7 days between successive stages. Soybean (Glycine max (L.) Merr.) cultivar Williams 82 (W82) was grown in 2025 at the Agronomy Experimental Station of Shandong Agricultural University (36 9 N, 117 9 E) under field conditions. At each sampling, pods were harvested from no fewer than 5 independent plants between 9:00 and 10:00 a.m., kept on ice, and seeds were dissected within 5 min. Two independent biological replicates were collected at each stage.	Raw data were imported into SCiLS Lab (Bruker Daltonics) and normalized by root mean square. Abundances were used as delivered for intensity comparisons and log1p transformed for all correlation analysis.	Five regions, the cotyledon, embryo axis, endosperm, hilum and seed coat, were first drawn manually on every section according to the seed anatomy at the corresponding developmental stage. A data-driven partition was then derived from the same data by smoothing the ion images, thresholding the smoothed intensities of region-characteristic ions and intersecting the result with the section geometry. The two partitions were compared through the Pearson correlation of the region spectra of each section.	Imaging was performed on a timsTOF fleX mass spectrometer (Bruker Daltonics) under DataImaging, with the tissue area selected from the optical image, an imaging resolution of 20 um (a pixel of 20 x 20 um), a mass range of m/z 50 to 1,300 and a continuous raster at constant laser energy, so that the eight sections share one m/z axis.	Features were annotated by matching accurate masses and fragment spectra against Metware's in-house database, and classified into three evidence levels: experimental MS2 fragment spectra (level 2), in-silico fragmentation, and accurate mass only (both level 3). For every feature and region, the abundance in that region and the mean abundance of the other four regions were compared within each of the eight sections. The mean of the paired log2 fold changes was used as the effect size, and the eight pairs were tested with the paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test. A feature was called region-enriched when that mean exceeded 1, the region value exceeded the mean of the other four regions in all eight sections, and reached significance after Benjamini-Hochberg correction (P<0.05).	Eight sections of developing soybean seeds, two replicates each of stages EMS, MMS-1, MMS-2 and MMS-3, were measured by MALDI-MSI. Seeds were embedded in 2% CMC solution, snap-frozen and stored at -80 C. Seeds were cryosectioned at 12 um on a Leica CM1950 cryostat after equilibration at -20 C for one hour; sections were thaw-mounted on ITO-coated conductive glass slides and dried under vacuum for 30 minutes. A solution of 15 mg/mL 2,5-dihydroxybenzoic acid in acetonitrile:water (90:10) was sprayed with a TM Sprayer (HTX Technologies) at 60 C, 0.12 mL/min and 8 psi over 25 passes, with 5 s drying between passes.

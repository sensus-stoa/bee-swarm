STUDY
Study Identifier	MTBLS16098
Study Title	ABCA1-mediated cholesterol transfer in gastric cancer immunity
Study Description	This study investigates how ABCA1-mediated cholesterol transfer from gastric cancer cells affects the antitumor function of human Vgamma9Vdelta2 T cells. It examines the relationship between cholesterol accumulation, NF-kappaB signaling, and T-cell effector function, and evaluates whether targeting this metabolic interaction can restore antitumor activity. The study aims to clarify a mechanism of immune evasion in gastric cancer and provide a basis for developing strategies to improve Vgamma9Vdelta2 T-cell-based immunotherapy.

Study Protocol Description	The RXBCD report identifies human cell-pellet samples stored at -80 degrees C until analysis; the RZZ report identifies human gastric-cancer and adjacent-tissue samples. Exact collection, clinical metadata and preservation details beyond these report statements are not supplied.	For RXBCD cell pellets, the report describes 200 microlitres water, three freeze-thaw cycles, 480 microlitres MTBE:MeOH (5:1, v/v) with isotope-labelled internal standards, 1 hour at -40 degrees C, centrifugation at 4 degrees C, vacuum drying, reconstitution in DCM:MeOH (1:1, v/v), and mixed-aliquot QC. The RZZ tissue extraction details are not supplied in the received report.	The RXBCD report states Vanquish UHPLC with a Phenomenex Kinetex C18 column coupled to Orbitrap Exploris 120, Xcalibur 4.4, electrospray, full-MS resolution 60000, MS/MS resolution 15000, NCE 15/30/45, and spray voltage +3.8 kV or -3.4 kV. The RZZ report identifies an LC-MS/MS combined quadrupole-Orbitrap system; its complete acquisition settings and configured scan m/z range are not supplied.	The reports describe ProteoWizard msconvert conversion to mzXML, XCMS retention-time correction, peak detection, extraction, integration and alignment, with minfrac 0.5 and annotation cutoff 0.3. The RZZ report additionally describes RSD/CV and missing-value filtering, half-minimum imputation and internal-standard normalization. The supplied mzXML headers record ProteoWizard 3.0.22335 and MS1 centroiding.	The RXBCD report states that lipid identification used spectral matching against the LipidBlast library with XCMS-based R code. The RZZ report describes metabolite identification and annotation outputs, but the complete identifier/quantification matrix and exact database fields were not supplied with the received reports.

Study Title	SS-31-Loaded Panax notoginseng-Derived Nanovesicles Embedded in a Composite Hydrogel for Multilevel Protection Against Blue-Light-Induced Corneal Injury
Study Description	Prolonged blue-light (BL) exposure from digital devices induces oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, inflammation, and apoptosis in human corneal epithelial cells (HCECs), yet effective multilevel protective strategies remain limited. Here, we developed a hierarchically assembled composite ocular delivery system, PDNs-SS31@Rhein (P-S@R), by embedding SS-31-loaded Panax notoginseng-derived nanovesicles (PDNs-SS31) within a Rhein/hyaluronic acid/Ca2+ composite hydrogel (Rhein gel).

Study Protocol Description	<p>Cells were harvested by trypsinization, centrifuged at 300 × g for 5 minutes, and stored in -80℃.</p>	<p>Metabolites were separated on a UHPLC system equipped with a HSS T3 column (100 mm × 2.1 mm, 1.8 μm; Waters, USA) using 0.1% formic acid in water/acetonitrile and 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile as mobile phases.</p>	<p>Variables present in at least 80% of samples in any group were retained, missing values were imputed with the minimum value, and peak intensities were normalized by total sum. Features with a relative standard deviation (RSD) &gt; 30% in QC samples were excluded. Multivariate analysis was performed using PCA and OPLS-DA with seven-cycle cross-validation via the R package ropls (v1.6.2). Differential metabolites were identified based on OPLS-DA VIP &gt; 1 and Student's t-test P &lt; 0.05, with false discovery rate (FDR, Benjamini-Hochberg) correction applied to control for multiple testing. Differential metabolites were annotated to the KEGG database, and pathway enrichment was evaluated using Fisher’s exact test (Python package scipy.stats) .</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted from cell samples under low-temperature conditions using prechilled methanol/water (4:1, v/v). Following sonication in an ice-water bath, low-temperature incubation, and centrifugation, the supernatants were collected for LC-MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p>Metabolites were analyzed on a Thermo UHPLC-Q Exactive HF-X Orbitrap mass spectrometer in both positive- and negative-ion modes. </p>	<p>Raw data were processed using Progenesis QI software (Waters, Milford, USA) for baseline filtering, peak recognition, integration, retention-time correction, and alignment. Metabolite annotation was performed by matching accurate masses and MS/MS spectra against HMDB and Metlin.</p>

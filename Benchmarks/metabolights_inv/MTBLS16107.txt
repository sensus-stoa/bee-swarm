Study Protocol Description	<p>Fecal samples from each mouse were freshly collected 3 d post-TAI, immediately transferred into sterile plastic tubes on ice, and frozen at −80 °C.&nbsp;</p><p>Briefly, after a week of acclimation, 6-week-old mice underwent a 2-week ABX treatment regimen. During the first week, ABX were administered daily via oral gavage at doses of 5 mg/mouse for ampicillin, vancomycin, and neomycin, and 4 mg/mouse for metronidazole. In the second week, ABX solutions prepared in chlorinated water were administered via drinking water containing ampicillin (1 g/L), vancomycin (0.5 g/L), neomycin (1 g/L), and metronidazole (1 g/L).</p>	<p>The data acquisition instrument system mainly consisted of an ultra-performance liquid chromatography (UPLC) system (ExionLC™ AD,&nbsp;https://sciex.com.cn/) coupled with a tandem mass spectrometry (MS/MS) system (QTRAP® 6500+,&nbsp;https://sciex.com.cn/) .</p><p>The UPLC conditions were as follows:</p><p>Column: Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 C18 column (1.8 μm, 100 mm × 2.1 mm i.d.);</p><p>Mobile phase: A, ultrapure water containing 0.1% formic acid; B, acetonitrile containing 0.1% formic acid;</p><p>Flow rate: 0.35 mL/min; column temperature: 40 °C; injection volume: 5 μL;</p><p>Mobile phase gradient: 0 min, A/B = 90:10 (V/V); 1 min, A/B = 90:10 (V/V); 8 min, A/B = 5:95 (V/V); 9.5 min, A/B = 5:95 (V/V); 9.6 min, A/B = 90:10 (V/V); 12 min, A/B = 90:10 (V/V).</p>	<p><em>MSConvert software used to transform the raw data.</em></p>	<p>Thaw the samples on ice (all subsequent procedures should be performed on ice);</p><p>After thawing, weigh 50 mg of each sample into a centrifuge tube;</p><p>Add 500 μL of methanol (containing 20 μL of internal standard working solution at a concentration of 250 ng/mL) to the centrifuge tube, vortex for 3 min to mix thoroughly, and then allow to stand at −20 °C for 30 min;</p><p>Centrifuge at 12,000 r/min for 10 min at 4 °C, and transfer 250 μL of the supernatant to a new centrifuge tube;</p><p>Centrifuge again, transfer 150 μL of the supernatant into an injection vial, and store temporarily at −20 °C for LC-MS/MS analysis.</p>	<p>The electrospray ionization (ESI) source temperature was set at 550 °C. The ion spray voltage was 5500 V in positive ion mode and −4500 V in negative ion mode. The curtain gas (CUR) pressure was 35 psi. On the QTRAP® 6500+ system, each ion pair (transition) was scanned and detected based on optimized declustering potential (DP) and collision energy (CE) values.</p><p>Note:&nbsp;The detection mode was multiple reaction monitoring (MRM), not positive- or negative-only mode. In MRM mode, there is no full-scan m/z range; instead, specific precursor ion to product ion transitions are set for each target analyte. Therefore, no specific scan m/z range information is available.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry data were processed using MultiQuant™ software (version 3.0.3). The retention times and peak shape information of reference standards were used to guide integration and manual correction of chromatographic peaks of target analytes detected in different samples, ensuring the accuracy of both qualification and quantification.</p>

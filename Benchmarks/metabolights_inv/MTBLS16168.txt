Study Identifier	MTBLS16168
Study Title	Camel milk exosomes alleviate high-fat diet-induced metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease and are associated with remodeling of the gut microbiota and hepatic metabolome
Study Description	Camel milk-derived exosomes (CM-exos) are bioactive, yet their role in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) is unknown. We evaluated oral CM-exos in high-fat diet-induced MASLD mice, assessing metabolic parameters, gut microbiota, colonic short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs), and hepatic metabolome. CM-exos significantly lowered serum triglycerides, LDL-cholesterol, and alanine aminotransferase, improved glucose tolerance, and reduced hepatic steatosis. These benefits coincided with partially restored gut dysbiosis: increased Bacteroides and Eubacterium coprostanoligenes group, decreased Escherichia-Shigella and Mucispirillum, and elevated colonic SCFAs (especially branched-chain fatty acids). Liver metabolomics identified 35 potential biomarkers reverting toward normal and highlighted modulation of taurine/hypotaurine metabolism and unsaturated fatty acid biosynthesis. Correlation analyses linked Bacteroides and Eubacterium coprostanoligenes group to improved metabolic indicators. Thus, CM-exos alleviate MASLD, likely via gut microbiota–liver metabolic remodeling.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Mouse liver samples were collected and transferred into microcentrifuge tubes, then immediately frozen and stored at 80 °C until metabolite extraction.</p>	<p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1394'>UHPLC-MS/MS analyses were performed using a Vanquish UHPLC system (ThermoFisher, Germany) coupled with an Orbitrap Q ExactiveTM HF mass spectrometer, Orbitrap Q ExactiveTM HF-X mass spectrometer, Orbitrap Exploris 120 mass spectrometer or Orbitrap Exploris 480 mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher, Germany) in Novogene Co., Ltd. (Beijing, China). Samples were injected onto a Hypersil Gold column (100 x 2.1 mm, 1.9 μm) using a 12-min linear gradient at a flow rate of 0.2 mL/min. The eluents for the positive and negative polarity modes were eluent A (0.1% FA in Water) and eluent B (Methanol). The solvent gradient was set as follows: 2% B, 1.5 min; 2-85% B, 3 min; 85-100% B, 10 min; 100-2% B, 10.1 min; 2% B, 12 min.</p>	<p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>The data files generated by UHPLC-MS/MS were processed by XCMS to perform&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>peak alignment, peak picking, and quantitation for each metabolite. Then, based on&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>adduct ions and setting mass deviation to 10 ppm[6], a comparison was made between&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>these data and the self-built high-quality secondary spectrum database (NovoMetDB)&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>to obtain results for metabolite identification. Metabolites with more than 50%&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>missing values in the samples were first filtered out and the remaining missing values&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>were imputed using KNN. After eliminating background ions according to blank&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>samples, the raw quantitative results were further processed to obtain relative peak&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>areas, which included normalization and filtering out compounds whose relative peak&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>areas exhibited a coefficient of variation (CV) greater than 30% in the quality control&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>(QC) samples[8]. Relative peak areas = Raw quantitative value of samples/ (The sum&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>of quantitative value of samples/ The sum of quantitative value of QC1). Data&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>processing is based on the Linux operating system (CentOS version 6.6), using R and&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>Python. For details on specific packages and software versions used, please refer to&nbsp;</p><p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1555'>the README file included in the results.</p>	<p>Liver tissues (100 mg) were individually grounded with liquid nitrogen and the homogenate was resuspended with prechilled 80% methanol by well vortex. The samples were incubated on ice for 5 min and then were centrifuged at 15,000 g, 4°C for 20 min. Some of supernatant was diluted to final concentration containing 53% methanol by LC-MS grade water.The samples were subsequently transferred to a fresh Eppendorf tube and thenwere centrifuged at 15000 g, 4°C for 20 min. Finally, the supernatant was injected into the LC-MS/MS system analysis.&nbsp;</p>	<p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1455'>The m/z scan range was set at 100-1500. The ESI source parameters were configured as follows: Spray Voltage: 3.5 kV; Sheath gas flow rate: 35 psi; Aux Gas flow rate: 10 L/min; Capillary Temp: 320 °C; S-lens RF level: 60; Aux gas heater temp: 350 °C. Polarity: positive and negative. MS/MS acquisition was performed using data-dependent scans. Q ExactiveTM HF mass spectrometer was operated in positive/negative polarity mode with spray voltage of 3.5 kV, capillary temperature of 320 °C, sheath gas flow rate of 35 arb and aux gas flow rate of 10 arb, S-lens RF level of 60, Aux gas heater temperature of 350 °C.</p>	<p _msttexthash='32273813' _msthash='1511'>These metabolites were annotated using the KEGG database (https://www.genome.jp/kegg/pathway.html), HMDB database (https://hmdb.ca/metabolites) and LIPIDMaps database (http://www.lipidmaps.org/). Principal components analysis (PCA) and Partial least squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA) were performed at metaX[9] (a flexible and comprehensive software for processing metabolomics data). We applied univariate analysis (t-test) to calculate the statistical significance (P-value). FC refers to the fold change, which is the ratio of the mean of all biological repeated quantitative values of each metabolite in the comparison group. The differential metabolites were screened using VIP, P-value and FC. The specific standard outlined in Table 5.3 of the report.</p>

Study Description	This study investigated the toxicological effects of histamine on the hepatopancreas of the Chinese mitten crab (Eriocheir sinensis) to reveal its ecological risks. Untargeted metabolomics profiling of hepatopancreatic tissues was performed using ultra-high performance liquid chromatography coupled with quadrupole time-of-flight mass spectrometry (UHPLC-Q-TOF MS). The aim was to identify metabolic perturbations and elucidate the underlying mechanisms of histamine-induced multi-systemic toxicity in aquatic organisms. hide

Study Protocol Description	<p>Healthy male juvenile Eriocheir sinensis (initial body weight 20.0 ± 0.5 g) were obtained from a commercial aquaculture farm in Nanjing, Jiangsu Province, China. Following a 5-day acclimation period, the crabs were subjected to a 5-day oral gavage exposure experiment. At the end of the exposure period, all crabs were fasted for 24 h to minimize the influence of gastrointestinal contents. For haemolymph collection, crabs were anaesthetized on ice for 5 min, and approximately 0.5 mL of haemolymph was collected from the second pereiopod using a 1 mL syringe preloaded with 0.5 mL of anticoagulant solution. The mixture was centrifuged at 900 × g for 10 min at 4°C, and the resulting plasma and haemocyte fractions were collected separately. For tissue collection, crabs were dissected on a precooled sterile surface, and the hepatopancreas was rapidly isolated. Tissue portions were fixed in 4% paraformaldehyde for H&amp;E staining or 2.5% glutaraldehyde for TEM, while the remaining tissues were snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen. All collected samples were stored at -80°C until subsequent analysis.</p>	<p>Metabolite profiling was performed using an Agilent 1290 ultra-high performance liquid chromatography (UHPLC) system. Separation was performed on an Agilent ZORBAX Eclipse Plus C18 column (2.1 × 100 mm, 1.8 μm) maintained at 40°C. The injection volume was 3 μL. Mobile phase A was 0.1% formic acid in water and mobile phase B was acetonitrile. The gradient program was set as follows: 5% B from 0–2 min, 5–95% B from 2–15 min, and 95% B from 15–18 min.</p>	<p>Raw data acquired from the LC-MS were processed using Progenesis QI v2.4 software for peak extraction, alignment, and noise reduction. Feature filtering retained features detected in at least 80% of samples within one group. Missing values were imputed by half-minimum substitution, and the data were normalized to the internal standard. Subsequently, Partial least-squares discriminant analysis (PLS-DA) was performed to screen for differential metabolites based on the processed dataset.</p>	<p>For metabolomics analysis, fifty mg of hepatopancreatic tissue was homogenized in 1 mL of pre-chilled methanol/water (80:20, v/v) containing 20 μL of isotopic internal standard mixture. The homogenate was vortexed for 30 s, ultrasonicated on ice for 30 min, and centrifuged at 12,000 g for 15 min at 4°C. The supernatant was then lyophilized, reconstituted in 100 μL of 50% acetonitrile, and filtered through a 0.22 μm membrane. A pooled quality control (QC) sample was prepared by mixing equal volumes of supernatants from all samples to monitor instrument stability.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was performed using an Agilent 6545 quadrupole time-of-flight (Q-TOF) mass spectrometer. Electrospray ionization (ESI) was operated in both positive and negative modes. The capillary voltage was set to 3.5 kV, the drying gas temperature was 325°C with a flow rate of 10 L/min, and the nebulizer pressure was 45 psi. The sheath gas temperature was 380°C with a flow rate of 12 L/min. The full scan mass range was set from m/z 50 to 1200, and Auto MS/MS acquisition was applied with collision energy ramped from 10–40 V.</p>	<p>Metabolites were annotated by matching accurate mass (within ±5 ppm) and MS/MS fragments against the Human Metabolome Database (HMDB), Metabolite Link database (METLIN), and National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) databases. The annotation confidence of the identified metabolites followed the Metabolomics Standards Initiative (MSI) Level 2 criteria. Differential metabolites were screened with thresholds of variable importance in projection (VIP) &gt; 1.0 and fold change (FC)&nbsp;1.2 or&nbsp;0.83.</p>

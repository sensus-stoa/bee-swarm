Study Protocol Description	<p>Absorb 100 μl of bacterial liquid (10 x 8 CFU/mL) and add it to 300 ml of 0.1% PDM-LB liquid medium (with pure LB medium as the control). Cultivate in the dark at 37°C and 200 rpm for 48 hours. Centrifuge at 4000 rpm for 10 minutes to collect the upper layer of the culture medium and the lower layer of the mixture of PDM raw material and bacterial cells.</p>	<p>(1) Chromatographic column: Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 Column 1.8 μm, 2.1 mm * 100 mm</p><p>(2) Mobile phase A: Ultra-pure water (0.1% formic acid); Mobile phase B: Acetonitrile (0.1% formic acid)</p><p>(3) Column temperature of the instrument: 40 °C; Flow rate: 0.40 mL/min; Injection volume: 4 μL</p>	<p>The raw data from the mass spectrometry run was converted into mzML format using ProteoWizard. The XCMS program was then used for peak extraction, alignment, and retention time correction. Peaks with a missing rate &gt;50% in each group of samples were filtered out, and blank values were filled with KNN + 1/5 minimum value (1/5 minimum value was used for blank values &gt;50%, and KNN was used for blank values &lt;50%). The peak areas were corrected using the SVR method.</p>	<p>And accurately measure 1 ml of dense bacterial cells, gently wash them 5 to 7 times with 1×PBS to remove the residual PDM and culture medium on the cell surface. After subjecting the cells to repeated freeze-thaw and ultrasonic disruption procedures, centrifuge to collect 1 ml of the intracellular product extract.</p><p>(1) Take the sample out of the -80°C refrigerator, thaw it until there is no ice in the sample, and vortex for 10 seconds to mix well.</p><p>(2) Take an appropriate amount of the sample and add it to the corresponding numbered 1.5 mL centrifuge tube. Add 70% methanol internal standard extraction solution at a ratio of 1:1 (V/V). The internal standard extraction solution is prepared by dissolving 1 mg of the standard in 1 mL of 70% methanol water to prepare a 1000 μg/mL stock solution. The 1000 μg/mL stock solution is further diluted with 70% methanol to prepare a 250 μg/mL internal standard solution.</p><p>(3) Vortex for 15 minutes, centrifuge at 12000 r/min and 4°C for 3 minutes.</p><p>(4) Take the supernatant, filter it through a micro-porous membrane (0.22 μm pore size), and store it in the injection bottle for LC-MS/MS detection.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry analysis was performed using a Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer in both ESI + and ESI - modes. The ionization voltages were 3500 V (ESI+) and 3200 V (ESI-), respectively; the sheath gas and auxiliary gas were both 30 Arb and 5 Arb; the ion transfer tube temperature was 320 °C, and the nebulization temperature was 300 °C. The MS1 mass scan range was 84-1250 Da, with a resolution of 35000 and an AGC target of 1.00E+06; the MS2 mass scan range was 84-1250 Da, with a resolution of 17500 and an AGC target of 2.00E+05. The collision voltages were 30, 40, and 50, the intensity threshold was 1.00E+05 cps, the number of candidate ions was 10, and the dynamic exclusion time was 3. This enabled the mass spectrometric analysis of the samples.</p>	<p>The corrected and filtered peaks were identified by searching the laboratory's self-built database, integrating public databases, prediction libraries, and the metDNA method. Finally, substances with a comprehensive identification score of 0.5 or above and a CV value of QC samples less than 0.5 were extracted, and then the positive and negative modes were merged (retaining the substance with the highest qualitative grade and the smallest CV value).</p>

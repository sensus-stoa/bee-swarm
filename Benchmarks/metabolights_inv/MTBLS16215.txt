INVESTIGATION
Investigation Identifier	MTBLS16215
Investigation Title	Day 0 Baseline Metabolomic Profiling during Mixed-Culture Fermentation of Dendrobium officinale Flowers
Investigation Description	This study provides the Day 0 baseline metabolomic dataset for a 60-day mixed-culture liquid fermentation of Dendrobium officinale flowers. Six biological samples were collected from six independent fermentation vessels immediately after inoculation and homogenization, before fermentation incubation began. Untargeted LC–MS/MS analysis was performed in positive and negative electrospray ionization modes using a UHPLC system coupled to a Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Exploris 240 mass spectrometer. The deposited dataset comprises raw mass spectrometry data from six biological samples and four pooled quality-control measurements per ionization mode, totaling 20 RAW files. This dataset supplements the previously published MetaboLights study MTBLS15132, which contains samples collected on fermentation days 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60. The Day 0 samples were analyzed in a separate analytical batch. Together, the two datasets provide coverage of the Day 0–60 fermentation time series and support the investigation of fermentation-associated metabolomic changes.

Study Protocol Description	Six baseline fermentation samples (JS0_1 to JS0_6) were collected from six independent vessels immediately after inoculation and homogenization and before fermentation incubation began. Aliquots were frozen and stored at -40 degrees C. Four pooled quality-control measurements per ionization mode were included in a separate Day 0 LC-MS/MS analytical batch.	Reversed-phase liquid chromatography was performed using a Thermo Scientific UHPLC system coupled to an Orbitrap Exploris 240 mass spectrometer and an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (100 mm x 2.1 mm, 1.8 micrometre; Waters). The flow rate was 0.40 mL/min and the column temperature was 40 degrees C.	LC-MS raw data were processed using Progenesis QI for baseline filtering, peak detection, integration, retention-time correction, and peak alignment. Features detected in at least 80% of samples in any group were retained. Missing values were imputed with the minimum value, peak intensities were sum-normalized, variables with QC RSD greater than 30% were excluded, and data were log10-transformed.	Fermentation supernatants were extracted using acetonitrile:methanol (1:1, v/v) containing four internal standards, including L-2-chlorophenylalanine (0.02 mg/mL). Samples were vortexed, sonicated at 5 degrees C, incubated at -20 degrees C, centrifuged at 13000 x g, dried under nitrogen, reconstituted in acetonitrile:water (1:1, v/v), sonicated, and centrifuged before LC-MS/MS analysis.	Mass spectrometry data were acquired on a Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Exploris 240 mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization source in separate positive and negative ion modes. The mass scan range was m/z 70-1050. Data-dependent MS/MS acquisition used stepped normalized collision energies of 20, 40, and 60.	MS and MS/MS spectra were matched against HMDB, METLIN, KEGG, and the Majorbio self-compiled database (MJDB). Internal-standard peaks and known false-positive features, including noise and column-bleed signals, were removed, and redundant features were pooled. Metabolite annotations were reported with metabolite name, mass-to-charge ratio, retention time, molecular formula, database identifier, and identification confidence.

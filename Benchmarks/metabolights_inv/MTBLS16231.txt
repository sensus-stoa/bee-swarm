Study Identifier	MTBLS16231
Study Title	Oleic acid deficiency reprograms PPARγ signaling to drive ovarian aging
Study Description	<p>This lipidomics study was performed to investigate the role of oleic acid deficiency in ovarian aging. Ovarian tissues from young (3-month-old) and reproductively aged (9-month-old) C57BL/6J mice were analysed by UPLC-MS/MS-based quantitative lipidomic profiling using multiple reaction monitoring (MRM) and an in-house lipid database. SCD1-knockdown granulosa cell models with or without oleic acid supplementation were subsequently used for mechanistic validation. We aimed to identify differentially abundant lipid species and clarify how reduced oleic acid reprograms PPARγ signaling to trigger granulosa cell pyroptosis and accelerate ovarian aging.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Ovarian tissue was collected from ten 3-month-old and ten 9-month-old C57BL/6J mice; because the weight of individual ovaries was insufficient for lipidomics analysis, four ovaries were pooled to constitute one biological replicate, yielding five biological replicates per group (n = 5) in the final dataset. Samples were stored at −80 °C before extraction.</p>	<p>Extracts were separated on an ExionLC AD UPLC using a Thermo Accucore C30 column (2.6 μm, 2.1 mm x 100 mm). Mobile phase A was acetonitrile/water (60/40, v/v) with 0.1% formic acid and 10 mmol/L ammonium formate; mobile phase B was acetonitrile/isopropanol (10/90, v/v) with the same additives. Flow rate was 0.35 mL/min, column temperature 45 ℃, injection volume 2 μL, with the reported gradient program.</p>	Raw MRM data were processed with Analyst 1.6.3. Peak areas were integrated and lipid quantities were calculated using internal-standard correction; downstream multivariate and differential analyses used the processed quantitative matrix.	<p>Approximately 20 mg tissue was homogenized at 30 Hz for 20 s with a steel bead, centrifuged at 3000 rpm and 4 ℃ for 30 s, extracted with 1 mL MTBE/methanol (3:1, v/v) containing internal standards, mixed for 15 min, supplemented with 200 μL water, vortexed for 1 min, centrifuged at 12000 rpm and 4 ℃ for 10 min, dried from 200 μL organic phase, and reconstituted in 200 μL acetonitrile/isopropanol (1:1, v/v).</p>	<p>Lipid species were identified and quantified by targeted MRM on an AB SCIEX QTRAP 6500+ with an ESI source in positive and negative ion modes. Data were acquired in Analyst 1.6.3; source temperature was 500 ℃, ion-spray voltage +5500/-4500 V, GS1/GS2/CUR 45/55/35 psi, and collision gas 5 psi.</p>	Lipid identities were assigned against the in-house MWDB/MetWare lipid database using retention time and MS/MS information, with MRM transitions and optimized DP/CE values for the monitored lipid species.

Study Identifier	MTBLS16234
Study Title	THEMIS2 promotes ovarian cancer progression and cisplatin resistance through inflammatory signaling and lipid metabolic reprogramming: an integrated multi-omics and experimental study
Study Description	We conducted an integrated pan-omics analysis of THEMIS2 across six molecular layers from TCGA-OV (mRNA, CNV, mutation, methylation, miRNA, RPPA), four independent GEO cohorts (n=450), DepMap CRISPR essentiality screens, LINCS L1000 drug signatures, single-cell RNA-seq (GSE165897, 51,786 cells), and spatial transcriptomics (GSE211956, 8 Visium samples). Functional validation was performed using THEMIS2 knockdown in A2780 cells with CCK-8, EdU, flow cytometry, and Transwell assays. A three-omics (transcriptomic, proteomic, and metabolomic) integrative analysis was conducted under cisplatin treatment with THEMIS2 knockdown to elucidate downstream mechanisms.

Study Protocol Description	<p>A2780/DDP cells were divided into 4 groups, with 3 samples per group: the nc group (nc1, nc2, nc3), serving as the control group without any treatment; the Si group (si1, si2, si3), in which THEMIS2 was knocked down by transfection with siTHEMIS2 at 50 nM; the Nccis group (nccis1, nccis2, nccis3), treated with 10 µM cisplatin for 24 h; and the Sicis group (sicis1, sicis2, sicis3), transfected with siTHEMIS2 for 24 h followed by incubation with 10 µM cisplatin for 24 h.</p>	<p>All samples were for two LC/MS methods. One aliquot was analyzed using positive ion conditions and was eluted from T3 column (Waters ACQUITY Premier HSS T3 Column 1.8 µm, 2.1 mm * 100 mm) using 0.1 % formic acid in water as solvent A and 0.1 % formic acid in acetonitrile as solvent B in the following gradient: 5 to 20 % in 1 min, increased to 99 % in the following 2 mins and held for 1.5 min, then come back to 5 % mobile phase B witnin 0.1 min, held for 1.4 min. The analytical conditions were as follows, column temperature, 40 °C; flow rate, 0.4 mL/min; injection volume, 3 μL; Another aliquot was using negative ion conditions and was the same as the elution gradient of positive mode.</p>	<p>The raw mass spectrometry data were converted to mzML format using ProteoWizard, and peak extraction, alignment, and retention time correction were performed using the XCMS program. Peaks with a missing rate &gt;50% in each group of samples were filtered out, and blank values were filled using KNN imputation + 1/5 minimum value (when blank values &gt;50%, 1/5 minimum value imputation was used; when blank values &lt;50%, KNN imputation was used). Peak areas were corrected using the SVR method. The peaks after correction and screening were subjected to metabolite identification by searching a laboratory-built database, integrating public libraries, and prediction libraries. Finally, substances with an integrated identification score above 0.5 and a QC sample CV value less than 0.3 were extracted, followed by merging of positive and negative modes (if duplicate substances existed, the substance with the highest identification level and the highest score was retained), yielding the all_sample_data.xlsx file.</p>	<p>The sample stored at -80 °C refrigerator was thawed on ice. A 500 μL solution (Methanol : Water = 4:1, V/V) containing internal standard was added into the cell sample and vortexed for 3 min. The sample was placed in liquid nitrogen for 5 min and on the dry ice for 5 min, and then thawed on ice and vortexed for 2 min. This freeze-thaw circle was repeated three times in total. The sample was centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 10 min (4 °C). A 300 μL of supernatant was collected and placed in -20 °C for 30 min. The sample was then centrifuged at 12000 rpm for 3 min (4 °C). A 200 μL aliquots of supernatant were transferred for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>All the methods alternated between full scan MS and data dependent MSn scans using dynamic exclusion. MS analyses were carried out using electrospray ionization in the positive ion mode and negative ion mode using full scan analysis over m/z 70-1000 at 60000 resolution. Additional MS settings are: ion spray voltage, 3.8 KV or 3.4 KV in positive or negative modes, respevtively; Sheath gas (Arb), 60; Aux gas, 20; Ion transfer tube temperature, 320 °C; Vaporizer temperature, 350 °C; Collision energy, 30,40,50 V; Top N vs Top speed, 10.</p>	<p>Identified metabolites were annotated using KEGG Compound database (http://www.kegg.jp/kegg/compound/), annotated metabolites were then mapped to KEGG Pathway database (http://www.kegg.jp/kegg/pathway.html).</p>

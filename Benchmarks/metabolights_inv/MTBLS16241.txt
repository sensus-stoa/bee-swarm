Study Protocol Description	<p>Mouse fecal samples were collected, snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at −80 °C until metabolite extraction. Repeated freeze-thaw was avoided.</p>	<p>UHPLC separation was performed on a SCIEX ExionLC AD UHPLC system. The analytical column was Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 C18 (1.8 μm, 100 mm × 2.1 mm i.d.). Mobile phase A: water containing 0.1% formic acid; mobile phase B: acetonitrile containing 0.1% formic acid. Gradient program: 0–1 min, 90% A / 10% B; 1–6 min, linear ramp to 5% A / 95% B; 6–7 min, hold 5% A / 95% B; 7–7.1 min, linear ramp back to 90% A / 10% B; 7.1–10 min, hold 90% A / 10% B. Flow rate: 0.35 mL/min, column temperature: 40 °C, injection volume: 2 μL.</p>	<p>Raw .wiff data files were converted to mzML format using SCIEX MSConvert. Peak integration and quantification of target metabolites were performed using MultiQuant software.</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted using ice-cold methanol-water solution. Quality control (QC) samples were prepared by pooling equal volumes of all study samples and interspersed among test samples to monitor instrument stability. Solvent blanks were included to assess background contamination. No derivatization was performed.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric detection was carried out on a SCIEX QTRAP 6500+ mass spectrometer equipped with an electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Data were acquired in alternating positive and negative ion modes (MRM). The m/z scanning range was set from 50 to 500. Source parameters including ion spray voltage, temperature, curtain gas and collision gas were optimized for targeted tryptophan pathway metabolites.</p>	<p>Target metabolites were identified by matching retention time and MRM transition pairs with authentic reference standards. Metabolite annotations were cross-referenced with HMDB database.</p>

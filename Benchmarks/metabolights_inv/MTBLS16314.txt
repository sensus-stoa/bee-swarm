Study Protocol Description	<p>Contents from five intestinal tracts (duodenum, jejunum, ileum, cecum and colon) were collected from eighteen Wuzhishan (WZS) pigs</p>	<p>Chromatographic separations were performed on an Acquity UHPLC system (Waters) equipped with a HSS T3 column (1.8 μm, 2.1 mm × 100 mm) applying the following binary gradient at a flow rate of 0.4 mL/min, 0.1% formic acid in water (solvent A) and 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile (solvent B), with an identical gradient applied in both ion modes: 5% B initially, increasing linearly to 20% B at 2 min, 60% B at 5 min, and 99% B at 6 min, held at 99% B for 1.5 min, decreased to 5% B within 0.1 min, and held at 5% B for 2.4 min to re-equilibrate the column. The injection volume was 3.0 μl (full loop injection).</p>	<p>Raw data were processed, and peaks were normalized in MZmine (v4.10.1), with molecular formulas predicted and samples showing a relative standard deviation (RSD) of metabolite peak areas greater than 30% in QC samples excluded.</p>	<p>Metabolites were extracted from intestinal tissue samples using a methanol/water solvent system, followed by vortexing, ultrasonication, and centrifugation. The supernatant was collected and filtered prior to LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric analysis was performed on a Q-Exactive Plus mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, USA). Data were acquired in both positive and negative ion modes over an m/z range of 70–1000. The ion source temperature was 320 °C, the capillary temperature was 320 °C, and the spray voltage was 3.8 kV (positive) and 3.4 kV (negative). MS/MS fragmentation used stepped collision energies of 30, 40, and 50 V, with the ten most intense precursor ions selected for fragmentation per survey scan.</p>	<p>Identification was based on matching against an in-house database integrated with public and predicted libraries, using the following criteria: MS1: m/z error &lt; 25 ppm; MS1–MS2 RT consistency: RT error &lt; 6 s; Database search: parent ion Q1 tolerance 25 ppm; MS2 tolerance 10 ppm; RT filter tolerance 60 s; MS2 scoring: minimum MS2 score 0.3; final MS2 score = 0.1 × fragment score + 0.3 × forward search score + 0.6 × reverse search score. The metabolites were searching against KEGG (accessed January 15th, 2025) and HMDB (v5.0) libraries without authentic standards and were assigned MSI Level 2.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Human fecal samples and mouse tumor tissue samples were collected after the experimental interventions. Samples were aliquoted in a laminar-flow hood, immediately labeled, flash-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at -80 degrees C until analysis.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed using a Thermo Scientific Vanquish Horizon UHPLC system equipped with a Waters ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (100 mm × 2.1 mm i.d., 1.8 µm). Mobile phase A consisted of 95% water and 5% acetonitrile containing 0.1% formic acid, and mobile phase B consisted of 47.5% acetonitrile, 47.5% isopropanol, and 5% water containing 0.1% formic acid. The column temperature was maintained at 40 °C and the injection volume was 3 µL.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were imported into Progenesis QI v3.0 (Waters Corporation, Milford, MA, USA). Baseline filtering, peak detection, peak integration, retention-time alignment, and peak alignment were performed. The resulting feature matrix contained retention time, mass-to-charge ratio (m/z), and peak intensity information for downstream analysis.</p>	<p>Each 100 uL sample was mixed with 210 uL extraction solvent consisting of methanol and acetonitrile (1:1, v/v) and containing four internal standards, including L-2-chlorophenylalanine (0.02 mg/mL). Samples were vortexed for 30 s and ultrasonicated at 5 degrees C and 40 kHz for 30 min. The mixture was incubated at -20 degrees C for 30 min and centrifuged at 13,000 g and 4 degrees C for 15 min. The supernatant was collected and dried under nitrogen, then reconstituted in 100 uL acetonitrile/water (1:1, v/v). After vortexing for 30 s, ultrasonication at 5 degrees C and 40 kHz for 5 min, and centrifugation at 13,000 g and 4 degrees C for 10 min, the supernatant was transferred to an autosampler vial for LC-MS analysis.</p>	<p>LC-MS analysis was performed using a Thermo Scientific Exploris 240 mass spectrometer with an Orbitrap mass analyzer and an electrospray ionization source. Data were acquired in separate positive and negative ion modes over an m/z range of 70-1050. The sheath gas and auxiliary gas flow rates were 60 and 20 arbitrary units, respectively. The heater temperature was 350 °C and the capillary temperature was 320 °C. The spray voltage was 3,400 V in positive mode and -3,000 V in negative mode. The S-lens RF level was 70, and the normalized collision energies were 20%, 40%, and 60%. The mass resolution was 60,000 for full MS and 15,000 for MS2.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was performed in Progenesis QI v3.0 by matching MS and MS/MS spectral information against the Human Metabolome Database (HMDB), METLIN, and an in-house metabolite database. A mass error threshold of less than 10 ppm combined with MS/MS spectral matching scores was used to annotate metabolites.</p>

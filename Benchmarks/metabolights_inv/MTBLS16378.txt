Study Protocol Type Term Source REF		CHMO	OBI	OBI	MI
Study Protocol Description	<p>Stool samples were collected with standardized kits 1–2 weeks before radiotherapy (baseline) and 1–2 weeks after radiotherapy from patients with rectal cancer undergoing total neoadjuvant therapy, and stored at −80 °C until analysis. A subset of these samples was used for short-chain fatty acid analysis.</p>	<p>Short-chain fatty acids were analyzed by headspace gas chromatography with flame ionization detection on an Agilent 7890B GC equipped with an Agilent 7697A headspace sampler. Separation was performed on an Agilent J&amp;W HP-INNOWax column (30 m × 0.25 mm I.D. × 0.25 μm film thickness), a high-polarity polyethylene glycol (PEG)-based capillary column.</p>	<p>Chromatograms acquired in Agilent ChemStation were exported in AIA netCDF (.cdf) format. Short-chain fatty acid concentrations were quantified from peak areas.</p>	<p>Fecal samples were analyzed by headspace sampling (Agilent 7697A Headspace Sampler) prior to GC-FID analysis.</p>	<p>Targeted analysis of short-chain fatty acids. Metabolites were identified by matching retention time.</p>

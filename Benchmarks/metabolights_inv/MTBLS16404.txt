Study Protocol Description	<p>Serum samples were collected from late preterm infants with hyperbilirubinemia. Blood was centrifuged to isolate serum, aliquoted immediately, and stored at -80°C until lipid extraction. Blank samples and pooled QC samples were prepared alongside study samples for quality control.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed on a Shimadzu LC-30A Nexera UHPLC system. An ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide column (1.7 µm, 2.1 × 100 mm; Waters) was used. Mobile phase A was 25 mM ammonium acetate + 25 mM ammonium hydroxide in water, mobile phase B was acetonitrile. Gradient: 0–1 min, 85% B; 1–12 min, 85%–65% B; 12–12.1 min, 65%–40% B; 12.1–15 min, 40% B; 15–15.1 min, 40%–85% B; 15.1–20 min, 85% B. Flow rate: 0.3 mL/min, column temperature: 55 °C, injection volume: 5 μL. No guard column was used.</p>	<p>Raw .wiff MS data were processed with SCIEX OS software for peak alignment, retention time correction and peak area extraction. Data normalization was carried out after feature extraction, and R software was used for subsequent statistical analysis and visualization.</p><p>&nbsp;</p>	<p>100 μL serum sample was mixed with 1000 μL extraction solvent (methanol:acetonitrile:water = 2:2:1, v/v), followed by vortex mixing. The mixture was ultrasonicated in ice water for 10 min and snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen for 1 min; this cycle was repeated three times. Samples were kept at -20 °C for 1 h, then centrifuged at 13,000 rpm, 4 °C for 15 min. The supernatant was collected and dried under nitrogen. The residue was reconstituted in 100 μL acetonitrile:water = 1:1 (v/v), vortexed for 30 s, ultrasonicated on ice for 10 min, and centrifuged again at 13,000 rpm, 4 °C for 15 min. The final supernatant was transferred for LC-MS analysis. Solvent blanks and pooled QC samples were prepared in parallel. No derivatization was performed.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometry detection was performed on an AB SCIEX 6500 Q-TRAP mass spectrometer equipped with electrospray ionization (ESI) source. Data were acquired in positive and negative ionization modes separately using multiple reaction monitoring (MRM). Ion source temperature was set at 600 ℃; ion spray voltage was 5500 V (positive) and -4500 V (negative). Curtain gas was 20 psi, nebulizer gas and heater gas were both 60 psi. The m/z scan range was set to 100–1200.</p>	<p>Lipid annotation was achieved by matching accurate mass and MS/MS fragment spectra with a mass tolerance of &lt;25 ppm against an in-house database (VGDB) and public databases. Lipid identification confidence followed standard lipidomics reporting guidelines.</p>

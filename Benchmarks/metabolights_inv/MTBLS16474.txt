Study Protocol Type Term Source REF		CHMO	OBI	EFO	CHMO	MI
Study Protocol Description	<p>Mice were anesthetized with 2.5% Avertin and transcardially perfused with cold PBS. One hemisphere was dissected, snap-frozen, weighed (~100 mg), and stored at –80 °C until use.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Reverse phase chromatography was selected for LC separation using Cortecs C18 column (2.1*100 mm, 2.7um, Waters). Mobile phase A was made by mixing 400 mL of HPLC-grade water containing 0.77 g of ammonium acetate with 600 mL of HPLC-grade acetonitrile. Mobile phase B contained 10% ACN and 90% IPA (v/v). Gradient elution was performed at a flow rate of 0.25 mL/min, and the column oven temperature was set to 40 ℃.The gradient was as follow: 0 min, 30%B; 3.0 min, 30% B; 4.5 min, 33% B; 7.0 min, 45% B; 8.0 min, 52% B; 11.0 min, 58% B; 14.0 min, 66% B; 17.0 min, 70% B; 21.0 min, 75% B; 23.0 min, 98% B; 30.0 min, 98% B; 30.5 min, 30% B; 35.0 min, 30% B.</p>	<p>&nbsp;Lipids were identified and quantified using LipidSearch 4.2 (Thermo Fisher, USA).Lipid Search v4.2 contains 18 lipid species and more than 1,500,000 fragment ions in the database. It allows lipid identification based on MS/MS match.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Lipids were extracted with chloroform/methanol (2:1, v/v), followed by phase separation and nitrogen drying.</p>	<p>Data was acquired using Orbitrap&nbsp;Exploris 240 mass spectrometer (Thermo, CA) coupled with UHPLC system Vanquish (Thermo, CA). &nbsp;The detailed mass spectrometer parameters were as follows: spray voltage, 3.2 kV for positive and 2.8 kV for negative; capillary temperature, 320 °C; sheath gas flow rate (arb),35; aux gas flow rate (arb),10; mass range(m/z), 240–2000 for positive and 200–2000 for negative. Full MS resolution, 60,000; MS/MS resolution, 15,000 ; top N,10; NCE,15/30/45; duty cycle,1.2s.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Mass tolerance for precursor and fragment was set to 8 ppm and 15 ppm respectively. The displayed m-score threshold was selected as 5 and grades A, B, C, D were all used for ID quality filter. All lipid classes in database including 71 sub-species were chosen for identification. Adducts of +H, +NH4 were applied for positive mode search and –H, +CH3COO were selected for negative mode since ammonium acetate was used in mobile phases. Only the lipids with chromatographic area &gt;5E6 were considered as confident identification. 0.25 min retention time shift was allowed for quantitation.</p>

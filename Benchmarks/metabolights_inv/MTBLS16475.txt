Study Protocol Description	<p>Rat brain tissues were collected and stored at −80 °C until analysis.</p>	<p>Chromatographic separation was performed on a Shimadzu Nexera X2 LC-30AD UHPLC system equipped with an ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (2.1 × 100 mm, 1.8 μm, Waters). The autosampler temperature was set at 4 ℃, injection volume 4 μL, column temperature 40 ℃, flow rate 0.3 mL/min. Mobile phase A was 0.1% formic acid in water, mobile phase B was acetonitrile. Gradient elution: 0–2 min, 0% B; 2–6 min, 0%–48% B; 6–10 min, 48%–100% B; 10–12 min, maintained at 100% B; 12–12.1 min, 100%–0% B; 12.1–15 min, maintained at 0% B.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were processed by MSDIAL software for peak alignment, retention-time correction and peak-area extraction. Metabolite annotation was performed by accurate-mass matching (mass tolerance &lt;10 ppm) and MS2 spectral matching against HMDB, MassBank, GNPS and in-house BP-DB database. Ion peaks with group missing value &gt;50% were discarded. Positive- and negative-mode data were normalized separately by total ion count, then merged. Unit variance scaling (UV) was applied for subsequent multivariate statistical analysis using Python.</p>	<p>Rat brain tissues were thawed at 4 ℃. 50 mg tissue sample was weighed for each specimen, two steel beads were added, followed by 400 μL pre-cooled methanol-water (4: 1, v/v). Samples were homogenized at low temperature using a tissue homogenizer. An additional 600 uL pre-cooled methanol-water (4: 1, v/v) was added and mixed. The mixture was ultrasonicated in ice-bath for 20 min and incubated at −20 ℃ for 1 h. Then samples were centrifuged at 16000 g, 4 ℃ for 20 min. The supernatant was collected and dried by vacuum centrifugal concentration. Prior to MS detection, the dried residue was reconstituted in 100 uL pre-cooled methanol-water (1: 1, v/v), centrifuged at 20000 g, 4 ℃ for 15 min, and the supernatant was used for injection. Pooled QC samples mixed equally from all experimental samples were prepared for system stability evaluation.</p>	<p>Mass spectrometric acquisition was carried out on a Thermo Q-Exactive Plus instrument with a HESI ion source. Data were collected in alternating positive and negative ionization modes. The MS scan range was m/z 75–1050. The top 10 most intense precursor ions were selected for dependent MS/MS acquisition.</p>	<p>Metabolite identification was carried out by combining accurate mass matching and MS/MS spectrum matching. The accurate mass tolerance was set to less than 10 ppm. Experimental fragmentation spectra were compared with reference spectra from HMDB, MassBank, GNPS and the in-house BP-DB database. Features with high matching scores were annotated as candidate metabolites.</p>

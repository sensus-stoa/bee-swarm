Study Identifier	MTBLS3485
Study Title	Phosphoglycolate phosphatase homologs act as glycerol-3-phosphate phosphatase to control stress and healthspan in C. elegans
Study Description	Metabolic stress due to nutrient excess and lipid accumulation is at the root of many age-associated disorders and the identification of therapeutic targets that mimic the beneficial effects of calorie restriction has clinical importance. Here, using C. elegans as a model organism, we study the roles of a recently discovered enzyme at the heart of metabolism in mammalian cells, glycerol-3-phosphate phosphatase (G3PP) (gene name Pgp) that hydrolyzes glucose-derived glycerol-3-phosphate to glycerol. We identify three Pgp homologues in C. elegans (pgph) and demonstrate in vivo that their protein products have G3PP activity, essential for glycerol synthesis. We demonstrate that PGPH/G3PP regulates the adaptation to various stresses, in particular hyperosmolarity and glucotoxicity. Enhanced G3PP activity reduces fat accumulation, promotes healthy aging and acts as a calorie restriction mimetic at normal food intake without altering fertility. Thus, PGP/G3PP can be considered as a target for age-related metabolic disorders.

Study Protocol Description	<p><strong>Salt treatment experiment:</strong></p><p>Wild-type and triple pgph mutant gravid adults (2 pellets of 250 μl) have been synchronized using bleaching technique. Worms have been arrested 12 h at the L1 stage then plated on Medium NGM plates seeded with OP50 <em>E.coli</em> bacteria. Worms were grown at 20 °C for 48 h. When the animals reached the L4/young adult stage, half the animals were collected with M9 buffer and replated on 400 mM NaCl containing plates for 2 h and the other half served as control.</p><p><br></p><p>Animals were the harvested washed with M9 buffer 3x adjusted to match plate salinity and then kept in pre-weighed bead-containing tubes. Pellets of approximately 200 μl were flash frozen in liquid nitrogen and kept for metabolites extraction at -80 degrees. For every independent experiment and repeat, we aimed for 4 biological samples from every group and the experiment has been repeated independently 2x.</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Glucose treatment experiment:</strong></p><p>Wild-type and triple pgph mutant gravid adults (2 pellets of 250 μl) have been synchronized using bleaching technique. Worms have been arrested 12 h at the L1 stage then plated either on Medium NGM plates seeded with OP50 E. coli bacteria (Control) or on plates supplemented with 2% glucose and seeded with OP50 E. coli. Worms were grown at 20 °C for 48 h. When the animals reached the L4/young adult stage, they were harvested and washed with M9 buffer 3x and then kept in pre-weighed bead-containing tubes. Pellets of approximately 200 μl were flash frozen in liquid nitrogen and kept for metabolites extraction at -80 °C. For every independent experiment and repeat, we aimed for 4 biological samples from every group and the experiment has been repeated independently 2x.</p><p><br></p><p>NGM plates and bleach solution have been prepared according to wormbook and standard worm protocols.</p>	<p>To frozen pellets, 4.25 volumes of extraction buffer (99% MeOH, 2mM Ammonium Acetate pH 9.0, 10µM IS-AMP) are added. Worms are homogenized first by bead beating in a cooled Precellys 24 (Bertin Technologies) using the following protocol: 6000 rpm, 2 x 25 s, 15 s pause; second by sonication at 150 W during 2 min (10 s on/10 s off) in a Cup Horn (Qsonica 431C2) filled with ice-cold water, attached to a Qsonica Q700 sonicator. Homogenates are then incubated on ice 15 min and centrifuged at 20,000 x g, 15 min at 4 °C. A portion of 800 µl of the supernatant is transferred into 12 x 75 mm glass tubes for liquid/liquid extraction (LLE). For LLE, 0.28 volumes of cold MilliQ water and 1.6 volumes of cold 3:1 CHCl3:Heptane are added to the glass tubes, which are vortex-mixed twice for 10 s, incubated 10 min on ice and centrifuged at 4,500 x g, 15 min at 4 °C. A portion of 700 µl of the upper aqueous phase is transferred into 1.5 ml screw cap PP tubes on ice and dried down in 2 steps: MeOH is first evaporated in a refrigerated CentriVap (Labconco) at 15 °C, then tubes are frozen in liquid nitrogen and freeze-dried.</p>	<p>Pellets are reconstituted in 30 µl of ice-cold MilliQ water. Tubes are centrifuged at 20,000 x g, 5 min at 4 °C and 20 µl of the supernatants transferred into glass inserts-equipped HPLC vials. Chromatographic separation is achieved on a Shimadzu Nexera X2 HPLC system at 0.75 ml/min using the following gradient: 0 min, 5% B; 0.69 min, 5% B; 3.05 min, 11% B; 3.64 min, 11% B; 5.41 min, 23% B; 8.65 min, 85.5% B; 8.66 min, 100% B; 10.12 min, 100% B; 10.13 min, 5% B to 12.6 min, where A is 10 mM terbutylamine, 15 mM acetic acid in water, pH 5.20, and B is acetonitrile 95% in water, 0.1% formic acid. Samples of 3 µl are injected and separated at 40 °C using a Poroshell 120 EC-C18 2.1 x 5 mm, 2.7 µm particles guard column (Agilent Technologies) followed by a Poroshell 120 EC-C18, 2.1 x 75 mm, 2.7 µm particles HPLC column.</p>	<p>Targeted ESI-MS/MS analyses are performed on a Sciex 4000 Qtrap with a Turbo V source in negative MRM mode using the following source parameters: CUR 30 psi; CAD High; IS -4500 V; TEM 725 °C; GS1 65 psi; GS2 70 psi. Transitions used are: pyruvate, 87.0/43.1; alanine, 87.9/87.9; leucine, 129.8/84.0; glutamine, 144.9/127.0; glutamate, 145.7/101.9; DHAP, 168.8/96.8; glycerol 3-phosphate, 170.9/62.9; lactate, 89.0/43.0; arginine, 173.0/130.9; adenosine, 265.9/133.9; glutathione, reduced, 305.9/271.7; GMP, 362.1/78.8; NAD, 662.2/540.2; aspartate, 131.9/88.0; acetoacetate, 100.8/56.9; succinate, 116.9/73.1; malate, 132.9/114.8; cAMP, 327.9/134.0; cGMP, 344.0/149.8; AMP, 345.9/78.9; IS-AMP 361.0/79.0; glutathione, oxidized, 611.3/306.4; oxaloacetate, 130.6/86.6; alpha-ketoglutarate, 144.9/57.1; GDP, 441.9/78.9; NADP, 742.2/620.0; isocitrate/citrate, 190.8/110.9; ADP, 426.0/78.9; NADH, 664.2/78.9; fumarate, 114.9/71.0; NADPH, 744.2/78.9; acetoacetyl-CoA, 850.2/78.8; ATP, 506.0/78.9; GTP, 521.9/78.9; acetyl-CoA, 808.7/79.0; HMG-CoA, 910.2/78.9; malonyl-CoA, 852.1/78.9; 2-phosphoglycolate, 154.8/78.8.</p>	<p>Raw peak areas were adjusted for IS-AMP internal standard peak area. When analytes were found in blanks, adjusted blank peak areas were subtracted from samples values. These values were then corrected to take into account volumes left over during extraction procedures and normalized to the protein content of the homogenates pellets. Values are expressed as relative abundance after adjustments and normalization.</p>	<p>This was a targeted analysis. A mix of pure standards was analyzed along the samples to verify retention times.</p>

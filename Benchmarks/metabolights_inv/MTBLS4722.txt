Study Identifier	MTBLS4722
Study Title	Limited nutrient availability in the tumor microenvironment renders pancreatic tumors sensitive to allosteric IDH1 inhibitors
Study Description	<p>Nutrient-deprived conditions in the tumor microenvironment (TME) restrain cancer cell viability due to increased free radicals and reduced energy production. In pancreatic cancer cells, a cytosolic metabolic enzyme, wild-type isocitrate dehydrogenase 1 (wtIDH1), enables the adaptation to these conditions. Under nutrient starvation, wtIDH1 oxidizes isocitrate to generate α-ketoglutarate (αKG) for anaplerosis, and NADPH to support antioxidant defense. In this study, we show that allosteric inhibitors of mutant IDH1 (mIDH1) are potent wtIDH1 inhibitors under conditions present in the TME. We demonstrate that low magnesium levels facilitate allosteric inhibition of wtIDH1, which is lethal to cancer cells when nutrients are limited. Furthermore, the FDA-approved mIDH1 inhibitor ivosidenib (AG-120) dramatically inhibited tumor growth in preclinical models of pancreatic cancer, highlighting this approach as a potential therapeutic strategy against wild-type IDH1 cancers.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p><strong>Cell lines, cell culture and reagents</strong></p><p>MiaPaCa-2 pancreatic cancer cells were obtained from ATCC (American Type Culture Collection) with catalog no. CRL-1420. Mycoplasma screening was performed using a MycoAlert detection kit (Lonza). Cell lines were maintained at 37 °C and 5% CO2. For standard cell culture, cells were grown in DMEM containing 25 mM glucose and 4 mM glutamine, and supplemented with 10% FBS, 1% penicillin/streptomycin and prophylactic doses of plasmocin (Life Technologies, MPP-01-03) to prevent mycoplasma infection. Glucose withdrawal was performed to simulate low glucose conditions in pancreatic cancer microenvironment. For low-glucose experiments, glucose-free DMEM (Life Technologies, 21013-024) was supplemented with 2.5 mM glucose, 10% FBS and penicillin-streptomycin.</p><p><br></p><p><strong>CRISPR/Cas9 editing of IDH1 in pancreatic cancer cells</strong></p><p><strong>IDH1-knockout</strong> was performed using a gRNA: GTAGATCCAATTCCACGTAGGG (Sigma, target ID, HS0000323225). A negative control plasmid (CRISPR06-1EA) was used in isogenic cells. Plasmid transfections were performed with lipofectamine 2000 (Life technology, 11668-027). After 48 h, EGFP-expressing cells were sorted by flow cytometry. Clones from parental cell line were expanded, and genomic DNA and protein were extracted for verification of IDH1 deletion. Herein, cells with <strong>IDH1 deletion</strong> and <strong>isogenic controls</strong> are referred to as <strong>IDH1-/-</strong> and <strong>IDH1+/+</strong>, respectively.</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Metabolic profiling</strong></p><p><strong>IDH1+/+</strong> and <strong>IDH1-/-</strong> cells under glucose withdrawal (2.5 mM glucose, 4 mM glutamine supplemented with 10% dialyzed FBS) for 48 h (individual biological replicates: <strong>IDH1+/+</strong>, n = 4; <strong>IDH1-/-</strong>, n = 3).</p>	<p>When the metabolites are ready to collect, cells were washed with cold PBS and residual extracellular media/PBS was completely removed from cell cultures. While plates on ice, 1 mL cold MeOH (80%) was added per well. After 10 min, the cell lysates were completely collected using a cell scraper into 1.5 mL tubes. The tubes were centrifuged at top speed, 4 °C for 5 min. The supernatant was transferred into another tube. A separate well was used to measure protein content for normalization.</p>	<p>LC-MS analyses were performed using an Agilent Technologies Triple Quad 6470 LC-MS/MS system with a 1290 Infinity II LC Flexible Pump (Quaternary Pump), 1290 Infinity II Multisampler and 1290 Infinity II Multicolumn Thermostat with 6 port valve.

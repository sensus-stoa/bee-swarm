Study Protocol Description	<p>Cabernet Sauvignon young wines vintage 2021 from San Vicente valley (Ensenada, Mexico) were collected from two different wineries (750 mL, bottled and sealed with natural cork, two bottles per tank). Three commercial yeasts were used for alcoholic fermentation (AF), WLP740 (White Labs, Inc., San Diego, CA, USA), ICV-D80 and ICV-D254 (LALLEMAND, Inc., Canada), and performed in stainless steel tanks.</p>	<p>10 mL of sample and 3 g of NaCl were added to 20 mL amber vial with an aluminum cap and an 18 mm blue PTFE/silicone septum (Agilent Technologies, Santa Clara, CA, USA). Sample extraction was performed with an autosampler PAL3 System (CTC Analytics AG, Zwingen, Switzerland), a head-space solid-phase micro-extraction (HS-SPME) module with 50/30 µm DVB/CAR/PDMS Stable Flex Supelco fiber at 40 °C/30 min after conditioning at 40 °C/5 min; desorption was at 240 °C/10 min in splitless mode</p>	<p>7890B GC System with a DB-WAX column (30 m/250 µm/0.25 µm) GC column (Agilent Technologies, Santa Clara, CA, USA). Flow rate was set at 0.8 mL/min (RT locked with 2-Undecanone at 35.461 min), oven initial temperature was at 40 °C for 5 min, increasing 3 °C/min to 180 °C, then 30 °C/min to 220 °c for 10 min</p>	<p>A 7200 mass spectrometer with quadrupole-time-to-flight (qTOF/MS) (Agilent Technologies, Santa Clara, CA, USA), detection was set in a mass range of 30 to 400 amu at an acquisition rate of 2.5 spectra/s with an electron ionization (EI) source at 230 °C in profile mode.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Extracted, aligned and identified components by recursive analysis were imported to Agilent MassHunter WorkStation Mass Profiler Professional version 15.0 to perform data interpretation and analysis. A completely randomized design was considered, samples were grouped by yeast (WLP740, ICV-D80 and ICV-D254), normalized with Median Fold Change (PQN) using PW and the baseline transformed with Pareto.&nbsp;</p>	<p>Accurate Mass Flavors Database (2) and NIST 17 were used to identify components and to create an internal library with Retention Time (RT), Retention Index (RI), compound name and mass spectrum with exact mass to ease component extraction, alignment and identification (level 2 and 4, as proposed by Sumner et al. 2007). Level 2 identification was assigned when match factor &gt;70, exact-mass accuracy &lt;20 pmm of mass spectrum, ΔRI&lt; 30, if one of these did not comply, the component was identified as Unknown + RI, which is a level 4 identification.&nbsp;</p>

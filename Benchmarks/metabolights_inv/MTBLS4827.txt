Study Identifier	MTBLS4827
Study Title	GC/MS data with mzQC: a versatile way to communicate quality information for biological mass spectrometry
Study Description	<p>Reliable quality control (QC) is essential for reproducible biological mass spectrometry, yet QC information is still typically stored in tool-specific, poorly interoperable formats that hinder comparison, reuse, and transparent reporting. mzQC is a community-developed standard from the HUPO Proteomics Standards Initiative for the compact, machine-readable representation of QC metrics and their associated metadata across mass spectrometry workflows. </p><p><br></p><p>We demonstrate the utility of mzQC across diverse analytical modalities and QC settings. This study contains the QC samples and measurements of nine standard compounds across several months of routine operation of the instrument, and the mzQC files obtained with a pipeline implemented with pyOpenMS. Raw data is available in vendor format, mzML and mzPeak.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The reference compounds were acquired from commercial vendors. No actual samples are included in this dataset.</p>	<p>The instrument was a Shimadzu GC-2010 Plus gas chromatograph equipped with a Restek Rxi-5Sil MS column (30 m x 0.25 mm, 0.25 µm) coupled to a Shimadzu GCMS-QP2010 Ultra mass spectrometer. Helium with a flow rate of 1 mL/min was used as carrier gas. The purge flow was set to 3 mL/min. The injection was done in splitless mode with a volume of 1 µL. The injector temperature was set to 250 °C. The temperature program was as follows: 1 min 50 °C, 50 °C -&gt; 330 °C with 25 K/min, 2.8 min 330 °C.</p>	<p>Data were saved in the Shimadzu instrument software as Agilent *.D format, and converted with Proteowizard msconvert version 3.0.25352 to *.mzML.The mzPeak files were converted from the *.mzML with mzml2mzpeak version 0.1.0&nbsp;</p>	<p>The reference mix was aliquoted and diluted from a master mix. No extraction was involved.</p>	<p>The instrument was a Shimadzu GCMS-QP2010 Ultra mass spectrometer coupled to a Shimadzu GC-2010 Plus gas chromatograph. The following settings were used for the mass spectrometer: interface temperature 250 °C, ion source temperature 200 °C, ionization voltage 70 V, emission current 60 µA, scan speed 3333 Da/sec, m/z range 50-450, threshold 150, solvent cut off 3 minutes.</p>	<p>The nine compounds in the reference mixture were identified based on their known GC/MS fragmentation pattern and retention time. </p>

Study Identifier	MTBLS570
Study Title	Metabolomic profiling of macrophages determines the discrete metabolomic signature and metabolomic interactome triggered by polarising immune stimuli
Study Description	Priming and activating immune stimuli have profound effects on macrophages, however, studies generally evaluate stimuli in isolation rather than in combination. In this study we have investigated the effects of pro-inflammatory and anti-inflammatory stimuli either alone or in combination on macrophage metabolism. These stimuli include host factors such as IFNγ and ovalbumin-immunoglobulin immune complexes, or pathogen factors such as LPS. Untargeted LC-MS based metabolomics provided an in-depth profile of the macrophage metabolome, and revealed specific changes in metabolite abundance upon either individual stimuli or combined stimuli. Here, by factoring in an interaction term in the linear model, we define the metabolome interactome. This approach allowed us to determine whether stimuli interact in a synergistic or antagonistic manner. In conclusion this study demonstrates a robust approach to interrogate immune-metabolism, especially systems that model host-pathogen interactions.

Study Protocol Description	Male C57 BL/6 mice, aged 8-12 weeks were used to generate bone marrow derived macrophages (BMDM). Approval for these procedures was given prior to their initiation by the University of Glasgow ethics committee and by the U.K. Home Office under License No. 60/4314. </p> On day 0, bones were cut at each end and a 23-gauge needle (Kenke Sass Wolf Fine-Ject®) was used to flush out bone marrow from leg bone with complete RPMI (RPMI (Thermo Fisher) supplemented with 2 mM L-glutamine, 1% (v/v) penicillin streptomycin solution (Sigma), 10% FBS (Thermo Fisher) :cRPMI) into a 9 cm Petri dish (Thermo Fisher). To obtain a single cell suspension, cells were passed through a 70 µM cell strainer (Easy strainer™ Greiner bio-one), pelleted by centrifugation (300 RCF, 5 min) and resuspended in cRMPI to obtain a density of 6-7 x 10^6 cells/ml. Cell number was calculated using Trypan blue exclusion (1:1 cells: Trypan blue (Sigma)) to ensure that >95% were viable. The BMDM were matured using 20% L929 supernatant over 7 days (5% CO2, 37 ºC). On day three, 5 ml cRMPI and 2 ml L929 supernatant were added. On day 6 BMDM were harvested. Medium was removed by aspiration and plates were washed once with warm dPBS to remove remaining non-adherent cells. Note that this wash was only done for experiments involving overnight IFNgamma treatments. To each plate 6 ml of ice-cold dPBS was added for 1-2 min. Cells were detached by gentle scraping, transferred to a 50 ml falcon tube. Plates rinsed once more with 6 ml of ice-cold dPBS, transferring the same 6 ml between plates to collect remaining cells. Cells were pelleted by centrifugation (300 RCF, 5 min), resuspended in cRMPI and viable cell number was calculated using Trypan blue exclusion. Cell density was then adjusted by dilution to desired density (10^6 cells/ml in this study). Next, cells were left to re-adhere overnight in tissue culture plates. Typically 6, and 98 flat well plates were used (Costar). Fore LCMS experiments 3 x 10^6 macrophages/well/replicate were seeded in a 6 well plate at a density of 10^6 cells/ml. The stimuli used were murine IFNgamma (Peprotech, 315-05), LPS (Sigma: E. coli 0111:B4, Ref L2630) and OIC. IFNgamma (100 U/ml: 0.02 µG/ml) was added for overnight stimulation, after which either LPS (100 nG/ml) or ova immune complexes (OIC) were added for 6 hr. To make ova immune complexes, 136.4 µg anti ova (Creative diagnostics: DPAB26522 polyclonal rabbit serpinb14)) and 13.63 µg albumin (Sigma: A3912) were dissolved in dPBS (Gibco, Magnesium and Calcium free), mixed and incubated at 37 ºC for 30 min prior to use. Note that LPS and OIC were added to wells without replacing medium (i.e. IFNgamma was not removed).	Samples were extracted in a volume of 400 µl chloroform/methanol/water, 1:3:1 (CMW). Medium was aspirated and cells were washed once with dPBS (Magnesium and Calcium free, no EDTA), aspirating immediately. Ice-cold extraction solvent (chloroform/methanol/water, 1:3:1) was added to each well, including an empty well (for solvent blank), and cells were left shaking (1 hr, 4 ºC). Solvent was then transferred to 1.5 ml micro-tubes, and centrifuged (18,000 RCF, 15 min, 4 ºC). Supernatant was immediately transferred to 2 ml screw-cap tubes. Sample from each tube was transferred to a new screw-cap tube for pooled sample. At this point, samples were capped with Argon, and then stored at -80 ºC until LC-MS analysis. To calculate protein levels post extraction, NaOH (0.1 M, 400 µl/10^6 cells) was added to each well. The plates were left shaking (15 min 4 ºC). Plates were next centrifuged (300 RCF, 5 min) and 10 µl of supernatant transferred to a 96 well plate. To the same plate, a 10 µl of a BSA (Sigma) dilution series (2 fold: 0.5-0 mg/ml in 0.1 M NaOH) was added. Finally, 190 µl of Bradford reagent (BioRad) was added to each plate on top of samples and standards. Absorbance was measured at 595 nm using a FLUOstar OPTIMA micro-plate reader.	All samples were separated with high performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) on a Dionex Ultimate 3000 RSLC system (Thermo) using ZIC-pHILIC (Merck) column. A 10 µl injection volume was used and samples were maintained at 4 ºC during analysis. A linear biphasic LC gradient was conducted from 80% B to 20% B over 26 min, where solvent B was acetonitrile and solvent A was 20 mM ammonium carbonate in water. The flow rate was 300 µl/min, and column temperature maintained at 25 ºC. Each sample was run in a randomized order, with a pooled sample run between every 4 samples.	The mass spectrometry platform used was a qExactive Orbitrap mass spectrometer (Thermo). Analysis was performed in positive and negative mode. The MS set up was calibrated [Thermo calmix (Pierce™ calibration solutions from Thermo Scientific) with masses at lower m/z; 74.0393 m/z (C2H6NO2: +ve) and 89.0244 (C3H5NO3: -ve)] in both ionization modes before analysis and a tune file targeted towards the lower m/z range was used. Full scan (MS1) data was acquired in both ionization modes in profile mode at 50,000 resolution (at m/z range 70-1400), an automatic gain control (AGC) target of 106 cts, with spray voltages +4.5 kV (capillary +50 V, tube: +70 kV, skimmer: +20 V) and -3.5 kV (capillary -50 V, tube: -70 kV, skimmer: -20 V), capillary temperature 275 ºC, probe temperature 150 ºC, sheath gas flow rate 40, auxiliary gas flow rate 5 a.u., and a sweep gas flow rate of 1 a.u. </p> For fragmentation the settings used were:</br> Full MS / dd-MS² (TopN)</br> General</br> Runtime: 0 to 46 min</br> Polarity: Positive</br> In-source CID: 0.0 eV</br> Default charge state: 1</br> Inclusion: -</br> Exclusion: -</br> Tags: -</br> Full MS</br> Microscans: 1</br> Resolution: 70,000</br> AGC target: 1e^6</br> Maximum IT: 250 ms</br> Number of scan ranges: 1</br> Scan range: 70 to 1050 m/z</br> Spectrum data type: Profile</br> dd-MS² / dd-SIM</br> Microscans: 1</br> Resolution: 35,000</br> AGC target: 2e^5</br> Maximum IT: 120 ms</br> Loop count: 5</br> MSX count: 1</br> TopN: 5</br> Isolation window: 4.0 m/z</br> Isolation offset: 0.0 m/z</br> Scan range: 200 to 2000 m/z</br> Fixed first mass: -</br> NCE / stepped NCE: 65</br> Spectrum data type: Profile</br> dd Settings</br> Underfill ratio: 20.0%</br> Intensity threshold: 3.3e^5</br> Apex trigger: -</br> Charge exclusion: -</br> Peptide match: Off</br> Exclude isotopes: on</br> Dynamic exclusion: 10.0 s</br> If idle ..: do not pick others</br> </p> Negative mode is the same but with Negative polarity. TopN is the number of features it fragments and NCE is the collision energy, it uses such a large isolation window as it is a quadrupole that picks the ions. There is no in-source CID. 10s dynamic exclusion so it doesn’t constantly fragment the same peak. The AGC is the automatic gain control, it collects a set number of ions and injects them into the orbitrap, the maximum IT is a hard limit on cycle time so it doesn’t sit idle if it can’t reach the AGC. Resolution is lower during MS2 to improve sensitivity.	Raw data files were converted to mzData format (splitting on polarity) using Proteowizard: pwiz\msconvert.exe (http://proteowizard.sourceforge.net/) and processed using the XCMS package (http://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/xcms.html). </p> XCMS settings for processing:</br> LC/MS data with centWave() were prefilter #points= 3 prefilter=(1000); Mzfif=0.001; snthr=3; ppm=2; peakwidth=(5,100). </p> mzMatch and IDEOM[1] software were used for untargeted analysis (R-3.1.2 x64). </p> Ref:</br> [1] Creek DJ, Jankevics A, Burgess KE, Breitling R, Barrett MP.

Study Identifier	MTBLS636
Study Title	Molecular structural diversity of mitochondrial cardiolipins
Study Description	Current strategies used to quantitatively describe the biological diversity of lipids by mass spectrometry are often limited in assessing the exact structural variability of individual molecular species in detail. A major challenge is represented by the extensive isobaric overlap present among lipids, hampering their accurate identification. This is especially true for cardiolipins, a mitochondria-specific class of phospholipids, which are functionally involved in many cellular functions, including energy metabolism, cristae structure, and apoptosis. Substituted with four fatty acyl side chains, cardiolipins offer a particularly high potential to achieve complex mixtures of molecular species. Here, we demonstrate how systematically generated high-performance liquid chromatography-mass spectral data can be utilized in a mathematical structural modeling approach, to comprehensively analyze and characterize the molecular diversity of mitochondrial cardiolipin compositions in cell culture and disease models, cardiolipin modulation experiments, and a broad variety of frequently studied model organisms.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Mammalian cell lines were grown under standard conditions. The study was conducted in accordance with the Helsinki Declaration of 1975, as revised in 2000, and was approved as part of the Biobank for Rare Diseases by the Ethics Committee of the Medical University Innsbruck, Austria (study no. UN4501) and experiments were performed after obtaining informed consent. Bacterial and fungal cells were grown from single colonies and pellets were collected by cold methanol precipitation or centrifugation. Plant and <em>S. scrofa</em> samples were obtained locally. <em>M. musculus</em> tissues were extracted from 8 week old C57BL/6J mice. <em>D. rerio</em> were collected as adults grown under standard conditions. <em>D. melanogaster</em> were collected after 7 days of growth under standard conditions. If not stated otherwise, the acquired biomass was homogenized in PBS.</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Media supplementation:</strong></p><p><em>Sus scrofa</em> heart tissue (purchased at the local butchery) was homogenized in 1 x PBS and lipids extracted following the Folch method with 3 x 2:1 chloroform:methanol. The collected organic phases were evaporated under N2 flow. A defined amount of lipid extract was mixed with Panserin 401 lipid-free medium and shook at 50 °C in a water bath overnight, and then sterilized with a 0.2 µm filter. HeLa cells were grown in 25 cm^2 flasks with either serum-free Panserin 401 without lipids or serum-free Panserin 401 with supplemented heart lipids for 3 days. 2-3 million cells were collected and resuspended in 300 µl mitochondrial respiration medium (Oroboros) for respiratory measurements.</p>	<p>Sample material was homogenized in water and an aliquot was withdrawn Bradford protein quantification. Lipids were then extracted following the Folch method with 2 x 500 µl 2:1 chloroform:methanol containing 500 nM CL(14:0)4 as ISTD. Organic phases were collected, evaporated under N2 flow, and dissolved in HPLC starting condition.</p>	<p>Lipids were separated by reversed-phase HPLC on a Dionex Ultimate 3000 HPLC (Thermo Fisher) using a Poroshell 120, EC-C8 column. Phase A: 10 mM ammonium formate and 0.2% formic acid in 6/4 acetonitrile/water. Phase B: 0 mM ammonium formate and 0.2% formic acid in 9/1 isopropanol/acetonitrile. Gradient: 0-5 min: 0.4 ml/min 62% B, 0.4 ml/min 5-12min: 62% - 74%B. 12-19 min: Washing step 0.6 ml/min 99% B. Lipid extracts were dissolved in 100 ul 62% Phase B and 38% Phase A.</p>	<p>A Thermo Velos Pro Dual-Pressure Linear Ion Trap Mass Spectrometer operated in negative mode and acquired MS/MS spectra on a data-dependent basis using a dynamic exclusion list. Eluting compounds were detected from 1000-1600 m/z at 3.8 kV, 20/5 auxilary/sheath gas, 275 °C and 38 CID energy.</p>	<p>Raw data (RAW-Format) was converted to the open mzML format (mzconvert from Proteowizard) and analyzed in MZMine2 (Version 2.2.21)<strong>[1]</strong>. The spectra were cropped to 1.8-12.5 min and baseline-corrected (TIC, 0.2 m/z bin width, 0.5 asym., 10^10 smoothing).</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Ref:</strong></p><p><strong>[1]</strong> Pluskal T, Castillo S, Villar-Briones A, Oresic M. MZmine 2: modular framework for processing, visualizing, and analyzing mass spectrometry-based molecular profile data. BMC Bioinformatics. 2010 Jul 23;11:395. doi:10.1186/1471-2105-11-395. PMID:20650010.</p>	<p>A template peak list with 135 CLs and 35 MLCL and their corresponding M+1 peaks was used for targeted peak integration and extraction.</p>

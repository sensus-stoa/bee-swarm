Study Identifier	MTBLS6366
Study Title	Single-cell time-resolved multi-omics reveal apoptotic and ferroptotic heterogeneity during foam cell formation
Study Description	<p>Macrophage-derived foam cell plays a pivotal role in the plaque formation and rupture during the progression of atherosclerosis. Foam cells are destined to divergent cell fate and functions in response to external stimuli based on their internal states, which however is hidden in the traditional studies based on population of cells. Herein, we used time-resolved and single-cell multi-omics to investigate the macrophage heterogeneity along foam cell formation. Dynamic metabolome and lipidome outlined the dual regulating axis of inflammation and ferroptosis. Single cell metabolomics and lipidomics further demonstrated a macrophage continuum featuring a differed susceptibility to apoptosis and ferroptosis. Using single-cell transcriptomic profiling, we verified the divergent cell fate toward apoptosis or ferroptosis. Therefore, the molecular choreography underlying the divergent cell fate during foam cell formation was revealed, which is of high significance for the understanding of the pathogenesis of atherosclerosis and development of new drug targets.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>THP-1 cells of low passage (passage 2 to 15, National collection of authenticated cell cultures, Beijing, China) were cultured in RPMI 1640 medium with 15% heat inactivated fetal bovine serum (Gibco, Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, United States) and 100 ug/mL penicillin and streptomycin (Gibco, Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, United States). THP-1 cells were used to mimic the monocytes (0T). THP-1 cells were then incubated in the complete medium containing 200 nM PMA (Sigma-Aldrich, St. Louis, MO, United States) for 24 h to differentiate into macrophages. THP-1 derived macrophages were subject to starvation in Eagle’s balanced salt solution EBSS containing 1 g/L glucose (Solarbio, Beijing, China) for 12 h. The macrophages were then incubated in 2% FBS medium containing 50 ug/mL oxLDL (Unionbiol, Beijing, China) or PBS. The macrophages were incubated with oxLDL for 24, 48 and 72 h to generate foam cells mimicking the early, middle and late stage of foam cells (2E, 3M and 4L, respectively). The macrophages incubated with PBS for 24 h were considered as the negative control (1C). The cells were all maintained in 37 °C and 5% CO2.</p>	<p><strong>Metabolomics</strong></p><p>Briefly, 5 groups of THP-1 derived cells with 3 biological replicates each were harvested for metabolite extraction. A sample of 2E^6&nbsp;cells was washed with PBS and quenched with ice-cold MeOH:H2O (80/20, v/v). The collected cells were then frozen in liquid nitrogen and thawed on dry ice for 3 times, followed by centrifugation at 11,500 x g, 10 min, -9 °C. The supernatant was collected and lyophilized overnight. The dry extracts were stored at -80 °C before MS analysis. The dried extracts were reconstituted in 160 uL of H2O:ACN:MeOH (2:1:1, v/v/v), sonicated for 1 min and centrifuged at 14,967 x g, 20 min, 4 uL. In total 155 uL of the supernatant was transferred to an LC auto-sampler vial (CNW technologies, Shanghai, China). A QC sample was prepared by mixing 10 uL of each sample and a blank sample was prepared by the solvent H2O:acetonitrile (ACN):MeOH (2:1:1, v/v/v), which were used to check the stability of the analytical conditions.</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Lipidomics</strong></p><p>Briefly, 5 groups of THP-1 derived cells with 3 biological replicates each were harvested for metabolite extraction. A sample of 2E^6&nbsp;cells was harvested, washed with PBS and freeze-thawed 3 times. And 625 uL of methanol:chloroform:formic acid (10:10:1, v/v/v) was mixed with the lysate. An internal standard cocktail containing isotope-labelled lipid standards of PC, TG, SM species, cholesterol, etc (Avanti Lipids Polar, 330707, Birmingham, AL, USA) was added to the sample according to the cell count. Next 275 uL of water solution containing 0.2 M phosphoric acid and 1 M potassium chloride were added to the mixture and stored overnight. The mixture was centrifuged at 1288 x g for 5 min at room temperature. The organic phase was collected and dried on ice under nitrogen flow. Quartz glass containers were used throughout the process to avoid polyenthylene glycol pollution. The dry extracts were stored at -80 °C before MS analysis. The dried extracts were reconstituted in 100 uL of H2O:ACN:isopropanol (IPA) (1:2:2, v/v/v), sonicated for 1 min and centrifuged at 14,967 x g, 20 min, 4 °C. In total 95 uL of the supernatant was transferred to an LC auto-sampler vial (CNW technologies, Shanghai, China). A QC sample was prepared by mixing 10 uL of each sample treated as above and a blank sample was prepared by the solvent H2O:ACN:IPA (1:2:2, v/v/v).</p>	<p><strong>Metabolomics</strong></p><p>Shimadzu LC-20AD XR high performance liquid chromatography system was used. The separation was performed using an Acquity UPLC HSS T3 column (100 x 2.1 mm, 1.8 µm, Waters, UK) in binary gradient mode. The mobile phase in different ionization modes were as follows: (A) 0.1% (v/v) formic acid in water and (B) pure ACN in the positive ion mode; (A) pure water and (B) pure ACN in the negative ion mode. The flow rate was 200 μL/min. In the positive ion mode, an optimized gradient of 0-5 min (3-10% B), 5-10 min (10-50% B), 10-20 min (50-90% B), 20-24 min (90% B) with a 5-min re-equilibration was applied. In the negative ion mode, an optimized gradient of 0-5 min (3-6% B), 5-10 min (6-50% B), 10-20 min (50-90% B), 20-24 min (90% B) with a 5-min re-equilibration was applied.</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Lipidomics</strong></p><p>Shimadzu LC-20AD XR high performance liquid chromatography system was used. The separation was performed using an Acquity UPLC HSS T3 column (100 x 2.1 mm, 1.8 µm, Waters, UK) in binary gradient mode. The mobile phase in different ionization modes were as follows: (A) 10 mM ammonium formate in ACN:H2O (60:40, v/v), (B) 10 mM ammonium formate in H2O:ACN:IPA (1:10:90, v/v/v) in the negative ion mode; 0.1% (v/v) formic acid was added to both A and B phases in the positive ionization mode. The flow rate was 180 μL/min. In both ionization modes, an optimized gradient of 0-0.5 min (40-45% B), 0.5-1 min (45-50% B), 1-9 min (50-85% B), 9-18 min (85-99% B), 18-20 min (99% B) with a 5-min re-equilibration was applied.</p>	<p><strong>Metabolomics</strong></p><p>AB Sciex TripleTOF 4600 mass spectrometer was used. The mass scanning range was 50-1000 m/z and the collision energy was set to 40 eV for positive mode and 30 eV for negative mode. The data-dependent acquisition (DDA) cycle took 1.3 s with a full MS scan for 0.25 s and at most 10 MS/MS spectra acquisition for 0.1 s each.</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Lipidomics</strong></p><p>AB Sciex TripleTOF 4600 mass spectrometer was used. The mass scanning range was 350-1000 m/z and the collision energy was set to 30 eV for both ionization modes. The data-dependent acquisition (DDA) cycle took 1.3 s with a full MS scan for 0.25 s and at most 10 MS/MS spectra acquisition for 0.1 s each.</p><p><br></p><p>Samples of different cohorts were placed in a randomised order. One QC sample and one solvent were injected after each of 5 injections of cell samples.</p>	<p>Metabolites identified using the <strong>xcms-MetDNA</strong> pipeline. The population metabolomic dataset was sum normalized. Lipid sepcies identified using the <strong>xcms-LipidIMMS</strong> pipeline. Lipid species were normalized by their corresponding internal standards and then by sum. One way-ANOVA analysis and PLSDA analysis were carried out using <strong>MetaboAnalyst</strong> 5.0 (https://www.metaboanalyst.ca). Fuzzy c-mean clustering was carried out by the <strong>R</strong> package <strong>M-fuzz</strong> (v2.48.0) after z-score scaling of the metabolomic and lipidomic dataset.</p>	<p>For the LC-MS/MS analysis, the structural identification of metabolites and lipids was performed by the <strong>MetDNA</strong> web server (http://metdna.zhulab.cn/) and the <strong>LipidIMMS</strong> web server (http://imms.zhulab.cn/LipidIMMS/), respectively. For the lipid identification, we used the retention time of internal standards to predict the subclass-specific elution range and applied the equivalent carbon number model to predict the local elution window for certain lipid species. We filtered the annotated lipids with a threshold of RT error = 100 s.</p><p><br></p><p>For the single cell MS analysis, the tentative annotation was performed by searching the exact mass acquired against the library constructed by bulk metabolomic and lipidomic analysis. The mass error was set to 0.01 Da.</p>

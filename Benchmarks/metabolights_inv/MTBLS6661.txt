Study Identifier	MTBLS6661
Study Title	Extracellular-acidosis restricts one-carbon metabolism and preserves T cell stemness
Study Description	The accumulation of acidic metabolic waste products within the tumor microenvironment profoundly inhibits effector functions of tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs). However, it is unclear whether extracellular-acidosis impacts T cell metabolic fitness and differentiation status. Here, we surprisingly found that prolonged acid exposure reprogrammed T cell intracellular metabolism and mitochondrial fitness, and preserved T cell stemness. Elevated extracellular-acidosis impaired methionine uptake and metabolism via downregulating SLC7A5, therefore altering H3K27me3 deposition at the promoters of crucial T cell stemness genes. These changes promoted the maintenance of a “stem-like memory” state and improved long-term in vivo persistence and anti-tumor efficacy. Our findings not only reveal the unexpected roles of extracellular-acidosis in the maintenance of stem-like properties of T cells, but also advance our understanding of the direct involvement of methionine metabolism in T cell stemness.

Study Protocol Description	<p>Metabolomic analysis and sample collection were performed as in previous reports<strong>[1]</strong>. In brief, Human peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) were activated individually in 24-well plates by human anti-CD3/CD28 for 3 d, and cultured in RPMI 1640 medium with control or lactic acid to day 12. 8 x 10^6 cells per sample (n = 4 independent samples per group) were collected</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Ref:</strong></p><p><strong>[1]</strong> Sellick CA, Hansen R, Stephens GM, Goodacre R, Dickson AJ. Metabolite extraction from suspension-cultured mammalian cells for global metabolite profiling. Nat Protoc. 2011 Jul 28;6(8):1241-9. doi:10.1038/nprot.2011.366. PMID:21799492.</p>	<p>The collected samples were transferred to a 1.5 ml tube for centrifugation at 300 x g for 5 min at 4 °C, and then washed with cold PBS. After centrifugation at 300 x g for 5 min again, 80% cold methanol was added and vigorously vortexed to disrupt the cell pellet completely, and then the cells were transferred to a freezer at -80 °C. Samples were centrifuged at 12,000 x g for 10 min at 4 °C and the supernatant collected. Equal volumes from each experimental sample were taken and mixed together to make pooled QC samples. 53% methanol aqueous solution replaced the experimental sample, and the pretreatment process was the same as that of the experimental sample. Finally, the extracts were lyophilized and analyzed by <strong>UHPLC-MS/MS</strong> in Novogene Co., Ltd. (Beijing, China).</p>	<p>The analyses were performed using a Vanquish UHPLC system (ThermoFisher, Germany) coupled to a Orbitrap Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher, Germany). Samples were injected onto a Hypersil Gold column (100 x 2.1 mm, 1.9μm) using a 17-min linear gradient at a flow rate of 0.2 ml/min. The eluents for the positive polarity mode were eluent A (0.1% Formic acid in Water) and eluent B (Methanol). The eluents for the negative polarity mode were eluent A (5 mM ammonium acetate, pH 9.0) and eluent B (Methanol). The solvent gradient was set as follows: 2% B, 1.5 min; 2-85% B, 3 min; 100% B, 10 min; 100-2% B, 10.1 min; 2% B, 12 min.</p>	<p>The analyses were performed using an Orbitrap Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher, Germany) in Novogene Co., Ltd. (Beijing, China) operated in positive/negative polarity mode with m/z range of 100-1500, spray voltage of 3.5 kV, capillary temperature of 320 °C, sheath gas flow rate of 35 psi and aux gas flow rate of 10 L/min, S-lens RF level of 60, Aux gas heater temperature of 350 °C.</p>	<p>The raw data files generated by <strong>UHPLC-MS/MS</strong> were processed using the <strong>Compound Discoverer v3.1 (ThermoFisher)</strong> to perform peak alignment, peak picking, and quantitation for each metabolite. The main parameters were set as follows: retention time tolerance, 0.2 min; actual mass tolerance, 5 ppm; signal intensity tolerance, 30%; signal/noise ratio, 3. After that, peak intensities were normalized to the total spectral intensity. The normalized data was used to predict the molecular formula based on additive ions, molecular ion peaks and fragment ions. <strong>Metaboanalyst v5.0</strong> software was used for further data analysis, and then significantly enriched pathways were screened by <strong>P-value</strong> &lt; 0.05.</p>	<p>The peaks were matched with the <strong>mzCloud</strong> (<a href='https://www.mzcloud.org/' rel='noopener noreferrer' target='_blank'>https://www.mzcloud.org/</a>), <strong>mzVault</strong> and <strong>MassList</strong> database to obtain the accurate qualitative and relative quantitative results.</p><p><br></p><p><strong>Statistical analysis:</strong></p><p>Statistical analysis was performed using <strong>GraphPad Prism version 8.0</strong>. Results are displayed as mean ± SEM. <strong>2-tailed student’s t-tests</strong> was used to compare treatments with control groups. <strong>2-way ANOVA with Tukey’s multiple-comparisons test</strong> was applied for multiple comparisons. <strong>P value</strong> &lt; 0.05 was considered as statistically significant.</p>

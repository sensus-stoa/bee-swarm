Study Title	Gut microbiota ameliorates hepatic fibrosis through modulating intestinal bile acid metabolism and hepatocyte pyroptosis (Hepatic fibrosis feces)
Study Description	<p>Hepatic fibrosis decreased feces bile acid level. Hepatic fibrosis increased serum bile acid. This result showed that hepatic fibrosis inhibited the excretion of bile acid in clinical patients. This result provided phenotype for hepatic fibrosis patients. Bile acid was important indexes for liver injury. TCDCA was important, and it play toxicity effect through ferroptosis and apoptosis. Study Protocol Description	<p><strong>Sample 2: </strong></p><p>In total 17 patients with hepatic fibrosis and 10 healthy volunteers were recruited to collect fecal samples.&nbsp;All volunteers were evaluated by clinicians during the study ensuring that they remained in good health. Written informed consents were obtained from each participant.</p>	<p>Briefly, 20 mg of feces were mixed with 200 µL of 50% aqueous acetonitrile containing 5 µM chlorpropamide (internal standard). After shaking at room temperature for 20 min, the samples were centrifuged at 15,000 rpm for 20 min to obtain feces content extract supernatants. Subsequently, 100 µL of supernatant was transferred to a new Eppendorf vial and diluted with 200 µL of 50% aqueous acetonitrile. After centrifugation at 15,000 rpm for 20 min, a 5 µL aliquot of the supernatant was injected into the the Vanquish UHPLC-Q Exactive plus MS.</p>	<p>The liquid chromatography system obtained a Vanquish autosampler, Vanquish detector, Vanquish pump (Thermo Fisher, USA) equipped with a ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (2.1 x 100 mm, 1.8 μM, Waters, USA). The mobile phase comprised 0.1 % aqueous formic acid and acetonitrile containing 0.1% formic acid. The flow rate was 0.3 mL/min with a gradient ranging from 2% to 98% acetonitrile in 17 min run.</p>	<p>The liquid chromatography system obtained a Vanquish autosampler, Vanquish detector, Vanquish pump (Thermo Fisher, USA) equipped with a ACQUITY UPLC HSS T3 column (2.1 x 100 mm, 1.8 μM, Waters, USA). The Vanquish UHPLC-Q Exactive plus MS (Thermo Fisher, USA) was collected in both positive and negative modes, which was operated in full-scan mode at 60-900 m/z.</p>	<p>Bile acid target analysis was carried with Thermo Xcalibur Qual Browser (Thermo Fisher). Principal component analysis (PCA) and orthogonal projection to latent structures-discriminant analysis (OPLS-DA) were carried out using SIMCAP 13.0 (Umetrics, Kinnelon, NJ).</p>	<p>The chemical structures of the altered metabolites were identified by authentic standard and MS/MS fragmentation patterns.</p>

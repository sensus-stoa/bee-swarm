Study Identifier	MTBLS7120
Study Title	SOX2 Drives Esophageal Squamous Carcinoma by Reprogramming Lipid Metabolism and Histone Acetylation Landscape
Study Description	<p>SOX2 is not only a pioneer transcription factor with critical roles in stem cell function and cell reprogramming but also a potent oncogene for&nbsp;a multitude of squamous cancers.&nbsp;How SOX2 exerts a potent oncogenic activity in squamous lineage is largely unknown. Here we uncovered SOX2 a potent role in promoting global histone acetylation and super-enhancer formation in esophageal squamous cancer cells. Metabolic analysis revealed a role of SOX2 in suppressing fatty acid synthesis. SOX2 promotes global histone acetylation by two distinct mechanisms: stimulating the expression of a panel of histone acetyltransferases and repressing the expression of ACSL5, which down-regulates fatty acid synthesis and thus channels acetyl-CoA toward histone acetylation. Notably, we found that SOX2 enhanced histone acetylation is required for the formation of most squamous lineage-specific super-enhancers. Our findings provide new explanation for why SOX2 is a powerful cell fate reprogrammer and potent oncogene for squamous lineage cancers.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>The cell samples were corrected to the consistent cell number (5 x 10^6) in 1.6 mL of 80% (v/v) methanol:HPLC water solution to each tube. Then, the sample was processed by 7 cycles of 2 min ultra-sonication and 2 min interval in ice-bath. The cell lysates were kept at 4 °C for 1 h prior to centrifugation at 14,000 x g and 4 °C for 15 min. Totally, all of supernatant was evaporated to dryness with the vacuum centrifuge.</p>	<p>Following treatment, 300 µL of precooled pure methanol with internal standard was added to each dish, cells were collected by scraping then freeze-thawed in liquid nitrogen 3 times, and sonicated for 3 min on ice. Next 1 mL MTBE and 300&nbsp;µL water were added to each sample separately, followed by vortex for 20 min and 5 min respectively. The mix was then centrifuged for 10 min (12,000 x g, 6 °C). After centrifuging, the mix was layered and the upper layer was lipid enrichment (approximately 1 mL).&nbsp;Then 400 uL&nbsp;of the upper layer’s supernatant was taken from each sample, and a small amount of supernatant from each sample was mixed together as QC. The supernatant&nbsp;was&nbsp;dried with a nitrogen blower at room temperature and the dry lipids were stored at -80 °C&nbsp;until ready to be analyzed. Before LC-MS&nbsp;analysis, lipids were resuspended in 50 µL of solvent (including 10 µL of solubilizing solvent and 40 µL of diluent), vortexed for 30 s and centrifuged to take 40 µL of the supernatant. Solubilizing solvent: CH2Cl2:MeOH=2:1. Diluent: ACN:IPA:H2O=65:30:5 with 5 mM ammonium acetate.</p>	<p>LC-MS&nbsp;analyses were performed using an Ultimate 3000 HPLC &amp; Thermo Q-Exactive system. In total 3 uL of sample were injected for each analysis. Reverse phase gradient elution chromatography was used for separations&nbsp;(waters C8 column (2.1 x 100 mm, 1.7 µm) with pre-column). Mobile phase A was composed of 40% water and 60% acetonitrile. Mobile phase B was composed of 90% isopropanol and 10% acetonitrile. 10 mM ammonium formate was&nbsp;added to both mobile phases to improve ionization. LC began with 1.5 min of 32% B at 0.3 mL/min, the mobile phase gradient began from 32% B to 85% B over 14 min, then from 85% B to 97% B only 0.1 min. Maintained at 97% B for 2.4 min, then switched to 32% B for 0.1 min and maintained 1.9 min.</p>	<p>LC-MS&nbsp;analyses were performed using an Ultimate 3000 HPLC &amp; Thermo Q-Exactive system. An&nbsp;electrospray ionization (ESI) source was used for MS analysis&nbsp;under alternating mode,&nbsp;with a capillary voltage of 3000 V for positive and 2400 V for negative. The vaporizer&nbsp;temperature was 350 °C. Mass&nbsp;range was&nbsp;from 150&nbsp;to 2000 m/z.</p>	<p>Raw LC-MS data were imported into Tracefinder software for peak picking&nbsp;and integration.&nbsp;The lipids with peaks less than 80% of the total number of samples were not retained. The response values of different lipids in each sample were derived, and finally,&nbsp;the data of protein quantification were used to normalize the data.</p>	<p>For untargeted lipidomics analysis,&nbsp;lipids were identified with the assist of internal standards and MS-DIAL software. Lipid retention time drift correction was performed using QC samples.</p>

Study Protocol Description	<p>Blood samples were collected via vein puncture and then centrifuged at 3000 x g for 10 min.&nbsp;</p>	<p>After the samples were thawed slowly at 4 °C, 50 μL samples were added to 4 times the volume of pre-cooled methanol:acetonitrile:water solution (2:2:1), vorticated for 30 s, placed at -20 °C for 1 h protein precipitation, 14,000 x g, centrifuged at 4 °C for 10 min, supernatant was taken into a new EP tube and vacuum dried at room temperature. Samples are stored at -80 °C. Preparation of quality control samples (QC): samples of equal volume are used to prepare QC samples. QC samples were prepared in parallel according to the above methods, freeze-dried and stored at low temperature for future use. It is used to determine the state of the instrument before injection and to balance the chromatography-mass spectrometry system, and to evaluate the stability of the system during the whole experiment.</p>	<p>Samples were separated using Nexera UHPLC LC-30A, an ultra-high performance liquid phase system with a microrise flow rate. The column was initially balanced with 98% mobile phase A. Samples were then transported to a HILIC column (Waters, ACQUITY UPLC BEH Amide 1.7 μm, 2.1 x 100 mm column) with an automatic injector at a flow rate of 0.3 mL/min. The elution gradient is as follows: the elution gradient starts at 2% mobile phase B for 0.5 min and rises to 98% mobile phase B for the following 11.5 min. After 98% of mobile phase B lasted for 4 min, 0.1 min of time was adjusted to 2% of mobile phase B, and continued cleaning for 1.9 min.</p>	<p>The samples were separated by analytical column and analyzed by Q Exactive HF-X mass spectrometer. In positive ion mode: parent ion scanning range, 70-1050 m/z; primary mass spectrometry resolution, 120,000; Automatic gain control (AGC) target, 3e^6; Maximum IT, 100 ms. Secondary mass spectrum resolution, 7500; Automatic gain control (AGC) target, 2e^5; Maximum IT, 50 ms, using HCD fragmentation mode; Normalized Collision Energy, 20, 40, 60; isolation window, 1.5 m/z; daughter ion scanning range, 200-2000 m/z. In negative ion mode: parent ion scanning range, 70-1050 m/z; primary mass spectrometry resolution, 120,000; Automatic gain control (AGC) target, 3e^6; Maximum IT, 200 ms. Secondary mass spectrum resolution, 7500; Automatic gain control (AGC) target, 2e^5; Maximum IT, 50 ms, using HCD fragmentation mode; Normalized Collision Energy, 20, 40, 60; isolation window, 1.5 m/z, daughter ion scanning range, 200-2000 m/z.</p>	<p>The original data (RAW file) collected by mass spectrometry was retrieved from the database by Progenesis_QI software, and the identification information of the sample was finally obtained.</p>	<p>Non-targeted analysis mode is usually established on the high-resolution mass spectrometer, relying on its powerful high-resolution mass analyzer, which can detect all kinds of metabolites in samples without bias, large-scale and systematic, and reflect the disturbance of metabolic level in organisms to the greatest extent, so it is suitable for the basic research in the early stage of the project.</p>
